=== Topic: Solving multiplication and division problems using repeated addition, grouping, sharing, scaling, and bar models ===
Domain: Multiplication and Division

--- Repeated addition ---

Question 1: There are 4 plates. Each plate has 5 biscuits. Write the repeated addition sentence and find the total number of biscuits.
Answer: 5 + 5 + 5 + 5 = 20
Working: Add 5 four times: 5 + 5 + 5 + 5 = 20 biscuits.

Question 2: There are 6 pairs of socks drying on a line. How many socks are there in total? Use repeated addition to find the answer.
Answer: 12
Working: Each pair has 2 socks. 2 + 2 + 2 + 2 + 2 + 2 = 12 socks.

Question 3: A packet holds 10 felt pens. Noah buys 3 packets. How many felt pens does Noah have altogether?
Answer: 30
Working: Noah has 3 groups of 10: 10 + 10 + 10 = 30 pens.

Question 4: A frog makes 7 equal jumps of 5 cm along a path. How far has the frog jumped in total?
Answer: 35 cm
Working: Add 5 seven times: 5 + 5 + 5 + 5 + 5 + 5 + 5 = 35 cm.

--- Grouping ---

Question 5: There are 20 stickers. They are put into bags of 5 stickers each. How many bags can be made?
Answer: 4
Working: Group 20 into 5s: 20 ÷ 5 = 4 bags.

Question 6: Priya has 18 crayons. She puts 2 crayons into each pot. How many pots does she fill?
Answer: 9
Working: Group 18 into 2s: 18 ÷ 2 = 9 pots.

Question 7: A teacher has 40 counters. She gives 10 counters to each group of children. How many groups get counters?
Answer: 4
Working: Group 40 into 10s: 40 ÷ 10 = 4 groups.

Question 8: A baker has 45 bread rolls. He packs them into bags of 5 rolls. How many bags of bread rolls does the baker make?
Answer: 9
Working: Count in 5s up to 45: 45 ÷ 5 = 9 bags.

--- Sharing ---

Question 9: 14 strawberries are shared equally between 2 children. How many strawberries does each child get?
Answer: 7
Working: Share 14 equally into 2 parts: 14 ÷ 2 = 7 strawberries.

Question 10: 25 apples are shared equally into 5 baskets. How many apples are in each basket?
Answer: 5
Working: Share 25 into 5 equal groups: 25 ÷ 5 = 5 apples.

Question 11: 30 pencils are shared equally among 10 pencil cases. How many pencils are put into each pencil case?
Answer: 3
Working: Share 30 into 10 equal parts: 30 ÷ 10 = 3 pencils.

Question 12: 50 millilitres of juice is poured equally into 5 cups. How many millilitres of juice are in each cup?
Answer: 10 ml
Working: Share 50 ml equally into 5 parts: 50 ÷ 5 = 10 ml.

--- Scaling ---

Question 13: A blue ribbon is 2 cm long. A red ribbon is 5 times as long as the blue ribbon. How long is the red ribbon?
Answer: 10 cm
Working: Scale 2 cm by 5: 5 × 2 = 10 cm.

Question 14: Bag A balances 2 weights on a balance scale. Bag B is 5 times as heavy as Bag A. How many weights does Bag B balance?
Answer: 10
Working: Multiply 2 weights by 5: 5 × 2 = 10 weights.

Question 15: Lucas has 10 marbles. Amy has 4 times as many marbles as Lucas. How many marbles does Amy have?
Answer: 40
Working: 4 times 10 marbles: 4 × 10 = 40 marbles.

Question 16: A small sunflower is 5 cm tall. A giant sunflower is 10 times as tall as the small sunflower. How tall is the giant sunflower?
Answer: 50 cm
Working: Multiply 5 cm by 10: 10 × 5 = 50 cm.

--- Bar models ---

Question 17: A bar model has 4 equal parts. Each part has a value of 10. What is the total value of the whole bar?
Answer: 40
Working: There are 4 parts of 10: 4 × 10 = 40.

Question 18: A bar model shows a total of 16 split into 2 equal parts. What is the value of each part?
Answer: 8
Working: Divide the total 16 into 2 equal parts: 16 ÷ 2 = 8.

Question 19: A bar model has 6 equal parts. Each part contains the number 5. What is the whole number shown by the bar?
Answer: 30
Working: 6 parts of 5: 6 × 5 = 30.

Question 20: A bar model has a total value of 35 and is split into 5 equal parts. What number goes into each equal part?
Answer: 7
Working: Divide the whole of 35 by 5 parts: 35 ÷ 5 = 7.
