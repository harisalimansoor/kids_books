=== Topic: Place value of two-digit numbers and flexible standard and non-standard partitioning ===
Domain: Number and Place Value

--- Place value of two-digit numbers ---

Question 1: What is the value of the digit 7 in the number 74?
Answer: 70
Working: In 74, the digit 7 is in the tens place, so its value is 7 tens or 70.

Question 2: In the number 83, which digit is in the ones place?
Answer: 3
Working: In 83, 8 is in the tens place and 3 is in the ones place.

Question 3: A number has 6 tens and 5 ones. What is the number?
Answer: 65
Working: 6 tens is 60. 60 + 5 = 65.

Question 4: A two-digit number has 9 ones and 4 tens. What is the number?
Answer: 49
Working: 4 tens is 40 and 9 ones is 9, making 49.

Question 5: How many tens are in the number 90?
Answer: 9
Working: 90 is equal to 9 tens and 0 ones, so there are 9 tens.

--- Standard partitioning ---

Question 6: Complete the number sentence: 56 = 50 + ____
Answer: 6
Working: 56 is partitioned into 50 and 6.

Question 7: Complete the number sentence: 87 = ____ + 7
Answer: 80
Working: 87 is made of 8 tens (80) and 7 ones, so 87 = 80 + 7.

Question 8: A number is partitioned into 40 and 8. What is the whole number?
Answer: 48
Working: 40 + 8 = 48.

Question 9: Complete the number sentence: 91 = 90 + ____
Answer: 1
Working: 91 is 9 tens and 1 one, so 91 = 90 + 1.

--- Flexible non-standard partitioning ---

Question 10: Complete the number sentence: 43 = 30 + ____
Answer: 13
Working: 43 - 30 = 13, so 43 = 30 + 13.

Question 11: Complete the number sentence: 65 = 40 + ____
Answer: 25
Working: 65 - 40 = 25, so 65 = 40 + 25.

Question 12: A number is partitioned into 2 tens and 17 ones. What is the number?
Answer: 37
Working: 2 tens is 20. 20 + 17 = 37.

Question 13: 52 can be partitioned as 50 + 2 and as 40 + 12. Complete the number sentence to show another way: 52 = 30 + ____
Answer: 22
Working: 52 - 30 = 22, so 52 = 30 + 22.

Question 14: The number 71 is partitioned into some tens and 21 ones. How many tens are there?
Answer: 5
Working: 71 - 21 = 50. 50 is equal to 5 tens.

Question 15: Complete the missing number: 84 = ____ + 14
Answer: 70
Working: 84 - 14 = 70, so 84 = 70 + 14.
