=== Topic: Reading and writing numbers to at least 100 in numerals and in words ===
Domain: Number and Place Value

--- Reading and writing numbers to at least 100 in numerals ---

Question 1: Write the number forty-six in numerals.
Answer: 46
Working: Forty is 4 tens (40) and six is 6 ones (6). Combining them gives 46.

Question 2: Write the number nineteen in numerals.
Answer: 19
Working: Nineteen is 1 ten and 9 ones, which is written as 19.

Question 3: Write the number seventy in numerals.
Answer: 70
Working: Seventy is 7 tens and 0 ones, which is written as 70.

Question 4: Write the number one hundred in numerals.
Answer: 100
Working: One hundred is written as the numeral 100.

Question 5: A reading book has ninety-two pages. Write ninety-two in numerals.
Answer: 92
Working: Ninety is 9 tens and two is 2 ones. Written as a numeral, this is 92.

Question 6: A number has 6 tens and 5 ones. Write this number in numerals.
Answer: 65
Working: 6 tens = 60 and 5 ones = 5. 60 + 5 = 65.

Question 7: Write the number eighty-four in digits.
Answer: 84
Working: Eighty is 8 tens and four is 4 ones. In digits, this is 84.

Question 8: A sticker costs thirty-seven pence. Write thirty-seven in numerals.
Answer: 37
Working: Thirty is 3 tens and seven is 7 ones. Written in numerals, it is 37.

--- Reading and writing numbers to at least 100 in words ---

Question 9: Write the number 35 in words.
Answer: thirty-five
Working: 3 tens is thirty and 5 ones is five, so 35 in words is thirty-five.

Question 10: Write the number 16 in words.
Answer: sixteen
Working: 16 in words is sixteen.

Question 11: Write the number 60 in words.
Answer: sixty
Working: 6 tens and 0 ones is sixty.

Question 12: Write the number 100 in words.
Answer: one hundred
Working: 100 is written in words as one hundred.

Question 13: A birthday badge has the number 42 on it. Write 42 in words.
Answer: forty-two
Working: 4 tens is forty and 2 ones is two, so 42 in words is forty-two.

Question 14: There are 27 pencils in a pot. Write 27 in words.
Answer: twenty-seven
Working: 2 tens is twenty and 7 ones is seven, so 27 in words is twenty-seven.

Question 15: A number is made of 8 tens and 3 ones. Write this number in words.
Answer: eighty-three
Working: 8 tens is 80 (eighty) and 3 ones is 3 (three). The number is eighty-three.

Question 16: Write the number 94 in words.
Answer: ninety-four
Working: 9 tens is ninety and 4 ones is four, so 94 is ninety-four.

Question 17: Ben has 51 stickers in his album. Write 51 in words.
Answer: fifty-one
Working: 5 tens is fifty and 1 one is one, so 51 is fifty-one.

Question 18: A number is 10 more than 65. Write this new number in words.
Answer: seventy-five
Working: 10 more than 65 is 75. In words, 75 is seventy-five.
