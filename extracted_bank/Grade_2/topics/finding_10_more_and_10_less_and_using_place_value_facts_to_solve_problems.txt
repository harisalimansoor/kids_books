=== Topic: Finding 10 more and 10 less and using place value facts to solve problems ===
Domain: Number and Place Value

--- Finding 10 more and 10 less ---

Question 1: What number is 10 more than 34?
Answer: 44
Working: 34 + 10 = 44

Question 2: What number is 10 less than 72?
Answer: 62
Working: 72 - 10 = 62

Question 3: Sam has 48 stickers. Freya has 10 more stickers than Sam. How many stickers does Freya have?
Answer: 58
Working: 48 + 10 = 58

Question 4: A box contains 65 crayons. A smaller box contains 10 fewer crayons. How many crayons are in the smaller box?
Answer: 55
Working: 65 - 10 = 55

Question 5: 10 more than a secret number is 81. What is the secret number?
Answer: 71
Working: 81 - 10 = 71

Question 6: 10 less than a number is 29. What is the number?
Answer: 39
Working: 29 + 10 = 39

Question 7: Tom has 37 marbles. Maya has 10 more marbles than Tom. Leo has 10 more marbles than Maya. How many marbles does Leo have?
Answer: 57
Working: Maya: 37 + 10 = 47. Leo: 47 + 10 = 57.

Question 8: On Monday, a baker made 53 bread rolls. On Tuesday, the baker made 10 fewer bread rolls. How many bread rolls did the baker make on Tuesday?
Answer: 43
Working: 53 - 10 = 43

Question 9: Start at 96. Subtract 1 ten. What is the new number?
Answer: 86
Working: 96 - 10 = 86

Question 10: A number sequence increases by 10 each time: 14, 24, 34, ... What is the next number in the sequence?
Answer: 44
Working: 34 + 10 = 44

--- Using place value facts to solve problems ---

Question 11: A two-digit number has 7 tens and 4 ones. What is the number?
Answer: 74
Working: 7 tens = 70. 4 ones = 4. 70 + 4 = 74.

Question 12: In the number 83, what is the value of the digit 8?
Answer: 80
Working: The digit 8 is in the tens column, so its value is 8 tens, which is 80.

Question 13: A number is partitioned into 3 tens and 15 ones. What is the number?
Answer: 45
Working: 3 tens = 30. 30 + 15 = 45.

Question 14: Liam has two digit cards: 4 and 9. What is the greatest two-digit number he can make using each card once?
Answer: 94
Working: Place the larger digit 9 in the tens column and 4 in the ones column to make 94.

Question 15: Ella has two digit cards: 6 and 2. What is the smallest two-digit number she can make using each card once?
Answer: 26
Working: Place the smaller digit 2 in the tens column and 6 in the ones column to make 26.

Question 16: I am a two-digit number. My tens digit is 5. My ones digit is 3 more than my tens digit. What number am I?
Answer: 58
Working: Tens digit = 5. Ones digit = 5 + 3 = 8. The number is 58.

Question 17: Fill in the missing number to complete the partition: 67 = 20 + [ ? ]
Answer: 47
Working: 67 - 20 = 47

Question 18: A teacher has 5 packs of 10 pencils and 8 single pencils. How many pencils does the teacher have altogether?
Answer: 58
Working: 5 packs of 10 = 50 pencils. 50 + 8 = 58 pencils.

Question 19: A number is partitioned into 50 and 6. It can also be partitioned into 40 and another number. What is the other number?
Answer: 16
Working: 50 + 6 = 56. 56 - 40 = 16.

Question 20: A number is greater than 45 and less than 55. Its ones digit is 2. What is the number?
Answer: 52
Working: The numbers between 45 and 55 with a ones digit of 2 are only 52.

Question 21: Jack has four 10p coins and seven 1p coins. How much money does Jack have in total?
Answer: 47p
Working: Four 10p coins = 40p. Seven 1p coins = 7p. 40p + 7p = 47p.

Question 22: I am a two-digit number less than 40. My tens digit is 3. The sum of my two digits is 9. What number am I?
Answer: 36
Working: The tens digit is 3. The digits add up to 9, so the ones digit is 9 - 3 = 6. The number is 36.
