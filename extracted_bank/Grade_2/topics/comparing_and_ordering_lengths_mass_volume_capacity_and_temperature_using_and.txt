=== Topic: Comparing and ordering lengths, mass, volume/capacity, and temperature using `<`, `>`, and `=` ===
Domain: Measurement

--- Comparing and ordering lengths ---

Question 1: Write <, >, or = to complete the statement: 45 cm [ ] 54 cm.
Answer: <
Working: Compare 45 and 54. 45 is less than 54, so 45 cm < 54 cm.

Question 2: Order these lengths from shortest to longest: 75 cm, 18 cm, 52 cm.
Answer: 18 cm, 52 cm, 75 cm
Working: Compare the numbers from smallest to greatest: 18 < 52 < 75. So the order is 18 cm, 52 cm, 75 cm.

Question 3: Write <, >, or = to make the statement true: 12 m + 8 m [ ] 20 m.
Answer: =
Working: 12 m + 8 m = 20 m. 20 m is equal to 20 m, so 12 m + 8 m = 20 m.

Question 4: Order these heights from tallest to shortest: 92 cm, 64 cm, 88 cm.
Answer: 92 cm, 88 cm, 64 cm
Working: Compare the numbers from greatest to smallest: 92 > 88 > 64. So the order is 92 cm, 88 cm, 64 cm.

Question 5: Write <, >, or = to make the statement true: 30 cm + 15 cm [ ] 50 cm.
Answer: <
Working: 30 cm + 15 cm = 45 cm. 45 cm is less than 50 cm, so 30 cm + 15 cm < 50 cm.

--- Comparing and ordering temperature ---

Question 16: Write <, >, or = to complete the statement: 24 °C [ ] 19 °C.
Answer: >
Working: 24 is greater than 19, so 24 °C > 19 °C.

Question 17: Order these temperatures from coldest to warmest: 31 °C, 12 °C, 25 °C.
Answer: 12 °C, 25 °C, 31 °C
Working: Compare the numbers from smallest to greatest: 12 < 25 < 31. So the order is 12 °C, 25 °C, 31 °C.

Question 18: On Monday the temperature was 18 °C. On Tuesday it was 22 °C. On Wednesday it was 14 °C. Which day was the warmest?
Answer: Tuesday
Working: Compare the temperatures: 22 °C is higher than 18 °C and 14 °C. Tuesday had the highest temperature.

Question 19: Order these temperatures from warmest to coldest: 16 °C, 29 °C, 21 °C.
Answer: 29 °C, 21 °C, 16 °C
Working: Compare the numbers from greatest to smallest: 29 > 21 > 16. So the order is 29 °C, 21 °C, 16 °C.

Question 20: Write <, >, or = to make the statement true: 10 °C + 15 °C [ ] 30 °C.
Answer: <
Working: 10 °C + 15 °C = 25 °C. 25 °C is less than 30 °C, so 10 °C + 15 °C < 30 °C.
