=== Topic: Construct and interpret tally charts, pictograms (keys of 1, 2, 5, 10), block diagrams, and simple tables ===
Domain: Statistics

--- Tally charts ---

Question 1: A tally chart shows the favourite pets in Year 2. The tally for 'Cat' has 2 full groups of 5 and 3 single tally marks. How many children chose cats?
Answer: 13
Working: 2 groups of 5 is 10. 10 + 3 single marks = 13.

Question 2: In a bird tally, Liam records 3 full groups of 5 for Robins and 1 full group of 5 and 2 single marks for Blackbirds. How many more Robins than Blackbirds did Liam see?
Answer: 8
Working: Robins = 3 × 5 = 15. Blackbirds = 5 + 2 = 7. 15 - 7 = 8.

Question 3: A teacher tallies fruit choices for lunch: Apples has 5 tallies, Bananas has 8 tallies, and Pears has 4 tallies. What is the total number of pieces of fruit tallied?
Answer: 17
Working: 5 + 8 + 4 = 17.

Question 4: Priya needs to record 19 cars in a tally chart. How many full groups of 5 and how many single marks should she draw?
Answer: 3 full groups of 5 and 4 single marks
Working: 19 = 5 + 5 + 5 + 4, which is 3 full groups of 5 and 4 single marks.

Question 5: Class 2 made a tally chart of minibeasts. They found 11 worms, 6 ladybirds, and some beetles. In total they found 25 minibeasts. How many beetles did they find?
Answer: 8
Working: 11 + 6 = 17. 25 - 17 = 8 beetles.

--- Pictograms (keys of 1, 2, 5, 10) ---

Question 6: In a pictogram showing favourite colours, each circle symbol stands for 1 child. Red has 7 circles and Blue has 9 circles. How many children chose Blue?
Answer: 9
Working: Each circle represents 1 child. Blue has 9 circles, so 9 children chose Blue.

Question 7: A pictogram shows the number of books read. The key shows: 1 book symbol = 2 books. Maya has 4 book symbols next to her name. How many books did Maya read?
Answer: 8
Working: 4 symbols × 2 books each = 8 books.

Question 8: A pictogram shows how many goals were scored. The key shows: 1 star = 2 goals. The Tigers have 3 full stars and 1 half star. How many goals did the Tigers score?
Answer: 7
Working: 3 full stars = 3 × 2 = 6 goals. 1 half star = 1 goal. 6 + 1 = 7 goals.

Question 9: In a pictogram showing snacks sold, the key is: 1 square = 5 snacks. Crisps has 6 squares and Fruit has 3 squares. How many snacks were sold altogether?
Answer: 45
Working: Total squares = 6 + 3 = 9 squares. 9 × 5 = 45 snacks.

Question 10: A pictogram shows the number of stickers collected. The key shows: 1 smiley face = 10 stickers. Oliver has 4 full smiley faces and 1 half smiley face. How many stickers does Oliver have?
Answer: 45
Working: 4 full faces = 4 × 10 = 40 stickers. 1 half face = 5 stickers. 40 + 5 = 45 stickers.

Question 11: A pictogram records vehicles passing a school. Key: 1 picture = 10 vehicles. Cars has 5 pictures. Buses has 2 pictures. How many more cars than buses passed the school?
Answer: 30
Working: Cars = 5 × 10 = 50. Buses = 2 × 10 = 20. 50 - 20 = 30 vehicles.

--- Block diagrams ---

Question 12: A block diagram shows children's favourite sports. The vertical scale goes up in 1s. The bar for Football is 8 blocks high and the bar for Swimming is 5 blocks high. How many children chose Football?
Answer: 8
Working: The scale goes up in 1s, so 8 blocks high means 8 children.

Question 13: A block diagram has a vertical axis numbered in 2s (0, 2, 4, 6, 8, 10, 12). The column for Tennis reaches the line marked 10. The column for Running reaches the line marked 6. How many children chose Tennis or Running in total?
Answer: 16
Working: Tennis = 10, Running = 6. 10 + 6 = 16.

Question 14: In a block diagram showing favourite juices, the scale goes up in steps of 5 (0, 5, 10, 15, 20). Orange juice reaches 20 and Apple juice reaches 15. How many more children chose Orange juice than Apple juice?
Answer: 5
Working: 20 - 15 = 5.

Question 15: A block diagram has a scale counting in 2s. The bar for Year 1 is 14. The bar for Year 2 is halfway between 8 and 10. What is the difference between Year 1 and Year 2?
Answer: 5
Working: Year 1 = 14. Halfway between 8 and 10 is 9 for Year 2. 14 - 9 = 5.

--- Simple tables ---

Question 16: Look at the table of lunch choices:
Sandwich: 12
Pasta: 9
Jacket Potato: 7
Which lunch choice was the most popular?
Answer: Sandwich
Working: 12 is the greatest number in the table, so Sandwich is the most popular.

Question 17: A table shows the number of points won by four teams:
Red Team: 14 points
Blue Team: 18 points
Green Team: 9 points
Yellow Team: 11 points
How many points did the Blue Team and Green Team score altogether?
Answer: 27
Working: Blue Team (18) + Green Team (9) = 18 + 9 = 27 points.

Question 18: A table shows flowers planted in the school garden:
Roses: 24
Tulips: 16
Daisies: 30
How many fewer Tulips than Daisies were planted?
Answer: 14
Working: Daisies (30) - Tulips (16) = 30 - 16 = 14.

Question 19: A table shows the number of children in three clubs:
Art Club: 15
Music Club: 12
Drama Club: missing
The total number of children across all three clubs is 40. How many children are in Drama Club?
Answer: 13
Working: 15 + 12 = 27. 40 - 27 = 13 children in Drama Club.
