=== Topic: Recognise, find, name, and write fractions \frac{1{3, \frac{1{4, \frac{2{4, \frac{3{4 of shapes, lengths, and sets ===
Domain: Fractions

--- Fractions of shapes ---

Question 1: A circle is divided into 4 equal parts. 1 part is shaded. What fraction of the circle is shaded?
Answer: 1/4
Working: The whole shape has 4 equal parts and 1 part is shaded, so the fraction is 1/4.

Question 2: A square is split into 3 equal parts. 1 part is coloured blue. What fraction of the square is coloured blue?
Answer: 1/3
Working: The shape is divided into 3 equal parts and 1 part is coloured blue, which makes the fraction 1/3.

Question 3: A rectangle is divided into 4 equal parts. 3 of the parts are shaded grey. What fraction of the rectangle is shaded grey?
Answer: 3/4
Working: There are 4 equal parts in total and 3 parts are shaded, so the fraction is 3/4.

Question 4: A circle is divided into 4 equal parts. 2 parts are shaded. Write the fraction of the circle that is shaded in quarters.
Answer: 2/4
Working: 2 out of 4 equal parts are shaded, so the fraction is 2/4.

Question 5: A pizza is cut into 4 equal slices. Jack eats 3 slices. What fraction of the pizza has Jack eaten?
Answer: 3/4
Working: The whole pizza has 4 equal slices. Jack eats 3 slices, which is 3/4 of the pizza.

--- Fractions of lengths ---

Question 6: A ribbon is 12 cm long. What is 1/4 of the length of the ribbon?
Answer: 3 cm
Working: To find 1/4 of 12 cm, divide 12 by 4: 12 ÷ 4 = 3 cm.

Question 7: A piece of string is 12 cm long. What is 1/3 of the length of the string?
Answer: 4 cm
Working: To find 1/3 of 12 cm, divide 12 by 3: 12 ÷ 3 = 4 cm.

Question 8: A strip of paper is 20 cm long. Mia cuts off 1/4 of the strip. How many centimetres does Mia cut off?
Answer: 5 cm
Working: To find 1/4 of 20 cm, divide 20 by 4: 20 ÷ 4 = 5 cm.

Question 9: A piece of wool is 8 cm long. What is 3/4 of the length of the wool?
Answer: 6 cm
Working: First find 1/4 of 8 cm: 8 ÷ 4 = 2 cm. Then multiply by 3: 2 × 3 = 6 cm.

Question 10: A garden path is 16 metres long. A snail crawls 2/4 of the way along the path. How many metres has the snail crawled?
Answer: 8 m
Working: 1/4 of 16 m is 16 ÷ 4 = 4 m. 2/4 of 16 m is 4 × 2 = 8 m.

Question 11: A piece of wire is 15 cm long. What is 1/3 of the length of the wire?
Answer: 5 cm
Working: To find 1/3 of 15 cm, divide 15 by 3: 15 ÷ 3 = 5 cm.

--- Fractions of sets ---

Question 12: There are 12 apples in a bowl. 1/4 of the apples are green. How many apples are green?
Answer: 3
Working: To find 1/4 of 12, share 12 into 4 equal groups: 12 ÷ 4 = 3.

Question 13: There are 9 toy cars in a box. 1/3 of the cars are red. How many cars are red?
Answer: 3
Working: To find 1/3 of 9, share 9 into 3 equal groups: 9 ÷ 3 = 3.

Question 14: A bag contains 16 marbles. Leo takes 3/4 of the marbles. How many marbles does Leo take?
Answer: 12
Working: 1/4 of 16 is 16 ÷ 4 = 4. To find 3/4, multiply 4 by 3: 4 × 3 = 12.

Question 15: There are 20 sheep in a field. 2/4 of the sheep are resting. How many sheep are resting?
Answer: 10
Working: 1/4 of 20 is 20 ÷ 4 = 5. 2/4 of 20 is 5 × 2 = 10.

Question 16: There are 18 counters in a tray. What is 1/3 of 18 counters?
Answer: 6
Working: To find 1/3 of 18, divide 18 by 3: 18 ÷ 3 = 6.

Question 17: Sam has 8 stickers. He gives 3/4 of the stickers to his sister. How many stickers does Sam give to his sister?
Answer: 6
Working: 1/4 of 8 is 8 ÷ 4 = 2. 3/4 of 8 is 2 × 3 = 6 stickers.

Question 18: There are 24 coloured pencils in a pot. 1/4 of the pencils are blue. How many pencils are blue?
Answer: 6
Working: To find 1/4 of 24, divide 24 by 4: 24 ÷ 4 = 6.
