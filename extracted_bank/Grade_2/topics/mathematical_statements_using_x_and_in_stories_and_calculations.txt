=== Topic: Mathematical statements using x, ÷, and = in stories and calculations ===
Domain: Multiplication and Division

--- Mathematical statements in stories ---

Question 1: There are 4 plates. Each plate has 2 biscuits. Write the multiplication calculation to find the total number of biscuits.
Answer: 4 × 2 = 8
Working: 4 groups of 2 is written as 4 × 2 = 8.

Question 2: A teacher shares 20 pencils equally between 2 pots. Write the division calculation to show how many pencils are in each pot.
Answer: 20 ÷ 2 = 10
Working: 20 pencils shared into 2 pots is written as 20 ÷ 2 = 10.

Question 3: There are 6 packs of stickers. Each pack has 5 stickers. Write the full multiplication statement to show the total number of stickers.
Answer: 6 × 5 = 30
Working: 6 groups of 5 is 6 × 5 = 30 stickers.

Question 4: Sam puts 35 apples into bags of 5 apples each. Write the division statement to show how many bags Sam fills.
Answer: 35 ÷ 5 = 7
Working: 35 grouped into 5s is written as 35 ÷ 5 = 7.

Question 5: A baker bakes 8 rows of buns. There are 10 buns in each row. Complete the mathematical statement: 8 × 10 = [ ? ].
Answer: 80
Working: 8 × 10 = 80 buns.

Question 6: Lily has 50p in 10p coins. Write the division statement that shows how many coins Lily has.
Answer: 50 ÷ 10 = 5
Working: 50p divided into 10p coins is 50 ÷ 10 = 5 coins.

Question 7: Ben writes the story: 'There are 9 pairs of shoes. That makes 18 shoes in total.' Write this story as a multiplication calculation using the numbers 9, 2, and 18.
Answer: 9 × 2 = 18
Working: 9 pairs means 9 groups of 2, so the calculation is 9 × 2 = 18.

Question 8: Maya has some boxes with 5 crayons in each box. She has 45 crayons altogether. Fill in the missing number in her story statement: [ ? ] × 5 = 45.
Answer: 9
Working: 45 ÷ 5 = 9, so 9 × 5 = 45.

--- Mathematical statements in calculations ---

Question 9: Calculate: 7 × 2 = [ ? ].
Answer: 14
Working: 7 multiplied by 2 is 14.

Question 10: Calculate: 16 ÷ 2 = [ ? ].
Answer: 8
Working: 16 divided by 2 is 8.

Question 11: Complete the mathematical statement: 5 × [ ? ] = 25.
Answer: 5
Working: 5 × 5 = 25.

Question 12: Which sign makes the calculation correct: × or ÷ ? 60 [ ? ] 10 = 6.
Answer: ÷
Working: 60 divided by 10 equals 6, so the sign is ÷.

Question 13: Complete the mathematical statement: [ ? ] ÷ 5 = 8.
Answer: 40
Working: 8 × 5 = 40, so 40 ÷ 5 = 8.

Question 14: Use the fact 10 × 4 = 40 to complete the related division statement: 40 ÷ 4 = [ ? ].
Answer: 10
Working: Since 10 × 4 = 40, 40 ÷ 4 = 10.

Question 15: Find the missing number to balance the calculation: 2 × 10 = [ ? ] × 5.
Answer: 4
Working: 2 × 10 = 20, and 4 × 5 = 20, so the missing number is 4.

Question 16: Write the missing symbol, + or ×, to make this statement true: 5 [ ? ] 5 = 25.
Answer: ×
Working: 5 × 5 = 25, while 5 + 5 = 10, so the symbol is ×.

Question 17: Complete the statement: 90 ÷ 10 = [ ? ] ÷ 2.
Answer: 18
Working: 90 ÷ 10 = 9. Since 18 ÷ 2 = 9, the missing number is 18.
