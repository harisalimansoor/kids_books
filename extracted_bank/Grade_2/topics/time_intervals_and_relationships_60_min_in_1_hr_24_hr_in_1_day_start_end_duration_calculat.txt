=== Topic: Time intervals and relationships (60 min in 1 hr, 24 hr in 1 day, start/end/duration calculations) ===
Domain: Measurement

--- 60 minutes in an hour ---

Question 1: How many minutes are in 1 hour?
Answer: 60
Working: 1 hour = 60 minutes.

Question 2: How many minutes are in half an hour?
Answer: 30
Working: Half of 60 minutes is 30 minutes.

Question 3: How many minutes are in a quarter of an hour?
Answer: 15
Working: A quarter of 60 minutes is 15 minutes (60 ÷ 4 = 15).

Question 4: How many minutes are in three-quarters of an hour?
Answer: 45
Working: One quarter of an hour is 15 minutes. Three quarters is 15 + 15 + 15 = 45 minutes.

Question 5: Write <, > or = to complete the statement: 1 hour ___ 50 minutes
Answer: >
Working: 1 hour = 60 minutes. 60 minutes is greater than 50 minutes, so 1 hour > 50 minutes.

Question 6: Write <, > or = to complete the statement: 60 minutes ___ 1 hour
Answer: =
Working: There are 60 minutes in 1 hour, so 60 minutes = 1 hour.

Question 7: A film lasts for 1 hour. A cartoon lasts for 40 minutes. How many minutes longer is the film than the cartoon?
Answer: 20 minutes
Working: 1 hour = 60 minutes. 60 - 40 = 20 minutes.

Question 8: A timer is set for 1 hour. It has been running for 35 minutes. How many minutes are left on the timer?
Answer: 25 minutes
Working: 1 hour = 60 minutes. 60 - 35 = 25 minutes.

--- 24 hours in a day ---

Question 9: How many hours are in 1 whole day?
Answer: 24
Working: There are 24 hours in 1 day.

Question 10: Write <, > or = to complete the statement: 1 day ___ 20 hours
Answer: >
Working: 1 day = 24 hours. 24 hours is greater than 20 hours, so 1 day > 20 hours.

Question 11: A bus ticket lasts for 24 hours. Maya buys the ticket on Monday at 9 o'clock. On which day and at what time will her ticket expire?
Answer: Tuesday at 9 o'clock
Working: 24 hours is 1 full day. 1 day after Monday at 9 o'clock is Tuesday at 9 o'clock.

Question 12: A car park pass lasts for 24 hours. Jack buys the pass on Thursday at 4 o'clock. On which day and at what time does the pass run out?
Answer: Friday at 4 o'clock
Working: 24 hours is 1 full day. 1 day after Thursday at 4 o'clock is Friday at 4 o'clock.

Question 13: There are 24 hours in a day. A shop is open for 10 hours and closed for the rest of the day. For how many hours is the shop closed?
Answer: 14 hours
Working: 24 - 10 = 14 hours.

Question 14: How many hours are in 2 full days?
Answer: 48
Working: 1 day = 24 hours. 2 days = 24 + 24 = 48 hours.

--- Start, end, and duration calculations ---

Question 15: Playtime starts at 10 o'clock and finishes at half past 10. How many minutes does playtime last?
Answer: 30 minutes
Working: From 10 o'clock to half past 10 is 30 minutes.

Question 16: A cartoon starts at 5 o'clock and ends at twenty past 5. How many minutes does the cartoon last?
Answer: 20 minutes
Working: From 5 o'clock to twenty past 5 is 20 minutes.

Question 17: A maths lesson starts at quarter past 9. It lasts for 30 minutes. What time does the maths lesson end?
Answer: quarter to 10
Working: Quarter past 9 is 15 minutes past 9. 15 + 30 = 45 minutes past 9, which is quarter to 10.

Question 18: A swimming lesson started 20 minutes ago. It is now twenty past 4. What time did the swimming lesson start?
Answer: 4 o'clock
Working: Twenty past 4 is 20 minutes after 4 o'clock. Counting back 20 minutes gives 4 o'clock.

Question 19: Leo starts reading at quarter to 3. He finishes reading at 3 o'clock. How many minutes did Leo read for?
Answer: 15 minutes
Working: Quarter to 3 is 15 minutes before 3 o'clock. From quarter to 3 to 3 o'clock is 15 minutes.

Question 20: A train leaves the station at half past 1. The journey takes 15 minutes. What time does the train arrive?
Answer: quarter to 2
Working: Half past 1 is 30 minutes past 1. 30 + 15 = 45 minutes past 1, which is quarter to 2.

Question 21: A football match starts at 2 o'clock. The first half lasts for 45 minutes. What time does the first half end?
Answer: quarter to 3
Working: 45 minutes after 2 o'clock is 45 minutes past 2, which is quarter to 3.

Question 22: Ella starts her homework at twenty past 5. She finishes 25 minutes later. What time does Ella finish her homework?
Answer: quarter to 6
Working: Twenty past 5 is 20 minutes past 5. 20 + 25 = 45 minutes past 5, which is quarter to 6.
