=== Topic: Patterns and sequences of mathematical objects (repeating units, rotations) ===
Domain: Geometry: Position and Direction

--- Repeating units ---

Question 1: Look at this repeating pattern of 2-D shapes: circle, square, triangle, circle, square, triangle, circle, square. What shape comes next?
Answer: triangle
Working: The repeating unit is circle, square, triangle. After circle and square, the next shape in the unit is triangle.

Question 2: Look at this repeating pattern of 3-D shapes: cube, cylinder, cone, cube, cylinder, cone, cube, cylinder. Which shape comes next?
Answer: cone
Working: The repeating unit is cube, cylinder, cone. The pattern ends on cylinder, so the next shape is cone.

Question 3: A bead pattern repeats every 3 beads: red, blue, yellow, red, blue, yellow. How many beads are in one repeating unit?
Answer: 3
Working: The pattern red, blue, yellow has 3 beads before it repeats.

Question 4: Look at this repeating pattern: star, heart, square, star, heart, square, star, heart, square. Which shape is the 7th shape in the pattern?
Answer: star
Working: Counting through the pattern: 1st is star, 2nd is heart, 3rd is square, 4th is star, 5th is heart, 6th is square, 7th is star.

Question 5: Look at this sequence of shapes: rectangle, rectangle, circle, rectangle, rectangle, circle, rectangle, rectangle. What shape will be the 9th shape in the sequence?
Answer: circle
Working: The repeating unit of 3 shapes is rectangle, rectangle, circle. The 7th is rectangle, the 8th is rectangle, so the 9th is circle.

Question 6: A repeating unit has 2 circles and 1 triangle: circle, circle, triangle. If the unit is repeated 5 times, how many triangles will there be in total?
Answer: 5
Working: Each unit has 1 triangle. For 5 repeats of the unit: 5 × 1 = 5 triangles.

Question 7: A sequence repeats the shapes cube, sphere, cone. If there are 12 shapes in the complete sequence, how many spheres are there?
Answer: 4
Working: The unit length is 3 shapes. In 12 shapes there are 12 ÷ 3 = 4 repeating units. Each unit has 1 sphere, so 4 × 1 = 4 spheres.

--- Rotations ---

Question 8: An arrow pointing up turns a quarter turn clockwise. Which direction does the arrow point now: left, right, or down?
Answer: right
Working: Turning a quarter turn clockwise from pointing up turns the arrow towards the right.

Question 9: Look at the pattern of an arrow rotating clockwise: pointing up, pointing right, pointing down. Where will the arrow point on the next quarter turn?
Answer: left
Working: Continuing the quarter turns clockwise after pointing down, the arrow points to the left.

Question 10: Look at the directions an arrow points in a sequence: up, down, up, down, up. What turn is the arrow making each time: a quarter turn or a half turn?
Answer: half turn
Working: Turning from pointing straight up to straight down is a half turn.

Question 11: Look at this sequence of arrow directions: up, left, down, right, up. Is the arrow turning a quarter turn clockwise or a quarter turn anticlockwise each time?
Answer: quarter turn anticlockwise
Working: Going from up to left, then left to down, is turning in the opposite direction of clock hands, which is anticlockwise.

Question 12: An arrow points up. It turns by a quarter turn clockwise each time. How many quarter turns must it make to point up again?
Answer: 4
Working: 1 quarter turn points right, 2 quarter turns point down, 3 quarter turns point left, and 4 quarter turns make a full turn back to pointing up.

Question 13: A pointer starts pointing at 12 on a clock face. It makes a quarter turn clockwise each step: 12, 3, 6, 9. What number will it point to on the 5th step?
Answer: 12
Working: The sequence of numbers is 12 (1st), 3 (2nd), 6 (3rd), 9 (4th). After 9, another quarter turn clockwise points back to 12 (5th).

Question 14: A shape pattern rotates a triangle by a quarter turn clockwise each time: pointing up (1st), pointing right (2nd), pointing down (3rd), pointing left (4th). Which direction will the 6th triangle point?
Answer: right
Working: The pattern has a repeating unit of 4 turns: 1st is up, 2nd is right, 3rd is down, 4th is left, 5th is up, 6th is right.
