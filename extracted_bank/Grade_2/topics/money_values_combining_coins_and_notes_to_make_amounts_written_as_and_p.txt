=== Topic: Money values, combining coins and notes to make amounts (written as £ and p) ===
Domain: Measurement

--- Money values in pounds (£) and pence (p) ---

Question 1: Which has a greater value: a 20p coin or a 50p coin?
Answer: 50p coin
Working: 50 is greater than 20, so a 50p coin has a greater value than a 20p coin.

Question 2: What is the total value of one 10p coin, one 5p coin, and one 2p coin?
Answer: 17p
Working: 10p + 5p = 15p, and 15p + 2p = 17p.

Question 3: Write <, >, or = to complete the statement: £10 [ ] £20
Answer: <
Working: 10 is less than 20, so £10 < £20.

Question 4: Put these coins in order from smallest value to greatest value: 50p, 5p, 20p, 2p.
Answer: 2p, 5p, 20p, 50p
Working: Comparing the values in pence from least to greatest gives 2p, 5p, 20p, 50p.

Question 5: Jack has two £10 notes and one £5 note. What is the total value of Jack's notes?
Answer: £25
Working: Two £10 notes = £10 + £10 = £20. Adding the £5 note: £20 + £5 = £25.

Question 6: Is one £2 coin worth more than, less than, or equal to four 50p coins?
Answer: equal to
Working: Four 50p coins = 50p + 50p + 50p + 50p = 200p. 200p is equal to £2. Therefore, one £2 coin is equal to four 50p coins.

--- Combining coins and notes to make amounts ---

Question 7: Leo has two 10p coins and one 5p coin. How much money does he have in total?
Answer: 25p
Working: 10p + 10p = 20p. 20p + 5p = 25p.

Question 8: Zara has three £2 coins and one £1 coin. How much money does she have altogether?
Answer: £7
Working: Three £2 coins = £2 + £2 + £2 = £6. Adding £1: £6 + £1 = £7.

Question 9: Oliver has one £10 note, one £2 coin, one 50p coin, and one 20p coin. What is the total amount of money he has?
Answer: £12 and 70p
Working: Pounds: £10 + £2 = £12. Pence: 50p + 20p = 70p. Total amount: £12 and 70p.

Question 10: Mia has one 50p coin, two 20p coins, and one 5p coin. How much money does she have altogether?
Answer: 95p
Working: 50p + 20p + 20p = 90p. 90p + 5p = 95p.

Question 11: Ben has two £5 notes, one £1 coin, and one 10p coin. How much money does he have altogether?
Answer: £11 and 10p
Working: Pounds: £5 + £5 = £10. £10 + £1 = £11. Pence: 10p. Total: £11 and 10p.

Question 12: Lucas wants to make 35p. He has one 20p coin and one 10p coin. Which coin does he need to add to make exactly 35p?
Answer: 5p
Working: 20p + 10p = 30p. 35p - 30p = 5p, so he needs a 5p coin.

Question 13: Hannah has £15 and 60p. She has one £10 note, one £5 note, and one 50p coin. Which coin is missing to make her total?
Answer: 10p
Working: £10 + £5 = £15. Her pence amount is 50p. To make 60p: 60p - 50p = 10p, so the missing coin is a 10p coin.

Question 14: Amira has one £20 note, two £2 coins, three 10p coins, and two 2p coins. How much money does she have in total?
Answer: £24 and 34p
Working: Pounds: £20 + £2 + £2 = £24. Pence: (3 × 10p) + (2 × 2p) = 30p + 4p = 34p. Total = £24 and 34p.
