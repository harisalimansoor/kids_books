=== Topic: Multiplication and division facts for the 2, 5, and 10 multiplication tables ===
Domain: Multiplication and Division

--- Multiplication and division facts for the 2 multiplication table ---

Question 1: Work out 6 × 2.
Answer: 12
Working: 6 × 2 = 12.

Question 2: Work out 18 ÷ 2.
Answer: 9
Working: 18 ÷ 2 = 9.

Question 3: There are 8 pairs of shoes on a rack. How many shoes are there in total?
Answer: 16
Working: 8 × 2 = 16 shoes.

Question 4: Fill in the missing number: ___ × 2 = 14
Answer: 7
Working: 14 ÷ 2 = 7, so 7 × 2 = 14.

Question 5: A teacher shares 20 colouring pencils equally between 2 pots. How many pencils are in each pot?
Answer: 10
Working: 20 ÷ 2 = 10 pencils in each pot.

Question 6: Fill in the missing number: ___ ÷ 2 = 8
Answer: 16
Working: 8 × 2 = 16, so 16 ÷ 2 = 8.

Question 7: Maya has 5 bags with 2 apples in each bag. Leo has 16 apples. How many more apples does Leo have than Maya?
Answer: 6
Working: Maya has 5 × 2 = 10 apples. 16 - 10 = 6 more apples.

--- Multiplication and division facts for the 5 multiplication table ---

Question 8: Work out 7 × 5.
Answer: 35
Working: 7 × 5 = 35.

Question 9: Work out 30 ÷ 5.
Answer: 6
Working: 30 ÷ 5 = 6.

Question 10: There are 6 packs of stickers. Each pack has 5 stickers. How many stickers are there in total?
Answer: 30
Working: 6 × 5 = 30 stickers.

Question 11: A baker puts 5 bread rolls into each bag. He has 45 bread rolls. How many bags can he fill?
Answer: 9
Working: 45 ÷ 5 = 9 bags.

Question 12: Fill in the missing number: 5 × ___ = 40
Answer: 8
Working: 40 ÷ 5 = 8, so 5 × 8 = 40.

Question 13: Fill in the missing number: ___ ÷ 5 = 5
Answer: 25
Working: 5 × 5 = 25, so 25 ÷ 5 = 5.

Question 14: A sticker card has 4 rows of 5 stickers. Sam uses 7 stickers. How many stickers are left on the card?
Answer: 13
Working: Total stickers = 4 × 5 = 20. Stickers left = 20 - 7 = 13.

--- Multiplication and division facts for the 10 multiplication table ---

Question 15: Work out 8 × 10.
Answer: 80
Working: 8 × 10 = 80.

Question 16: Work out 70 ÷ 10.
Answer: 7
Working: 70 ÷ 10 = 7.

Question 17: There are 4 boxes of felt-tip pens. Each box contains 10 pens. How many felt-tip pens are there altogether?
Answer: 40
Working: 4 × 10 = 40 pens.

Question 18: A teacher has 60 counters. She puts them into equal bags of 10 counters. How many bags does she make?
Answer: 6
Working: 60 ÷ 10 = 6 bags.

Question 19: Fill in the missing number: ___ × 10 = 90
Answer: 9
Working: 90 ÷ 10 = 9, so 9 × 10 = 90.

Question 20: Fill in the missing number: 100 ÷ ___ = 10
Answer: 10
Working: 100 ÷ 10 = 10.

Question 21: Ben has seven 10p coins. He spends 25p on a rubber. How many pence does Ben have left?
Answer: 45p
Working: Ben starts with 7 × 10p = 70p. 70p - 25p = 45p.
