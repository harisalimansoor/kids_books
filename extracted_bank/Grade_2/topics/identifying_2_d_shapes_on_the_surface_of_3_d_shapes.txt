=== Topic: Identifying 2-D shapes on the surface of 3-D shapes ===
Domain: Geometry: Properties of Shapes

--- Identifying 2-D shapes on the surface of 3-D shapes ---

Question 1: What 2-D shape is each flat face of a cube?
Answer: square
Working: A cube has 6 flat faces, and every flat face is a square.

Question 2: Sam places the flat bottom of a cone onto a piece of paper and draws around it. What 2-D shape does Sam draw?
Answer: circle
Working: The flat face at the bottom of a cone is a circle.

Question 3: How many circular faces does a cylinder have?
Answer: 2
Working: A cylinder has 2 flat circular faces (one at the top and one at the bottom).

Question 4: A square-based pyramid has 1 square face on the bottom. What 2-D shape are all of its other faces?
Answer: triangle
Working: The base is a square, and the 4 sloping side faces are triangles.

Question 5: Which 2-D shape can you find on both a cube and a square-based pyramid?
Answer: square
Working: A cube has square faces, and a square-based pyramid has a square base.

Question 6: A triangular prism has 5 faces in total. How many of its faces are triangles?
Answer: 2
Working: A triangular prism has 2 triangular faces and 3 rectangular faces.

Question 7: A cereal box is shaped like a cuboid. What 2-D shape are its flat faces?
Answer: rectangle
Working: A cuboid has 6 flat faces that are rectangles (or squares, which are special rectangles).

Question 8: How many triangular faces are on a square-based pyramid?
Answer: 4
Working: A square-based pyramid has 1 square base and 4 triangular faces meeting at the top vertex.

Question 9: Maya dips one flat face of a 3-D shape in paint and prints a stamp. The stamp is a circle. Name two different 3-D shapes that Maya could have used.
Answer: cylinder and cone
Working: Both a cylinder and a cone have at least one flat circular face.

Question 10: A 3-D shape has 2 triangular faces and 3 rectangular faces. What is the name of this 3-D shape?
Answer: triangular prism
Working: A triangular prism is made up of 2 identical triangular faces and 3 rectangular faces.

Question 11: Liam says, 'A sphere has one circular flat face.' Is Liam correct? Write yes or no.
Answer: no
Working: A sphere has only 1 curved surface and no flat faces, so it has no flat circular faces.

Question 12: Anika traces around every face of a triangular prism on a piece of paper. How many rectangles does she draw in total?
Answer: 3
Working: A triangular prism has 2 triangular faces and 3 rectangular faces, so she draws 3 rectangles.
