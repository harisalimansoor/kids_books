=== Topic: Recall and use addition and subtraction facts to 20 and complete 4-fact families ===
Domain: Addition and Subtraction

--- Recall and use addition and subtraction facts to 20 ---

Question 1: What is 13 + 7?
Answer: 20
Working: 13 + 7 = 20

Question 2: What is 20 - 6?
Answer: 14
Working: 20 - 6 = 14

Question 3: What is 8 + 8?
Answer: 16
Working: Double 8 is 16, so 8 + 8 = 16

Question 4: What is 17 - 9?
Answer: 8
Working: 17 - 7 = 10, and 10 - 2 = 8, so 17 - 9 = 8

Question 5: Work out the missing number: 6 + ___ = 15
Answer: 9
Working: 15 - 6 = 9, so 6 + 9 = 15

Question 6: Work out the missing number: 20 - ___ = 11
Answer: 9
Working: 20 - 11 = 9, so 20 - 9 = 11

Question 7: Sam has 7 red counters and 8 blue counters. How many counters does Sam have in total?
Answer: 15
Working: 7 + 8 = 15 counters in total

Question 8: A box holds 20 pencils. Lily puts 8 pencils in the box. Jack puts 5 pencils in the box. How many more pencils are needed to fill the box?
Answer: 7
Working: 8 + 5 = 13 pencils in the box. 20 - 13 = 7 more pencils needed

--- Complete 4-fact families ---

Question 9: Here are three facts in a fact family:
4 + 9 = 13
9 + 4 = 13
13 - 4 = 9
Write the missing subtraction fact to complete the family.
Answer: 13 - 9 = 4
Working: The four related facts using 4, 9, and 13 are 4 + 9 = 13, 9 + 4 = 13, 13 - 4 = 9, and 13 - 9 = 4

Question 10: Here are three facts in a fact family:
18 - 6 = 12
18 - 12 = 6
12 + 6 = 18
Write the missing addition fact to complete the family.
Answer: 6 + 12 = 18
Working: Addition can be done in any order, so the missing fact is 6 + 12 = 18

Question 11: Complete the missing number in this fact family:
7 + 8 = 15
8 + 7 = 15
15 - 8 = 7
15 - ___ = 8
Answer: 7
Working: In the fact family for 7, 8, and 15, the final subtraction fact is 15 - 7 = 8

Question 12: A part-whole model has the whole as 20 and the two parts as 14 and 6.
Write the subtraction fact starting with 20 that gives the answer 14.
Answer: 20 - 6 = 14
Working: Whole - part = other part, so 20 - 6 = 14

Question 13: Write the inverse subtraction fact for 5 + 11 = 16 that starts with 16 and subtracts 5.
Answer: 16 - 5 = 11
Working: The inverse subtraction fact is 16 - 5 = 11

Question 14: Mia writes this fact family for the numbers 8, 9, and 17:
8 + 9 = 17
9 + 8 = 17
17 - 8 = 9
8 - 17 = 9
One of Mia's number sentences is incorrect. Write the correct subtraction sentence that Mia should have written.
Answer: 17 - 9 = 8
Working: Subtraction starts with the whole (17). The correct missing subtraction fact is 17 - 9 = 8
