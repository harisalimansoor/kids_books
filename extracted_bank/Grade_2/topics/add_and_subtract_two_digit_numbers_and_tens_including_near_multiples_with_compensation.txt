=== Topic: Add and subtract two-digit numbers and tens, including near multiples with compensation ===
Domain: Addition and Subtraction

--- Add and subtract two-digit numbers and tens ---

Question 1: What is 43 + 20?
Answer: 63
Working: 43 has 4 tens and 3 ones. Adding 2 tens gives 6 tens and 3 ones, which is 63.

Question 2: What is 68 - 30?
Answer: 38
Working: 68 has 6 tens and 8 ones. Subtracting 3 tens gives 3 tens and 8 ones, which is 38.

Question 3: Mia has 35 stickers. She buys 40 more stickers. How many stickers does Mia have now?
Answer: 75
Working: 35 + 40 = 75 stickers.

Question 4: Find the missing number: 27 + [ ? ] = 67
Answer: 40
Working: 67 - 27 = 40. The ones digit is unchanged and the tens digit increases by 4 tens, so 40 is added.

Question 5: Find the missing number: 84 - [ ? ] = 34
Answer: 50
Working: 84 - 34 = 50. The ones digit stays at 4 and 8 tens decreases to 3 tens, which is a decrease of 50.

Question 6: A baker bakes 72 bread rolls. He sells 50 rolls. How many bread rolls are left?
Answer: 22
Working: 72 - 50 = 22 bread rolls.

Question 7: Start at 24. Count on 10, then count on another 10. What number do you reach?
Answer: 44
Working: 24 + 10 = 34. 34 + 10 = 44.

Question 8: Liam scores 38 points in a game. Sam scores 30 points more than Liam. Tara scores 20 points less than Sam. How many points does Tara score?
Answer: 48
Working: Liam = 38 points. Sam = 38 + 30 = 68 points. Tara = 68 - 20 = 48 points.

--- Add and subtract near multiples with compensation ---

Question 9: Work out 54 + 9 by adding 10 and subtracting 1.
Answer: 63
Working: 54 + 10 = 64. 64 - 1 = 63.

Question 10: Find the missing number to complete the method: 36 + 19 = 36 + 20 - [ ? ]
Answer: 1
Working: 19 is 1 less than 20, so after adding 20, you must subtract 1.

Question 11: What is 25 + 29?
Answer: 54
Working: 25 + 30 = 55. 55 - 1 = 54.

Question 12: Find the missing number to complete the method: 54 - 19 = 54 - 20 + [ ? ]
Answer: 1
Working: Subtracting 20 subtracts 1 too many, so you must add 1 back.

Question 13: What is 73 - 29?
Answer: 44
Working: 73 - 30 = 43. 43 + 1 = 44.

Question 14: Jack has 47 marbles. He collects 19 more marbles. How many marbles does Jack have in total?
Answer: 66
Working: 47 + 20 = 67. 67 - 1 = 66 marbles.

Question 15: To calculate 62 - 19, Amy works out 62 - 20 = 42. What number must Amy add to 42 to get the correct answer?
Answer: 1
Working: Amy subtracted 20, which is 1 too many. She must add 1 back to 42 to get 43.

Question 16: A toy shop has 65 toy cars. It sells 19 cars in the morning and 29 cars in the afternoon. How many toy cars are left?
Answer: 17
Working: 65 - 19 = 65 - 20 + 1 = 46. 46 - 29 = 46 - 30 + 1 = 17 toy cars.
