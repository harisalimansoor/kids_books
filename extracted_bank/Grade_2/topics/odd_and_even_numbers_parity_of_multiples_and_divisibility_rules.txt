=== Topic: Odd and even numbers, parity of multiples, and divisibility rules ===
Domain: Multiplication and Division

--- Odd and even numbers ---

Question 1: Is the number 16 odd or even?
Answer: even
Working: The ones digit of 16 is 6. Numbers ending in 0, 2, 4, 6, or 8 are even, so 16 is even.

Question 2: Sam has 11 counters. Can he share them equally between 2 plates with none left over? Write yes or no.
Answer: no
Working: 11 is an odd number. Odd numbers cannot be shared equally between 2 groups without 1 left over, so the answer is no.

Question 3: Which of these numbers is odd: 14, 28, 35, 42?
Answer: 35
Working: Odd numbers end in 1, 3, 5, 7, or 9. 35 ends in 5, so 35 is the odd number.

Question 4: What is the next even number after 18?
Answer: 20
Working: Even numbers count up in 2s: 18 + 2 = 20.

Question 5: Look at these numbers: 21, 34, 47, 59, 62. How many of these numbers are even?
Answer: 2
Working: Check the ones digits: 34 (ends in 4) and 62 (ends in 2) are even. 21, 47, and 59 are odd. There are 2 even numbers.

Question 6: A number is greater than 25 and less than 30. It is an even number that ends in 8. What is the number?
Answer: 28
Working: The numbers between 25 and 30 are 26, 27, 28, 29. The even number ending in 8 is 28.

Question 7: Maya has 15 socks. She puts them into pairs of 2. How many complete pairs does she make?
Answer: 7
Working: 15 ÷ 2 = 7 with 1 left over. Maya makes 7 complete pairs.

--- Parity of multiples ---

Question 8: Is the total of 4 × 2 an odd number or an even number?
Answer: even
Working: 4 × 2 = 8. 8 is an even number.

Question 9: Choose the correct word to complete the sentence: Every multiple of 2 is an [odd / even] number.
Answer: even
Working: Counting in 2s gives 2, 4, 6, 8, 10, ... All multiples of 2 end in 0, 2, 4, 6, or 8, so they are always even.

Question 10: Look at these multiples of 5: 5, 10, 15, 20, 25, 30. Which digit do all the odd multiples of 5 end in?
Answer: 5
Working: The odd multiples in the list are 5, 15, and 25. They all end in the digit 5.

Question 11: Ben says, "All multiples of 10 are even numbers." Is Ben correct? Write yes or no.
Answer: yes
Working: Multiples of 10 end in 0 (10, 20, 30, 40, ...). Any number ending in 0 is even, so Ben is correct.

Question 12: 7 children have 2 pencils each. Is the total number of pencils odd or even?
Answer: even
Working: 7 × 2 = 14. 14 ends in 4, which is an even number.

Question 13: Look at these multiples of 5: 15, 35, 40, 55. Which one is an even number?
Answer: 40
Working: 15, 35, and 55 end in 5, so they are odd. 40 ends in 0, so 40 is the even multiple of 5.

--- Divisibility rules ---

Question 14: Can 26 be divided by 2 with no remainder? Write yes or no.
Answer: yes
Working: 26 is an even number because it ends in 6. All even numbers divide by 2 with no remainder, so the answer is yes.

Question 15: Which of these numbers can be divided by 10 with no remainder: 25, 40, 55, 65?
Answer: 40
Working: Numbers divisible by 10 end in 0. 40 ends in 0, so 40 can be divided by 10 with no remainder.

Question 16: A number ends in 5. Can this number be divided by 5 with no remainder? Write yes or no.
Answer: yes
Working: Any whole number ending in 0 or 5 is a multiple of 5, so it divides by 5 with no remainder.

Question 17: Which of these numbers can be shared equally into 5 equal groups with none left over: 18, 23, 35, 41?
Answer: 35
Working: To share into 5 equal groups with none left over, the number must end in 0 or 5. 35 ends in 5, so 35 is the correct number.

Question 18: A two-digit number can be divided by both 2 and 5 with no remainder. What digit must be in its ones place?
Answer: 0
Working: To divide by 2, it must be even (0, 2, 4, 6, 8). To divide by 5, it must end in 0 or 5. The only digit in both lists is 0.

Question 19: Which number between 41 and 49 can be divided by 5 with no remainder?
Answer: 45
Working: Numbers divisible by 5 end in 0 or 5. Between 41 and 49, the number ending in 5 is 45.

Question 20: A box contains 50 stickers. Can the stickers be shared equally among 10 children with none left over? Write yes or no.
Answer: yes
Working: 50 ends in 0, so 50 is divisible by 10. 50 ÷ 10 = 5 with none left over, so the answer is yes.
