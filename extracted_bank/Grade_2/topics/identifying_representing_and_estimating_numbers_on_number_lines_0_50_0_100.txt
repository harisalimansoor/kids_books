=== Topic: Identifying, representing, and estimating numbers on number lines (0–50, 0–100) ===
Domain: Number and Place Value

--- Identifying numbers on number lines ---

Question 1: A number line starts at 0 and ends at 20 with tick marks counting up in 2s. What number is on the tick mark directly between 12 and 16?
Answer: 14
Working: Counting in 2s: 12, 14, 16. The number between 12 and 16 is 14.

Question 2: A number line starts at 0 and ends at 50. It is divided into 5 equal sections with tick marks at 10, 20, 30, 40, and 50. What number is at the second tick mark after 0?
Answer: 20
Working: The first tick mark is 10 and the second tick mark is 20.

Question 3: A number line goes from 0 to 100 with tick marks every 10. An arrow points to the tick mark that is 3 steps of 10 after 40. What number does the arrow point to?
Answer: 70
Working: 40 + 10 + 10 + 10 = 70.

Question 4: A number line starts at 0 and ends at 50 with tick marks counting up in 5s. What number is 2 marks before 35?
Answer: 25
Working: Counting back in 5s from 35: one mark back is 30, two marks back is 25.

Question 5: A number line starts at 20 and ends at 30 with tick marks counting in ones. What number is exactly halfway between 20 and 30?
Answer: 25
Working: Halfway between 20 and 30 is 20 + 5 = 25.

--- Representing numbers on number lines ---

Question 6: A frog starts at 0 on a number line and makes 4 jumps of 10 forward. What number does the frog land on?
Answer: 40
Working: 4 jumps of 10: 10, 20, 30, 40.

Question 7: To show the number 36 on a number line, Ben starts at 30 and jumps forward in ones. How many jumps of 1 must Ben make to reach 36?
Answer: 6
Working: 36 - 30 = 6 jumps of 1.

Question 8: A number line has marks counting in 5s from 0 to 50. How many jumps of 5 are needed to go from 0 to 45?
Answer: 9
Working: 45 ÷ 5 = 9 jumps.

Question 9: Maya starts at 70 on a number line and makes 3 jumps of 10 backwards. What number does Maya land on?
Answer: 40
Working: 70 - 10 - 10 - 10 = 40.

Question 10: Sam wants to show 48 on an empty number line. He starts at 40, makes 1 jump of 10 forward to 50, and then jumps backwards. How many jumps of 1 must Sam make backwards from 50 to land on 48?
Answer: 2
Working: 50 - 48 = 2 jumps of 1 backwards.

--- Estimating numbers on number lines ---

Question 11: An empty number line starts at 0 and ends at 100. A point is marked exactly halfway between 0 and 100. What number does this point show?
Answer: 50
Working: Half of 100 is 50, so the halfway point is 50.

Question 12: An empty number line starts at 0 and ends at 50. A point is placed exactly halfway between 0 and 50. What number is at this halfway point?
Answer: 25
Working: Half of 50 is 25.

Question 13: An empty number line starts at 0 and ends at 100, with 50 marked in the middle. A point is marked halfway between 50 and 100. What number does this point represent?
Answer: 75
Working: Halfway between 50 and 100 is 50 + 25 = 75.

Question 14: An empty number line starts at 0 and ends at 100, with 50 marked in the middle. A point is marked halfway between 0 and 50. What number does this point represent?
Answer: 25
Working: Halfway between 0 and 50 is 25.

Question 15: An empty number line starts at 0 and ends at 50. A mark is placed very close to 50, just before the end. Which number is the best estimate: 12, 26, or 48?
Answer: 48
Working: 48 is the number closest to 50.
