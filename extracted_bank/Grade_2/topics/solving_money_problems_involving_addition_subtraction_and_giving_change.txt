=== Topic: Solving money problems involving addition, subtraction, and giving change ===
Domain: Measurement

--- Solving money problems involving addition ---

Question 1: A rubber costs 20p and a pencil costs 30p. How much do they cost altogether?
Answer: 50p
Working: 20p + 30p = 50p

Question 2: Maya buys a notebook for 45p and a ruler for 28p. How much does she spend in total?
Answer: 73p
Working: 45p + 28p = 73p

Question 3: Sam has £14. His granny gives him £25 for his birthday. How much money does Sam have now?
Answer: £39
Working: £14 + £25 = £39

Question 4: A toy car costs £18 and a book costs £12. How much do both items cost altogether?
Answer: £30
Working: £18 + £12 = £30

Question 5: Leo buys an apple for 34p, a banana for 26p, and a sticker for 15p. What is the total cost?
Answer: 75p
Working: 34p + 26p = 60p, then 60p + 15p = 75p

Question 6: Ben buys two packs of stickers. Each pack costs 35p. How much does he pay altogether?
Answer: 70p
Working: 35p + 35p = 70p

--- Solving money problems involving subtraction ---

Question 7: Nina has 60p. She spends 20p on a sticker. How much money does she have left?
Answer: 40p
Working: 60p - 20p = 40p

Question 8: Jack has 85p. He spends 37p on a postcard. How much money does Jack have left?
Answer: 48p
Working: 85p - 37p = 48p

Question 9: A comic costs £15. A colouring book costs £8. How much more does the comic cost than the colouring book?
Answer: £7
Working: £15 - £8 = £7

Question 10: Oliver has £50. He buys a jacket for £38. How much money does he have left?
Answer: £12
Working: £50 - £38 = £12

Question 11: Amy has 92p. She buys a pen for 46p. How much money does she have left?
Answer: 46p
Working: 92p - 46p = 46p

Question 12: Freya has 75p. A badge costs 48p. How much more money does Freya have than the cost of the badge?
Answer: 27p
Working: 75p - 48p = 27p

--- Giving change ---

Question 13: An apple costs 30p. Priya pays with a 50p coin. How much change does she get?
Answer: 20p
Working: 50p - 30p = 20p

Question 14: A felt-tip pen costs 65p. Tom pays with a £1 coin. How much change does he get?
Answer: 35p
Working: £1 = 100p. 100p - 65p = 35p

Question 15: Ella buys a ribbon for 82p. She pays with a £1 coin. How much change should she receive?
Answer: 18p
Working: £1 = 100p. 100p - 82p = 18p

Question 16: Harry buys a toy dinosaur for £7. He pays with a £10 note. How much change does he get?
Answer: £3
Working: £10 - £7 = £3

Question 17: Mia buys a sticker for 45p and a pencil for 25p. She pays with a £1 coin. How much change does she get?
Answer: 30p
Working: 45p + 25p = 70p. £1 = 100p. 100p - 70p = 30p

Question 18: Jacob buys a board game for £14. He pays with a £20 note. How much change does he receive?
Answer: £6
Working: £20 - £14 = £6
