=== Topic: Properties of 3-D shapes (edges, vertices, faces, flat vs curved surfaces) ===
Domain: Geometry: Properties of Shapes

--- Edges ---

Question 1: How many straight edges does a cube have?
Answer: 12
Working: A cube has 12 straight edges.

Question 2: How many edges does a sphere have?
Answer: 0
Working: A sphere is completely round and has 0 edges.

Question 3: How many curved edges does a cylinder have?
Answer: 2
Working: A cylinder has 2 circular edges where the flat circles meet the curved surface.

Question 4: How many straight edges does a square-based pyramid have altogether?
Answer: 8
Working: A square-based pyramid has 4 edges around the bottom square base and 4 edges going up to the top point: 4 + 4 = 8 edges.

Question 5: A triangular prism has 9 edges. A cuboid has 12 edges. How many more edges does a cuboid have than a triangular prism?
Answer: 3
Working: 12 - 9 = 3 edges.

--- Vertices ---

Question 6: How many vertices does a cube have?
Answer: 8
Working: A cube has 8 vertices (corners).

Question 7: How many vertices does a sphere have?
Answer: 0
Working: A sphere has no corners, so it has 0 vertices.

Question 8: How many vertices does a square-based pyramid have?
Answer: 5
Working: A square-based pyramid has 4 vertices on the square base and 1 vertex at the top point: 4 + 1 = 5 vertices.

Question 9: How many vertices does a triangular prism have?
Answer: 6
Working: A triangular prism has 3 vertices on each of its 2 triangular ends: 3 + 3 = 6 vertices.

Question 10: A cuboid has 8 vertices. A cone has 1 vertex. How many vertices do a cuboid and a cone have in total?
Answer: 9
Working: 8 + 1 = 9 vertices in total.

--- Faces ---

Question 11: How many flat faces does a cube have?
Answer: 6
Working: A cube has 6 flat square faces.

Question 12: Which 3-D shape has 6 identical square faces: a cube or a cuboid?
Answer: cube
Working: A cube has 6 identical square faces.

Question 13: How many triangular faces does a square-based pyramid have?
Answer: 4
Working: A square-based pyramid has 1 square face on the bottom and 4 triangular faces on the sides.

Question 14: How many faces does a triangular prism have in total?
Answer: 5
Working: A triangular prism has 2 triangular faces and 3 rectangular faces: 2 + 3 = 5 faces in total.

Question 15: A cuboid has 6 faces. A triangular prism has 5 faces. How many faces do they have altogether?
Answer: 11
Working: 6 + 5 = 11 faces altogether.

--- Flat vs curved surfaces ---

Question 16: How many curved surfaces does a sphere have?
Answer: 1
Working: A sphere has 1 curved surface.

Question 17: A cylinder has 2 flat circular faces and 1 curved surface. How many surfaces does a cylinder have in total?
Answer: 3
Working: 2 flat faces + 1 curved surface = 3 surfaces in total.

Question 18: How many curved surfaces does a cube have?
Answer: 0
Working: A cube only has flat faces, so it has 0 curved surfaces.

Question 19: Which 3-D shape has only 1 curved surface and 0 flat faces: a cylinder or a sphere?
Answer: sphere
Working: A sphere has 1 curved surface and 0 flat faces.

Question 20: A 3-D shape has 1 flat circular face and 1 curved surface that comes to a point. Name this 3-D shape.
Answer: cone
Working: A cone has 1 flat circular face and 1 curved surface.
