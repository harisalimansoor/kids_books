=== Topic: Standard units and measuring length/height (m/cm), mass (kg/g), capacity/volume (l/ml), and temperature (^ degreesC) ===
Domain: Measurement

--- Measuring length and height (m and cm) ---

Question 1: A pencil is lined up along a centimetre ruler. One end is at 0 cm and the other end is at 14 cm. How long is the pencil?
Answer: 14 cm
Working: The pencil starts at 0 cm and ends at 14 cm, so its length is 14 cm.

Question 2: Which standard unit, metres or centimetres, is best to measure the length of a school playground?
Answer: metres
Working: A playground is very large, so metres is the appropriate standard unit to measure its length.

Question 3: A ribbon is 45 cm long. Ben cuts 18 cm off the ribbon. What is the length of the ribbon left?
Answer: 27 cm
Working: 45 cm - 18 cm = 27 cm.

Question 4: A sunflower was 24 cm tall last week. This week it is 31 cm tall. How many centimetres has the sunflower grown?
Answer: 7 cm
Working: 31 cm - 24 cm = 7 cm.

Question 5: A toy train starts at the 5 cm mark on a ruler and ends at the 19 cm mark. How long is the toy train in centimetres?
Answer: 14 cm
Working: 19 cm - 5 cm = 14 cm.

Question 6: A red strip of paper is 36 cm long. A blue strip of paper is 52 cm long. How many centimetres longer is the blue strip than the red strip?
Answer: 16 cm
Working: 52 cm - 36 cm = 16 cm.

--- Measuring mass (kg and g) ---

Question 7: A toy car is placed on a balance scale. It balances equally with five 10 g weights. What is the mass of the toy car?
Answer: 50 g
Working: 5 × 10 g = 50 g.

Question 8: Which standard unit, grams or kilograms, is best to measure the mass of a heavy sack of potatoes?
Answer: kilograms
Working: A heavy sack of potatoes has a large mass, so kilograms is the appropriate unit.

Question 9: A balance scale is level. On one side is a wooden block. On the other side are two 20 g weights and one 5 g weight. What is the mass of the wooden block?
Answer: 45 g
Working: 20 g + 20 g + 5 g = 45 g.

Question 10: A baker has a bowl with 65 g of sugar. She uses 28 g for a cake. How many grams of sugar are left in the bowl?
Answer: 37 g
Working: 65 g - 28 g = 37 g.

Question 11: An apple has a mass of 75 g. A strawberry has a mass of 20 g. How much heavier is the apple than the strawberry?
Answer: 55 g
Working: 75 g - 20 g = 55 g.

Question 12: Box A has a mass of 43 kg. Box B is 16 kg lighter than Box A. What is the mass of Box B?
Answer: 27 kg
Working: 43 kg - 16 kg = 27 kg.

--- Measuring capacity and volume (l and ml) ---

Question 13: A measuring jug has marks counting up in tens: 10 ml, 20 ml, 30 ml, 40 ml, 50 ml. The water level reaches the line halfway between 30 ml and 40 ml. What is the volume of water in the jug?
Answer: 35 ml
Working: Halfway between 30 ml and 40 ml is 35 ml.

Question 14: Which standard unit, litres or millilitres, is best to measure the capacity of a garden paddling pool?
Answer: litres
Working: A paddling pool holds a large amount of liquid, so litres is the appropriate unit.

Question 15: A bucket contains 15 litres of water. Jack pours in another 9 litres of water. How many litres of water are in the bucket now?
Answer: 24 litres
Working: 15 litres + 9 litres = 24 litres.

Question 16: A bottle contains 45 ml of medicine. Each dose is 5 ml. How many full 5 ml doses are in the bottle?
Answer: 9
Working: 45 ÷ 5 = 9.

Question 17: A large watering can holds 10 litres of water. A small can holds 2 litres of water. How many small cans of water are needed to fill the large watering can?
Answer: 5
Working: 10 ÷ 2 = 5.

Question 18: A jug contains 80 ml of juice. Amy drinks 25 ml of juice and then pours out another 18 ml. How many millilitres of juice remain in the jug?
Answer: 37 ml
Working: 80 ml - 25 ml = 55 ml; 55 ml - 18 ml = 37 ml.

--- Measuring temperature (°C) ---

Question 19: A thermometer scale counts up in steps of 2 °C: 0, 2, 4, 6, 8, 10, 12. The red liquid reaches the mark for 10 °C. What is the temperature shown on the thermometer?
Answer: 10 °C
Working: The liquid level is at the 10 °C mark, so the temperature is 10 °C.

Question 20: A cup of hot soup has a temperature of 55 °C. A glass of cold water has a temperature of 8 °C. Which item has the higher temperature?
Answer: the cup of hot soup
Working: 55 °C is greater than 8 °C, so the cup of hot soup has the higher temperature.

Question 21: A thermometer scale has marks counting up in 2s: 20 °C, 22 °C, 24 °C, 26 °C, 28 °C. The red liquid stops halfway between 24 °C and 26 °C. What temperature does the thermometer show?
Answer: 25 °C
Working: Halfway between 24 and 26 is 25, so the temperature is 25 °C.

Question 22: In the morning, the classroom temperature was 15 °C. By the afternoon, the temperature rose by 7 °C. What was the temperature in the afternoon?
Answer: 22 °C
Working: 15 °C + 7 °C = 22 °C.

Question 23: On Monday, the temperature in a greenhouse was 29 °C. On Tuesday, it was 8 °C cooler. What was the temperature on Tuesday?
Answer: 21 °C
Working: 29 °C - 8 °C = 21 °C.

Question 24: In London, the temperature is 23 °C. In Edinburgh, the temperature is 15 °C. How many degrees Celsius warmer is London than Edinburgh?
Answer: 8 °C
Working: 23 °C - 15 °C = 8 °C.
