###################################
Add and subtract multiples of ten and two-digit numbers without regrouping
###################################
Domain: Addition and Subtraction

1. Add and subtract multiples of ten
   From: multiples of 10 ($40 + 60 = 100$, $56 + 20 = 76$)

2. Add and subtract two-digit numbers without regrouping
   From: Add and subtract 1-digit or 2-digit numbers without regrouping up to 100
