=== Topic: Compare, describe, and solve practical problems for lengths, heights, mass, capacity, and time ===
Domain: Measurement

--- Lengths and heights ---

Question 1: A red ribbon is 12 cm long. A blue ribbon is 8 cm long. Which ribbon is longer?
Answer: Red ribbon
Working: 12 cm is greater than 8 cm, so the red ribbon is longer.

Question 2: Tree A is 9 metres tall. Tree B is 4 metres tall. Which tree is shorter?
Answer: Tree B
Working: 4 metres is less than 9 metres, so Tree B is shorter.

Question 3: A toy tower is 7 cubes tall. A second tower is 13 cubes tall. How many cubes taller is the second tower?
Answer: 6 cubes
Working: 13 − 7 = 6 cubes.

Question 4: A green crayon is 6 cm long. A yellow crayon is double the length of the green crayon. How long is the yellow crayon?
Answer: 12 cm
Working: Double 6 is 6 + 6 = 12 cm.

Question 5: Maya builds a paper chain that is 14 cm long. Noah builds a paper chain that is half the length of Maya's chain. How long is Noah's paper chain?
Answer: 7 cm
Working: Half of 14 is 7, so Noah's paper chain is 7 cm long.

Question 6: A piece of string is 16 cm long. A piece measuring 9 cm is cut off. How many centimetres of string are left?
Answer: 7 cm
Working: 16 − 9 = 7 cm.

--- Mass ---

Question 7: A teddy bear balances 5 wooden blocks on a balance scale. A toy train balances 9 wooden blocks. Which toy is heavier?
Answer: Toy train
Working: 9 blocks is more than 5 blocks, so the toy train is heavier.

Question 8: An apple is lighter than a pumpkin. Which object is heavier?
Answer: Pumpkin
Working: If the apple is lighter than the pumpkin, the pumpkin must be heavier.

Question 9: A bag of flour has a mass of 15 kg. A bag of rice has a mass of 11 kg. Which bag is lighter?
Answer: Bag of rice
Working: 11 kg is less than 15 kg, so the bag of rice is lighter.

Question 10: A book balances 8 marbles on a balance scale. A pencil case balances 3 marbles fewer than the book. How many marbles balance the pencil case?
Answer: 5 marbles
Working: 8 − 3 = 5 marbles.

Question 11: A melon balances 10 wooden cubes on a scale. Two identical pears together balance the same melon. How many cubes does one pear balance?
Answer: 5 cubes
Working: Half of 10 is 5, so one pear balances 5 cubes.

--- Capacity ---

Question 12: Bottle A holds 6 cups of water. Bottle B holds 10 cups of water. Which bottle holds more water?
Answer: Bottle B
Working: 10 cups is more than 6 cups, so Bottle B holds more water.

Question 13: Glass 1 has no juice in it at all. Glass 2 is filled to the top with juice. Which word describes Glass 1: full or empty?
Answer: Empty
Working: A container with nothing inside it is empty.

Question 14: A jug holds 18 cups of water when full. How many cups of water are in the jug when it is half full?
Answer: 9 cups
Working: Half of 18 is 9, so there are 9 cups of water.

Question 15: A teapot holds 8 mugs of tea when full. 5 mugs of tea have been poured out. How many mugs of tea are left in the teapot?
Answer: 3 mugs
Working: 8 − 5 = 3 mugs.

Question 16: A small fish tank needs 12 bottles of water to be completely full. Leo pours in 4 bottles in the morning and 3 bottles in the afternoon. How many more bottles of water are needed to fill the tank?
Answer: 5 bottles
Working: 4 + 3 = 7 bottles poured so far. 12 − 7 = 5 bottles needed.

--- Time ---

Question 17: Amy runs a race in 14 seconds. Liam runs the same race in 18 seconds. Who is quicker?
Answer: Amy
Working: 14 seconds is fewer seconds than 18 seconds, so Amy is quicker.

Question 18: A lesson starts at 9 o'clock. Oliver arrives at 8 o'clock. Did Oliver arrive earlier or later than 9 o'clock?
Answer: Earlier
Working: 8 o'clock comes before 9 o'clock, so Oliver arrived earlier.

Question 19: Tom takes 10 minutes to walk to the park. Sara takes 6 minutes to walk to the park. Who takes a shorter time?
Answer: Sara
Working: 6 minutes is less than 10 minutes, so Sara takes a shorter time.

Question 20: A loaf of bread takes 20 minutes to bake. A batch of rolls takes 13 minutes to bake. How many minutes quicker do the rolls bake than the loaf of bread?
Answer: 7 minutes
Working: 20 − 13 = 7 minutes.

Question 21: Dan finishes his puzzle in 15 minutes. Ruby takes 4 minutes longer than Dan. How many minutes does Ruby take to finish her puzzle?
Answer: 19 minutes
Working: 15 + 4 = 19 minutes.
