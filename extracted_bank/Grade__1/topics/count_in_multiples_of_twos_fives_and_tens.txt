=== Topic: Count in multiples of twos, fives, and tens ===
Domain: Number and Place Value

--- Count in multiples of twos ---

Question 1: What is the next number in this sequence: 2, 4, 6, 8, ___?
Answer: 10
Working: Counting forwards in twos: 2, 4, 6, 8, 10. The next number is 10.

Question 2: Fill in the missing number: 10, 12, ___, 16, 18.
Answer: 14
Working: The numbers increase by 2 each time. 12 + 2 = 14.

Question 3: There are 6 pairs of socks on the line. How many socks are there in total?
Answer: 12
Working: Each pair has 2 socks. Count in twos 6 times: 2, 4, 6, 8, 10, 12 socks.

Question 4: Count backwards in twos to find the missing number: 20, 18, 16, 14, ___.
Answer: 12
Working: Counting backwards in twos from 14 gives 12 (14 - 2 = 12).

Question 5: Lucas has four 2p coins. How much money does he have altogether?
Answer: 8p
Working: Count in twos for 4 coins: 2p, 4p, 6p, 8p.

Question 6: What is the missing number in this backward sequence: 16, 14, ___, 10, 8?
Answer: 12
Working: Counting backwards in twos: 16, 14, 12, 10, 8. The missing number is 12.

--- Count in multiples of fives ---

Question 7: What is the next number in this sequence: 5, 10, 15, 20, ___?
Answer: 25
Working: Counting forwards in fives: 5, 10, 15, 20, 25. The next number is 25.

Question 8: Fill in the missing number: 20, 25, ___, 35, 40.
Answer: 30
Working: The numbers increase by 5 each time. 25 + 5 = 30.

Question 9: There are 4 hands. Each hand has 5 fingers. How many fingers are there altogether?
Answer: 20
Working: Count in fives for 4 hands: 5, 10, 15, 20 fingers.

Question 10: Maya has five 5p coins in her purse. How much money does Maya have in total?
Answer: 25p
Working: Count in fives for 5 coins: 5p, 10p, 15p, 20p, 25p.

Question 11: Count backwards in fives: 45, 40, 35, 30, ___.
Answer: 25
Working: Counting backwards in fives: 30 - 5 = 25.

Question 12: What is the missing number in this sequence: 15, 20, 25, ___, 35?
Answer: 30
Working: Counting forwards in fives: 15, 20, 25, 30, 35. The missing number is 30.

--- Count in multiples of tens ---

Question 13: What is the next number: 10, 20, 30, 40, ___?
Answer: 50
Working: Counting forwards in tens: 10, 20, 30, 40, 50. The next number is 50.

Question 14: Fill in the missing number: 40, 50, ___, 70, 80.
Answer: 60
Working: The numbers increase by 10 each time. 50 + 10 = 60.

Question 15: There are 6 packs of pencils. Each pack contains 10 pencils. How many pencils are there in total?
Answer: 60
Working: Count in tens 6 times: 10, 20, 30, 40, 50, 60 pencils.

Question 16: Sam has seven 10p coins. How much money does Sam have altogether?
Answer: 70p
Working: Count in tens for 7 coins: 10p, 20p, 30p, 40p, 50p, 60p, 70p.

Question 17: Count backwards in tens: 90, 80, 70, 60, ___.
Answer: 50
Working: Counting backwards in tens: 60 - 10 = 50.

Question 18: What is the missing number in this sequence: 100, 90, ___, 70, 60?
Answer: 80
Working: Counting backwards in tens from 100: 100, 90, 80, 70, 60. The missing number is 80.
