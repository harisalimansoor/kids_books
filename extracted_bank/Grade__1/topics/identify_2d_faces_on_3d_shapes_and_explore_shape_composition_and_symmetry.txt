=== Topic: Identify 2D faces on 3D shapes and explore shape composition and symmetry ===
Domain: Geometry: Properties of Shapes

--- Identify 2D faces on 3D shapes ---

Question 1: A cube has 6 flat faces. What 2D shape is every face of a cube?
Answer: square
Working: All 6 flat faces on a cube are squares.

Question 2: A cone has 1 flat face at the bottom. What 2D shape is the flat face of a cone?
Answer: circle
Working: The flat base of a cone is a circle.

Question 3: A cylinder has 2 flat faces. What 2D shape are the flat faces of a cylinder?
Answer: circle
Working: The two flat end faces of a cylinder are circles.

Question 4: A square-based pyramid has 1 square face on the bottom and some triangular faces. How many triangular faces does it have?
Answer: 4
Working: A square base has 4 sides, so there are 4 triangular faces that meet at the top point.

Question 5: How many flat faces does a sphere have?
Answer: 0
Working: A sphere has only 1 curved surface and 0 flat faces.

Question 6: Ruby presses a flat face of a cuboid into sand. The print in the sand has 4 sides, with 2 sides longer than the other 2 sides. What 2D shape did she print?
Answer: rectangle
Working: A 4-sided flat face with 2 longer sides and 2 shorter sides is a rectangle.

--- Explore shape composition ---

Question 7: Ethan puts 2 identical squares side by side so their edges touch. What 2D shape do the 2 squares make together?
Answer: rectangle
Working: Joining two identical squares side by side forms a rectangle.

Question 8: Sophie cuts a square straight from one corner to the opposite corner. How many triangles does she make?
Answer: 2
Working: Cutting a square from one corner to the opposite corner splits it into 2 triangles.

Question 9: Liam puts 2 identical rectangles together along their long sides. The new shape has 4 sides of equal length. What 2D shape has he made?
Answer: square
Working: A 4-sided shape with all sides of equal length is a square.

Question 10: Zara makes a larger triangle using small identical triangles. She uses 1 small triangle at the top and 3 small triangles in the bottom row. How many small triangles did she use in total?
Answer: 4
Working: 1 + 3 = 4 small triangles in total.

Question 11: Ben joins 2 identical triangles edge to edge along one side. How many sides does the new shape have?
Answer: 4
Working: When two 3-sided triangles join along one edge, the two joined edges become the inside, leaving 4 outer sides.

--- Explore symmetry ---

Question 12: A ladybird has a line of symmetry down its back. It has 3 spots on its left side. How many spots are on its right side?
Answer: 3
Working: Because the ladybird is symmetrical, both sides are identical, so there are 3 spots on the right side.

Question 13: A butterfly has 5 spots on its left wing. The pattern on its wings is symmetrical. How many spots does the butterfly have altogether?
Answer: 10
Working: A symmetrical butterfly has 5 spots on each wing: 5 + 5 = 10 spots altogether.

Question 14: Chloe folds a paper shape in half so the two sides match exactly. How many equal parts has the shape been folded into?
Answer: 2
Working: Folding a shape in half divides it into 2 equal matching parts.

Question 15: A vertical line of symmetry divides a shape into two halves that match exactly. If the left half has 4 stripes, how many stripes are on the right half?
Answer: 4
Working: In a symmetrical shape with a vertical line of symmetry, both halves match, so the right half has 4 stripes.

Question 16: Tom paints half of a symmetrical mask with 3 yellow circles. How many yellow circles will the whole mask have when both halves are completed?
Answer: 6
Working: Each half has 3 yellow circles. 3 + 3 = 6 yellow circles altogether.
