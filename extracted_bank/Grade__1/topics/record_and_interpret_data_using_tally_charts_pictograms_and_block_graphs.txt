=== Topic: Record and interpret data using tally charts, pictograms, and block graphs ===
Domain: Statistics and Data Handling

--- Tally charts ---

Question 1: A tally chart shows 1 bundle of 5 tally marks and 2 single tally marks for red cars. How many red cars are there in total?
Answer: 7
Working: 1 bundle of 5 plus 2 single marks is 5 + 2 = 7.

Question 2: In a class tally chart, 4 children chose cats and 1 bundle of 5 children chose dogs. How many children chose dogs?
Answer: 5
Working: 1 bundle of tally marks equals 5.

Question 3: In a tally chart of favourite fruits, apples have 1 bundle of 5 and 3 single tallies. Bananas have 1 bundle of 5 and 1 single tally. How many more children chose apples than bananas?
Answer: 2
Working: Apples: 5 + 3 = 8. Bananas: 5 + 1 = 6. Difference: 8 − 6 = 2.

Question 4: Sara recorded birds in her garden with a tally chart. Robins have 1 bundle of 5. Sparrows have 1 bundle of 5 and 4 single tallies. What is the total number of robins and sparrows?
Answer: 14
Working: Robins = 5. Sparrows = 5 + 4 = 9. Total = 5 + 9 = 14.

Question 5: A tally chart has 7 tally marks for blue counters and 12 tally marks for yellow counters. How many fewer blue counters are there than yellow counters?
Answer: 5
Working: 12 − 7 = 5 fewer blue counters.

--- Pictograms ---

Question 6: In a pictogram, 1 star picture stands for 1 sticker. Leo has 6 star pictures next to his name. How many stickers does Leo have?
Answer: 6
Working: Each star picture is 1 sticker, so 6 stars = 6 stickers.

Question 7: In a pictogram, 1 circle represents 1 book. Class 1 has 8 circles and Class 2 has 5 circles. How many books did the two classes read altogether?
Answer: 13
Working: 8 + 5 = 13 books altogether.

Question 8: A pictogram shows favourite pets. The key shows that 1 paw picture stands for 2 pets. The dog row has 3 paw pictures. How many pets are dogs?
Answer: 6
Working: Count in twos for 3 pictures: 2, 4, 6. There are 6 dogs.

Question 9: In a pictogram, 1 smiley face stands for 2 children. The row for pizza has 4 smiley faces. The row for pasta has 2 smiley faces. How many more children chose pizza than pasta?
Answer: 4
Working: Pizza: 4 groups of 2 = 8. Pasta: 2 groups of 2 = 4. 8 − 4 = 4 more children.

--- Block graphs ---

Question 10: In a block graph, each block stands for 1 child. The tower for green has 7 blocks. The tower for red has 4 blocks. Which colour has the most blocks?
Answer: green
Working: 7 is greater than 4, so green has the most blocks.

Question 11: In a block graph, each block represents 1 animal. There are 9 blocks for sheep and 6 blocks for cows. How many more sheep are there than cows?
Answer: 3
Working: 9 − 6 = 3 more sheep.

Question 12: A block graph shows favourite sports. The running column has 5 blocks and the swimming column has 8 blocks. Each block is 1 vote. How many votes did running and swimming get altogether?
Answer: 13
Working: 5 + 8 = 13 votes altogether.

Question 13: In a block graph, each block is 1 vote. Football has 10 blocks, tennis has 4 blocks, and swimming has 6 blocks. How many more votes does football have than tennis?
Answer: 6
Working: Football has 10 and tennis has 4. 10 − 4 = 6 more votes.
