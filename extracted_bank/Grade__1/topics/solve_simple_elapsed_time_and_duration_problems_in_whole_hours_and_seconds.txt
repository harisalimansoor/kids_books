=== Topic: Solve simple elapsed time and duration problems in whole hours and seconds ===
Domain: Measurement

--- Elapsed time and duration in whole hours ---

Question 1: A film starts at 2 o'clock and lasts for 2 hours. What time does the film finish?
Answer: 4 o'clock
Working: 2 o'clock + 2 hours = 4 o'clock

Question 2: Sam starts playing at 3 o'clock and stops at 5 o'clock. How many hours does Sam play for?
Answer: 2 hours
Working: 5 - 3 = 2 hours

Question 3: A train leaves the station at 8 o'clock. The journey takes 3 hours. What time does the train arrive?
Answer: 11 o'clock
Working: 8 + 3 = 11, so the train arrives at 11 o'clock.

Question 4: A party finishes at 6 o'clock. It lasted for 2 hours. What time did the party start?
Answer: 4 o'clock
Working: 6 - 2 = 4, so the party started at 4 o'clock.

Question 5: Mia visits the farm from 10 o'clock until 1 o'clock. How many hours did she spend at the farm?
Answer: 3 hours
Working: Count on from 10 to 1: 10 to 11 (1 hour), 11 to 12 (2 hours), 12 to 1 (3 hours).

Question 6: Jack bakes bread from 1 o'clock to 4 o'clock. Lily bakes cakes from 2 o'clock to 6 o'clock. Who spent more hours baking?
Answer: Lily
Working: Jack baked for 4 - 1 = 3 hours. Lily baked for 6 - 2 = 4 hours. 4 is more than 3, so Lily spent more hours baking.

--- Elapsed time and duration in seconds ---

Question 7: Ben runs a race in 12 seconds. Tom runs the race in 15 seconds. Who is quicker?
Answer: Ben
Working: 12 seconds is fewer seconds than 15 seconds, so Ben is quicker.

Question 8: A timer beeps after 7 seconds. Another timer beeps 5 seconds later. How many seconds does the second timer take altogether?
Answer: 12 seconds
Working: 7 + 5 = 12 seconds

Question 9: Ava washes her hands for 20 seconds. Leo washes his hands for 14 seconds. How many more seconds did Ava wash her hands for?
Answer: 6 seconds
Working: 20 - 14 = 6 seconds

Question 10: Noah skips for 8 seconds and then hops for 9 seconds. How many seconds did he exercise for in total?
Answer: 17 seconds
Working: 8 + 9 = 17 seconds

Question 11: Ella builds a block tower in 16 seconds. Maya takes 4 seconds less. How many seconds does Maya take?
Answer: 12 seconds
Working: 16 - 4 = 12 seconds

Question 12: In a running race, Ruby took 18 seconds, Max took 13 seconds, and Oliver took 15 seconds. Who won the race?
Answer: Max
Working: The winner is the person with the fewest seconds. 13 is less than 15 and 18, so Max won.
