=== Topic: Recognise and know the value of UK coins and notes and calculate simple change ===
Domain: Measurement

--- Recognise and know the value of UK coins and notes ---

Question 1: Which coin has a greater value: a 2p coin or a 10p coin?
Answer: 10p coin
Working: 10p is worth more than 2p because 10 is greater than 2.

Question 2: How many 1p coins have the exact same value as one 5p coin?
Answer: 5
Working: One 5p coin is equal to five 1p coins.

Question 3: Name the smallest value UK note.
Answer: £5 note
Working: The UK notes are £5, £10, £20, and £50. The note with the smallest value is £5.

Question 4: Sam has four 2p coins in his pocket. How many pence does Sam have in total?
Answer: 8p
Working: Count in twos: 2p, 4p, 6p, 8p. 4 × 2p = 8p.

Question 5: Amira has three 5p coins. How many pence does she have altogether?
Answer: 15p
Working: Count in fives: 5p, 10p, 15p. 3 × 5p = 15p.

Question 6: How many 10p coins make 50p?
Answer: 5
Working: Count in tens to 50: 10, 20, 30, 40, 50. That is 5 coins.

Question 7: Jack has one £10 note and two £5 notes. How much money does Jack have altogether?
Answer: £20
Working: Two £5 notes make £10. £10 + £10 = £20.

Question 8: Ava has one 20p coin and one 5p coin. Ben has three 10p coins. Who has more money?
Answer: Ben
Working: Ava has 20p + 5p = 25p. Ben has 10p + 10p + 10p = 30p. 30p is more than 25p, so Ben has more money.

Question 9: True or False? Ten 1p coins have the exact same value as one 10p coin.
Answer: True
Working: Ten 1p coins equal 10p, which is the same value as one 10p coin.

--- Calculate simple change ---

Question 10: Mia has a 10p coin. She buys a sticker for 7p. How much change should Mia receive?
Answer: 3p
Working: 10p - 7p = 3p.

Question 11: Tom pays with a 10p coin for an apple that costs 4p. How much change does he get?
Answer: 6p
Working: 10p - 4p = 6p.

Question 12: Ella uses a 20p coin to buy a pencil that costs 12p. How much change does she get?
Answer: 8p
Working: 20p - 12p = 8p.

Question 13: Noah buys a badge for 15p. He pays with a 20p coin. How much change does Noah receive?
Answer: 5p
Working: 20p - 15p = 5p.

Question 14: Harry pays for a book with a £10 note. The book costs £6. How much change does Harry get?
Answer: £4
Working: £10 - £6 = £4.

Question 15: Lily buys a toy for £14. She pays with a £20 note. How much change should she receive?
Answer: £6
Working: £20 - £14 = £6.

Question 16: Lucas buys two small bouncy balls. Each ball costs 5p. He pays with a 20p coin. How much change does Lucas get?
Answer: 10p
Working: Total cost = 5p + 5p = 10p. Change = 20p - 10p = 10p.
