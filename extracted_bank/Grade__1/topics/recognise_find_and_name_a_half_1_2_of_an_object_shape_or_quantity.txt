=== Topic: Recognise, find, and name a half (1/2) of an object, shape, or quantity ===
Domain: Fractions

--- Half of an object ---

Question 1: A piece of ribbon is cut into 2 equal parts. What fraction is 1 of the parts?
Answer: 1/2
Working: When an object is split into 2 equal parts, each part is 1 of 2 equal parts, which is 1/2 (a half).

Question 2: Sam cuts an apple into 2 equal pieces. What is 1 of the pieces called?
Answer: a half
Working: An apple cut into 2 equal pieces makes 2 halves. 1 of the pieces is a half.

Question 3: A strip of paper is folded down the middle into 2 equal parts. One part is coloured in. What fraction of the paper strip is coloured in?
Answer: 1/2
Working: 1 out of 2 equal parts is coloured in, so 1/2 of the strip is coloured in.

Question 4: A piece of string is 10 cm long. It is cut into 2 equal halves. How long is each half?
Answer: 5 cm
Working: Half of 10 cm is 10 cm ÷ 2 = 5 cm.

Question 5: A stick of modelling clay is 12 cm long. Ben cuts it in half. How long is one half of the stick?
Answer: 6 cm
Working: Half of 12 cm is 12 cm ÷ 2 = 6 cm.

Question 6: Mia cuts a whole cucumber into two pieces. One piece is much longer than the other piece. Has Mia cut the cucumber in half? Write yes or no.
Answer: no
Working: To make a half, the two parts must be equal. Mia's pieces are not equal, so she has not cut it in half.

Question 7: Jack says, "To find half of a piece of ribbon, I can cut it into 2 parts of any size." Is Jack correct? Write yes or no.
Answer: no
Working: A half is 1 of 2 equal parts. The parts cannot be any size; they must be equal in length.

--- Half of a shape ---

Question 8: A square is divided into 2 equal parts. One part is shaded. What fraction of the square is shaded?
Answer: 1/2
Working: 1 of 2 equal parts of the shape is shaded, which is 1/2.

Question 9: A circle is split into 2 equal parts. What is each part called?
Answer: a half
Working: Each of the 2 equal parts of a whole shape is called a half.

Question 10: A rectangle is split by a straight line into 2 equal triangles. What fraction of the rectangle is 1 of the triangles?
Answer: 1/2
Working: The rectangle is divided into 2 equal parts, so 1 part is 1/2 of the rectangle.

Question 11: A circle is divided into 2 parts. One part is smaller than the other part. Is each part a half of the circle? Write yes or no.
Answer: no
Working: A half must be 1 of 2 equal parts. Since the parts are unequal, they are not halves.

Question 12: A large square is made of 4 equal smaller squares. How many of the smaller squares need to be shaded to shade half of the large square?
Answer: 2
Working: Half of 4 equal squares is 4 ÷ 2 = 2 squares.

Question 13: A shape is divided into 2 equal parts. Ella shades 1 of the parts. What fraction of the shape is left unshaded?
Answer: 1/2
Working: There are 2 equal parts in total. 1 part is shaded, so 1 of 2 equal parts is unshaded, which is 1/2.

Question 14: How many equal halves make 1 whole shape?
Answer: 2
Working: 2 equal halves join together to make 1 whole shape.

--- Half of a quantity ---

Question 15: What is half of 6?
Answer: 3
Working: Share 6 into 2 equal groups: 6 ÷ 2 = 3.

Question 16: What is half of 10?
Answer: 5
Working: Share 10 into 2 equal groups: 10 ÷ 2 = 5.

Question 17: What is half of 4?
Answer: 2
Working: Share 4 into 2 equal groups: 4 ÷ 2 = 2.

Question 18: There are 8 apples in a bowl. Tom eats half of the apples. How many apples does Tom eat?
Answer: 4
Working: Half of 8 is 8 ÷ 2 = 4 apples.

Question 19: There are 14 counters on a table. Half of them are blue. How many counters are blue?
Answer: 7
Working: Half of 14 is 14 ÷ 2 = 7 counters.

Question 20: Share 12 strawberries equally between 2 plates. How many strawberries are on each plate?
Answer: 6
Working: Sharing 12 equally into 2 groups gives 12 ÷ 2 = 6 strawberries on each plate.

Question 21: What is half of 16?
Answer: 8
Working: Share 16 into 2 equal groups: 16 ÷ 2 = 8.

Question 22: There are 18 pencils in a tin. Half of the pencils are red. How many red pencils are there?
Answer: 9
Working: Half of 18 is 18 ÷ 2 = 9 pencils.

Question 23: What is half of 20?
Answer: 10
Working: Share 20 into 2 equal groups: 20 ÷ 2 = 10.

Question 24: Half of a group of buttons is 5. How many buttons are in the whole group?
Answer: 10
Working: If 1 half is 5, the whole group is double 5: 5 + 5 = 10 buttons.

Question 25: Half of a number is 4. What is the number?
Answer: 8
Working: Double 4 gives the whole number: 4 + 4 = 8.

Question 26: Lily has 12 stickers. She gives half of her stickers to Noah. How many stickers does Lily have left?
Answer: 6
Working: Half of 12 is 6. Lily gives away 6 stickers, so she has 12 − 6 = 6 stickers left.
