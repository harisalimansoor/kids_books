=== Topic: Recognise, find, and name a quarter (1/4) of an object, shape, or quantity ===
Domain: Fractions

--- Quarter of an object ---

Question 1: How many equal parts must an object be cut into to make quarters?
Answer: 4
Working: A quarter means 1 of 4 equal parts, so the object must be cut into 4 equal parts.

Question 2: Sam cuts a paper strip into 4 equal parts. What is the name of 1 of the parts?
Answer: a quarter
Working: 1 of 4 equal parts of an object is called a quarter (or one quarter).

Question 3: An apple is sliced into 4 equal pieces. Ava eats 1 piece. What fraction of the apple did Ava eat?
Answer: 1/4
Working: Ava ate 1 out of 4 equal pieces, which is 1/4 (one quarter).

Question 4: A pizza is cut into 4 equal slices. How many quarter slices make 1 whole pizza?
Answer: 4
Working: There are 4 quarters in 1 whole, so 4 quarter slices make the whole pizza.

Question 5: Leo cuts a ribbon into 4 pieces, but the pieces are not equal in size. Has Leo cut the ribbon into quarters? Write yes or no.
Answer: no
Working: Quarters must be equal parts. Since the pieces are not equal, they are not quarters.

--- Quarter of a shape ---

Question 6: A circle is split into 4 equal parts. 1 part is shaded. What fraction of the circle is shaded?
Answer: 1/4
Working: 1 part out of 4 equal parts is shaded, which represents 1/4.

Question 7: A square is split into 4 equal parts. How many parts must you colour to show one quarter (1/4)?
Answer: 1
Working: One quarter is 1 out of 4 equal parts, so colour 1 part.

Question 8: A rectangle is divided into 4 equal squares. Tom colours 1 square blue. What fraction of the rectangle is blue?
Answer: 1/4
Working: 1 out of 4 equal parts is coloured blue, which is 1/4.

Question 9: Amy draws a shape and splits it into 4 equal parts. She colours 1 part. Has Amy coloured 1/4 of the shape? Write yes or no.
Answer: yes
Working: The shape is split into 4 equal parts and 1 part is coloured, which is 1/4.

Question 10: A circle is divided into 4 parts: 2 large parts and 2 small parts. 1 small part is coloured red. Is one quarter (1/4) of the circle coloured red? Write yes or no.
Answer: no
Working: To show quarters, all 4 parts must be equal in size. The parts are not equal, so it is not 1/4.

--- Quarter of a quantity ---

Question 11: Find 1/4 of 4 counters.
Answer: 1
Working: Share 4 counters into 4 equal groups. There is 1 counter in each group: 4 ÷ 4 = 1.

Question 12: What is one quarter of 8?
Answer: 2
Working: Share 8 into 4 equal groups. There are 2 in each group: 8 ÷ 4 = 2.

Question 13: Jack has 12 stickers. He gives 1/4 of his stickers to Ben. How many stickers does Ben get?
Answer: 3
Working: Share 12 stickers into 4 equal groups: 12 ÷ 4 = 3 stickers.

Question 14: There are 16 pencils shared equally among 4 pots. How many pencils are in 1 pot?
Answer: 4
Working: Finding 1/4 of 16 means sharing 16 into 4 equal pots: 16 ÷ 4 = 4.

Question 15: What is 1/4 of 20?
Answer: 5
Working: Share 20 into 4 equal groups: 20 ÷ 4 = 5.

Question 16: One quarter (1/4) of a group of marbles is 2. How many marbles are in the whole group altogether?
Answer: 8
Working: There are 4 equal quarters in the whole group. 4 groups of 2 is 2 + 2 + 2 + 2 = 8.
