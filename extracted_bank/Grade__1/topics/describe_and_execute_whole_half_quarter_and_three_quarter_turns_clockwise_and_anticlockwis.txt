=== Topic: Describe and execute whole, half, quarter, and three-quarter turns (clockwise and anticlockwise) ===
Domain: Geometry: Position and Direction

--- Whole turns ---

Question 1: An arrow points up. It makes 1 whole turn. Which way does the arrow point now?
Answer: up
Working: A whole turn brings an object back to its starting position, so it points up.

Question 2: Jack is facing the classroom door. He makes 1 whole turn. What is he facing now?
Answer: the classroom door
Working: After 1 whole turn, Jack faces the same direction he started in, which is the classroom door.

Question 3: How many half turns make 1 whole turn?
Answer: 2
Working: 2 half turns make 1 whole turn.

Question 4: How many quarter turns make 1 whole turn?
Answer: 4
Working: 4 quarter turns make 1 whole turn.

--- Half turns ---

Question 5: An arrow points up. It makes a half turn. Which way does the arrow point now: up or down?
Answer: down
Working: A half turn turns an object to face the opposite direction, so it points down.

Question 6: An arrow points to the right. It makes a half turn. Which way does the arrow point now: left or right?
Answer: left
Working: The opposite of right is left, so after a half turn it points left.

Question 7: A toy robot is facing the window. It makes a half turn. Is it now facing the window or facing away from the window?
Answer: facing away from the window
Working: A half turn faces the opposite direction, so the robot is facing away from the window.

Question 8: How many quarter turns make a half turn?
Answer: 2
Working: 2 quarter turns combine to make a half turn.

--- Quarter turns ---

Question 9: An arrow points up. It makes a quarter turn clockwise. Which direction is it pointing now: left or right?
Answer: right
Working: A quarter turn clockwise from up points to the right.

Question 10: An arrow points up. It makes a quarter turn anticlockwise. Which direction is it pointing now: left or right?
Answer: left
Working: A quarter turn anticlockwise from up points to the left.

Question 11: A pointer points to the right. It makes a quarter turn clockwise. Which way is it pointing now: up or down?
Answer: down
Working: From right, a quarter turn clockwise moves the pointer to point down.

Question 12: Sam is facing forwards. He makes 1 quarter turn to the right. Which way is he facing now: left or right?
Answer: right
Working: Turning a quarter turn to the right means Sam is now facing right.

--- Three-quarter turns ---

Question 13: How many quarter turns make a three-quarter turn?
Answer: 3
Working: 3 quarter turns make a three-quarter turn.

Question 14: An arrow points up. It makes a three-quarter turn clockwise. Which direction is it pointing now: up, down, left, or right?
Answer: left
Working: From up: 1 quarter turn clockwise points right, 2 points down, and 3 points left.

Question 15: An arrow points up. It makes a three-quarter turn anticlockwise. Which direction is it pointing now: up, down, left, or right?
Answer: right
Working: From up: 1 quarter turn anticlockwise points left, 2 points down, and 3 points right.

Question 16: A pointer points to the right. It makes a three-quarter turn clockwise. Which way does it point now: up, down, left, or right?
Answer: up
Working: From right: 1 quarter turn clockwise points down, 2 points left, and 3 points up.

--- Clockwise and anticlockwise turns ---

Question 17: Do the hands on a clock turn clockwise or anticlockwise?
Answer: clockwise
Working: The hands on a clock move in the clockwise direction.

Question 18: Turning the opposite way to the hands on a clock is called what: clockwise or anticlockwise?
Answer: anticlockwise
Working: Moving in the opposite direction to clock hands is anticlockwise.

Question 19: An arrow points up. It turns a quarter turn to the right to point right. Did it turn clockwise or anticlockwise?
Answer: clockwise
Working: Turning to the right from the top follows clock hands, so it is a clockwise turn.

Question 20: An arrow points up. It turns a quarter turn to the left to point left. Did it turn clockwise or anticlockwise?
Answer: anticlockwise
Working: Turning to the left from the top is opposite to clock hands, so it is an anticlockwise turn.
