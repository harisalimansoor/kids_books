=== Topic: Measure and record length, height, mass, and capacity using non-standard and standard units ===
Domain: Measurement

--- Measure and record length and height ---

Question 1: A toy car is 4 cubes long. A toy bus is 7 cubes long. How many cubes long are the two toys altogether?
Answer: 11 cubes
Working: Add the two lengths: 4 + 7 = 11 cubes.

Question 2: A pencil starts at 0 cm on a ruler and ends at 12 cm. What is the length of the pencil?
Answer: 12 cm
Working: The pencil goes from 0 cm to 12 cm, so its length is 12 cm.

Question 3: A sunflower is 15 cm tall. A daffodil is 9 cm tall. How much taller is the sunflower than the daffodil?
Answer: 6 cm
Working: Subtract to find the difference: 15 cm - 9 cm = 6 cm.

Question 4: Mia has a blue ribbon that is 8 cm long and a yellow ribbon that is 6 cm long. She joins them end to end. What is the total length of the ribbon?
Answer: 14 cm
Working: Add the two lengths together: 8 + 6 = 14 cm.

Question 5: A tower made of wooden blocks is 18 cubes high. Jack takes 5 cubes off the top. How many cubes high is the tower now?
Answer: 13 cubes
Working: Subtract the cubes removed: 18 - 5 = 13 cubes.

Question 6: A garden path is 10 metres long. Sam walks 4 metres along the path. How many metres does Sam have left to walk?
Answer: 6 metres
Working: Subtract the distance walked from the total length: 10 - 4 = 6 metres.

--- Measure and record mass ---

Question 7: An apple balances exactly with 6 glass marbles on a balance scale. How many marbles balance the apple?
Answer: 6 marbles
Working: The scale balances at 6 marbles, so the mass is 6 marbles.

Question 8: A book has a mass of 8 wooden cubes. A pencil case has a mass of 5 wooden cubes. What is the total mass of the book and the pencil case in cubes?
Answer: 13 cubes
Working: Add the masses: 8 + 5 = 13 cubes.

Question 9: A bag of flour has a mass of 14 grams. A bag of oats has a mass of 8 grams. How many grams heavier is the bag of flour than the bag of oats?
Answer: 6 grams
Working: Subtract to find the difference in mass: 14 - 8 = 6 grams.

Question 10: A parcel has a mass of 7 kg. Another parcel has a mass of 9 kg. What is their total mass in kg?
Answer: 16 kg
Working: Add the two masses: 7 kg + 9 kg = 16 kg.

Question 11: A pumpkin has a mass of 12 kg. A melon has a mass that is half the mass of the pumpkin. What is the mass of the melon?
Answer: 6 kg
Working: Half of 12 kg is 6 kg.

--- Measure and record capacity ---

Question 12: It takes 5 full cups of water to fill a small teapot. How many cups of water are needed to fill 2 teapots of the same size?
Answer: 10 cups
Working: Add the cups for 2 teapots: 5 + 5 = 10 cups (or double 5).

Question 13: A bottle holds 9 glasses of juice. Ben pours out 4 glasses of juice. How many glasses of juice are left in the bottle?
Answer: 5 glasses
Working: Subtract the juice poured out: 9 - 4 = 5 glasses.

Question 14: A bucket contains 11 litres of water. Leo adds 6 more litres of water to the bucket. How many litres of water are in the bucket now?
Answer: 17 litres
Working: Add the litres: 11 + 6 = 17 litres.

Question 15: A red jug holds 15 ml of paint. A blue jug holds 7 ml of paint. How many more millilitres does the red jug hold than the blue jug?
Answer: 8 ml
Working: Subtract to find the difference: 15 - 7 = 8 ml.

Question 16: A large bottle holds 20 litres of water when full. It is currently half full. How many litres of water are in the bottle?
Answer: 10 litres
Working: Find half of 20 litres: 20 divided by 2 = 10 litres.
