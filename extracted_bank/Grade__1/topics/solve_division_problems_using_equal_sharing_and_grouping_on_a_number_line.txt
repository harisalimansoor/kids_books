=== Topic: Solve division problems using equal sharing and grouping on a number line ===
Domain: Multiplication and Division

--- Equal sharing ---

Question 1: Share 6 apples equally between 2 plates. How many apples are on each plate?
Answer: 3
Working: Share 6 equally into 2 groups: 3 + 3 = 6. There are 3 apples on each plate.

Question 2: 8 stickers are shared equally between 2 children. How many stickers does each child get?
Answer: 4
Working: Share 8 equally into 2 groups: 8 shared by 2 is 4.

Question 3: Share 10 biscuits equally between 2 friends. How many biscuits does each friend get?
Answer: 5
Working: Share 10 equally between 2: half of 10 is 5.

Question 4: There are 12 crayons. They are shared equally into 2 pots. How many crayons are in each pot?
Answer: 6
Working: Share 12 into 2 equal parts: 6 + 6 = 12. Each pot has 6 crayons.

Question 5: Share 14 counters equally between 2 bowls. How many counters are in each bowl?
Answer: 7
Working: 14 shared equally into 2 groups gives 7 in each group.

Question 6: 16 marbles are shared equally between 2 boxes. How many marbles are in each box?
Answer: 8
Working: 16 shared equally between 2 is 8 because 8 + 8 = 16.

Question 7: Share 8 cherries equally between 4 children. How many cherries does each child get?
Answer: 2
Working: Share 8 into 4 equal groups: each child gets 2 cherries.

Question 8: Share 12 strawberries equally between 4 plates. How many strawberries are on each plate?
Answer: 3
Working: Share 12 into 4 equal groups: 3 + 3 + 3 + 3 = 12. Each plate gets 3 strawberries.

Question 9: Share 20 pencils equally between 2 pots. How many pencils are in each pot?
Answer: 10
Working: 20 shared equally into 2 groups gives 10 in each pot.

Question 10: Share 20 cubes equally among 4 children. How many cubes does each child receive?
Answer: 5
Working: 20 shared equally into 4 groups gives 5 in each group (5 + 5 + 5 + 5 = 20).

Question 11: Share 15 buttons equally among 5 jars. How many buttons are in each jar?
Answer: 3
Working: 15 shared equally into 5 groups gives 3 in each jar (3 + 3 + 3 + 3 + 3 = 15).

Question 12: Share 30 pennies equally among 3 bags. How many pennies are in each bag?
Answer: 10
Working: 30 shared equally into 3 groups: 10 + 10 + 10 = 30. Each bag has 10 pennies.

--- Grouping on a number line ---

Question 13: Start at 6 on a number line. Count back in jumps of 2 to reach 0: 6 to 4, 4 to 2, 2 to 0. How many jumps of 2 did you make?
Answer: 3
Working: There are 3 jumps of 2 from 6 back to 0: 6 − 2 = 4, 4 − 2 = 2, 2 − 2 = 0.

Question 14: Start at 0 on a number line. Make jumps of 2 forwards until you land on 8. How many jumps of 2 are there?
Answer: 4
Working: Count in 2s to 8: 2, 4, 6, 8. That is 4 jumps of 2.

Question 15: Start at 10 on a number line. Count back in jumps of 2 to 0. How many jumps of 2 do you make?
Answer: 5
Working: Count back in 2s from 10 to 0: 10, 8, 6, 4, 2, 0. There are 5 jumps of 2.

Question 16: A frog starts at 0 on a number line and jumps forwards in jumps of 5. It stops at 15. How many jumps did the frog make?
Answer: 3
Working: Count in 5s up to 15: 5, 10, 15. The frog makes 3 jumps.

Question 17: Start at 14 on a number line. Make jumps of 2 backwards until you reach 0. How many jumps of 2 are there in 14?
Answer: 7
Working: Count backwards in 2s from 14 to 0: 14, 12, 10, 8, 6, 4, 2, 0. There are 7 jumps of 2.

Question 18: Start at 20 on a number line. Count back in jumps of 10 to reach 0. How many jumps of 10 do you make?
Answer: 2
Working: Count back from 20 in 10s: 20 to 10, 10 to 0. That is 2 jumps of 10.

Question 19: A grasshopper makes jumps of 5 along a number line from 0 to 20. How many jumps of 5 does it make?
Answer: 4
Working: Count in 5s to 20: 5, 10, 15, 20. That is 4 jumps of 5.

Question 20: Start at 18 on a number line. Count back in jumps of 2 until you reach 0. How many equal groups of 2 did you subtract?
Answer: 9
Working: Count back in 2s from 18 to 0: 18, 16, 14, 12, 10, 8, 6, 4, 2, 0. There are 9 groups of 2.

Question 21: A counter is moved along a number line from 0 to 30 in equal jumps of 10. How many jumps does it take?
Answer: 3
Working: Count in 10s up to 30: 10, 20, 30. That is 3 jumps of 10.

Question 22: There are 16 socks. Put them into pairs (groups of 2). On a number line, count back in 2s from 16 to 0. How many pairs of socks are there?
Answer: 8
Working: Count back in 2s from 16: 16, 14, 12, 10, 8, 6, 4, 2, 0. There are 8 pairs of socks.
