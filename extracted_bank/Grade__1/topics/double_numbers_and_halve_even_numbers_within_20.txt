=== Topic: Double numbers and halve even numbers within 20 ===
Domain: Multiplication and Division

--- Double numbers within 20 ---

Question 1: What is double 3?
Answer: 6
Working: Double 3 means 3 + 3 = 6.

Question 2: Double 4 is _____.
Answer: 8
Working: 4 + 4 = 8.

Question 3: A ladybird has 5 spots on one wing and 5 spots on the other wing. How many spots does it have altogether?
Answer: 10
Working: 5 + 5 = 10.

Question 4: What is double 2?
Answer: 4
Working: 2 + 2 = 4.

Question 5: What is double 6?
Answer: 12
Working: Double 6 is 6 + 6 = 12.

Question 6: Double 7 is _____.
Answer: 14
Working: 7 + 7 = 14.

Question 7: Sam has 8 toy cars. Ella has double that amount. How many toy cars does Ella have?
Answer: 16
Working: Double 8 is 8 + 8 = 16.

Question 8: What is double 9?
Answer: 18
Working: 9 + 9 = 18.

Question 9: What is double 10?
Answer: 20
Working: 10 + 10 = 20.

Question 10: Noah builds a tower of 7 cubes. Ava builds a tower that is double the height of Noah's tower. How many cubes are in Ava's tower?
Answer: 14
Working: Double 7 cubes is 7 + 7 = 14 cubes.

Question 11: I think of a number. When I double it, the answer is 16. What number am I thinking of?
Answer: 8
Working: 8 + 8 = 16, so the starting number is 8.

--- Halve even numbers within 20 ---

Question 12: What is half of 6?
Answer: 3
Working: Sharing 6 into 2 equal parts gives 3 in each part.

Question 13: Half of 8 is _____.
Answer: 4
Working: 8 shared equally into 2 parts is 4.

Question 14: There are 10 apples. Maya eats half of them. How many apples does Maya eat?
Answer: 5
Working: Half of 10 is 5.

Question 15: What is half of 4?
Answer: 2
Working: 4 shared into 2 equal groups is 2.

Question 16: What is half of 12?
Answer: 6
Working: 12 shared equally into 2 groups is 6.

Question 17: Half of 14 is _____.
Answer: 7
Working: 14 shared equally between 2 is 7.

Question 18: Ben has 16 pencils. He shares half of the pencils with Leo. How many pencils does Leo get?
Answer: 8
Working: Half of 16 is 8.

Question 19: What is half of 18?
Answer: 9
Working: 18 shared equally into 2 parts is 9.

Question 20: What is half of 20?
Answer: 10
Working: 20 shared equally into 2 groups is 10.

Question 21: A ribbon is 18 cm long. It is cut into 2 equal halves. How long is each half?
Answer: 9 cm
Working: Half of 18 cm is 9 cm.

Question 22: Lily has some stickers. She gives half of them to Kai. Kai gets 6 stickers. How many stickers did Lily have at the start?
Answer: 12
Working: If half is 6, the whole amount is double 6: 6 + 6 = 12.
