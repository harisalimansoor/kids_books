=== Topic: Sequence events chronologically and use time vocabulary (days, weeks, months, years) ===
Domain: Measurement

--- Sequence events chronologically ---

Question 1: Mia eats breakfast in the morning and goes to bed at night. Which event happens first?
Answer: Mia eats breakfast
Working: Morning comes before night, so eating breakfast happens first.

Question 2: Today is Monday. What day will it be tomorrow?
Answer: Tuesday
Working: The day straight after Monday is Tuesday.

Question 3: Today is Friday. What day was it yesterday?
Answer: Thursday
Working: The day straight before Friday is Thursday.

Question 4: Look at these three times of day: afternoon, morning, evening. Write the time of day that comes first in a normal day.
Answer: morning
Working: The chronological order is morning, then afternoon, then evening. Morning comes first.

Question 5: Oliver puts on his socks before he puts on his shoes. Which does he put on after his socks?
Answer: shoes
Working: Oliver puts on socks before shoes, so his shoes come after his socks.

Question 6: Yesterday was Saturday. What day is today?
Answer: Sunday
Working: The day straight after Saturday is Sunday, so today is Sunday.

Question 7: Maya has three events in her day: waking up, eating dinner, and eating lunch. Which event happens 2nd?
Answer: eating lunch
Working: The order of events is 1st: waking up, 2nd: eating lunch, 3rd: eating dinner.

Question 8: Tomorrow will be Thursday. What day was yesterday?
Answer: Tuesday
Working: If tomorrow is Thursday, today is Wednesday. The day before Wednesday is Tuesday.

Question 9: Noah washed his hands, ate his lunch, and then read a book. What did Noah do before he ate his lunch?
Answer: washed his hands
Working: Noah washed his hands before eating lunch.

--- Use time vocabulary (days, weeks, months, years) ---

Question 10: How many days are there in 1 week?
Answer: 7
Working: There are 7 days in 1 week.

Question 11: How many months are there in 1 year?
Answer: 12
Working: There are 12 months in 1 year.

Question 12: Which is longer: 1 week or 1 day?
Answer: 1 week
Working: 1 week is 7 days, so 1 week is longer than 1 day.

Question 13: Which day comes straight after Wednesday?
Answer: Thursday
Working: Counting the days of the week: Monday, Tuesday, Wednesday, Thursday.

Question 14: Which month comes straight after January?
Answer: February
Working: January is the 1st month and February is the 2nd month.

Question 15: Which day comes straight before Sunday?
Answer: Saturday
Working: Saturday is the day straight before Sunday.

Question 16: Which month comes straight before December?
Answer: November
Working: November is the 11th month and December is the 12th month.

Question 17: Which is shorter: 1 month or 1 year?
Answer: 1 month
Working: 1 year has 12 months, so 1 month is shorter than 1 year.

Question 18: What is the first month of the year?
Answer: January
Working: The months of the year begin with January.

Question 19: Today is Saturday. What day was it 2 days ago?
Answer: Thursday
Working: 1 day before Saturday is Friday. 2 days before Saturday is Thursday.
