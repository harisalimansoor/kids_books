=== Topic: Recognise equivalence of 2/4 = 1/2 and find three-quarters (3/4) ===
Domain: Fractions

--- Recognise equivalence of 2/4 and 1/2 ---

Question 1: A paper strip is divided into 4 equal parts. 2 of the parts are coloured blue. Is the amount coloured equal to one half or one quarter of the strip?
Answer: one half
Working: 2 out of 4 equal parts is 2 quarters, which is equal to 1 half.

Question 2: Amy says that two quarters of a cake is the same amount as one half of the cake. Is Amy correct? Write yes or no.
Answer: yes
Working: Two quarters is equal to one half, so Amy is correct.

Question 3: Complete the sentence: 2 quarters of a shape is equal to ___ half of the shape.
Answer: 1
Working: 2 quarters is equivalent to 1 half.

Question 4: A pizza is cut into 4 equal slices. Jack eats 2 slices. What fraction of the pizza did Jack eat? Write one half or three quarters.
Answer: one half
Working: Jack ate 2 out of 4 slices. 2 quarters is equal to one half.

Question 5: There are 8 counters in a row. Half of 8 is 4. How many counters are in 2 quarters of 8?
Answer: 4
Working: 2 quarters is equal to 1 half. Half of 8 is 4, so 2 quarters of 8 is 4.

Question 6: A ribbon is cut into 4 equal pieces. Tim uses 2 of the pieces. Did Tim use half of the ribbon? Write yes or no.
Answer: yes
Working: Tim used 2 of 4 equal pieces, which is 2 quarters. 2 quarters is equal to half.

Question 7: One quarter of 12 is 3. What is two quarters of 12?
Answer: 6
Working: Two quarters is two groups of 3. 3 + 3 = 6.

Question 8: There are 16 buttons. Half of 16 is 8. What is two-quarters of 16?
Answer: 8
Working: Two-quarters is equivalent to one half. Half of 16 is 8, so two-quarters of 16 is 8.

--- Find three-quarters (3/4) ---

Question 9: A circle is divided into 4 equal parts. 3 parts are shaded yellow. What fraction of the circle is shaded: one-quarter or three-quarters?
Answer: three-quarters
Working: 3 of 4 equal parts are shaded, which is three-quarters.

Question 10: A square is divided into 4 equal smaller squares. Ella paints 3 of the smaller squares blue. How many quarters of the square are painted blue?
Answer: 3
Working: Ella painted 3 of the 4 equal parts, which is 3 quarters.

Question 11: A chocolate bar is split into 4 equal chunks. Leo eats 3 chunks. What fraction of the chocolate bar did Leo eat? Write three-quarters or one-half.
Answer: three-quarters
Working: Leo eats 3 out of 4 equal parts, which is three-quarters.

Question 12: There are 4 apples in a bowl. What is three-quarters of 4?
Answer: 3
Working: 1 quarter of 4 is 1. Three quarters is 1 + 1 + 1 = 3.

Question 13: There are 8 toy cars. One quarter of 8 is 2. What is three-quarters of 8?
Answer: 6
Working: One quarter is 2. Three quarters is 2 + 2 + 2 = 6.

Question 14: There are 12 crayons shared equally into 4 pots. Each pot has 3 crayons. How many crayons are in 3 of the pots altogether?
Answer: 9
Working: Each quarter is 3 crayons. Three quarters is 3 + 3 + 3 = 9.

Question 15: There are 16 pencils in a box. One quarter of 16 is 4. What is three-quarters of 16?
Answer: 12
Working: One quarter is 4 pencils. Three quarters is 4 + 4 + 4 = 12.

Question 16: There are 20 counters. One quarter of 20 is 5. What is three-quarters of 20?
Answer: 15
Working: One quarter is 5 counters. Three quarters is 5 + 5 + 5 = 15.
