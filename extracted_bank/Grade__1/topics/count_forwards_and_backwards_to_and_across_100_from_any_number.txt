=== Topic: Count forwards and backwards to and across 100 from any number ===
Domain: Number and Place Value

--- Count forwards to and across 100 from any number ---

Question 1: Start at 6 and count forwards. What number comes next in this sequence: 6, 7, 8, __?
Answer: 9
Working: Counting forwards by 1 from 8 gives 9.

Question 2: Write the next number in this counting sequence: 14, 15, 16, __.
Answer: 17
Working: Counting forwards by 1 from 16 gives 17.

Question 3: Sam counts forwards: 37, 38, 39, __. What number comes next?
Answer: 40
Working: Counting forwards by 1 across the decade boundary from 39 gives 40.

Question 4: Fill in the missing number: 58, 59, __, 61, 62.
Answer: 60
Working: The number that comes after 59 and before 61 when counting forwards is 60.

Question 5: Ella is counting forwards from 76. She says 76, 77, 78, 79. What is the next number she will say?
Answer: 80
Working: Counting forwards 1 more from 79 gives 80.

Question 6: Write the missing number to complete the sequence: 87, 88, 89, __, 91.
Answer: 90
Working: Counting forwards by 1: 89 is followed by 90.

Question 7: A frog jumps forwards 1 number at a time on a number track. It starts on 69 and makes 1 jump forwards. What number does it land on?
Answer: 70
Working: 1 jump forwards from 69 is 69 + 1 = 70.

Question 8: Start at 97 and count forwards. Write the missing number: 97, 98, 99, __.
Answer: 100
Working: Counting forwards across 100: the number after 99 is 100.

Question 9: Leo counts forwards: 98, 99, 100, __. What number comes next?
Answer: 101
Working: Counting forwards by 1 past 100: 100 + 1 = 101.

Question 10: Fill in the missing number in this sequence: 99, __, 101, 102.
Answer: 100
Working: The number between 99 and 101 when counting forwards is 100.

--- Count backwards to and across 100 from any number ---

Question 11: Start at 10 and count backwards: 10, 9, 8, __. What number comes next?
Answer: 7
Working: Counting backwards by 1 from 8 gives 7.

Question 12: Write the next number in this counting backwards sequence: 19, 18, 17, __.
Answer: 16
Working: Counting backwards by 1 from 17 gives 16.

Question 13: Count backwards to find the missing number: 32, 31, 30, __.
Answer: 29
Working: Counting backwards by 1 across the decade boundary from 30 gives 29.

Question 14: Write the missing number in this sequence: 51, 50, __, 48.
Answer: 49
Working: Counting backwards by 1: 1 less than 50 is 49.

Question 15: Maya counts backwards: 83, 82, 81, 80, __. What number does she say next?
Answer: 79
Working: Counting backwards by 1 from 80 gives 79.

Question 16: Fill in the missing number: 70, 69, 68, __, 66.
Answer: 67
Working: Counting backwards by 1: the number after 68 is 67.

Question 17: Tom steps backwards on a number track from 91 to 90. He takes 1 more step backwards. What number does he land on?
Answer: 89
Working: 1 step backwards from 90 is 90 - 1 = 89.

Question 18: Count backwards across 100. What number comes next in the sequence: 102, 101, 100, __?
Answer: 99
Working: Counting backwards by 1 from 100 gives 99.

Question 19: Write the missing number in this sequence: 101, 100, __, 98.
Answer: 99
Working: The number between 100 and 98 when counting backwards is 99.

Question 20: A countdown starts at 100 and counts backwards: 100, 99, 98, __. What is the next number in the countdown?
Answer: 97
Working: Counting backwards by 1 from 98 gives 97.
