=== Topic: Solve multiplication problems using repeated addition, equal groups, and arrays ===
Domain: Multiplication and Division

--- Repeated addition ---

Question 1: What is 2 + 2 + 2 + 2?
Answer: 8
Working: 2 + 2 = 4, 4 + 2 = 6, 6 + 2 = 8.

Question 2: What is 5 + 5 + 5?
Answer: 15
Working: 5 + 5 = 10, 10 + 5 = 15.

Question 3: What is 10 + 10 + 10 + 10?
Answer: 40
Working: 10 + 10 = 20, 20 + 10 = 30, 30 + 10 = 40.

Question 4: There are 5 vases. Each vase has 2 flowers. Use repeated addition to find how many flowers there are altogether.
Answer: 10
Working: 2 + 2 + 2 + 2 + 2 = 10 flowers.

Question 5: There are 4 pencil pots. Each pot has 5 pencils. Use repeated addition to find the total number of pencils.
Answer: 20
Working: 5 + 5 + 5 + 5 = 20 pencils.

Question 6: Complete the addition sentence: 2 + 2 + 2 + 2 + 2 + 2 = ___
Answer: 12
Working: Counting in twos 6 times: 2, 4, 6, 8, 10, 12.

--- Equal groups ---

Question 7: There are 3 equal groups of 2 socks. How many socks are there in total?
Answer: 6
Working: 3 groups of 2 is 2 + 2 + 2 = 6 socks.

Question 8: There are 2 bags. Each bag contains 5 apples. How many apples are there altogether?
Answer: 10
Working: 2 equal groups of 5 is 5 + 5 = 10 apples.

Question 9: There are 4 nests. Each nest holds 2 eggs. How many eggs are there altogether?
Answer: 8
Working: 4 groups of 2 is 2 + 2 + 2 + 2 = 8 eggs.

Question 10: There are 3 packets of stickers. Each packet has 10 stickers. How many stickers are there altogether?
Answer: 30
Working: 3 groups of 10 is 10 + 10 + 10 = 30 stickers.

Question 11: There are 6 children. Each child has 2 pencils. How many pencils do they have in total?
Answer: 12
Working: 6 groups of 2 is 2 + 2 + 2 + 2 + 2 + 2 = 12 pencils.

Question 12: Maya has 5 boxes. Each box contains 5 crayons. How many crayons does Maya have altogether?
Answer: 25
Working: 5 groups of 5 is 5 + 5 + 5 + 5 + 5 = 25 crayons.

--- Arrays ---

Question 13: An array has 2 rows of 3 counters. How many counters are there in total?
Answer: 6
Working: 2 rows of 3 is 3 + 3 = 6 counters.

Question 14: An array has 3 rows of 2 stars. How many stars are there altogether?
Answer: 6
Working: 3 rows of 2 is 2 + 2 + 2 = 6 stars.

Question 15: An array has 2 rows with 5 dots in each row. How many dots are in the array altogether?
Answer: 10
Working: 2 rows of 5 is 5 + 5 = 10 dots.

Question 16: An array has 4 rows with 2 cubes in each row. How many cubes are there in total?
Answer: 8
Working: 4 rows of 2 is 2 + 2 + 2 + 2 = 8 cubes.

Question 17: An array has 2 rows with 10 buttons in each row. How many buttons are there altogether?
Answer: 20
Working: 2 rows of 10 is 10 + 10 = 20 buttons.

Question 18: An array has 3 rows with 5 stamps in each row. How many stamps are in the array?
Answer: 15
Working: 3 rows of 5 is 5 + 5 + 5 = 15 stamps.
