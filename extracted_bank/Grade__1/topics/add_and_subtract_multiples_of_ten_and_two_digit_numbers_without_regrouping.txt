=== Topic: Add and subtract multiples of ten and two-digit numbers without regrouping ===
Domain: Addition and Subtraction

--- Add and subtract multiples of ten ---

Question 1: What is 30 + 20?
Answer: 50
Working: 3 tens + 2 tens = 5 tens, which is 50.

Question 2: What is 50 − 20?
Answer: 30
Working: 5 tens − 2 tens = 3 tens, which is 30.

Question 3: There are 20 birds in a tree. Then 40 more birds land in the tree. How many birds are in the tree now?
Answer: 60
Working: 20 + 40 = 60 birds.

Question 4: What is 40 + 60?
Answer: 100
Working: 4 tens + 6 tens = 10 tens, which is 100.

Question 5: What is 90 − 40?
Answer: 50
Working: 9 tens − 4 tens = 5 tens, which is 50.

Question 6: Sam has 40p. He gets 30p more. How much money does Sam have altogether?
Answer: 70p
Working: 40p + 30p = 70p.

Question 7: A box has 80 counters. 30 counters are taken out. How many counters are left in the box?
Answer: 50
Working: 80 − 30 = 50 counters.

Question 8: Fill in the missing number: 50 + ___ = 80
Answer: 30
Working: 80 − 50 = 30.

Question 9: Fill in the missing number: 100 − ___ = 30
Answer: 70
Working: 100 − 30 = 70.

Question 10: Fill in the missing number: ___ − 20 = 50
Answer: 70
Working: 50 + 20 = 70.

--- Add and subtract two-digit numbers without regrouping ---

Question 11: What is 32 + 4?
Answer: 36
Working: 2 ones + 4 ones = 6 ones. 3 tens + 6 ones = 36.

Question 12: What is 47 − 5?
Answer: 42
Working: 7 ones − 5 ones = 2 ones. 4 tens + 2 ones = 42.

Question 13: What is 56 + 20?
Answer: 76
Working: 5 tens + 2 tens = 7 tens. The 6 ones stay the same. 56 + 20 = 76.

Question 14: What is 78 − 30?
Answer: 48
Working: 7 tens − 3 tens = 4 tens. The 8 ones stay the same. 78 − 30 = 48.

Question 15: What is 34 + 23?
Answer: 57
Working: 3 tens + 2 tens = 5 tens. 4 ones + 3 ones = 7 ones. 5 tens and 7 ones is 57.

Question 16: What is 47 − 15?
Answer: 32
Working: 4 tens − 1 ten = 3 tens. 7 ones − 5 ones = 2 ones. 3 tens and 2 ones is 32.

Question 17: Ben has 24 toy cars. He gets 13 more cars for his birthday. How many toy cars does Ben have altogether?
Answer: 37
Working: 24 + 13: 2 tens + 1 ten = 3 tens, 4 ones + 3 ones = 7 ones. Total = 37 cars.

Question 18: A ribbon is 68 cm long. Ella cuts off 25 cm. How long is the ribbon now?
Answer: 43 cm
Working: 68 cm − 25 cm: 6 tens − 2 tens = 4 tens, 8 ones − 5 ones = 3 ones. 43 cm.

Question 19: Liam has 56 stickers. He gives 20 stickers to his friend. How many stickers does Liam have left?
Answer: 36
Working: 56 − 20 = 36 stickers.

Question 20: Fill in the missing number: 42 + ___ = 65
Answer: 23
Working: 65 − 42: 6 tens − 4 tens = 2 tens, 5 ones − 2 ones = 3 ones. The missing number is 23.

Question 21: Fill in the missing number: 89 − ___ = 54
Answer: 35
Working: 89 − 54: 8 tens − 5 tens = 3 tens, 9 ones − 4 ones = 5 ones. The missing number is 35.
