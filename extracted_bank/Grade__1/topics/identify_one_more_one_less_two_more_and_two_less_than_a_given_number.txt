=== Topic: Identify one more, one less, two more, and two less than a given number ===
Domain: Number and Place Value

--- One more ---

Question 1: What is 1 more than 6?
Answer: 7
Working: Count on 1 from 6: 6 + 1 = 7.

Question 2: Maya has 8 apples. She gets 1 more apple. How many apples does Maya have now?
Answer: 9
Working: 8 and 1 more is 9.

Question 3: What is 1 more than 19?
Answer: 20
Working: Count on 1 from 19: 19 + 1 = 20.

Question 4: What is 1 more than 45?
Answer: 46
Working: 1 more than 45 is 46.

Question 5: What is 1 more than 79?
Answer: 80
Working: The number after 79 is 80, so 1 more than 79 is 80.

Question 6: A counter is on number 63 on a number track. Move it forward 1 space. Which number is the counter on now?
Answer: 64
Working: Moving forward 1 space from 63 gives 63 + 1 = 64.

Question 7: Jack thinks of a number. 1 more than his number is 30. What is Jack's number?
Answer: 29
Working: If 1 more than the number is 30, the number is 1 less than 30: 30 - 1 = 29.

--- One less ---

Question 8: What is 1 less than 7?
Answer: 6
Working: Count back 1 from 7: 7 - 1 = 6.

Question 9: Tom has 5 stickers. He gives 1 sticker away. How many stickers does Tom have left?
Answer: 4
Working: 1 less than 5 is 4.

Question 10: What is 1 less than 20?
Answer: 19
Working: Count back 1 from 20: 20 - 1 = 19.

Question 11: What is 1 less than 54?
Answer: 53
Working: 1 less than 54 is 53.

Question 12: What is 1 less than 90?
Answer: 89
Working: Count back 1 from 90: 90 - 1 = 89.

Question 13: A frog is on number 38 on a number track. It jumps back 1 space. Which number does it land on?
Answer: 37
Working: Jump back 1 from 38: 38 - 1 = 37.

Question 14: 1 less than a secret number is 49. What is the secret number?
Answer: 50
Working: If 1 less than the number is 49, the secret number is 1 more than 49: 49 + 1 = 50.

--- Two more ---

Question 15: What is 2 more than 4?
Answer: 6
Working: Count on 2 from 4: 4 + 2 = 6.

Question 16: Lily has 7 crayons. Her friend gives her 2 more crayons. How many crayons does Lily have now?
Answer: 9
Working: 7 and 2 more is 9.

Question 17: What is 2 more than 18?
Answer: 20
Working: Count on 2 from 18: 18 + 2 = 20.

Question 18: What is 2 more than 35?
Answer: 37
Working: Count on 2 from 35: 35 + 2 = 37.

Question 19: What is 2 more than 89?
Answer: 91
Working: Count on 2 from 89: 89, 90, 91.

Question 20: Start at 62 on a number line and count on 2. What number do you reach?
Answer: 64
Working: Counting on 2 from 62: 63, 64.

Question 21: Sam thinks of a number. 2 more than his number is 50. What is Sam's number?
Answer: 48
Working: If 2 more than the number is 50, count back 2 from 50: 50 - 2 = 48.

--- Two less ---

Question 22: What is 2 less than 9?
Answer: 7
Working: Count back 2 from 9: 9 - 2 = 7.

Question 23: There are 8 birds on a fence. 2 birds fly away. How many birds are left on the fence?
Answer: 6
Working: 2 less than 8 is 6.

Question 24: What is 2 less than 20?
Answer: 18
Working: Count back 2 from 20: 20 - 2 = 18.

Question 25: What is 2 less than 47?
Answer: 45
Working: Count back 2 from 47: 47 - 2 = 45.

Question 26: What is 2 less than 71?
Answer: 69
Working: Count back 2 from 71: 70, 69.

Question 27: A counter is on number 83 on a number track. Move it back 2 spaces. Which number does it land on?
Answer: 81
Working: Move back 2 spaces from 83: 83 - 2 = 81.

Question 28: 2 less than a mystery number is 38. What is the mystery number?
Answer: 40
Working: If 2 less than the mystery number is 38, count on 2 from 38: 38 + 2 = 40.
