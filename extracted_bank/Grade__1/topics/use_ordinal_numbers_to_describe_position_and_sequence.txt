=== Topic: Use ordinal numbers to describe position and sequence ===
Domain: Number and Place Value

--- Use ordinal numbers to describe position ---

Question 1: Five children stand in a queue: Sam, Ben, Mia, Leo, and Ava. Sam is 1st. What position is Mia?
Answer: 3rd
Working: Sam is 1st, Ben is 2nd, and Mia is 3rd in the queue.

Question 2: In a running race, Jack finishes 1st. Ella finishes immediately after Jack. What position does Ella finish?
Answer: 2nd
Working: The position immediately after 1st is 2nd.

Question 3: Look at this row of shapes from left to right: Circle, Square, Triangle, Star. Which shape is 2nd from the left?
Answer: Square
Working: Counting from the left: 1st is Circle, 2nd is Square.

Question 4: Noah is 4th in a queue for lunch. How many children are in front of Noah?
Answer: 3
Working: Noah is in 4th position, so there are 3 children in front of him (1st, 2nd, and 3rd).

Question 5: Look at these animals in a line from left to right: Cat, Dog, Duck, Frog, Hen, Fox. Which animal is in the 5th position?
Answer: Hen
Working: Count from left to right: 1st Cat, 2nd Dog, 3rd Duck, 4th Frog, 5th Hen.

Question 6: In a swimming race, Maya finished 6th and Oliver finished right before Maya. What position did Oliver finish?
Answer: 5th
Working: The position right before 6th is 5th.

Question 7: Seven toys are lined up on a shelf: Car, Ball, Teddy, Doll, Robot, Train, Drum. Which toy is 2nd from the right?
Answer: Train
Working: Starting from the right end: Drum is 1st from the right, Train is 2nd from the right.

--- Use ordinal numbers to describe sequence ---

Question 8: Complete the ordinal sequence: 1st, 2nd, 3rd, 4th, ___
Answer: 5th
Working: The ordinal number that comes after 4th is 5th.

Question 9: Look at the order of letters in the word PLANET: P, L, A, N, E, T. What is the 3rd letter?
Answer: A
Working: 1st letter is P, 2nd letter is L, 3rd letter is A.

Question 10: Write the missing ordinal number in this sequence: 6th, 7th, ___, 9th, 10th.
Answer: 8th
Working: Counting up in ordinal order: 6th, 7th, 8th, 9th, 10th. The missing ordinal number is 8th.

Question 11: Here are the first five days of the school week in order: Monday, Tuesday, Wednesday, Thursday, Friday. Which day is 4th in the sequence?
Answer: Thursday
Working: 1st is Monday, 2nd is Tuesday, 3rd is Wednesday, 4th is Thursday.

Question 12: A baker follows four steps in order: 1st mix flour, 2nd add water, 3rd knead dough, 4th bake bread. Which step comes immediately after adding water?
Answer: 3rd
Working: Adding water is the 2nd step. The step immediately after 2nd is the 3rd step.
