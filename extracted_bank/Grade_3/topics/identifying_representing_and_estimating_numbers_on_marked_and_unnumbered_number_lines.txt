=== Topic: Identifying, representing, and estimating numbers on marked and unnumbered number lines ===
Domain: Number and Place Value

--- Identifying and representing numbers on marked number lines ---

Question 1: A number line starts at 0 and ends at 1000. It is divided into 10 equal intervals. What number is at the 4th tick mark after 0?
Answer: 400
Working: 1000 divided into 10 equal intervals means each interval is 1000 ÷ 10 = 100. The 4th tick mark after 0 is 4 × 100 = 400.

Question 2: A number line starts at 200 and ends at 300. It is divided into 10 equal intervals. What number is at the 7th tick mark after 200?
Answer: 270
Working: The distance from 200 to 300 is 100. Divided into 10 equal intervals, each step is 100 ÷ 10 = 10. Counting 7 steps of 10 from 200 gives 200 + (7 × 10) = 270.

Question 3: A number line starts at 0 and ends at 800. It has 8 equal intervals. An arrow points to the 5th tick mark after 0. What number is the arrow pointing to?
Answer: 500
Working: Each interval is 800 ÷ 8 = 100. The 5th tick mark is 5 × 100 = 500.

Question 4: A number line starts at 450 and counts up in equal steps of 50. What number is at the 6th tick mark after 450?
Answer: 750
Working: 6 steps of 50 is 6 × 50 = 300. Adding this to the start: 450 + 300 = 750.

Question 5: A number line starts at 0 and ends at 1000. It is split into 4 equal parts. What number is at the 3rd tick mark after 0?
Answer: 750
Working: 1000 ÷ 4 = 250 per interval. The 3rd tick mark is 3 × 250 = 750.

Question 6: A number line starts at 320 and ends at 400. It is divided into 8 equal intervals. What number is at the 3rd tick mark after 320?
Answer: 350
Working: Total distance is 400 - 320 = 80. Each interval is 80 ÷ 8 = 10. The 3rd tick mark after 320 is 320 + (3 × 10) = 350.

Question 7: A number line starts at 600 and ends at 700. It is divided into 5 equal intervals. What number is at the 2nd tick mark after 600?
Answer: 640
Working: Total distance is 700 - 600 = 100. Each interval is 100 ÷ 5 = 20. The 2nd tick mark after 600 is 600 + (2 × 20) = 640.

Question 8: A number line starts at 0 and ends at 500 with 10 equal intervals. An arrow points to the 8th tick mark after 0. What number does the arrow point to?
Answer: 400
Working: 500 ÷ 10 = 50 per interval. The 8th tick mark after 0 is 8 × 50 = 400.

--- Estimating numbers on unnumbered number lines ---

Question 9: An unnumbered number line has 0 at the left end and 1000 at the right end. What number is located exactly halfway along the line?
Answer: 500
Working: Half of 1000 is 1000 ÷ 2 = 500.

Question 10: An unnumbered number line starts at 0 and ends at 1000. A point is placed halfway between 0 and 500. What number is at this point?
Answer: 250
Working: Half of 500 is 500 ÷ 2 = 250.

Question 11: An unnumbered number line starts at 300 and ends at 400. An arrow points exactly halfway along the line. What number is the arrow pointing to?
Answer: 350
Working: The distance between 300 and 400 is 100. Half of 100 is 50. 300 + 50 = 350.

Question 12: An unnumbered number line starts at 0 and ends at 1000. An arrow is placed three-quarters of the way along the line from 0. What number does the arrow represent?
Answer: 750
Working: One quarter of 1000 is 250. Three quarters of 1000 is 3 × 250 = 750.

Question 13: An unnumbered number line starts at 200 and ends at 600. What number lies exactly halfway along the line?
Answer: 400
Working: The difference between 200 and 600 is 400. Half of 400 is 200. 200 + 200 = 400.

Question 14: An unnumbered number line starts at 500 and ends at 1000. What number is positioned exactly halfway between 500 and 1000?
Answer: 750
Working: The difference between 500 and 1000 is 500. Half of 500 is 250. 500 + 250 = 750.

Question 15: An unnumbered number line starts at 0 and ends at 1000. A mark is placed one-tenth of the total distance along the line from 0. What number does this mark represent?
Answer: 100
Working: One tenth of 1000 is 1000 ÷ 10 = 100.

Question 16: An unnumbered number line starts at 0 and ends at 500. A point is placed halfway between 0 and the midpoint 250. What number is at this point?
Answer: 125
Working: The midpoint between 0 and 250 is half of 250. 250 ÷ 2 = 125.
