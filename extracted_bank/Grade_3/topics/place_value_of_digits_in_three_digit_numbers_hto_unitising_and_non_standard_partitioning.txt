=== Topic: Place value of digits in three-digit numbers (HTO), unitising, and non-standard partitioning ===
Domain: Number and Place Value

--- Place value of digits in three-digit numbers (HTO) ---

Question 1: What is the value of the digit 7 in the number 472?
Answer: 70
Working: In 472, the 4 is in the hundreds place (400), the 7 is in the tens place (70), and the 2 is in the ones place (2). So the value of the digit 7 is 70.

Question 2: Write the number that has 6 hundreds, 0 tens, and 9 ones.
Answer: 609
Working: 6 hundreds = 600, 0 tens = 0, 9 ones = 9. Combining these gives 600 + 0 + 9 = 609.

Question 3: A number has 8 hundreds, 3 tens, and 5 ones. If the tens digit is changed to 7, what is the new number?
Answer: 875
Working: The original number is 835. Replacing the tens digit 3 with 7 gives 8 hundreds, 7 tens, and 5 ones, which is 875.

Question 4: Write the missing number: 500 + 6 = ____
Answer: 506
Working: 500 + 6 has 5 hundreds, 0 tens, and 6 ones, which is 506.

Question 5: A three-digit number has a hundreds digit that is double its ones digit. The tens digit is 0. The ones digit is 3. What is the number?
Answer: 603
Working: The ones digit is 3. The hundreds digit is double 3, which is 6. The tens digit is 0. So the number is 603.

--- Unitising ---

Question 6: How many tens are there in 100?
Answer: 10
Working: 10 groups of 10 make 100, so there are 10 tens in 100.

Question 7: What number is equal to 43 tens?
Answer: 430
Working: Each ten is worth 10. 43 tens = 43 × 10 = 430.

Question 8: How many tens are equal to 7 hundreds?
Answer: 70
Working: 1 hundred is equal to 10 tens. Therefore, 7 hundreds = 7 × 10 = 70 tens.

Question 9: A shopkeeper packs 10 pencils in each box. She has 580 pencils in total. How many full boxes of pencils does she have?
Answer: 58
Working: 580 divided into groups of 10 gives 58 tens, so she has 58 boxes.

Question 10: A baker has 24 bags of 10 rolls and 5 single rolls. How many rolls does the baker have altogether?
Answer: 245
Working: 24 bags of 10 rolls = 24 tens = 240 rolls. 240 + 5 single rolls = 245 rolls.

--- Non-standard partitioning ---

Question 11: Write the missing number: 345 = 300 + 40 + ____
Answer: 5
Working: 345 is partitioned into 300 + 40 + 5. The missing number is 5.

Question 12: Write the missing number: 450 = 300 + ____
Answer: 150
Working: 450 - 300 = 150. So 450 = 300 + 150.

Question 13: Write the missing number: 628 = 500 + ____ + 8
Answer: 120
Working: 628 - 8 = 620. 620 - 500 = 120. So 628 = 500 + 120 + 8.

Question 14: Maya partitions 734 into 6 hundreds, some tens, and 4 ones. How many tens are there?
Answer: 13
Working: 734 = 600 + 130 + 4. 130 is 13 tens. So there are 13 tens.

Question 15: Write the missing number to complete the partition: 512 = 400 + ____ + 2
Answer: 110
Working: 512 - 400 = 112. 112 - 2 = 110. So the missing number is 110.
