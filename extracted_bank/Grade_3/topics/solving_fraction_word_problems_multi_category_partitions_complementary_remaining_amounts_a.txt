=== Topic: Solving fraction word problems (multi-category partitions, complementary remaining amounts, and combined measures) ===
Domain: Fractions

--- Multi-category partitions ---

Question 1: A florist has a vase of 30 flowers. 1/10 of the flowers are lilies and 4/10 are roses. The rest of the flowers are tulips. What fraction of the flowers are tulips?
Answer: 5/10
Working: 1/10 + 4/10 = 5/10. The whole vase is 10/10. 10/10 - 5/10 = 5/10.

Question 2: In a pack of 24 coloured pencils, 1/4 are red, 2/4 are blue, and the rest are green. How many pencils are green?
Answer: 6
Working: 1/4 + 2/4 = 3/4. The rest is 1 - 3/4 = 1/4. 1/4 of 24 = 24 ÷ 4 = 6 pencils.

Question 3: There are 40 animals on a farm. 3/8 of the animals are sheep, 2/8 are cows, and the rest are goats. How many goats are on the farm?
Answer: 15
Working: 3/8 + 2/8 = 5/8. Fraction of goats = 8/8 - 5/8 = 3/8. 1/8 of 40 = 40 ÷ 8 = 5. 3/8 of 40 = 3 × 5 = 15 goats.

Question 4: A box contains 32 counters. 3/8 of the counters are yellow, 1/8 are red, and the rest are blue. How many counters are blue?
Answer: 16
Working: 3/8 + 1/8 = 4/8. Fraction of blue counters = 8/8 - 4/8 = 4/8. 1/8 of 32 = 32 ÷ 8 = 4. 4/8 of 32 = 4 × 4 = 16 counters.

Question 5: A school has 48 children in Year 3. 2/6 of the children choose apples for snack, 3/6 choose bananas, and the rest choose oranges. How many children choose oranges?
Answer: 8
Working: 2/6 + 3/6 = 5/6. Fraction of children choosing oranges = 6/6 - 5/6 = 1/6. 1/6 of 48 = 48 ÷ 6 = 8 children.

--- Complementary remaining amounts ---

Question 6: A bag contains 24 apples. Jack eats 1/4 of the apples. How many apples are left in the bag?
Answer: 18
Working: 1/4 of 24 = 24 ÷ 4 = 6 apples eaten. 24 - 6 = 18 apples left.

Question 7: A piece of ribbon is 40 cm long. Freya cuts off 3/5 of the ribbon. How many centimetres of ribbon are left?
Answer: 16 cm
Working: 1/5 of 40 cm = 40 ÷ 5 = 8 cm. Fraction remaining = 5/5 - 3/5 = 2/5. 2/5 of 40 cm = 2 × 8 cm = 16 cm.

Question 8: A book has 72 pages. Sam has read 5/8 of the book. How many pages does Sam have left to read?
Answer: 27
Working: Pages left to read = 8/8 - 5/8 = 3/8 of the book. 1/8 of 72 = 72 ÷ 8 = 9. 3/8 of 72 = 3 × 9 = 27 pages.

Question 9: A runner has completed 4/5 of a 50 m sprint course. How many metres does the runner have left to reach the finish line?
Answer: 10 m
Working: Fraction left = 5/5 - 4/5 = 1/5. 1/5 of 50 m = 50 ÷ 5 = 10 m.

Question 10: Maya spent 3/4 of her pocket money on a book. She has £5 left. How much pocket money did Maya have at first?
Answer: £20
Working: Remaining fraction = 4/4 - 3/4 = 1/4. 1/4 of total = £5. Total = 4 × £5 = £20.

--- Combined measures ---

Question 11: A film lasts 60 minutes. Mia watches 1/3 of the film before lunch and 1/3 of the film after lunch. How many minutes of the film has Mia watched in total?
Answer: 40 minutes
Working: 1/3 + 1/3 = 2/3. 1/3 of 60 minutes = 60 ÷ 3 = 20 minutes. 2/3 of 60 minutes = 2 × 20 = 40 minutes.

Question 12: A bottle holds 400 ml of juice. Liam drinks 1/5 of the juice and Noah drinks 2/5 of the juice. How many millilitres of juice did they drink altogether?
Answer: 240 ml
Working: Total fraction drunk = 1/5 + 2/5 = 3/5. 1/5 of 400 ml = 400 ÷ 5 = 80 ml. 3/5 of 400 ml = 3 × 80 ml = 240 ml.

Question 13: A baker has 800 g of flour. She uses 3/8 of the flour for bread and 2/8 of the flour for muffins. How many grams of flour has she used altogether?
Answer: 500 g
Working: Total fraction used = 3/8 + 2/8 = 5/8. 1/8 of 800 g = 800 ÷ 8 = 100 g. 5/8 of 800 g = 5 × 100 g = 500 g.

Question 14: A strip of card is 90 cm long. Anish cuts a piece that is 2/10 of the strip, and Ben cuts a piece that is 3/10 of the strip. What is the total length of the two pieces cut?
Answer: 45 cm
Working: Total fraction cut = 2/10 + 3/10 = 5/10. 1/10 of 90 cm = 90 ÷ 10 = 9 cm. 5/10 of 90 cm = 5 × 9 cm = 45 cm.

Question 15: A water tank contains 50 litres of water. In the morning, 1/10 of the water is used. In the afternoon, 3/10 of the water is used. How many litres of water are left in the tank?
Answer: 30 litres
Working: Fraction used = 1/10 + 3/10 = 4/10. Fraction left = 10/10 - 4/10 = 6/10. 1/10 of 50 litres = 50 ÷ 10 = 5 litres. 6/10 of 50 litres = 6 × 5 = 30 litres.
