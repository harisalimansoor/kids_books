=== Topic: Proper fractions, fractions equal to 1, and simple mixed numbers on number lines (0–1 and 0–2) ===
Domain: Fractions

--- Proper fractions on number lines (0–1) ---

Question 1: A number line starts at 0 and ends at 1. It is divided into 4 equal parts. What fraction is at the 1st mark after 0?
Answer: 1/4
Working: The number line from 0 to 1 has 4 equal parts, so each interval represents 1/4. The 1st mark after 0 is 1/4.

Question 2: A number line from 0 to 1 is split into 3 equal intervals. Write the fraction that belongs at the 2nd mark after 0.
Answer: 2/3
Working: The whole is split into 3 equal intervals, each representing 1/3. Counting 2 intervals from 0 gives 2/3.

Question 3: A number line starts at 0 and ends at 1. It has 5 equal spaces between 0 and 1. Which fraction sits at the 3rd mark after 0?
Answer: 3/5
Working: With 5 equal spaces, each step is 1/5. Moving 3 steps from 0 reaches 3/5.

Question 4: A number line starts at 0 and ends at 1. It is divided into 8 equal parts. A counter starts at 0 and moves forward 5 jumps of 1/8. What fraction does the counter land on?
Answer: 5/8
Working: Starting at 0 and making 5 jumps of 1/8 gives 5 × 1/8 = 5/8.

Question 5: A number line is marked from 0 to 1 with 10 equal steps. What fraction is shown at the 7th tick mark after 0?
Answer: 7/10
Working: 10 equal steps means each step is 1/10. The 7th tick mark represents 7/10.

Question 6: On a number line from 0 to 1, point A is at 2/6 and point B is 3 steps of 1/6 to the right of point A. What fraction is at point B?
Answer: 5/6
Working: Point A is at 2/6. Moving 3 steps of 1/6 to the right gives 2/6 + 3/6 = 5/6.

Question 7: A number line from 0 to 1 is divided into 8 equal sections. How many steps of 1/8 are there between 3/8 and 7/8?
Answer: 4
Working: The number of eighths between 3/8 and 7/8 is 7 - 3 = 4 steps.

--- Fractions equal to 1 ---

Question 8: A number line starts at 0 and ends at 1. It is split into 4 equal parts. Write the number 1 as a fraction with a denominator of 4.
Answer: 4/4
Working: One whole divided into 4 equal parts is represented as 4/4.

Question 9: On a number line from 0 to 1 divided into tenths, the mark for 1 whole is labelled as a fraction. What fraction is equal to 1 whole?
Answer: 10/10
Working: When a whole is divided into 10 equal parts, 1 whole is equal to 10/10.

Question 10: A number line shows fifths from 0 to 1. Complete the missing numerator: 1 = ?/5.
Answer: 5
Working: 5 fifths make 1 whole, so 1 = 5/5. The missing numerator is 5.

Question 11: A number line from 0 to 1 is divided into 6 equal parts. A frog starts at 0 and takes 6 jumps of 1/6. What fraction has the frog reached at the end of the line?
Answer: 6/6
Working: 6 jumps of 1/6 from 0 reach 6/6, which is equal to 1 whole.

Question 12: On a number line, a point is placed at 8/8. What whole number is this point equal to?
Answer: 1
Working: When the numerator and denominator are both 8, the fraction 8/8 represents 1 whole.

Question 13: A number line is divided into eighths. How many more eighths are needed to get from 5/8 to 1 whole?
Answer: 3/8
Working: 1 whole is equal to 8/8. 8/8 - 5/8 = 3/8.

--- Simple mixed numbers on number lines (0–2) ---

Question 14: A number line goes from 0 to 2. Exactly halfway between 1 and 2 is a mark. What mixed number does this mark show?
Answer: 1 1/2
Working: Halfway between 1 and 2 is 1 whole and 1/2, written as the mixed number 1 1/2.

Question 15: A number line goes from 0 to 2. Each whole is split into 4 equal parts. What mixed number is at the 1st mark after 1?
Answer: 1 1/4
Working: Each whole is split into quarters. Moving 1 mark past the whole number 1 gives 1 1/4.

Question 16: A number line from 0 to 2 has each whole divided into 4 equal parts. Write the mixed number shown at the 3rd mark after 1.
Answer: 1 3/4
Working: Each interval is 1/4. Starting at 1 and counting 3 intervals forward gives 1 3/4.

Question 17: A number line goes from 0 to 2. Each whole is split into 3 equal intervals. What mixed number is at the 2nd mark past 1?
Answer: 1 2/3
Working: Each whole is split into thirds. 2 marks past 1 is 1 whole and 2/3, which is 1 2/3.

Question 18: A number line from 0 to 2 is divided into tenths. What mixed number is at the 4th tick mark after 1?
Answer: 1 4/10
Working: Each interval is 1/10. 4 tenths after 1 is 1 4/10.

Question 19: A number line from 0 to 2 is divided into fifths. A token is placed at 1 1/5 and moves 3 fifths to the right. What mixed number does the token land on?
Answer: 1 4/5
Working: Starting at 1 1/5 and moving 3 fifths to the right gives 1 and (1/5 + 3/5) = 1 4/5.

Question 20: A number line from 0 to 2 is divided into eighths. How many steps of 1/8 are needed to jump from 1 whole to 1 5/8?
Answer: 5
Working: 1 whole is 1. The fraction past 1 is 5/8, which corresponds to 5 steps of 1/8.
