--- Multiplication and division facts for the 3, 4, and 8 tables ---

Question 1: Calculate: 7 × 3
Answer: 21
Working: 7 × 3 = 21

Question 2: Calculate: 6 × 4
Answer: 24
Working: 6 × 4 = 24

Question 3: Calculate: 8 × 8
Answer: 64
Working: 8 × 8 = 64

Question 4: Calculate: 24 ÷ 3
Answer: 8
Working: 24 ÷ 3 = 8

Question 5: Calculate: 56 ÷ 8
Answer: 7
Working: 56 ÷ 8 = 7

Question 6: A toy car has 4 wheels. How many wheels are there on 9 toy cars?
Answer: 36
Working: 9 × 4 = 36

Question 7: There are 8 pencils in each pack. Mrs Patel buys 6 packs. How many pencils does she buy altogether?
Answer: 48
Working: 6 × 8 = 48

Question 8: 32 children are split into 4 equal teams. How many children are in each team?
Answer: 8
Working: 32 ÷ 4 = 8

--- Doubling connections ---

Question 9: We know that 2 × 6 = 12. Double 12 to find the answer to 4 × 6.
Answer: 24
Working: 12 + 12 = 24, so 4 × 6 = 24

Question 10: Use the fact that 4 × 7 = 28 to calculate 8 × 7 by doubling.
Answer: 56
Working: Double 28 is 28 + 28 = 56, so 8 × 7 = 56

Question 11: If 2 × 9 = 18, what is 4 × 9?
Answer: 36
Working: 4 × 9 is double 2 × 9. Double 18 = 36

Question 12: To find 8 × 5, double 4 × 5. Since 4 × 5 = 20, what is 8 × 5?
Answer: 40
Working: Double 20 is 40, so 8 × 5 = 40

Question 13: If 4 × 3 = 12, double this value to find 8 × 3.
Answer: 24
Working: Double 12 is 24, so 8 × 3 = 24

--- Commutativity ---

Question 14: Find the missing number to make the statement true: 3 × 8 = 8 × ?
Answer: 3
Working: Multiplication is commutative, so 3 × 8 = 8 × 3

Question 15: Mia knows that 4 × 9 = 36. What is the value of 9 × 4?
Answer: 36
Working: By commutativity, 9 × 4 = 4 × 9 = 36

Question 16: Find the missing number: 8 × 5 = ? × 8
Answer: 5
Working: By commutativity, 8 × 5 = 5 × 8

Question 17: Find the missing number: 4 × ? = 7 × 4
Answer: 7
Working: 4 × 7 = 7 × 4

--- 4-fact families ---

Question 18: The numbers 3, 8, and 24 form a fact family. Complete the division fact: 24 ÷ 8 = ?
Answer: 3
Working: Since 3 × 8 = 24, 24 ÷ 8 = 3

Question 19: A fact family uses the numbers 4, 6, and 24. One fact is 4 × 6 = 24. What is the value of 24 ÷ 4?
Answer: 6
Working: 24 ÷ 4 = 6

Question 20: A fact family contains the numbers 8, 9, and 72. Find the missing number: 72 ÷ ? = 9
Answer: 8
Working: 72 ÷ 8 = 9

Question 21: The numbers 4, 7, and 28 form a fact family. What is 28 ÷ 7?
Answer: 4
Working: 28 ÷ 7 = 4
