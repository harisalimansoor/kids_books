=== Topic: Measuring, comparing, adding, and subtracting mass (kg/g) and dial reading ===
Domain: Measurement

--- Measuring mass (kg/g) ---

Question 1: A bag of sugar has a mass of 1 kg. How many grams are in 1 kg?
Answer: 1000 g
Working: 1 kilogram is equal to 1000 grams.

Question 2: Which unit of measurement, grams (g) or kilograms (kg), is most suitable to measure the mass of a pencil?
Answer: grams
Working: A pencil is a very light object, so grams are the most suitable unit.

Question 3: A packet of butter has a mass of 1/2 kg. What is the mass of the butter in grams?
Answer: 500 g
Working: 1 kg = 1000 g. 1/2 of 1000 g = 1000 ÷ 2 = 500 g.

Question 4: A baker has 4 bags of flour, each with a mass of 250 g. What is the total mass of the flour in kilograms?
Answer: 1 kg
Working: 4 × 250 g = 1000 g. 1000 g = 1 kg.

Question 5: What fraction of 1 kg is 750 g? Give your answer in its simplest form.
Answer: 3/4
Working: 1 kg = 1000 g. 750 g out of 1000 g is 3 lots of 250 g out of 4 lots of 250 g, which is 3/4.

--- Comparing mass (kg/g) ---

Question 6: Parcel A has a mass of 540 g. Parcel B has a mass of 504 g. Which parcel is heavier?
Answer: Parcel A
Working: Compare the hundreds and tens: 540 g is greater than 504 g, so Parcel A is heavier.

Question 7: Order these masses from lightest to heaviest: 750 g, 200 g, 1 kg, 450 g.
Answer: 200 g, 450 g, 750 g, 1 kg
Working: Convert 1 kg to 1000 g. Ordering from lightest to heaviest gives: 200 g, 450 g, 750 g, 1 kg.

Question 8: A pumpkin has a mass of 820 g. A squash has a mass of 670 g. How many grams heavier is the pumpkin than the squash?
Answer: 150 g
Working: 820 g - 670 g = 150 g.

Question 9: Jack says that 600 g is heavier than 1/2 kg. Is Jack correct? Write yes or no.
Answer: Yes
Working: 1/2 kg = 500 g. 600 g is greater than 500 g, so Jack is correct.

Question 10: A blue box has a mass of 375 g. A red box has a mass of 395 g. What is the difference in mass between the two boxes?
Answer: 20 g
Working: 395 g - 375 g = 20 g.

--- Adding and subtracting mass (kg/g) ---

Question 11: A small dog has a mass of 14 kg. A puppy has a mass of 6 kg. What is the total mass of the two dogs?
Answer: 20 kg
Working: 14 kg + 6 kg = 20 kg.

Question 12: A baker mixes 365 g of white flour and 248 g of wholemeal flour. What is the total mass of the flour?
Answer: 613 g
Working: 365 g + 248 g = 613 g.

Question 13: A bowl of fruit has a mass of 840 g. Liam eats 265 g of fruit. What is the mass of the fruit remaining in the bowl?
Answer: 575 g
Working: 840 g - 265 g = 575 g.

Question 14: A chef needs 1 kg of potatoes. She already has 680 g. How many more grams of potatoes does she need?
Answer: 320 g
Working: 1 kg = 1000 g. 1000 g - 680 g = 320 g.

Question 15: Three parcels have masses of 140 g, 280 g, and 330 g. What is their combined mass in grams?
Answer: 750 g
Working: 140 g + 280 g + 330 g = 750 g.

Question 16: A tin of paint has a total mass of 900 g. The empty tin has a mass of 175 g. What is the mass of the paint inside the tin?
Answer: 725 g
Working: 900 g - 175 g = 725 g.

--- Dial reading ---

Question 17: A weighing scale dial has 10 equal divisions between 0 g and 100 g. How many grams does each division represent?
Answer: 10 g
Working: 100 g ÷ 10 = 10 g per division.

Question 18: A kitchen scale dial goes from 0 g to 1000 g with numbered marks every 100 g. There is an unnumbered mark halfway between each 100 g mark. The pointer points to the mark halfway between 400 g and 500 g. What mass is shown on the scale?
Answer: 450 g
Working: Halfway between 400 g and 500 g is 400 g + 50 g = 450 g.

Question 19: A scale dial has marks every 50 g from 0 g to 500 g. The pointer is pointing 3 marks past 200 g. What mass is shown on the dial?
Answer: 350 g
Working: 3 intervals of 50 g = 150 g. 200 g + 150 g = 350 g.

Question 20: A dial scale has marks at 0 g, 200 g, 400 g, 600 g, 800 g, and 1000 g. The pointer is pointing exactly halfway between 600 g and 800 g. What mass is shown on the scale?
Answer: 700 g
Working: Halfway between 600 g and 800 g is 700 g.

Question 21: A weighing scale dial has 10 equal divisions between 0 g and 1000 g, so each division represents 100 g. The pointer points 7 divisions past 0 g. What mass is shown on the scale?
Answer: 700 g
Working: 7 × 100 g = 700 g.
