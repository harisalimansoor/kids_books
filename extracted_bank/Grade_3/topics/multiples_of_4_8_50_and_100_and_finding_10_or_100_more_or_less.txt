=== Topic: Multiples of 4, 8, 50, and 100, and finding 10 or 100 more or less ===
Domain: Number and Place Value

--- Multiples of 4, 8, 50, and 100 ---

Question 1: Starting from 0, write the next number in this sequence: 0, 4, 8, 12, ___
Answer: 16
Working: The sequence counts up in 4s. 12 + 4 = 16.

Question 2: Write the next number when counting up in multiples of 8: 16, 24, 32, ___
Answer: 40
Working: Counting in multiples of 8: add 8 to 32. 32 + 8 = 40.

Question 3: Count up in fifties from 250. Write the missing number: 250, 300, 350, ___
Answer: 400
Working: Adding 50 to 350 gives 350 + 50 = 400.

Question 4: Count up in hundreds from 400. Write the missing number: 400, 500, 600, ___
Answer: 700
Working: Adding 100 to 600 gives 600 + 100 = 700.

Question 5: Ava counts backwards in multiples of 4 from 36: 36, 32, 28, ___. Which number comes next?
Answer: 24
Working: Subtract 4 from 28: 28 - 4 = 24.

Question 6: A toy car has 4 wheels. How many wheels are on 8 of these toy cars altogether?
Answer: 32
Working: Count 8 multiples of 4: 8 × 4 = 32 wheels.

Question 7: Sam saves 50p each week. How much money in pence has Sam saved after 7 weeks?
Answer: 350p
Working: Counting in 50s seven times: 50, 100, 150, 200, 250, 300, 350p (or 7 × 50 = 350p).

Question 8: Which of these numbers is a multiple of 8: 28, 48, 58, 68?
Answer: 48
Working: Count in multiples of 8: 8, 16, 24, 32, 40, 48, 56, 64. 48 is in the 8 times table because 6 × 8 = 48.

Question 9: Write the missing number in this pattern: 650, 700, 750, ___, 850
Answer: 800
Working: The sequence counts in steps of 50. 750 + 50 = 800.

--- Finding 10 or 100 more or less ---

Question 10: What is 10 more than 432?
Answer: 442
Working: Add 1 ten to the tens digit: 3 tens become 4 tens. 432 + 10 = 442.

Question 11: What is 100 more than 581?
Answer: 681
Working: Add 1 hundred to the hundreds digit: 5 hundreds become 6 hundreds. 581 + 100 = 681.

Question 12: What is 10 less than 764?
Answer: 754
Working: Subtract 1 ten from the tens digit: 6 tens become 5 tens. 764 - 10 = 754.

Question 13: What is 100 less than 925?
Answer: 825
Working: Subtract 1 hundred from the hundreds digit: 9 hundreds become 8 hundreds. 925 - 100 = 825.

Question 14: What number is 10 more than 294?
Answer: 304
Working: 29 tens add 1 ten equals 30 tens. 294 + 10 = 304.

Question 15: What number is 10 less than 603?
Answer: 593
Working: 60 tens subtract 1 ten equals 59 tens. 603 - 10 = 593.

Question 16: A library has 375 books. The librarian buys 100 more books. How many books are there now?
Answer: 475
Working: Find 100 more than 375: 375 + 100 = 475.

Question 17: Oliver has 510 points in a game. He loses 10 points. How many points does he have left?
Answer: 500
Working: Find 10 less than 510: 510 - 10 = 500.

Question 18: Maya thinks of a three-digit number. 100 less than her number is 709. What is Maya's number?
Answer: 809
Working: Use the inverse operation: find 100 more than 709. 709 + 100 = 809.
