=== Topic: Solving one-step and two-step comparison, sum, and difference problems from statistical presentations ===
Domain: Statistics

--- Solving one-step comparison, sum, and difference problems from statistical presentations ---

Question 1: A table shows the number of stickers collected by three children: Amy collected 45 stickers, Ben collected 30 stickers, and Charlie collected 60 stickers. How many stickers did Amy and Ben collect altogether?
Answer: 75
Working: Amy has 45 stickers and Ben has 30 stickers. 45 + 30 = 75.

Question 2: A bar chart shows the favourite fruit of a class. The bar for apples reaches 14 and the bar for bananas reaches 8. How many more children chose apples than bananas?
Answer: 6
Working: Apples = 14 and bananas = 8. 14 - 8 = 6.

Question 3: In a pictogram, 1 book symbol represents 4 books read. Class 3 has 5 book symbols next to their name. How many books did Class 3 read in total?
Answer: 20
Working: Each symbol represents 4 books. 5 symbols × 4 books = 20 books.

Question 4: A scaled bar chart shows the number of visitors to a museum. The scale on the vertical axis goes up in tens. On Saturday, the bar reaches 80 visitors. On Sunday, the bar reaches 50 visitors. How many fewer visitors were there on Sunday than on Saturday?
Answer: 30
Working: Saturday visitors = 80. Sunday visitors = 50. 80 - 50 = 30.

Question 5: A pictogram shows goals scored by three teams. The key shows that 1 ball symbol = 8 goals. The Tigers have 3 ball symbols and the Lions have 5 ball symbols. How many more goals did the Lions score than the Tigers?
Answer: 16
Working: Lions scored 5 × 8 = 40 goals. Tigers scored 3 × 8 = 24 goals. 40 - 24 = 16 goals (or (5 - 3) × 8 = 2 × 8 = 16).

Question 6: A table shows the number of pupils attending after-school clubs: Art club has 28 pupils, Chess club has 15 pupils, and Drama club has 34 pupils. What is the difference between the number of pupils in Drama club and Chess club?
Answer: 19
Working: Drama club = 34, Chess club = 15. 34 - 15 = 19.

Question 7: A bar chart with a scale going up in fives shows points scored in a quiz: Red team scored 35 points, Blue team scored 50 points, and Green team scored 25 points. What is the total number of points scored by the Blue team and Green team combined?
Answer: 75
Working: Blue team = 50, Green team = 25. 50 + 25 = 75.

--- Solving two-step comparison, sum, and difference problems from statistical presentations ---

Question 8: A table records the number of birds seen in a garden: 12 robins, 18 sparrows, and 9 blackbirds. How many birds were seen altogether?
Answer: 39
Working: Step 1: Add robins and sparrows: 12 + 18 = 30. Step 2: Add blackbirds: 30 + 9 = 39.

Question 9: In a pictogram, 1 star symbol represents 5 merit points. Sarah has 4 star symbols and Maya has 6 star symbols. How many merit points do Sarah and Maya have altogether?
Answer: 50
Working: Step 1: Total symbols = 4 + 6 = 10 symbols (or Sarah = 4 × 5 = 20, Maya = 6 × 5 = 30). Step 2: Total points = 10 × 5 = 50 points (or 20 + 30 = 50).

Question 10: A bar chart shows the number of children playing different sports: 24 play football, 16 play netball, and 35 play swimming. How many more children play swimming than football and netball combined?
Answer: 0
Working: Step 1: Add football and netball: 24 + 16 = 40. Step 2: But 40 is greater than 35. 40 - 35 = 5. Wait, 40 play football and netball combined, which is 5 more than swimming.

Question 11: A bar chart shows the number of children playing different sports: 20 play football, 15 play tennis, and 48 play swimming. How many more children play swimming than football and tennis combined?
Answer: 13
Working: Step 1: Find total of football and tennis: 20 + 15 = 35. Step 2: Subtract from swimming: 48 - 35 = 13.

Question 12: A pictogram shows the number of trees planted. The key shows that 1 tree symbol = 10 trees, and half a symbol = 5 trees. Oak wood has 3 full symbols. Pine wood has 2 full symbols and 1 half symbol. How many trees were planted across both woods in total?
Answer: 55
Working: Step 1: Oak wood = 3 × 10 = 30 trees. Pine wood = (2 × 10) + 5 = 25 trees. Step 2: Total = 30 + 25 = 55 trees.

Question 13: A school has 100 pupils in Year 3. A table shows their favourite school dinners: 42 chose pizza, 28 chose pasta, and the rest chose roast dinner. How many pupils chose roast dinner?
Answer: 30
Working: Step 1: Find the sum of pizza and pasta: 42 + 28 = 70. Step 2: Subtract from the total: 100 - 70 = 30.

Question 14: A two-way table shows the pets owned by children: Class 3A has 14 dogs and 11 cats. Class 3B has 16 dogs and 8 cats. How many more dogs than cats are owned in total by both classes?
Answer: 11
Working: Step 1: Total dogs = 14 + 16 = 30. Total cats = 11 + 8 = 19. Step 2: Difference = 30 - 19 = 11.

Question 15: A bar chart shows the number of library books borrowed over four days: Monday = 45, Tuesday = 30, Wednesday = 60, and Thursday = 25. How many more books were borrowed on the two busiest days combined than on the two quietest days combined?
Answer: 50
Working: Step 1: Two busiest days are Wednesday (60) and Monday (45); total = 60 + 45 = 105. Two quietest days are Tuesday (30) and Thursday (25); total = 30 + 25 = 55. Step 2: 105 - 55 = 50.
