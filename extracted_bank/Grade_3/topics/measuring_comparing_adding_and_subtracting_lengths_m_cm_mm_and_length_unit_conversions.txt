=== Topic: Measuring, comparing, adding, and subtracting lengths (m/cm/mm) and length unit conversions ===
Domain: Measurement

--- Measuring lengths ---

Question 1: A piece of ribbon is placed alongside a ruler. It starts at the 0 cm mark and ends at the 14 cm mark. What is the length of the ribbon in centimetres?
Answer: 14 cm
Working: The ribbon starts at 0 cm and ends at 14 cm. 14 cm - 0 cm = 14 cm.

Question 2: A strip of card is placed along a ruler. The left end is at the 4 cm mark and the right end is at the 19 cm mark. What is the length of the strip of card in centimetres?
Answer: 15 cm
Working: Subtract the starting mark from the ending mark: 19 cm - 4 cm = 15 cm.

Question 3: A wooden rod is placed against a millimetre ruler. It starts at the 30 mm mark and ends at the 115 mm mark. What is the length of the wooden rod in millimetres?
Answer: 85 mm
Working: Subtract the start position from the end position: 115 mm - 30 mm = 85 mm.

Question 4: A toy car is measured against a centimetre ruler. The front bumper is at the 7 cm mark and the back bumper is at the 22 cm mark. What is the total length of the toy car in centimetres?
Answer: 15 cm
Working: Length = end mark - start mark. 22 cm - 7 cm = 15 cm.

--- Comparing lengths ---

Question 5: Which length is longer: 250 cm or 2 m?
Answer: 250 cm
Working: 2 m = 200 cm. 250 cm is greater than 200 cm, so 250 cm is longer.

Question 6: Sam has a string that is 85 mm long. Leo has a string that is 9 cm long. Who has the longer string?
Answer: Leo
Working: 9 cm = 90 mm. 90 mm is greater than 85 mm, so Leo has the longer string.

Question 7: Write these three lengths in order from shortest to longest: 1 m 45 cm, 120 cm, 1 m 05 cm.
Answer: 1 m 05 cm, 120 cm, 1 m 45 cm
Working: Convert all lengths to centimetres: 1 m 45 cm = 145 cm; 120 cm; 1 m 05 cm = 105 cm. Ordering from shortest to longest gives 105 cm (1 m 05 cm), 120 cm, 145 cm (1 m 45 cm).

Question 8: Ella has a ribbon that is 3 m 4 cm long. Jack has a ribbon that is 340 cm long. How many centimetres longer is Jack's ribbon than Ella's ribbon?
Answer: 36 cm
Working: Convert Ella's ribbon to centimetres: 3 m 4 cm = 304 cm. Subtract Ella's length from Jack's length: 340 cm - 304 cm = 36 cm.

--- Adding and subtracting lengths ---

Question 9: A carpenter joins two pieces of wood. One piece is 48 cm long and the other piece is 37 cm long. What is the total length of the joined wood in centimetres?
Answer: 85 cm
Working: Add the two lengths: 48 cm + 37 cm = 85 cm.

Question 10: A red ribbon is 1 m 45 cm long. A blue ribbon is 2 m 35 cm long. What is the total length of the two ribbons in metres and centimetres?
Answer: 3 m 80 cm
Working: Add the metres: 1 m + 2 m = 3 m. Add the centimetres: 45 cm + 35 cm = 80 cm. Total = 3 m 80 cm.

Question 11: Maya has a piece of wool that is 2 m 60 cm long. She cuts off 85 cm to tie a parcel. What length of wool is left? Give your answer in metres and centimetres.
Answer: 1 m 75 cm
Working: Convert to centimetres: 2 m 60 cm = 260 cm. Subtract: 260 cm - 85 cm = 175 cm. Convert back: 175 cm = 1 m 75 cm.

Question 12: An engineer starts with a metal rod measuring 600 mm. She cuts off three pieces, each measuring 70 mm. How many millimetres of the metal rod are left?
Answer: 390 mm
Working: Total cut off: 3 × 70 mm = 210 mm. Subtract from total length: 600 mm - 210 mm = 390 mm.

Question 13: A roll of fabric is 5 m long. A dressmaker cuts 1 m 30 cm for a top and 2 m 45 cm for a skirt. How many centimetres of fabric are left on the roll?
Answer: 125 cm
Working: Total used: 130 cm + 245 cm = 375 cm. Total fabric: 5 m = 500 cm. Fabric remaining: 500 cm - 375 cm = 125 cm.

--- Length unit conversions ---

Question 14: How many millimetres are there in 7 centimetres?
Answer: 70 mm
Working: There are 10 mm in 1 cm. 7 × 10 mm = 70 mm.

Question 15: Convert 600 centimetres into metres.
Answer: 6 m
Working: There are 100 cm in 1 m. 600 ÷ 100 = 6 m.

Question 16: A notebook is 18 centimetres and 6 millimetres long. What is its length in millimetres?
Answer: 186 mm
Working: 18 cm = 180 mm. 180 mm + 6 mm = 186 mm.

Question 17: Convert 4 metres and 9 centimetres into centimetres.
Answer: 409 cm
Working: 4 m = 400 cm. 400 cm + 9 cm = 409 cm.

Question 18: A sunflower is 358 cm tall. Write its height in metres and centimetres.
Answer: 3 m 58 cm
Working: Partition 358 cm into hundreds and tens/ones: 300 cm + 58 cm. 300 cm = 3 m. So 358 cm = 3 m 58 cm.

Question 19: A strip of paper is 745 mm long. Write this length in centimetres and millimetres.
Answer: 74 cm 5 mm
Working: Partition 745 mm: 740 mm + 5 mm. 740 mm ÷ 10 = 74 cm. So 745 mm = 74 cm 5 mm.
