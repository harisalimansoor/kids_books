=== Topic: Multiplying 2-digit by 1-digit numbers using mental partitioning, expanded layouts, and formal written methods ===
Domain: Multiplication and Division

--- Mental partitioning ---

Question 1: Calculate 32 × 3 by partitioning 32 into tens and ones.
Answer: 96
Working: Partition 32 into 30 and 2. 30 × 3 = 90. 2 × 3 = 6. 90 + 6 = 96.

Question 2: To work out 43 × 4 mentally, Mia calculates 40 × 4 = 160 and 3 × 4 = 12. What is the total answer to 43 × 4?
Answer: 172
Working: Add the two partitioned products: 160 + 12 = 172.

Question 3: Fill in the missing number to complete the mental partitioning statement: 62 × 3 = (60 × 3) + (__ × 3).
Answer: 2
Working: 62 is partitioned into 60 and 2. Therefore, 62 × 3 = (60 × 3) + (2 × 3). The missing number is 2.

Question 4: A sticker book has 45 stickers on each page. How many stickers are on 8 pages? Use mental partitioning to find the answer.
Answer: 360
Working: Partition 45 into 40 and 5. 40 × 8 = 320. 5 × 8 = 40. 320 + 40 = 360.

--- Expanded layouts ---

Question 5: In an expanded layout for 34 × 4, the partial products are: 4 × 4 = 16 and 30 × 4 = 120. What is 34 × 4?
Answer: 136
Working: Add the partial products: 120 + 16 = 136.

Question 6: A pupil sets out the calculation 56 × 3 using an expanded method:
6 × 3 = 18
50 × 3 = [ ]
What is the missing number in the brackets?
Answer: 150
Working: 5 tens × 3 = 15 tens, so 50 × 3 = 150.

Question 7: A farmer packs 68 apples into each crate. She fills 4 crates. Using an expanded layout, calculate 60 × 4 and 8 × 4, then add them together. How many apples are there in total?
Answer: 272
Working: 60 × 4 = 240. 8 × 4 = 32. 240 + 32 = 272.

Question 8: To calculate 73 × 8 using an expanded layout, you multiply the tens by 8 and the ones by 8. What is the value of the partial product 70 × 8?
Answer: 560
Working: 7 tens × 8 = 56 tens. 70 × 8 = 560.

--- Formal written methods ---

Question 9: Calculate 31 × 3 using a formal written method.
Answer: 93
Working: Multiply the ones: 1 × 3 = 3. Multiply the tens: 30 × 3 = 90. 90 + 3 = 93.

Question 10: Calculate 46 × 4 using a formal written method.
Answer: 184
Working: 6 ones × 4 = 24 ones (write 4, regroup 2 tens). 4 tens × 4 = 16 tens. 16 tens + 2 tens = 18 tens. Total is 184.

Question 11: There are 58 pencils in a box. A school buys 4 boxes. Use a formal written method to find the total number of pencils.
Answer: 232
Working: 58 × 4: 8 ones × 4 = 32 (write 2, regroup 3 tens). 5 tens × 4 = 20 tens. 20 tens + 3 tens = 23 tens. Total = 232 pencils.

Question 12: Calculate 87 × 8 using a formal written method.
Answer: 696
Working: 7 ones × 8 = 56 ones (write 6, regroup 5 tens). 8 tens × 8 = 64 tens. 64 tens + 5 tens = 69 tens. Total is 696.

Question 13: A train carriage has 63 seats. How many seats are there altogether in 8 identical carriages?
Answer: 504
Working: 63 × 8: 3 ones × 8 = 24 (write 4, regroup 2 tens). 6 tens × 8 = 48 tens. 48 tens + 2 tens = 50 tens. Total = 504 seats.
