=== Topic: Telling and writing analogue time to the nearest minute past and to, 12-hour/24-hour clock, and Roman numerals (I to XII) ===
Domain: Measurement

--- Telling and writing analogue time to the nearest minute past and to ---

Question 1: On an analogue clock, the hour hand is just past 3 and the minute hand points to the number 4. How many minutes past 3 is the clock showing?
Answer: 20 minutes
Working: Each number on the clock represents 5 minutes. 4 × 5 = 20 minutes past 3.

Question 2: On an analogue clock, the hour hand is between 5 and 6. The minute hand points 3 small minute marks past the number 2. How many minutes past 5 is the time?
Answer: 13 minutes
Working: The number 2 represents 10 minutes (2 × 5 = 10). 3 marks past 10 gives 10 + 3 = 13 minutes past 5.

Question 3: A clock shows 40 minutes past 7. Write this time using 'minutes to' the next hour.
Answer: 20 minutes to 8
Working: There are 60 minutes in an hour. 60 - 40 = 20. The next hour after 7 is 8, so the time is 20 minutes to 8.

Question 4: On an analogue clock, the minute hand points 2 small minute marks before the number 9. How many minutes to the next hour is the clock showing?
Answer: 17 minutes
Working: The number 9 represents 15 minutes to the hour. Being 2 small marks before 9 means it is 15 + 2 = 17 minutes to the hour.

Question 5: The hour hand on a clock is pointing between 8 and 9. The minute hand is pointing 1 small mark past the number 7. Write the complete time in words using 'minutes to'.
Answer: 24 minutes to 9
Working: The number 7 is 35 minutes past the hour, so 1 mark past is 36 minutes past 8. Subtract 36 from 60: 60 - 36 = 24. The time is 24 minutes to 9.

Question 6: A clock currently shows 14 minutes to 4. How many minutes past 3 is this time?
Answer: 46 minutes
Working: There are 60 minutes in an hour. 60 - 14 = 46. So 14 minutes to 4 is the same as 46 minutes past 3.

--- 12-hour and 24-hour clock ---

Question 7: Convert 4:00 pm to a 24-hour clock time.
Answer: 16:00
Working: For times in the afternoon, add 12 to the hour: 4 + 12 = 16. The time is 16:00.

Question 8: A digital clock shows 08:30. Is this time in the morning or in the evening?
Answer: morning
Working: In 24-hour time, hours before 12:00 represent morning times (am). 08:30 is 8:30 am in the morning.

Question 9: A train departs at 7:15 pm. Write this departure time using the 24-hour clock.
Answer: 19:15
Working: To convert a pm time to 24-hour format, add 12 to the hour: 7 + 12 = 19. The minutes stay the same: 19:15.

Question 10: A school event finishes at 15:45. Write this time using 12-hour clock notation with am or pm.
Answer: 3:45 pm
Working: The hour 15 is greater than 12, so subtract 12: 15 - 12 = 3. This gives 3:45 pm.

Question 11: A film starts at 18:20 on a 24-hour clock. Write the start time in words, stating whether it is in the morning or the evening.
Answer: 20 minutes past 6 in the evening
Working: 18:20 converted to a 12-hour clock is 18 - 12 = 6, so 6:20 pm. In words, this is 20 minutes past 6 in the evening.

--- Roman numerals (I to XII) ---

Question 12: What Arabic number is represented by the Roman numeral VII on a clock face?
Answer: 7
Working: V = 5 and II = 2. 5 + 2 = 7.

Question 13: Write the number 9 as a Roman numeral as it appears on a clock face.
Answer: IX
Working: 9 is written as one before ten: IX.

Question 14: On a clock with Roman numerals, the hour hand points to IV and the minute hand points to XII. What time is shown?
Answer: 4 o'clock
Working: IV represents the number 4 and XII represents the number 12. When the minute hand points to XII, it is an o'clock time: 4 o'clock.

Question 15: On a clock with Roman numerals, the hour hand is between VI and VII. The minute hand points directly at III. What time is shown?
Answer: quarter past 6
Working: VI is 6 and III is 3. The minute hand on 3 means 15 minutes past, which is quarter past. The hour hand is past 6, so the time is quarter past 6 (or 6:15).

Question 16: A Roman numeral clock has its hour hand between IX and X, and its minute hand pointing directly at VIII. Write the time in words using 'minutes to'.
Answer: 20 minutes to 10
Working: IX is 9 and X is 10. VIII represents 8 on the clock dial, which corresponds to 40 minutes past or 20 minutes to the next hour. The hour hand is moving towards X (10), so the time is 20 minutes to 10.
