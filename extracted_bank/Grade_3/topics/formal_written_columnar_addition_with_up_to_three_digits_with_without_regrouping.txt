=== Topic: Formal written columnar addition with up to three digits (with/without regrouping) ===
Domain: Addition and Subtraction

--- Column addition without regrouping ---

Question 1: Use column addition to calculate: 234 + 153
Answer: 387
Working: Add the ones: 4 + 3 = 7. Add the tens: 3 + 5 = 8. Add the hundreds: 2 + 1 = 3. The total is 387.

Question 2: Use column addition to calculate: 425 + 53
Answer: 478
Working: Align 53 under the tens and ones of 425. Add the ones: 5 + 3 = 8. Add the tens: 2 + 5 = 7. Add the hundreds: 4 + 0 = 4. The total is 478.

Question 3: A school library has 312 story books and 265 information books. How many books are there in total?
Answer: 577
Working: Calculate 312 + 265 using column addition. Ones: 2 + 5 = 7. Tens: 1 + 6 = 7. Hundreds: 3 + 2 = 5. Total = 577.

Question 4: Class 3A collects 143 stickers. Class 3B collects 234 stickers. How many stickers do both classes collect altogether?
Answer: 377
Working: Calculate 143 + 234 using column addition. Ones: 3 + 4 = 7. Tens: 4 + 3 = 7. Hundreds: 1 + 2 = 3. Total = 377.

Question 5: In the column addition 3[?]4 + 215 = 569, what digit does [?] stand for?
Answer: 5
Working: Look at the tens column: [?] + 1 = 6. Therefore, [?] = 6 - 1 = 5.

Question 6: Use column addition to calculate: 512 + 246
Answer: 758
Working: Add the ones: 2 + 6 = 8. Add the tens: 1 + 4 = 5. Add the hundreds: 5 + 2 = 7. The total is 758.

Question 7: In the column addition 432 + 35[?] = 789, what digit does [?] stand for?
Answer: 7
Working: Look at the ones column: 2 + [?] = 9. Therefore, [?] = 9 - 2 = 7.

Question 8: A baker has 320 g of white flour and 165 g of wholemeal flour. How many grams of flour does the baker have altogether?
Answer: 485 g
Working: Calculate 320 + 165. Ones: 0 + 5 = 5. Tens: 2 + 6 = 8. Hundreds: 3 + 1 = 4. The total is 485 g.

--- Column addition with regrouping ---

Question 9: Use column addition to calculate: 248 + 137
Answer: 385
Working: Add the ones: 8 + 7 = 15 ones = 1 ten and 5 ones. Write 5 in the ones column and regroup 1 ten. Add the tens: 4 + 3 + 1 = 8. Add the hundreds: 2 + 1 = 3. The total is 385.

Question 10: Use column addition to calculate: 382 + 173
Answer: 555
Working: Add the ones: 2 + 3 = 5. Add the tens: 8 + 7 = 15 tens = 1 hundred and 5 tens. Write 5 in the tens column and regroup 1 hundred. Add the hundreds: 3 + 1 + 1 = 5. The total is 555.

Question 11: Use column addition to calculate: 458 + 27

Question 14: On Saturday, 348 people visited a museum. On Sunday, 275 people visited. How many people visited the museum over the weekend altogether?
Answer: 623
Working: Calculate 348 + 275. Ones: 8 + 5 = 13, regroup 1 ten. Tens: 4 + 7 + 1 = 12, regroup 1 hundred. Hundreds: 3 + 2 + 1 = 6. Total = 623 people.

Question 15: In the column addition 258 + 16[?] = 425, what digit does [?] stand for?
Answer: 7
Working: The ones column ends in 5, so 8 + [?] = 15. This gives [?] = 7 (regrouping 1 ten). Check tens: 5 + 6 + 1 = 12 (regrouping 1 hundred). Check hundreds: 2 + 1 + 1 = 4. The missing digit is 7.

Question 16: In the column addition 3[?]7 + 284 = 671, what digit does [?] stand for?
Answer: 8
Working: Ones: 7 + 4 = 11 (write 1, regroup 1 ten). Tens: [?] + 8 + 1 = 17 (write 7, regroup 1 hundred). So [?] + 9 = 17, giving [?] = 8. Check hundreds: 3 + 2 + 1 = 6. The missing digit is 8.

Working: Calculate 375 + 468. Ones: 5 + 8 = 13, regroup 1 ten. Tens: 7 + 6 + 1 = 14, regroup 1 hundred. Hundreds: 3 + 4 + 1 = 8. Total = 843 metres.
