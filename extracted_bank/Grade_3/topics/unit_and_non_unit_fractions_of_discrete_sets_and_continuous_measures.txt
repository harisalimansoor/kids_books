=== Topic: Unit and non-unit fractions of discrete sets and continuous measures ===
Domain: Fractions

--- Unit and non-unit fractions of discrete sets ---

Question 1: What is 1/4 of 24 marbles?
Answer: 6
Working: To find 1/4 of 24, divide 24 by 4. 24 ÷ 4 = 6.

Question 2: What is 1/3 of 27 pencils?
Answer: 9
Working: To find 1/3 of 27, divide 27 by 3. 27 ÷ 3 = 9.

Question 3: Work out 3/4 of 32 counters.
Answer: 24
Working: First find 1/4: 32 ÷ 4 = 8. Then multiply by the numerator: 8 × 3 = 24.

Question 4: Work out 2/3 of 18 apples.
Answer: 12
Working: First find 1/3: 18 ÷ 3 = 6. Then multiply by the numerator: 6 × 2 = 12.

Question 5: Liam has 35 football cards. He gives 2/5 of them to his friend. How many football cards does he give to his friend?
Answer: 14
Working: Find 1/5 of 35: 35 ÷ 5 = 7 cards. Then find 2/5: 7 × 2 = 14 cards.

Question 6: Work out 5/8 of 48 books.
Answer: 30
Working: First find 1/8: 48 ÷ 8 = 6. Then multiply by the numerator: 6 × 5 = 30.

Question 7: Work out 7/10 of 90 toy cars.
Answer: 63
Working: First find 1/10: 90 ÷ 10 = 9. Then multiply by the numerator: 9 × 7 = 63.

Question 8: A box contains 40 crayons. 3/8 of the crayons are red. How many crayons are not red?
Answer: 25
Working: Find 1/8 of 40: 40 ÷ 8 = 5. The fraction that is not red is 5/8 (since 8/8 - 3/8 = 5/8). 5 × 5 = 25 crayons (or 40 - 15 = 25).

--- Unit and non-unit fractions of continuous measures ---

Question 9: What is 1/4 of 28 cm?
Answer: 7 cm
Working: To find 1/4 of 28 cm, divide 28 by 4. 28 ÷ 4 = 7 cm.

Question 10: What is 3/4 of 200 g?
Answer: 150 g
Working: First find 1/4 of 200 g: 200 ÷ 4 = 50 g. Then multiply by 3: 50 × 3 = 150 g.

Question 11: A bottle contains 450 ml of juice. Jack drinks 2/5 of the juice. How many millilitres of juice did Jack drink?
Answer: 180 ml
Working: Find 1/5 of 450 ml: 450 ÷ 5 = 90 ml. Then find 2/5: 90 × 2 = 180 ml.

Question 12: Work out 2/3 of 60 minutes.
Answer: 40 minutes
Working: First find 1/3 of 60 minutes: 60 ÷ 3 = 20 minutes. Then multiply by 2: 20 × 2 = 40 minutes.

Question 13: A piece of ribbon is 80 cm long. Maya cuts off 3/10 of the ribbon. How many centimetres did Maya cut off?
Answer: 24 cm
Working: Find 1/10 of 80 cm: 80 ÷ 10 = 8 cm. Then find 3/10: 8 × 3 = 24 cm.

Question 14: A runner participates in a 56 m race. She runs 5/8 of the race before stopping. How many metres has she left to run?
Answer: 21 m
Working: Find 1/8 of 56 m: 56 ÷ 8 = 7 m. The remaining fraction is 3/8 (since 8/8 - 5/8 = 3/8). 7 × 3 = 21 m (or 56 - 35 = 21 m).
