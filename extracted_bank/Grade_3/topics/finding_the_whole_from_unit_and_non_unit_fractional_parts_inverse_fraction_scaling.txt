=== Topic: Finding the whole from unit and non-unit fractional parts (inverse fraction scaling) ===
Domain: Fractions

--- Finding the whole from unit fractional parts ---

Question 1: One third of a number is 7. What is the number?
Answer: 21
Working: Multiply the unit fractional part by 3: 7 × 3 = 21.

Question 2: One quarter of a length of ribbon is 8 cm. What is the total length of the ribbon?
Answer: 32 cm
Working: Multiply 8 cm by 4: 8 × 4 = 32 cm.

Question 3: One fifth of the counters in a bag is 6. How many counters are in the whole bag?
Answer: 30
Working: Multiply 6 by 5: 6 × 5 = 30.

Question 4: One eighth of a cake has a mass of 30 g. What is the mass of the whole cake?
Answer: 240 g
Working: Multiply 30 g by 8: 30 × 8 = 240 g.

Question 5: One tenth of a bottle of juice is 45 ml. How many millilitres of juice are in the full bottle?
Answer: 450 ml
Working: Multiply 45 ml by 10: 45 × 10 = 450 ml.

Question 6: Maya spends 1/4 of her pocket money on a book. The book costs £6. How much pocket money did Maya have altogether?
Answer: £24
Working: Multiply £6 by 4: £6 × 4 = £24.

Question 7: In a flowerbed, 1/3 of the tulips are red. There are 16 red tulips. How many tulips are there in total?
Answer: 48
Working: Multiply 16 by 3: 16 × 3 = 48.

Question 8: A piece of wood is cut into 8 equal parts. One part is 12 cm long. What was the length of the piece of wood before it was cut?
Answer: 96 cm
Working: Multiply 12 cm by 8: 12 × 8 = 96 cm.

--- Finding the whole from non-unit fractional parts ---

Question 9: Two thirds of a number is 18. What is the number?
Answer: 27
Working: Find 1/3 by dividing 18 by 2: 18 ÷ 2 = 9. Find the whole by multiplying by 3: 9 × 3 = 27.

Question 10: Three quarters of a box of pencils is 24 pencils. How many pencils are in the full box?
Answer: 32
Working: Find 1/4: 24 ÷ 3 = 8. Find the whole: 8 × 4 = 32 pencils.

Question 11: Two fifths of the distance along a running track is 14 m. How long is the whole track?
Answer: 35 m
Working: Find 1/5: 14 ÷ 2 = 7 m. Find the whole: 7 × 5 = 35 m.

Question 12: Three eighths of the apples in a crate is 24 apples. How many apples are in the whole crate?
Answer: 64
Working: Find 1/8: 24 ÷ 3 = 8. Find the whole: 8 × 8 = 64 apples.

Question 13: Leo has read 2/3 of his book. He has read 48 pages so far. How many pages are there in the whole book?
Answer: 72
Working: Find 1/3: 48 ÷ 2 = 24 pages. Find the whole: 24 × 3 = 72 pages.

Question 14: Four fifths of a bag of flour has a mass of 36 kg. What is the mass of the full bag of flour?
Answer: 45 kg
Working: Find 1/5: 36 ÷ 4 = 9 kg. Find the whole: 9 × 5 = 45 kg.

Question 15: Three tenths of a bottle of squash is 60 ml. How many millilitres of squash are in the full bottle?
Answer: 200 ml
Working: Find 1/10: 60 ÷ 3 = 20 ml. Find the whole: 20 × 10 = 200 ml.

Question 16: A swimmer has completed 5/8 of her swim. She has swum 40 m. What is the total length of her swim?
Answer: 64 m
Working: Find 1/8: 40 ÷ 5 = 8 m. Find the whole: 8 × 8 = 64 m.
