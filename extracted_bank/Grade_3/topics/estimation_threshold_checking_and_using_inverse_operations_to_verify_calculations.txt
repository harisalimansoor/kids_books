=== Topic: Estimation, threshold checking, and using inverse operations to verify calculations ===
Domain: Addition and Subtraction

--- Estimation ---

Question 1: Estimate the answer to 487 + 312 by rounding each number to the nearest hundred.
Answer: 800
Working: 487 rounds to 500 to the nearest hundred. 312 rounds to 300 to the nearest hundred. 500 + 300 = 800.

Question 2: Estimate the answer to 674 - 231 by rounding each number to the nearest ten.
Answer: 440
Working: 674 rounds to 670 to the nearest ten. 231 rounds to 230 to the nearest ten. 670 - 230 = 440.

Question 3: A baker bakes 295 bread rolls in the morning and 188 bread rolls in the afternoon. Round each number to the nearest hundred to estimate the total number of rolls baked.
Answer: 500
Working: 295 rounds to 300. 188 rounds to 200. 300 + 200 = 500 rolls.

Question 4: Round 819 and 492 to the nearest hundred to estimate the difference between them.
Answer: 300
Working: 819 rounds to 800 to the nearest hundred. 492 rounds to 500 to the nearest hundred. 800 - 500 = 300.

Question 5: A jumper costs £49 and a jacket costs £38. Round each price to the nearest £10 to estimate the total cost.
Answer: £90
Working: £49 rounds to £50. £38 rounds to £40. £50 + £40 = £90.

--- Threshold checking ---

Question 6: A cinema has 500 seats. 268 children and 245 adults want to watch a film. Are there enough seats for everyone? Write yes or no.
Answer: no
Working: 268 + 245 = 513 people. 513 is greater than 500, so there are not enough seats.

Question 7: Without calculating the exact total, decide if 384 + 229 is greater than 600 or less than 600.
Answer: greater than 600
Working: 384 + 229: 380 + 220 = 600, so 384 + 229 must be greater than 600.

Question 8: Ella has a £50 gift card. She wants to buy a board game for £28 and a puzzle for £24. Does Ella have enough money on her gift card to buy both items? Write yes or no.
Answer: no
Working: £28 + £24 = £52. £52 is greater than the £50 threshold, so Ella does not have enough money.

Question 9: A school library needs to collect 700 books. Key Stage 1 collects 342 books and Key Stage 2 collects 385 books. Have they reached their target of 700 books? Write yes or no.
Answer: yes
Working: 342 + 385 = 727 books. 727 is greater than 700, so they have reached their target.

Question 10: A small bridge has a safe load limit of 900 kg. A trailer weighing 420 kg carries a load of 465 kg. Is the total mass under the 900 kg safety limit? Write yes or no.
Answer: yes
Working: 420 kg + 465 kg = 885 kg. 885 kg is less than the 900 kg threshold, so it is under the safety limit.

--- Using inverse operations to verify calculations ---

Question 11: Ravi calculates 648 - 235 = 413. Write the addition calculation Ravi can use to check his answer.
Answer: 413 + 235 = 648
Working: The inverse of subtracting 235 is adding 235 to the result: 413 + 235 = 648.

Question 12: Beth calculates 357 + 219 = 576. Write a subtraction calculation Beth can use to check her answer.
Answer: 576 - 219 = 357
Working: The inverse of adding 219 to 357 is subtracting 219 from 576: 576 - 219 = 357 (or 576 - 357 = 219).

Question 13: Marcus works out 754 - 281 = 473. He checks his answer by calculating 473 + 281 and gets 754. Is his original subtraction calculation correct? Write yes or no.
Answer: yes
Working: 473 + 281 = 754. Because the inverse addition equals the starting number 754, the original calculation is correct.

Question 14: Fill in the missing number to check the addition using an inverse operation: 428 + 316 = 744. Check: 744 - 316 = ___
Answer: 428
Working: Using the inverse relationship between addition and subtraction: 744 - 316 = 428.

Question 15: Use the inverse operation to find the missing number: ___ - 245 = 380.
Answer: 625
Working: Use addition as the inverse of subtraction: 380 + 245 = 625.
