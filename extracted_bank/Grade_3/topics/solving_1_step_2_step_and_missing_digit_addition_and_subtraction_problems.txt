=== Topic: Solving 1-step, 2-step, and missing digit addition and subtraction problems ===
Domain: Addition and Subtraction

--- 1-step addition and subtraction problems ---

Question 1: A library has 348 fiction books and 235 non-fiction books. How many books are there in total?
Answer: 583
Working: 348 + 235 = 583

Question 2: A farmer picked 615 apples. He sold 287 of them at the market. How many apples does he have left?
Answer: 328
Working: 615 - 287 = 328

Question 3: A school hall has 450 chairs set out for an assembly. 278 children sit down. How many chairs are empty?
Answer: 172
Working: 450 - 278 = 172

Question 4: On Saturday, 386 people visited a museum. On Sunday, 458 people visited. How many people visited the museum altogether over the weekend?
Answer: 844
Working: 386 + 458 = 844

Question 5: A baker baked 500 bread rolls. By the afternoon, 364 bread rolls were sold. How many bread rolls were left?
Answer: 136
Working: 500 - 364 = 136

--- 2-step addition and subtraction problems ---

Question 6: A toy shop has 245 red balls and 180 blue balls. During the day, 163 balls are sold. How many balls are left in the shop?
Answer: 262
Working: Step 1: 245 + 180 = 425 balls in total. Step 2: 425 - 163 = 262 balls left.

Question 7: A baker has 650 eggs. He uses 215 eggs to bake cakes and 188 eggs to bake biscuits. How many eggs does he have left?
Answer: 247
Working: Step 1: 215 + 188 = 403 eggs used. Step 2: 650 - 403 = 247 eggs left.

Question 8: Maya has 175 stickers. Liam has 48 more stickers than Maya. How many stickers do Maya and Liam have altogether?
Answer: 398
Working: Step 1: Find Liam's stickers: 175 + 48 = 223. Step 2: Find total stickers: 175 + 223 = 398.

Question 9: A bus starts a journey with 58 passengers. At the first stop, 19 passengers get off and 27 new passengers get on. How many passengers are on the bus now?
Answer: 66
Working: Step 1: 58 - 19 = 39 passengers. Step 2: 39 + 27 = 66 passengers.

Question 10: Oliver has 340p in his money box. He spends 125p on a comic and 85p on a pen. How many pence does Oliver have left?
Answer: 130
Working: Step 1: 125p + 85p = 210p spent. Step 2: 340p - 210p = 130p left.

Question 11: Class 3A collects 324 plastic bottles for recycling. Class 3B collects 78 fewer bottles than Class 3A. How many bottles did the two classes collect in total?
Answer: 570
Working: Step 1: Find Class 3B's bottles: 324 - 78 = 246. Step 2: Find total: 324 + 246 = 570.

--- Missing digit addition and subtraction problems ---

Question 12: Find the missing tens digit in this addition calculation: 3 [ ] 6 + 248 = 624.
Answer: 7
Working: Ones: 6 + 8 = 14 ones (4 ones, regroup 1 ten). Tens: [ ] + 4 + 1 = 12 tens (2 tens, regroup 1 hundred), so [ ] + 5 = 12, which gives [ ] = 7. Hundreds: 3 + 2 + 1 = 6 hundreds. The missing digit is 7.

Question 13: Find the missing tens digit in this subtraction calculation: 753 - 2 [ ] 8 = 465.
Answer: 8
Working: Ones: Regroup 1 ten into 10 ones: 13 - 8 = 5. Tens: 4 tens remain; regroup 1 hundred into 10 tens to make 14 tens: 14 - [ ] = 6, so [ ] = 8. Hundreds: 6 - 2 = 4. The missing digit is 8.

Question 14: Find the missing ones digit in this addition calculation: 47 [ ] + 186 = 661.
Answer: 5
Working: Ones: [ ] + 6 must equal 11, so [ ] = 5. Tens: 7 + 8 + 1 (regrouped) = 16 tens (6 tens, regroup 1 hundred). Hundreds: 4 + 1 + 1 = 6 hundreds. The missing digit is 5.

Question 15: Find the missing hundreds digit in this subtraction calculation: [ ] 24 - 358 = 266.
Answer: 6
Working: Inverse check: 266 + 358 = 624. Therefore, the starting number is 624 and the missing hundreds digit is 6.

Question 16: Find the missing hundreds digit in this addition calculation: 264 + [ ] 79 = 743.
Answer: 4
Working: Ones: 4 + 9 = 13 (3 ones, regroup 1 ten). Tens: 6 + 7 + 1 = 14 (4 tens, regroup 1 hundred). Hundreds: 2 + [ ] + 1 = 7, so [ ] + 3 = 7, which gives [ ] = 4.

Question 17: Find the missing ones digit in this subtraction calculation: 804 - 43 [ ] = 367.
Answer: 7
Working: Inverse check: 804 - 367 = 437. Therefore, the subtracted number is 437 and the missing ones digit is 7.
