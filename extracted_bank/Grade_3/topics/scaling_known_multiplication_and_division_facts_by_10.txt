=== Topic: Scaling known multiplication and division facts by 10 ===
Domain: Multiplication and Division

--- Scaling known multiplication facts by 10 ---

Question 1: Use the fact 3 × 4 = 12 to calculate 3 × 40.
Answer: 120
Working: 40 is 4 tens. 3 × 4 = 12, so 3 × 4 tens = 12 tens = 120.

Question 2: Calculate: 50 × 4
Answer: 200
Working: 5 × 4 = 20. Scale by 10: 20 tens = 200.

Question 3: A shopkeeper has 8 boxes of crayons. There are 30 crayons in each box. How many crayons are there altogether?
Answer: 240
Working: 8 × 3 = 24, so 8 × 30 = 240 crayons.

Question 4: Find the missing number: 4 × ___ = 320
Answer: 80
Working: 4 × 8 = 32, so 4 × 80 = 320.

Question 5: A sticker book costs 40p. Maya buys 5 sticker books. How much does she spend in total, in pence?
Answer: 200p
Working: 5 × 4 = 20, so 5 × 40p = 200p.

Question 6: Fill in the missing number to make the statement true: 30 × 8 = 3 × ___
Answer: 80
Working: 30 × 8 = 240. 3 × 80 = 240. So the missing number is 80.

Question 7: Leo runs 80 metres in one lap. He runs 4 laps. How many metres has he run altogether?
Answer: 320 m
Working: 4 × 8 = 32, so 4 × 80 m = 320 m.

--- Scaling known division facts by 10 ---

Question 8: Use the fact 24 ÷ 3 = 8 to calculate 240 ÷ 3.
Answer: 80
Working: 24 tens ÷ 3 = 8 tens = 80.

Question 9: Calculate: 160 ÷ 8
Answer: 20
Working: 16 ÷ 8 = 2, so 16 tens ÷ 8 = 2 tens = 20.

Question 10: A ribbon is 320 cm long. It is cut into 4 equal pieces. How long is each piece, in centimetres?
Answer: 80 cm
Working: 32 ÷ 4 = 8, so 320 ÷ 4 = 80 cm.

Question 11: A teacher shares 280 counters equally among 4 groups. How many counters does each group get?
Answer: 70
Working: 28 ÷ 4 = 7, so 280 ÷ 4 = 70 counters.

Question 12: Find the missing number: ___ ÷ 3 = 50
Answer: 150
Working: 50 × 3 = 150, so 150 ÷ 3 = 50.

Question 13: Sam has 400p. He shares the money equally between 8 money boxes. How much money, in pence, does each box get?
Answer: 50p
Working: 40 ÷ 8 = 5, so 400p ÷ 8 = 50p.

Question 14: Find the missing number: 480 ÷ ___ = 60
Answer: 8
Working: 48 tens ÷ 8 = 6 tens = 60, so the missing number is 8.
