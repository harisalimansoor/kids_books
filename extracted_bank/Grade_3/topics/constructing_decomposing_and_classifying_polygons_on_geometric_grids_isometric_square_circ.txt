=== Topic: Constructing, decomposing, and classifying polygons on geometric grids (isometric, square, circular geoboards) ===
Domain: Geometry (Properties of Shapes

--- Isometric geoboards ---

Question 1: On an isometric dot grid, Ben draws a 2-D shape with 3 equal sides and 3 vertices. What type of polygon has he drawn?
Answer: Triangle
Working: A 2-D polygon with 3 straight sides and 3 vertices is a triangle.

Question 2: Sam makes a regular hexagon on an isometric geoboard. He joins small, identical equilateral triangles together to fill the hexagon completely. How many of these triangles are needed to make the hexagon?
Answer: 6
Working: A regular hexagon on an isometric grid decomposes into 6 congruent equilateral triangles meeting at the centre point.

Question 3: Layla joins 2 identical equilateral triangles side by side along one full edge on an isometric grid. How many sides does the new combined polygon have?
Answer: 4
Working: Joining two 3-sided triangles along one shared edge creates a 4-sided polygon (a rhombus), which has 4 sides.

Question 4: A regular hexagon is made on an isometric geoboard. A straight band is placed across the middle from one vertex to the opposite vertex, cutting the hexagon in half. What 4-sided shape is each half?
Answer: Trapezium
Working: Cutting a regular hexagon directly across the centre from vertex to opposite vertex splits it into two equal 4-sided shapes, each with one pair of parallel sides, which is a trapezium.

--- Square geoboards ---

Question 5: Harry stretches a rubber band on a square geoboard to make a shape with 4 equal sides and 4 right angles. What shape has he made?
Answer: Square
Working: A 4-sided shape with 4 equal straight sides and 4 right angles is a square.

Question 6: On a square geoboard, Mia makes a rectangle. She places a straight band from one top corner to the opposite bottom corner. Into what two shapes has the rectangle been decomposed?
Answer: Triangles
Working: A diagonal line across a rectangle divides it into 2 right-angled triangles.

Question 7: Liam makes a triangle on a square geoboard. One side is horizontal, one side is vertical, and the third side is sloped. What type of angle is formed between the horizontal and vertical sides?
Answer: Right angle
Working: A horizontal line and a vertical line meet at a perpendicular angle, which is a right angle.

Question 8: On a square geoboard, Ella makes a 4-sided shape that has only 1 pair of parallel sides. What is the name of this quadrilateral?
Answer: Trapezium
Working: A 4-sided polygon (quadrilateral) with exactly one pair of parallel sides is a trapezium.

--- Circular geoboards ---

Question 9: An 8-pin circular geoboard has 8 pins spaced evenly in a circle. A child stretches a band to connect all 8 pins in order around the circle. What is the name of this 8-sided polygon?
Answer: Octagon
Working: An 8-sided 2-D polygon is an octagon.

Question 10: A circular geoboard has 12 pins arranged like the numbers on a clock face. An elastic band connects pin 12, pin 4, and pin 8. How many sides does this shape have?
Answer: 3
Working: Connecting 3 points (pins 12, 4, and 8) with straight lines forms a triangle, which has 3 sides.

Question 11: On a 12-pin circular geoboard numbered 1 to 12 like a clock, a band connects pin 12, pin 3, pin 6, and pin 9. The shape has 4 equal straight sides and 4 right angles. What shape is formed?
Answer: Square
Working: Connecting the 4 equally spaced quarter-turn points (12, 3, 6, 9) forms a 4-sided polygon with equal sides and right angles, which is a square.

Question 12: On a 12-pin circular geoboard, a pupil connects every second pin in order: pin 12, pin 2, pin 4, pin 6, pin 8, and pin 10. What is the name of the 6-sided polygon created?
Answer: Hexagon
Working: Connecting the 6 equally spaced pins forms a 6-sided polygon, which is a hexagon.
