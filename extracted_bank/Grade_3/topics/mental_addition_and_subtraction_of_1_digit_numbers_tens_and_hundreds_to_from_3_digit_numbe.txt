=== Topic: Mental addition and subtraction of 1-digit numbers, tens, and hundreds to/from 3-digit numbers ===
Domain: Addition and Subtraction

--- Mental addition and subtraction of 1-digit numbers to and from 3-digit numbers ---

Question 1: Calculate: 452 + 6
Answer: 458
Working: Add 6 to the ones digit: 2 + 6 = 8. So, 452 + 6 = 458.

Question 2: Calculate: 789 - 5
Answer: 784
Working: Subtract 5 from the ones digit: 9 - 5 = 4. So, 789 - 5 = 784.

Question 3: Calculate: 348 + 7
Answer: 355
Working: Add 2 to bridge to 350, then add the remaining 5: 350 + 5 = 355.

Question 4: Calculate: 523 - 8
Answer: 515
Working: Subtract 3 to reach 520, then subtract the remaining 5: 520 - 5 = 515.

Question 5: A baker has 235 bread rolls. He bakes 8 more bread rolls. How many bread rolls does he have in total?
Answer: 243
Working: 235 + 8 = 235 + 5 + 3 = 240 + 3 = 243.

Question 6: A sticker book has 462 stickers. Maya sticks 9 stickers into her notebook. How many stickers are left in the book?
Answer: 453
Working: 462 - 9 = 462 - 2 - 7 = 460 - 7 = 453.

Question 7: Calculate: 396 + 7
Answer: 403
Working: Add 4 to bridge to the next hundred (400), then add the remaining 3: 400 + 3 = 403.

Question 8: Calculate: 604 - 8
Answer: 596
Working: Subtract 4 to reach 600, then subtract the remaining 4: 600 - 4 = 596.

Question 9: Find the missing number: 738 + ___ = 745
Answer: 7
Working: Subtract 738 from 745: 745 - 738 = 7.

Question 10: Find the missing number: ___ - 7 = 519
Answer: 526
Working: Use the inverse operation: 519 + 7 = 526.

--- Mental addition and subtraction of tens to and from 3-digit numbers ---

Question 11: Calculate: 324 + 50
Answer: 374
Working: Add 5 tens to 2 tens: 20 + 50 = 70. Keep the hundreds and ones the same: 374.

Question 12: Calculate: 678 - 30
Answer: 648
Working: Subtract 3 tens from 7 tens: 70 - 30 = 40. Keep the hundreds and ones the same: 648.

Question 13: Calculate: 472 + 60
Answer: 532
Working: Add 30 to reach 502, then add the remaining 30: 502 + 30 = 532.

Question 14: Calculate: 536 - 70
Answer: 466
Working: Subtract 30 to reach 506, then subtract the remaining 40: 506 - 40 = 466.

Question 15: A library has 345 books on a shelf. The librarian puts 40 more books on the shelf. How many books are on the shelf now?
Answer: 385
Working: 345 + 40 = 385.

Question 16: There are 682 sheep in a field. A farmer moves 50 sheep to another field. How many sheep are left in the field?
Answer: 632
Working: 682 - 50 = 632.

Question 17: Calculate: 795 + 40
Answer: 835
Working: Add 10 to reach 805, then add the remaining 30: 805 + 30 = 835.

Question 18: Calculate: 814 - 60
Answer: 754
Working: Subtract 14 to reach 800, then subtract 46: 800 - 46 = 754 (or 814 - 10 = 804, 804 - 50 = 754).

Question 19: Find the missing number: 283 + ___ = 353
Answer: 70
Working: Count up from 283: 283 + 17 = 300, 300 + 53 = 353. 17 + 53 = 70 (or 353 - 283 = 70).

Question 20: Find the missing number: ___ - 40 = 478
Answer: 518
Working: Use the inverse operation: 478 + 40 = 518.

--- Mental addition and subtraction of hundreds to and from 3-digit numbers ---

Question 21: Calculate: 247 + 300
Answer: 547
Working: Add 3 hundreds to 2 hundreds: 200 + 300 = 500. Keep the tens and ones the same: 547.

Question 22: Calculate: 865 - 400
Answer: 465
Working: Subtract 4 hundreds from 8 hundreds: 800 - 400 = 400. Keep the tens and ones the same: 465.

Question 23: Calculate: 519 + 400
Answer: 919
Working: 500 + 400 = 900. Add 19 to get 919.

Question 24: Calculate: 734 - 500
Answer: 234
Working: 700 - 500 = 200. Add 34 to get 234.

Question 25: A charity raised £320 in the morning and £500 in the afternoon. How much money did the charity raise altogether?
Answer: £820
Working: £320 + £500 = £820.

Question 26: A train journey is 785 km long. The train has already travelled 300 km. How many kilometres does the train have left to travel?
Answer: 485 km
Working: 785 - 300 = 485 km.

Question 27: Calculate: 368 + 600
Answer: 968
Working: 300 + 600 = 900. Add 68 to get 968.

Question 28: Find the missing number: 194 + ___ = 794
Answer: 600
Working: Subtract 194 from 794: 794 - 194 = 600.

Question 29: Find the missing number: ___ - 400 = 382
Answer: 782
Working: Use the inverse operation: 382 + 400 = 782.

Question 30: A warehouse stores 256 boxes. A lorry delivers 600 more boxes. How many boxes are in the warehouse altogether?
Answer: 856
Working: 256 + 600 = 856.
