=== Topic: Number bonds, complements to 100, 500, and 1000, and additive inverse relationships ===
Domain: Addition and Subtraction

--- Number bonds ---

Question 1: What number is added to 43 to make 100?
Answer: 57
Working: 100 - 43 = 57.

Question 2: Find the missing number to complete the number bond: 74 + ___ = 100.
Answer: 26
Working: 100 - 74 = 26.

Question 3: A piece of ribbon is 100 cm long. Mia cuts off 36 cm. How many centimetres of ribbon are left?
Answer: 64
Working: 100 - 36 = 64 cm.

Question 4: A baker needs 100 eggs to bake cakes. He already has 68 eggs. How many more eggs does he need?
Answer: 32
Working: 100 - 68 = 32 eggs.

Question 5: Liam has 100 stickers in total. He gives 27 stickers to Jack and 35 stickers to Maya. How many stickers does Liam have left?
Answer: 38
Working: 27 + 35 = 62. 100 - 62 = 38 stickers.

--- Complements to 100, 500, and 1000 ---

Question 6: What number must be added to 340 to make 500?
Answer: 160
Working: 500 - 340 = 160.

Question 7: A runner is taking part in a 1000 m race. She has run 650 m so far. How many metres does she have left to run?
Answer: 350
Working: 1000 - 650 = 350 m.

Question 8: Calculate: 1000 - 430
Answer: 570
Working: 1000 - 430 = 570.

Question 9: A water tank holds 500 litres when full. It currently contains 375 litres. How many more litres are needed to fill the tank?
Answer: 125
Working: 500 - 375 = 125 litres.

Question 10: What number added to 715 gives a total of 1000?
Answer: 285
Working: 1000 - 715 = 285.

Question 11: Find the missing number: 500 - ___ = 265
Answer: 235
Working: 500 - 265 = 235.

--- Additive inverse relationships ---

Question 12: If 340 + 260 = 600, what is the value of 600 - 340?
Answer: 260
Working: Using the inverse of addition, 600 - 340 = 260.

Question 13: Use the inverse operation to find the missing number: ___ - 145 = 230
Answer: 375
Working: The inverse of subtracting 145 is adding 145: 230 + 145 = 375.

Question 14: A teacher writes the subtraction fact: 520 - 180 = 340. Use the inverse operation to complete the addition fact: 340 + ___ = 520.
Answer: 180
Working: By inverse relationships, if 520 - 180 = 340, then 340 + 180 = 520.

Question 15: Find the missing number in this calculation: ___ + 215 = 650
Answer: 435
Working: Use the inverse operation: 650 - 215 = 435.

Question 16: Find the missing number in this calculation: ___ - 240 = 510
Answer: 750
Working: Use the inverse operation: 510 + 240 = 750.
