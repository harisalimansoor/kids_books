=== Topic: Dividing 2-digit by 1-digit numbers with and without remainders using grouping, sharing, and friendly partitioning ===
Domain: Multiplication and Division

--- Dividing using grouping ---

Question 1: A sports teacher has 48 tennis balls. She puts them into tubes of 4 balls. How many tubes can she fill?
Answer: 12
Working: 48 ÷ 4 = 12. She can fill 12 tubes.

Question 2: A gardener has 64 daffodil bulbs. She plants them in groups of 8 in each row. How many rows does she plant?
Answer: 8
Working: 64 ÷ 8 = 8. She plants 8 rows.

Question 3: A ribbon is 96 cm long. It is cut into shorter pieces that are each 3 cm long. How many pieces are made?
Answer: 32
Working: 96 ÷ 3 = 32. 32 pieces of ribbon are made.

Question 4: A teacher puts 36 children into equal groups of 4 for a maths game. How many groups are made?
Answer: 9
Working: 36 ÷ 4 = 9. 9 groups are made.

--- Dividing using sharing ---

Question 5: Share 69 counters equally among 3 children. How many counters does each child receive?
Answer: 23
Working: 69 ÷ 3 = 23. Each child receives 23 counters.

Question 6: 84 pencils are shared equally into 4 pots. How many pencils are in each pot?
Answer: 21
Working: 84 ÷ 4 = 21. There are 21 pencils in each pot.

Question 7: Three friends share £96 equally. How much money does each friend receive?
Answer: £32
Working: £96 ÷ 3 = £32. Each friend receives £32.

Question 8: 88 sheets of coloured paper are shared equally among 8 tables in an art room. How many sheets of paper are on each table?
Answer: 11
Working: 88 ÷ 8 = 11. Each table receives 11 sheets.

--- Dividing using friendly partitioning ---

Question 9: To calculate 52 ÷ 4, partition 52 into 40 and 12. Find 40 ÷ 4 and 12 ÷ 4, then add the answers together. What is 52 ÷ 4?
Answer: 13
Working: 40 ÷ 4 = 10 and 12 ÷ 4 = 3. 10 + 3 = 13.

Question 10: To calculate 75 ÷ 3, Sam partitions 75 into 60 and a missing number. What is the missing number?
Answer: 15
Working: 75 - 60 = 15. The missing number is 15.

Question 11: Calculate 72 ÷ 3 by partitioning 72 into 60 and 12.
Answer: 24
Working: 60 ÷ 3 = 20. 12 ÷ 3 = 4. 20 + 4 = 24.

Question 12: Calculate 96 ÷ 8 by partitioning 96 into 80 and 16.
Answer: 12
Working: 80 ÷ 8 = 10. 16 ÷ 8 = 2. 10 + 2 = 12.

Question 13: A baker divides 68 rolls equally into 4 trays. She partitions 68 into 40 and 28. How many rolls are placed on each tray?
Answer: 17
Working: 40 ÷ 4 = 10. 28 ÷ 4 = 7. 10 + 7 = 17 rolls.

--- Dividing with remainders ---

Question 14: Calculate: 38 ÷ 3. Give your answer in the form quotient r remainder (for example, 5 r 2).
Answer: 12 r 2
Working: 3 × 12 = 36. 38 - 36 = 2. The answer is 12 r 2.

Question 15: A baker packs 5 biscuits into each box. She has 67 biscuits. How many full boxes can she pack?
Answer: 13
Working: 67 ÷ 5 = 13 r 2. She can pack 13 full boxes.

Question 16: 43 apples are packed into bags of 4. How many apples are left over?
Answer: 3
Working: 43 ÷ 4 = 10 r 3. There are 3 apples left over.

Question 17: Calculate: 59 ÷ 4. Give your answer in the form quotient r remainder (for example, 5 r 2).
Answer: 14 r 3
Working: 4 × 14 = 56. 59 - 56 = 3. The answer is 14 r 3.

Question 18: A teacher puts 75 stickers into rows of 8 on a chart. How many full rows of stickers can she make?
Answer: 9
Working: 75 ÷ 8 = 9 r 3. She can make 9 full rows.
