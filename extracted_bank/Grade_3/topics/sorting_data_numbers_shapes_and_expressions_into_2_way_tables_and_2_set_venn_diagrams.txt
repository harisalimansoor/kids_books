=== Topic: Sorting data, numbers, shapes, and expressions into 2-way tables and 2-set Venn diagrams ===
Domain: Statistics

--- 2-way tables ---

Question 1: A teacher sorts 30 pupils into a two-way table showing who plays football and who plays netball.

• Boys: 8 play football, 6 play netball.
• Girls: 7 play football, 9 play netball.

How many pupils play football in total?
Answer: 15
Working: Number of boys playing football = 8. Number of girls playing football = 7. Total playing football = 8 + 7 = 15.

Question 2: The table shows the pets owned by children in Year 3.

Cats: 14 Boys, 18 Girls
Dogs: 16 Boys, 12 Girls

How many more children own cats than dogs?
Answer: 4
Working: Total owning cats = 14 + 18 = 32. Total owning dogs = 16 + 12 = 28. Difference = 32 - 28 = 4.

Question 3: A two-way table sorts numbers into two rows ('Multiples of 4' and 'Not multiples of 4') and two columns ('Odd' and 'Even'). Into which cell should the number 28 be placed: 'Multiple of 4 and Even', 'Multiple of 4 and Odd', 'Not a multiple of 4 and Even', or 'Not a multiple of 4 and Odd'?
Answer: Multiple of 4 and Even
Working: 28 ÷ 4 = 7, so 28 is a multiple of 4. 28 ends in 8, so it is an even number. Therefore, 28 belongs in 'Multiple of 4 and Even'.

Question 4: A class records how they travel to school in a two-way table:

• Walk: 11 Boys, 13 Girls
• Bus: 9 Boys, ? Girls
• Total: 20 Boys, 21 Girls

How many girls travel by bus?
Answer: 8
Working: Total number of girls = 21. Girls walking = 13. Girls travelling by bus = 21 - 13 = 8.

Question 5: A two-way table sorts 2-D shapes using two categories: 'Has 4 sides' and 'Has at least one right angle'. Which cell does an equilateral triangle belong to: 'Has 4 sides and at least one right angle', 'Has 4 sides and no right angles', 'Fewer than 4 sides and at least one right angle', or 'Fewer than 4 sides and no right angles'?
Answer: Fewer than 4 sides and no right angles
Working: An equilateral triangle has 3 sides (fewer than 4 sides) and all three angles are acute (no right angles). So it belongs in 'Fewer than 4 sides and no right angles'.

Question 6: A two-way table sorts the following calculations: 12 × 4, 15 × 3, 8 × 4, and 9 × 3.
The rows are 'Answer is even' and 'Answer is odd'.
The columns are 'Answer is greater than 40' and 'Answer is 40 or less'.
Which calculation belongs in the cell for 'Answer is odd and greater than 40'?
Answer: 15 × 3
Working: Calculate each: 12 × 4 = 48 (even, >40); 15 × 3 = 45 (odd, >40); 8 × 4 = 32 (even, ≤40); 9 × 3 = 27 (odd, ≤40). The calculation with an odd answer greater than 40 is 15 × 3.

--- 2-set Venn diagrams ---

Question 7: A Venn diagram has two overlapping circles: Set A = 'Multiples of 3' and Set B = 'Even numbers'. Where does the number 12 go: 'Set A only', 'Set B only', 'Both sets', or 'Outside both sets'?
Answer: Both sets
Working: 12 is a multiple of 3 (3 × 4 = 12) and 12 is an even number. Because it satisfies both rules, it is placed in the intersection ('Both sets').

Question 8: A Venn diagram has two circles labelled 'Multiples of 4' and 'Multiples of 8'. Where does the number 20 go: 'Multiples of 4 only', 'Multiples of 8 only', 'Both circles', or 'Outside both circles'?
Answer: Multiples of 4 only
Working: 20 is a multiple of 4 (4 × 5 = 20), but 20 is not a multiple of 8 (8, 16, 24...). So 20 belongs in 'Multiples of 4 only'.

Question 9: A 2-set Venn diagram sorts the numbers 6, 9, 12, 15, 16, 24.
Circle 1: Multiples of 3
Circle 2: Multiples of 4

How many of these numbers belong in the middle section (both circles)?
Answer: 2
Working: Check which numbers are multiples of both 3 and 4: 6 (3 only), 9 (3 only), 12 (3 and 4), 15 (3 only), 16 (4 only), 24 (3 and 4). There are 2 numbers (12 and 24) in the middle section.

Question 10: A Venn diagram has two circles:
Circle A: 'Has 4 straight sides'
Circle B: 'Has at least one pair of parallel lines'

Where should a regular pentagon be placed: 'Circle A only', 'Circle B only', 'Both circles', or 'Outside both circles'?
Answer: Outside both circles
Working: A regular pentagon has 5 sides (not 4 sides) and has no pairs of parallel lines. Therefore, it sits outside both circles.

Question 11: In a class of 28 children, a Venn diagram shows who attends Art club and Music club.
• 12 children attend Art club only.
• 8 children attend Music club only.
• 5 children attend both clubs.

How many children attend neither club (outside both circles)?
Answer: 3
Working: Total children in at least one club = 12 + 8 + 5 = 25. Total children = 28. Children outside both circles = 28 - 25 = 3.

Question 12: Sam sorts these numbers into a 2-set Venn diagram: 16, 21, 24, 30, 32, 35, 40.
Set X: Multiples of 8
Set Y: Multiples of 5

Which of these numbers belongs outside both sets?
Answer: 21
Working: Test the numbers: 16 (multiple of 8), 21 (not a multiple of 8 or 5), 24 (multiple of 8), 30 (multiple of 5), 32 (multiple of 8), 35 (multiple of 5), 40 (multiple of 8 and 5). 21 is the only number outside both sets.
