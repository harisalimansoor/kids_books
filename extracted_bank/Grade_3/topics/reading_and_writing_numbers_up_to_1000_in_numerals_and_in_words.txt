=== Topic: Reading and writing numbers up to 1000 in numerals and in words ===
Domain: Number and Place Value

--- Reading and writing numbers up to 1000 in numerals ---

Question 1: Write the number three hundred and forty-two in numerals.
Answer: 342
Working: Three hundred = 300, forty = 40, two = 2. 300 + 40 + 2 = 342.

Question 2: Write the number five hundred and eighty in numerals.
Answer: 580
Working: Five hundred = 500, eighty = 80, zero ones = 0. 500 + 80 = 580.

Question 3: Write the number seven hundred and six in numerals.
Answer: 706
Working: Seven hundred = 700, zero tens = 0, six = 6. 700 + 0 + 6 = 706.

Question 4: A village hall has nine hundred and fifteen chairs. Write the number of chairs in numerals.
Answer: 915
Working: Nine hundred = 900, fifteen = 15. 900 + 15 = 915.

Question 5: Write the number one thousand in numerals.
Answer: 1000
Working: One thousand is written as 1000.

Question 6: A number has 4 hundreds, 0 tens, and 8 ones. Write this number in numerals.
Answer: 408
Working: 4 hundreds = 400, 0 tens = 0, 8 ones = 8. 400 + 0 + 8 = 408.

Question 7: Write the number six hundred and seventy-four in numerals.
Answer: 674
Working: Six hundred = 600, seventy = 70, four = 4. 600 + 70 + 4 = 674.

Question 8: Sam writes: 'eight hundred and three = 830'. What is the correct numeral for eight hundred and three?
Answer: 803
Working: Eight hundred and three has 8 hundreds, 0 tens, and 3 ones, which is 803.

--- Reading and writing numbers up to 1000 in words ---

Question 9: Write 235 in words.
Answer: two hundred and thirty-five
Working: 2 hundreds = two hundred, 3 tens and 5 ones = thirty-five. Combined: two hundred and thirty-five.

Question 10: Write 600 in words.
Answer: six hundred
Working: 6 hundreds with 0 tens and 0 ones is written as six hundred.

Question 11: A school has 509 pupils. Write 509 in words.
Answer: five hundred and nine
Working: 5 hundreds = five hundred, 0 tens, 9 ones = nine. Combined: five hundred and nine.

Question 12: Write 780 in words.
Answer: seven hundred and eighty
Working: 7 hundreds = seven hundred, 8 tens = eighty, 0 ones. Combined: seven hundred and eighty.

Question 13: Write 417 in words.
Answer: four hundred and seventeen
Working: 4 hundreds = four hundred, 1 ten and 7 ones = seventeen. Combined: four hundred and seventeen.

Question 14: Write 1000 in words.
Answer: one thousand
Working: 1000 is written as one thousand.

Question 15: A runner has a race number made of 8 hundreds, 9 tens, and 1 one. Write this race number in words.
Answer: eight hundred and ninety-one
Working: 8 hundreds = 800, 9 tens = 90, 1 one = 1. The number is 891, which in words is eight hundred and ninety-one.

Question 16: Ella has 304 stickers. Write the number of stickers she has in words.
Answer: three hundred and four
Working: 304 is partitioned into 300 and 4. In words, this is three hundred and four.
