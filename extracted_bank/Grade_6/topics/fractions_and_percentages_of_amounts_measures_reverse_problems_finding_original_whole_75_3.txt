=== Topic: Fractions and percentages of amounts/measures; reverse problems finding original whole (75\% = £330 \implies total = £440); multi-stage remainder problems ===
Domain: Fractions, Decimals, Percentages

--- Fractions and percentages of amounts and measures ---

Question 1: Calculate 35% of 420 m.
Answer: 147 m
Working: 10% of 420 m = 42 m. 30% of 420 m = 42 × 3 = 126 m. 5% of 420 m = 42 ÷ 2 = 21 m. 35% = 126 m + 21 m = 147 m.

Question 2: Calculate 3/8 of 640 kg.
Answer: 240 kg
Working: 1/8 of 640 kg = 640 ÷ 8 = 80 kg. 3/8 of 640 kg = 80 × 3 = 240 kg.

Question 3: Find 65% of £480.
Answer: £312
Working: 50% of £480 = £240. 10% of £480 = £48. 5% of £480 = £24. 65% = £240 + £48 + £24 = £312.

Question 4: Calculate 3/5 of 2.5 litres. Give your answer in millilitres.
Answer: 1500 ml
Working: 2.5 litres = 2500 ml. 1/5 of 2500 ml = 2500 ÷ 5 = 500 ml. 3/5 of 2500 ml = 500 × 3 = 1500 ml.

--- Reverse problems finding the original whole ---

Question 5: In a sale, all prices are reduced by 25%. The sale price of a coat is £330. What was the original price of the coat?
Answer: £440
Working: The sale price represents 100% - 25% = 75% of the original price. 75% = £330. 25% = £330 ÷ 3 = £110. Original price (100%) = £110 × 4 = £440.

Question 6: Maya cuts a piece of ribbon. She uses 3/8 of the ribbon, which measures 45 cm. What was the total length of the original ribbon?
Answer: 120 cm
Working: 3/8 of the total length = 45 cm. 1/8 of the total length = 45 ÷ 3 = 15 cm. Total length (8/8) = 15 × 8 = 120 cm.

Question 7: A television is reduced by 30% in a sale. The sale price is £350. What was the normal price before the sale?
Answer: £500
Working: The sale price is 100% - 30% = 70% of the normal price. 70% = £350. 10% = £350 ÷ 7 = £50. 100% = £50 × 10 = £500.

Question 8: After using 60% of the water in a water butt, 28 litres of water remain. How many litres did the water butt hold when completely full?
Answer: 70 litres
Working: The remaining water represents 100% - 60% = 40% of the total capacity. 40% = 28 litres. 10% = 28 ÷ 4 = 7 litres. 100% = 7 × 10 = 70 litres.

Question 9: In a sale, a bicycle is reduced by 15%. The sale price is £68. What was the price of the bicycle before the sale?
Answer: £80
Working: The sale price is 100% - 15% = 85% of the original price. 85% = £68. 1% = £68 ÷ 85 = £0.80. 100% = £0.80 × 100 = £80.

--- Multi-stage remainder problems ---

Question 10: A fruit box contains 800 g of mixed berries. 25% of the mass is strawberries, 35% is cherries, and the remainder is raspberries. What is the mass of the raspberries?
Answer: 320 g
Working: Percentage of strawberries and cherries combined = 25% + 35% = 60%. Percentage of raspberries = 100% - 60% = 40%. Mass of raspberries = 40% of 800 g = 4 × 80 g = 320 g.

Question 11: A bakery bakes 150 loaves of bread. In the morning, they sell 2/5 of the loaves. In the afternoon, they sell 50% of the remaining loaves. How many loaves of bread are left at the end of the day?
Answer: 45
Working: Morning sales: 2/5 of 150 = (150 ÷ 5) × 2 = 60 loaves. Loaves remaining = 150 - 60 = 90 loaves. Afternoon sales: 50% of 90 = 45 loaves. Loaves left at the end of the day = 90 - 45 = 45.

Question 12: Jack has £240. He spends 1/4 of his money on a coat. He then spends 60% of the remaining money on a bicycle helmet. How much money does Jack have left?
Answer: £72
Working: Coat cost: 1/4 of £240 = £60. Money remaining = £240 - £60 = £180. Helmet cost: 60% of £180 = 6 × £18 = £108. Money left = £180 - £108 = £72.

Question 13: A storage tank holds 600 litres of water. On Monday, 1/3 of the water is used. On Tuesday, 25% of the remaining water is used. How many litres of water are left in the tank?
Answer: 300 litres
Working: Monday water used: 1/3 of 600 = 200 litres. Water remaining = 600 - 200 = 400 litres. Tuesday water used: 25% of 400 = 1/4 of 400 = 100 litres. Water left = 400 - 100 = 300 litres.

Question 14: Amira has a 400-page book. On Friday, she reads 30% of the book. On Saturday, she reads 3/7 of the remaining pages. How many pages does Amira have left to read?
Answer: 160 pages
Working: Friday: 30% of 400 = 3 × 40 = 120 pages. Pages remaining = 400 - 120 = 280 pages. Saturday: 3/7 of 280 = (280 ÷ 7) × 3 = 40 × 3 = 120 pages. Pages left to read = 280 - 120 = 160 pages.
