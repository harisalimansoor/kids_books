Domain: Number & Place Value

--- Number line interval scaling ---

Question 1: A number line starts at 0 and ends at 10 000. There are 4 equal intervals between 0 and 10 000. What is the value of each interval?
Answer: 2500
Working: Divide the total distance by the number of intervals: 10 000 ÷ 4 = 2500.

Question 2: A number line starts at 200 000 and ends at 300 000. The distance between these two numbers is divided into 5 equal intervals. What number is at the third tick mark after 200 000?
Answer: 260 000
Working: Total distance = 300 000 - 200 000 = 100 000. Value of one interval = 100 000 ÷ 5 = 20 000. Third tick mark = 200 000 + (3 × 20 000) = 200 000 + 60 000 = 260 000.

Question 3: A number line starts at 1 000 000 and ends at 5 000 000. It is divided into 8 equal intervals by tick marks. What is the value of each interval?
Answer: 500 000
Working: Total range = 5 000 000 - 1 000 000 = 4 000 000. Value of each interval = 4 000 000 ÷ 8 = 500 000.

--- Lettered placement ---

Question 4: A number line starts at 0 and ends at 1 000 000 with 10 equal intervals. Point A is placed at the 3rd tick mark and Point B is placed at the 8th tick mark. What is the value of B - A?
Answer: 500 000
Working: Each interval = 1 000 000 ÷ 10 = 100 000. Point A = 3 × 100 000 = 300 000. Point B = 8 × 100 000 = 800 000. B - A = 800 000 - 300 000 = 500 000.

Question 5: On a number line from 4 500 000 to 4 600 000, there are 4 equal intervals. Letter P is located at the first tick mark after 4 500 000. What number does P represent?
Answer: 4 525 000
Working: Total distance = 4 600 000 - 4 500 000 = 100 000. Interval value = 100 000 ÷ 4 = 25 000. P = 4 500 000 + 25 000 = 4 525 000.

Question 6: On a number line, letter X represents 120 000 and letter Y represents 280 000. Letter M is positioned exactly halfway between X and Y. What number does letter M represent?
Answer: 200 000
Working: Midpoint M = (120 000 + 280 000) ÷ 2 = 400 000 ÷ 2 = 200 000.

--- Chained inequalities ---

Question 7: Look at the inequality chain: 738 450 < 73[_] 200 < 740 000. What single digit must replace [_] to make the statement true?
Answer: 9
Working: If [_] is 8, 738 200 is less than 738 450, which is false. If [_] is 9, 738 450 < 739 200 < 740 000, which is true. Therefore, the digit is 9.

Question 8: Find the single digit that makes this chained inequality correct: 2 458 000 < 2 4[_]2 000 < 2 470 000.
Answer: 6
Working: If [_] is 5, 2 452 000 < 2 458 000 (false). If [_] is 7, 2 472 000 > 2 470 000 (false). If [_] is 6, 2 458 000 < 2 462 000 < 2 470 000 (true). The digit is 6.

Question 9: What single digit must replace [_] in the chained inequality: 546 180 < 546 1[_]5 < 546 192?
Answer: 8
Working: Comparing the tens digits: 546 180 < 546 185 < 546 192 is true when the missing digit is 8. If the digit were 9, 546 195 > 546 192 (false). If 7, 546 175 < 546 180 (false). Thus, the digit is 8.
