=== Topic: Rounding to powers of 10 and determining upper/lower rounding bounds ===
Domain: Number & Place Value

--- Rounding to powers of 10 ---

Question 1: Round 438,274 to the nearest 1,000.
Answer: 438,000
Working: The digit in the hundreds place is 2, which is less than 5, so round down to 438,000.

Question 2: Round 3,682,519 to the nearest 10,000.
Answer: 3,680,000
Working: The digit in the thousands place is 2, which is less than 5, so round down to 3,680,000.

Question 3: Round 7,495,300 to the nearest 100,000.
Answer: 7,500,000
Working: The digit in the ten thousands place is 9, which is 5 or more, so round up to 7,500,000.

Question 4: Round 5,500,000 to the nearest 1,000,000.
Answer: 6,000,000
Working: The digit in the hundred thousands place is 5, which rounds up to 6,000,000.

Question 5: A stadium has an exact attendance of 68,495 spectators. What is the attendance rounded to the nearest 100?
Answer: 68,500
Working: The digit in the tens place is 9, which is 5 or more, so round up 495 to 500, giving 68,500.

Question 6: Round 9,996,400 to the nearest 10,000.
Answer: 10,000,000
Working: The digit in the thousands place is 6, which rounds up. Adding 1 to the ten thousands column rolls over the digits to give 10,000,000.

Question 7: A city has a population of 2,499,620. What is this population rounded to the nearest 1,000?
Answer: 2,500,000
Working: The digit in the hundreds place is 6, so round up to the next thousand. 2,499,000 + 1,000 = 2,500,000.

--- Determining upper and lower rounding bounds ---

Question 8: What is the smallest whole number that rounds to 4,000 when rounded to the nearest 1,000?
Answer: 3,500
Working: The lower bound for rounding to the nearest 1,000 is 4,000 - 500 = 3,500.

Question 9: What is the largest whole number that rounds to 4,000 when rounded to the nearest 1,000?
Answer: 4,499
Working: Any whole number up to 4,499 has a hundreds digit of 4 or less and rounds down to 4,000.

Question 10: What is the smallest whole number that rounds to 60,000 when rounded to the nearest 10,000?
Answer: 55,000
Working: The lower bound for rounding to the nearest 10,000 is 60,000 - 5,000 = 55,000.

Question 11: What is the largest whole number that rounds to 60,000 when rounded to the nearest 10,000?
Answer: 64,999
Working: The upper bound for whole numbers rounding to 60,000 to the nearest 10,000 is 60,000 + 5,000 - 1 = 64,999.

Question 12: A whole number is rounded to the nearest 100,000 to give 450,000. Is this possible? Write Yes or No.
Answer: No
Working: Multiples of 100,000 end in five zeros (such as 400,000 or 500,000), so 450,000 cannot be a multiple of 100,000.

Question 13: When rounded to the nearest 10,000, a town's population is 450,000. What is the smallest whole number the population could be?
Answer: 445,000
Working: 450,000 - (10,000 ÷ 2) = 450,000 - 5,000 = 445,000.

Question 14: When rounded to the nearest 10,000, a town's population is 450,000. What is the largest whole number the population could be?
Answer: 454,999
Working: 450,000 + 5,000 - 1 = 454,999.

Question 15: What is the smallest whole number that rounds to 3,000,000 when rounded to the nearest 1,000,000?
Answer: 2,500,000
Working: 3,000,000 - 500,000 = 2,500,000.

Question 16: What is the largest whole number that rounds to 3,000,000 when rounded to the nearest 1,000,000?
Answer: 3,499,999
Working: 3,000,000 + 500,000 - 1 = 3,499,999.

Question 17: A whole number rounds to 800 when rounded to the nearest 100, and rounds to 750 when rounded to the nearest 10. What is the smallest possible value of this number?
Answer: 750
Working: Rounding to 800 (nearest 100) requires a value from 750 to 849. Rounding to 750 (nearest 10) requires a value from 745 to 754. The overlap is 750 to 754, so the smallest whole number is 750.

Question 18: A whole number rounds to 800 when rounded to the nearest 100, and rounds to 750 when rounded to the nearest 10. What is the largest possible value of this number?
Answer: 754
Working: Rounding to 800 to the nearest 100 means the number is between 750 and 849. Rounding to 750 to the nearest 10 means the number is between 745 and 754. The largest whole number satisfying both conditions is 754.
