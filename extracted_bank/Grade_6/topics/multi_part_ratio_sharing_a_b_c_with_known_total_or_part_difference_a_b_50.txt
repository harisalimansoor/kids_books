=== Topic: Multi-part ratio sharing (a:b:c) with known total or part difference (a - b = 50) ===
Domain: Ratio and Proportion

--- Multi-part ratio sharing with a known total ---

Question 1: Jack, Maya and Noah share 48 stickers in the ratio 1 : 2 : 3. How many stickers does Noah receive?
Answer: 24
Working: 1 + 2 + 3 = 6 equal parts. 48 ÷ 6 = 8 stickers per part. Noah receives 3 parts: 3 × 8 = 24 stickers.

Question 2: A ribbon of length 140 cm is cut into three pieces in the ratio 2 : 3 : 5. What is the length of the longest piece?
Answer: 70 cm
Working: Total parts = 2 + 3 + 5 = 10 parts. 140 ÷ 10 = 14 cm per part. The longest piece has 5 parts: 5 × 14 cm = 70 cm.

Question 3: A total of £360 is shared between three charities in the ratio 3 : 4 : 5. How much money does the charity with the smallest share receive?
Answer: £90
Working: Total parts = 3 + 4 + 5 = 12 parts. £360 ÷ 12 = £30 per part. The smallest share has 3 parts: 3 × £30 = £90.

Question 4: A baker mixes flour, sugar and butter in the ratio 5 : 3 : 2 by mass to make shortbread. The total mass of the mixture is 1,200 g. How many more grams of flour than butter are used?
Answer: 360 g
Working: Total parts = 5 + 3 + 2 = 10 parts. 1,200 g ÷ 10 = 120 g per part. Flour = 5 × 120 = 600 g. Butter = 2 × 120 = 240 g. Difference = 600 g - 240 g = 360 g.

Question 5: A school library has 720 books in total, split into fiction, non-fiction and poetry in the ratio 5 : 3 : 1. How many non-fiction books are in the library?
Answer: 240
Working: Total parts = 5 + 3 + 1 = 9 parts. 720 ÷ 9 = 80 books per part. Non-fiction books = 3 parts: 3 × 80 = 240.

Question 6: Red, blue and green paint are mixed in the ratio 2 : 5 : 3 to make 4.5 litres of paint. How many millilitres of blue paint are used?
Answer: 2,250 ml
Working: 4.5 litres = 4,500 ml. Total parts = 2 + 5 + 3 = 10 parts. 4,500 ml ÷ 10 = 450 ml per part. Blue paint = 5 parts: 5 × 450 ml = 2,250 ml.

--- Multi-part ratio sharing with a part difference ---

Question 7: Red, yellow and green counters in a box are in the ratio 2 : 3 : 7. There are 25 more green counters than red counters. How many counters are in the box in total?
Answer: 60
Working: Difference between green and red parts = 7 - 2 = 5 parts. 5 parts = 25 counters, so 1 part = 25 ÷ 5 = 5 counters. Total parts = 2 + 3 + 7 = 12 parts. Total counters = 12 × 5 = 60.

Question 8: Liam, Olivia and Ethan share some money in the ratio 3 : 5 : 8. Ethan receives £45 more than Liam. How much money does Olivia receive?
Answer: £45
Working: Difference between Ethan and Liam in parts = 8 - 3 = 5 parts. 5 parts = £45, so 1 part = £45 ÷ 5 = £9. Olivia receives 5 parts: 5 × £9 = £45.

Question 9: The ratio of small, medium and large apples in a crate is 4 : 5 : 9. There are 28 more large apples than medium apples. How many small apples are in the crate?
Answer: 28
Working: Difference between large and medium parts = 9 - 5 = 4 parts. 4 parts = 28 apples, so 1 part = 28 ÷ 4 = 7 apples. Small apples = 4 parts: 4 × 7 = 28.

Question 10: In a wildlife park, the ratio of meerkats to otters to penguins is 6 : 4 : 9. There are 30 more penguins than meerkats. What is the total number of meerkats, otters and penguins in the park?
Answer: 190
Working: Difference between penguins and meerkats in parts = 9 - 6 = 3 parts. 3 parts = 30 animals, so 1 part = 30 ÷ 3 = 10 animals. Total parts = 6 + 4 + 9 = 19 parts. Total animals = 19 × 10 = 190.

Question 11: Three runners, Ava, Ben and Chloe, run distances in the ratio 3 : 7 : 10. Ben runs 2.4 km further than Ava. What is the total distance run by all three runners in kilometres?
Answer: 12 km
Working: Difference between Ben and Ava in parts = 7 - 3 = 4 parts. 4 parts = 2.4 km, so 1 part = 2.4 ÷ 4 = 0.6 km. Total parts = 3 + 7 + 10 = 20 parts. Total distance = 20 × 0.6 km = 12 km.

Question 12: A gardener mixes sand, compost and topsoil in the ratio 2 : 5 : 8. The gardener uses 45 kg more topsoil than compost. How many kilograms of sand are used?
Answer: 30 kg
Working: Difference between topsoil and compost in parts = 8 - 5 = 3 parts. 3 parts = 45 kg, so 1 part = 45 ÷ 3 = 15 kg. Sand = 2 parts: 2 × 15 kg = 30 kg.
