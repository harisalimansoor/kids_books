Domain: Fractions, Decimals, Percentages

--- Multiplying pairs of proper fractions via 2D grids ---

Question 1: Calculate 1/3 × 1/4. Give your answer in its simplest form.
Answer: 1/12
Working: Multiply the numerators: 1 × 1 = 1. Multiply the denominators: 3 × 4 = 12. So 1/3 × 1/4 = 1/12.

Question 2: Calculate 1/2 × 3/5. Give your answer in its simplest form.
Answer: 3/10
Working: Multiply the numerators: 1 × 3 = 3. Multiply the denominators: 2 × 5 = 10. The result is 3/10.

Question 3: Calculate 2/3 × 3/4. Give your answer in its simplest form.
Answer: 1/2
Working: Multiply the numerators: 2 × 3 = 6. Multiply the denominators: 3 × 4 = 12. Simplify 6/12 by dividing the numerator and denominator by 6 to get 1/2.

Question 4: A large square is split into a grid. Vertically, 3 out of 5 equal columns are shaded. Horizontally, 2 out of 3 equal rows are shaded. What fraction of the whole square is shaded by both?
Answer: 2/5
Working: The overlapping fraction is 3/5 × 2/3. Multiply numerators: 3 × 2 = 6. Multiply denominators: 5 × 3 = 15. Simplify 6/15 by dividing by 3 to get 2/5.

Question 5: Calculate 4/7 × 5/8. Give your answer in its simplest form.
Answer: 5/14
Working: Multiply the numerators: 4 × 5 = 20. Multiply the denominators: 7 × 8 = 56. Simplify 20/56 by dividing numerator and denominator by 4 to get 5/14.

Question 6: A vegetable patch takes up 4/9 of a rectangular garden. Carrots are planted on 3/8 of the vegetable patch. What fraction of the whole garden is planted with carrots? Give your answer in its simplest form.
Answer: 1/6
Working: Find 3/8 of 4/9 by calculating 4/9 × 3/8. Multiply numerators: 4 × 3 = 12. Multiply denominators: 9 × 8 = 72. Simplify 12/72 by dividing both by 12 to get 1/6.

Question 7: In a 2D fraction grid representing a unit square, the square is divided into 6 equal vertical columns and 7 equal horizontal rows, making 42 small rectangles in total. If 5 columns and 4 rows overlap, what fraction of the square is in the overlap? Give your answer in its simplest form.
Answer: 10/21
Working: The overlap represents 5/6 × 4/7. Multiply numerators: 5 × 4 = 20. Multiply denominators: 6 × 7 = 42. Simplify 20/42 by dividing by 2 to get 10/21.

--- Dividing proper fractions by integers ---

Question 8: Calculate 1/3 ÷ 2. Give your answer as a proper fraction.
Answer: 1/6
Working: Dividing 1/3 into 2 equal parts gives 1/(3 × 2) = 1/6.

Question 9: Calculate 4/5 ÷ 2. Give your answer in its simplest form.
Answer: 2/5
Working: Divide the numerator by the integer: 4 ÷ 2 = 2. The denominator stays 5. So 4/5 ÷ 2 = 2/5.

Question 10: Calculate 3/5 ÷ 4. Give your answer as a proper fraction in its simplest form.
Answer: 3/20
Working: Multiply the denominator by the whole number: 5 × 4 = 20. The numerator remains 3. So 3/5 ÷ 4 = 3/20.

Question 11: A jug contains 6/7 of a litre of juice. The juice is poured equally into 3 glasses. What fraction of a litre is in each glass? Give your answer in its simplest form.
Answer: 2/7
Working: Calculate 6/7 ÷ 3. Divide the numerator by 3: 6 ÷ 3 = 2. So each glass contains 2/7 of a litre.

Question 12: Calculate 4/9 ÷ 6. Give your answer in its simplest form.
Answer: 2/27
Working: 4/9 ÷ 6 = 4/(9 × 6) = 4/54. Simplify 4/54 by dividing the numerator and denominator by 2 to get 2/27.

Question 13: A ribbon of length 5/8 of a metre is cut into 4 equal shorter pieces. What is the length of one piece in metres?
Answer: 5/32 m
Working: Calculate 5/8 ÷ 4. Multiply the denominator by 4: 8 × 4 = 32. The length of one piece is 5/32 m.

Question 14: A piece of wood has a mass of 8/11 kg. It is cut into 6 equal smaller blocks. What is the mass of one block in kilograms? Give your answer in its simplest form.
Answer: 4/33 kg
Working: Calculate 8/11 ÷ 6 = 8/(11 × 6) = 8/66. Simplify 8/66 by dividing numerator and denominator by 2 to get 4/33 kg.
