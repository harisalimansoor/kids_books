=== Topic: Long multiplication (4-digit x 2-digit) and short multiplication ===
Domain: Addition, Subtraction, Multiplication, Division & Operations

--- Long multiplication ---

Question 1: Calculate 2,345 × 16.
Answer: 37,520
Working: Multiply 2,345 by 6 = 14,070. Multiply 2,345 by 10 = 23,450. Add partial products: 14,070 + 23,450 = 37,520.

Question 2: Calculate 4,183 × 24.
Answer: 100,392
Working: Multiply 4,183 by 4 = 16,732. Multiply 4,183 by 20 = 83,660. Add partial products: 16,732 + 83,660 = 100,392.

Question 3: A concert hall sells 1,248 tickets for £35 each. What is the total amount of money raised from ticket sales?
Answer: £43,680
Working: Multiply 1,248 by 35: 1,248 × 5 = 6,240; 1,248 × 30 = 37,440. Total = 6,240 + 37,440 = £43,680.

Question 4: Calculate 3,509 × 47.
Answer: 164,923
Working: Multiply 3,509 by 7 = 24,563. Multiply 3,509 by 40 = 140,360. Add partial products: 24,563 + 140,360 = 164,923.

Question 5: A warehouse packs 2,150 boxes of cereal onto each lorry. How many boxes of cereal are loaded onto 28 lorries?
Answer: 60,200
Working: Multiply 2,150 by 28: 2,150 × 8 = 17,200; 2,150 × 20 = 43,000. Total = 17,200 + 43,000 = 60,200 boxes.

Question 6: A rectangular solar farm is 1,125 metres long and 42 metres wide. Calculate the total area of the solar farm in square metres.
Answer: 47,250
Working: Area = length × width = 1,125 × 42. 1,125 × 2 = 2,250; 1,125 × 40 = 45,000. Total area = 2,250 + 45,000 = 47,250 square metres.

Question 7: Given that 324 × 18 = 5,832, find the value of 324 × 19.
Answer: 6,156
Working: 324 × 19 = (324 × 18) + 324 = 5,832 + 324 = 6,156.

Question 8: Calculate 5,614 × 38.
Answer: 213,332
Working: Multiply 5,614 by 8 = 44,912. Multiply 5,614 by 30 = 168,420. Add partial products: 44,912 + 168,420 = 213,332.

Question 9: A train makes 24 journeys each day. On each journey, it carries 1,450 passengers. How many passengers does the train carry in total over 7 days?
Answer: 243,600
Working: Passengers per day = 1,450 × 24. 1,450 × 4 = 5,800; 1,450 × 20 = 29,000; total per day = 34,800. For 7 days: 34,800 × 7 = 243,600 passengers.

--- Short multiplication ---

Question 10: Calculate 2,483 × 6.
Answer: 14,898
Working: Multiply using short multiplication: 3 × 6 = 18 (write 8, carry 1 ten); 8 × 6 = 48, plus 1 = 49 (write 9, carry 4 hundreds); 4 × 6 = 24, plus 4 = 28 (write 8, carry 2 thousands); 2 × 6 = 12, plus 2 = 14. Total = 14,898.

Question 11: Calculate 3,074 × 9.
Answer: 27,666
Working: Multiply each digit by 9: 4 × 9 = 36 (write 6, carry 3); 7 × 9 = 63, plus 3 = 66 (write 6, carry 6); 0 × 9 = 0, plus 6 = 6; 3 × 9 = 27. Total = 27,666.

Question 12: A school buys 7 tablets costing £465 each. What is the total cost of the tablets?
Answer: £3,255
Working: Multiply £465 by 7: 5 × 7 = 35 (carry 3); 6 × 7 = 42, plus 3 = 45 (carry 4); 4 × 7 = 28, plus 4 = 32. The total cost is £3,255.

Question 13: A bakery bakes 1,358 loaves of bread each day. How many loaves does it bake in 8 days?
Answer: 10,864
Working: Multiply 1,358 by 8: 8 × 8 = 64 (carry 6); 5 × 8 = 40, plus 6 = 46 (carry 4); 3 × 8 = 24, plus 4 = 28 (carry 2); 1 × 8 = 8, plus 2 = 10. Total = 10,864.

Question 14: In the calculation 4,[ ]36 × 7 = 31,752, find the missing hundreds digit.
Answer: 5
Working: Divide 31,752 by 7: 31,752 ÷ 7 = 4,536. Comparing 4,536 to 4,[ ]36 gives the missing digit as 5.

Question 15: A cinema has 8 rows with 145 seats in the main auditorium and 6 rows with 98 seats in the balcony. What is the total number of seats in the cinema?
Answer: 1,748
Working: Main auditorium seats: 145 × 8 = 1,160. Balcony seats: 98 × 6 = 588. Total seats = 1,160 + 588 = 1,748.

Question 16: Given that 426 × 8 = 3,408, work out 426 × 9 without using long multiplication.
Answer: 3,834
Working: 426 × 9 = (426 × 8) + 426 = 3,408 + 426 = 3,834.
