Domain: Addition, Subtraction, Multiplication, Division & Operations

--- Mental deduction from known products ---

Question 1: Given that 48 × 6 = 288, use mental deduction to calculate 148 × 6.
Answer: 888
Working: 148 × 6 = (100 × 6) + (48 × 6) = 600 + 288 = 888.

Question 2: Given that 30 × 30 = 900, use mental deduction to find the value of 29 × 30.
Answer: 870
Working: 29 × 30 = (30 × 30) - (1 × 30) = 900 - 30 = 870.

Question 3: Given that 56 × 8 = 448, use mental deduction to find the value of 56 × 9.
Answer: 504
Working: 56 × 9 = (56 × 8) + 56 = 448 + 56 = 504.

Question 4: Given that 40 × 40 = 1,600, use mental deduction to calculate 41 × 40.
Answer: 1,640
Working: 41 × 40 = (40 × 40) + (1 × 40) = 1,600 + 40 = 1,640.

Question 5: Given that 73 × 5 = 365, use mental deduction to calculate 273 × 5.
Answer: 1,365
Working: 273 × 5 = (200 × 5) + (73 × 5) = 1,000 + 365 = 1,365.

Question 6: Given that 25 × 18 = 450, use mental deduction to find the value of 25 × 17.
Answer: 425
Working: 25 × 17 = (25 × 18) - 25 = 450 - 25 = 425.

--- Functional step equivalence ---

Question 7: A number is multiplied by 6 and then divided by 3. What single multiplication operation is equivalent to these two steps?
Answer: × 2
Working: Multiplying by 6 and then dividing by 3 is equivalent to multiplying by (6 ÷ 3) = 2. So the single operation is × 2.

Question 8: A function machine multiplies an input number by 12 and then divides the result by 4. What single multiplication operation gives the same output?
Answer: × 3
Working: 12 ÷ 4 = 3, so multiplying by 12 and then dividing by 4 is equivalent to multiplying by 3 (× 3).

Question 9: A number is divided by 5 and then multiplied by 20. What single multiplication operation is equivalent to these two steps?
Answer: × 4
Working: Dividing by 5 and then multiplying by 20 is equivalent to multiplying by (20 ÷ 5) = 4 (× 4).

Question 10: A counter starts at 15. The counter is multiplied by 18 and then divided by 6. Using a single equivalent operation, calculate the final value of the counter.
Answer: 45
Working: Multiplying by 18 and dividing by 6 is equivalent to multiplying by (18 ÷ 6) = 3. 15 × 3 = 45.

--- Units digit prediction ---

Question 12: What is the ones digit of the answer to 453 × 289?
Answer: 7

Question 13: Without working out the full calculation, find the units digit of 64 × 38 + 15.
Answer: 7
Working: The units digit of 64 × 38 is 4 × 8 = 32, which ends in 2. Adding the units digit of 15 gives 2 + 5 = 7.

Question 14: Without working out the full calculation, find the ones digit of 76 × 49 - 23.
Answer: 1
Working: The ones digit of 76 × 49 is given by 6 × 9 = 54, which ends in 4. Subtracting 3 from 4 gives 1.
