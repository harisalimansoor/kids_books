=== Topic: Decimals to 3 d.p.; multiplying/dividing by 10, 100, 1000; column arithmetic; formal division with decimal quotients (up to 2–3 d.p.) ===
Domain: Fractions, Decimals, Percentages

--- Decimals to 3 decimal places ---

Question 1: What is the value of the digit 7 in the number 4.375?
Answer: 7 hundredths (or 0.07)
Working: The digit 4 is in the ones place, 3 is in the tenths place, 7 is in the hundredths place, and 5 is in the thousandths place. So the value of 7 is 7 hundredths or 0.07.

Question 2: Write the number that is made of 5 ones, 0 tenths, 8 hundredths and 4 thousandths as a decimal.
Answer: 5.084
Working: 5 ones = 5, 0 tenths = 0.0, 8 hundredths = 0.08, 4 thousandths = 0.004. Combining these gives 5.084.

Question 3: Order these decimal numbers from smallest to largest: 0.405, 0.45, 0.045, 0.504.
Answer: 0.045, 0.405, 0.45, 0.504
Working: Comparing by place value: 0.045 has 0 tenths (smallest). 0.405 and 0.450 have 4 tenths; 0.405 has 0 hundredths, 0.450 has 5 hundredths. 0.504 has 5 tenths (largest).

Question 4: Round 6.378 to the nearest hundredth (2 decimal places).
Answer: 6.38
Working: The hundredths digit is 7. The following thousandths digit is 8. Since 8 is 5 or more, round up 7 to 8, giving 6.38.

Question 5: Find the number that lies halfway between 3.48 and 3.49.
Answer: 3.485
Working: Write both numbers to 3 decimal places: 3.480 and 3.490. Halfway between 3.480 and 3.490 is 3.485.

--- Multiplying and dividing by 10, 100 and 1000 ---

Question 6: Calculate 0.38 × 100.
Answer: 38
Working: Multiplying by 100 shifts the digits two places to the left: 0.38 × 100 = 38.

Question 7: Calculate 4.075 × 1000.
Answer: 4075
Working: Multiplying by 1000 shifts the digits three places to the left: 4.075 × 1000 = 4075.

Question 8: Calculate 62.4 ÷ 100.
Answer: 0.624
Working: Dividing by 100 shifts the digits two places to the right: 62.4 ÷ 100 = 0.624.

Question 9: What is 7 ÷ 1000 as a decimal?
Answer: 0.007
Working: Dividing 7 by 1000 shifts the digit 7 three places to the right into the thousandths column: 7 ÷ 1000 = 0.007.

Question 10: Find the missing number: 0.054 × _______ = 54.
Answer: 1000
Working: To move the digits three places to the left from 0.054 to 54, multiply by 1000.

Question 11: A stack of 100 identical sheets of card is 3.5 cm tall. What is the thickness of one sheet of card in centimetres?
Answer: 0.035 cm (or 0.035)
Working: Divide the total thickness by 100: 3.5 ÷ 100 = 0.035 cm.

--- Column arithmetic with decimals ---

Question 12: Calculate 14.62 + 8.75 using a written method.
Answer: 23.37
Working: Align the decimal points: 2 + 5 = 7 (hundredths), 6 + 7 = 13 (regroup 1 to ones), 4 + 8 + 1 = 13 (regroup 1 to tens), 1 + 1 = 2. Total = 23.37.

Question 13: Calculate 9 - 3.468.
Answer: 5.532
Working: Align 9.000 - 3.468: regroup across zeros to get 8.99(10) - 3.468. 10 - 8 = 2, 9 - 6 = 3, 9 - 4 = 5, 8 - 3 = 5. Result is 5.532.

Question 14: Calculate 6.28 × 7.
Answer: 43.96
Working: Multiply 628 by 7: 600 × 7 = 4200, 20 × 7 = 140, 8 × 7 = 56. 4200 + 140 + 56 = 4396. Place the decimal point two places from the right: 43.96.

Question 15: A parcel weighs 1.485 kg. A second parcel weighs 2.75 kg, and a third parcel weighs 0.89 kg. What is the total mass of the three parcels in kilograms?
Answer: 5.125 kg (or 5.125)
Working: Align the decimal points: 1.485 + 2.750 + 0.890. Thousandths: 5 + 0 + 0 = 5. Hundredths: 8 + 5 + 9 = 22 (regroup 2). Tenths: 4 + 7 + 8 + 2 = 21 (regroup 2). Ones: 1 + 2 + 0 + 2 = 5. Total = 5.125 kg.

--- Formal division with decimal quotients (up to 2–3 d.p.) ---

Question 16: Calculate 37 ÷ 4, giving your answer as a decimal.
Answer: 9.25
Working: Set up 37.00 ÷ 4. 4 into 37 goes 9 remainder 1. 4 into 10 goes 2 remainder 2. 4 into 20 goes 5. 37 ÷ 4 = 9.25.

Question 17: Use a formal written method of division to calculate 19.35 ÷ 3.
Answer: 6.45
Working: 19.35 ÷ 3: 3 into 19 is 6 remainder 1. Place the decimal point. 3 into 13 is 4 remainder 1. 3 into 15 is 5. Quotient = 6.45.

Question 18: Calculate 15 ÷ 8, giving your answer as a decimal.
Answer: 1.875
Working: Set up 15.000 ÷ 8. 8 into 15 goes 1 remainder 7. 8 into 70 goes 8 remainder 6. 8 into 60 goes 7 remainder 4. 8 into 40 goes 5. 15 ÷ 8 = 1.875.

Question 19: A ribbon of length 9 metres is cut into 8 equal pieces. What is the length of one piece in metres?
Answer: 1.125 m (or 1.125)
Working: Divide 9 by 8: 9.000 ÷ 8 = 1.125 m.

Question 20: Calculate 93 ÷ 12, giving your answer as a decimal.
Answer: 7.75
Working: 12 into 93 goes 7 times (7 × 12 = 84) with remainder 9. Place decimal point and add zero: 12 into 90 goes 7 times (7 × 12 = 84) remainder 6. 12 into 60 goes 5 times. 93 ÷ 12 = 7.75.
