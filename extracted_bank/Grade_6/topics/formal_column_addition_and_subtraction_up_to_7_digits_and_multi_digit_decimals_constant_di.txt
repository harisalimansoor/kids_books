Domain: Addition, Subtraction, Multiplication, Division & Operations

--- Formal column addition and subtraction up to 7 digits ---

Question 1: Calculate 3,425,180 + 1,253,604.
Answer: 4,678,784
Working: Align the digits in place value columns and add from right to left: 0 + 4 = 4; 8 + 0 = 8; 1 + 6 = 7; 5 + 3 = 8; 2 + 5 = 7; 4 + 2 = 6; 3 + 1 = 4. The total is 4,678,784.

Question 2: Calculate 5,834,291 - 2,461,835.
Answer: 3,372,456
Working: Align in columns and subtract with regrouping: 1 - 5 requires regrouping: 11 - 5 = 6; 8 - 3 = 5; 2 - 8 requires regrouping: 12 - 8 = 4; 3 - 1 = 2; 3 - 6 requires regrouping: 13 - 6 = 7; 7 - 4 = 3; 5 - 2 = 3. The difference is 3,372,456.

Question 3: In one year, an airport recorded 4,879,350 arriving passengers and 4,642,875 departing passengers. What was the total number of passengers recorded that year?
Answer: 9,522,225
Working: 4,879,350 + 4,642,875: 0 + 5 = 5; 5 + 7 = 12 (write 2, carry 1); 3 + 8 + 1 = 12 (write 2, carry 1); 9 + 2 + 1 = 12 (write 2, carry 1); 7 + 4 + 1 = 12 (write 2, carry 1); 8 + 6 + 1 = 15 (write 5, carry 1); 4 + 4 + 1 = 9. The total is 9,522,225.

Question 4: A city had a population of 2,105,040 at the start of a decade. Over ten years, the population increased by 386,975 and then decreased by 149,820. What was the population at the end of the decade?
Answer: 2,342,195
Working: First, add the increase: 2,105,040 + 386,975 = 2,492,015. Next, subtract the decrease: 2,492,015 - 149,820 = 2,342,195.

Question 5: In this column addition, find the missing digit represented by the letter A: 4,3A6,512 + 1,472,835 = 5,859,347.
Answer: 8
Working: Look at the thousands and ten-thousands columns: 6,512 + 2,835 = 9,347 (no carry into the ten-thousands column). In the ten-thousands column, A + 7 = 15 (5 ten-thousands and 1 carried to the hundred-thousands column). A = 15 - 7 = 8. Check: 4,386,512 + 1,472,835 = 5,859,347.

--- Column addition and subtraction with multi-digit decimals ---

Question 6: Calculate 46.38 + 27.85.
Answer: 74.23
Working: Align the decimal points: 8 + 5 = 13 (write 3, carry 1); 3 + 8 + 1 = 12 (write 2, carry 1); 6 + 7 + 1 = 14 (write 4, carry 1); 4 + 2 + 1 = 7. The answer is 74.23.

Question 7: Calculate 83.4 - 29.675.
Answer: 53.725
Working: Pad with zeros to align decimal places: 83.400 - 29.675. Regroup: 0 - 5 requires regrouping from tenths: 83.39(10) - 29.675; 10 - 5 = 5; 9 - 7 = 2; 13 - 6 = 7; 12 - 9 = 3; 7 - 2 = 5. The result is 53.725.

Question 8: Calculate the total of 14.8, 6.275, and 129.46.
Answer: 150.535
Working: Align decimal points using zero place-holders: 14.800 + 6.275 + 129.460. Thousandths: 0 + 5 + 0 = 5; Hundredths: 0 + 7 + 6 = 13 (write 3, carry 1); Tenths: 8 + 2 + 4 + 1 = 15 (write 5, carry 1); Ones: 4 + 6 + 9 + 1 = 20 (write 0, carry 2); Tens: 1 + 2 + 2 = 5; Hundreds: 1. Total = 150.535.

Question 9: A plank of wood is 4.2 metres long. A carpenter cuts two pieces from it: one measuring 1.65 metres and another measuring 0.875 metres. How many metres of wood are left?
Answer: 1.675 metres
Working: Total cut length = 1.650 + 0.875 = 2.525 metres. Length remaining = 4.200 - 2.525 = 1.675 metres.

--- Constant difference mental subtraction ---

Question 10: Fill in the missing number to make the statement correct: 6,000 - 2,347 = 5,999 - [ ].
Answer: 2,346
Working: Subtracting 1 from 6,000 gives 5,999. By the constant difference rule, we must also subtract 1 from 2,347: 2,347 - 1 = 2,346.

Question 11: Use the constant difference strategy to calculate 8,000 - 3,468 mentally.
Answer: 4,532
Working: Subtract 1 from both numbers to avoid regrouping across zeros: 8,000 - 1 = 7,999 and 3,468 - 1 = 3,467. Then calculate 7,999 - 3,467: 7,000 - 3,000 = 4,000; 900 - 400 = 500; 90 - 60 = 30; 9 - 7 = 2. The answer is 4,532.

Question 12: Calculate 50,000 - 18,426 by subtracting 1 from both numbers first.
Answer: 31,574
Working: 50,000 - 18,426 = 49,999 - 18,425. 49,999 - 18,425: 4 - 1 = 3; 9 - 8 = 1; 9 - 4 = 5; 9 - 2 = 7; 9 - 5 = 4. The result is 31,574.

Question 13: Use the constant difference method to calculate 10 - 3.64 mentally by subtracting 0.01 from both numbers.
Answer: 6.36
Working: Subtract 0.01 from both numbers: 10 - 0.01 = 9.99 and 3.64 - 0.01 = 3.63. Then calculate 9.99 - 3.63: 9 - 3 = 6; 0.9 - 0.6 = 0.3; 0.09 - 0.03 = 0.06. The answer is 6.36.

Question 14: A runner has a target of 20 km. So far, she has run 14.375 km. Use constant difference by subtracting 0.001 from both numbers to find the remaining distance.
Answer: 5.625 km
Working: 20.000 - 14.375 is equivalent to 19.999 - 14.374. 19.999 - 14.374 = 5.625 km.
