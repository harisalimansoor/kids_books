=== Topic: Scale conversions on maps and models (1 cm : 2.5 m); similar shapes scale factors ===
Domain: Ratio and Proportion

--- Scale conversions on maps and models ---

Question 1: On a map, the scale is 1 cm : 2.5 m. A path measures 6 cm on the map. What is the real length of the path in metres?
Answer: 15 m
Working: Each 1 cm represents 2.5 m. Real length = 6 × 2.5 = 15 m.

Question 2: On a map of a nature reserve, 1 cm represents 4 km. The distance between two viewpoints on the map is 7 cm. What is the actual distance between the viewpoints in kilometres?
Answer: 28 km
Working: Each 1 cm represents 4 km. Actual distance = 7 × 4 = 28 km.

Question 3: A scale drawing of a playground uses the scale 1 cm : 3 m. The real length of the climbing frame is 18 m. How long is the climbing frame on the scale drawing in centimetres?
Answer: 6 cm
Working: Each 1 cm represents 3 m. Length on drawing = 18 ÷ 3 = 6 cm.

Question 4: A model of a ship uses a scale of 1 cm : 2.5 m. The actual ship is 35 m long. What is the length of the model ship in centimetres?
Answer: 14 cm
Working: Each 1 cm represents 2.5 m. Length of model = 35 ÷ 2.5 = 14 cm.

Question 5: On a floor plan of a house, the scale is 1 cm : 2 m. The living room on the plan is 4.5 cm long. What is the actual length of the living room in metres?
Answer: 9 m
Working: Each 1 cm represents 2 m. Actual length = 4.5 × 2 = 9 m.

Question 6: On a town map, a scale of 1 cm represents 200 m. Two libraries are 1.6 km apart in real life. What is the distance between the two libraries on the map in centimetres?
Answer: 8 cm
Working: Convert 1.6 km to metres: 1.6 km = 1600 m. Distance on map = 1600 ÷ 200 = 8 cm.

Question 7: On a plan of a school hall, the scale is 1 cm : 2.5 m. The hall is 20 m long and 15 m wide in real life. What are the length and width of the hall on the plan in centimetres?
Answer: 8 cm by 6 cm
Working: Length on plan = 20 ÷ 2.5 = 8 cm. Width on plan = 15 ÷ 2.5 = 6 cm. The dimensions are 8 cm by 6 cm.

--- Similar shapes scale factors ---

Question 8: Rectangle A is 4 cm long and 3 cm wide. Rectangle B is an enlargement of Rectangle A by a scale factor of 3. What are the length and width of Rectangle B?
Answer: 12 cm by 9 cm
Working: Multiply each dimension by the scale factor of 3: Length = 4 × 3 = 12 cm. Width = 3 × 3 = 9 cm.

Question 9: A triangle has side lengths of 5 cm, 7 cm, and 8 cm. A similar larger triangle has side lengths of 15 cm, 21 cm, and 24 cm. What is the scale factor of enlargement?
Answer: 3
Working: Divide any corresponding side of the larger triangle by the smaller triangle: 15 ÷ 5 = 3.

Question 10: Shape A and Shape B are similar shapes. A side of 6 cm on Shape A corresponds to a side of 24 cm on Shape B. Another side on Shape A is 9 cm. What is the length of the corresponding side on Shape B in centimetres?
Answer: 36 cm
Working: Scale factor = 24 ÷ 6 = 4. Corresponding side on Shape B = 9 × 4 = 36 cm.

Question 11: An equilateral triangle has a perimeter of 18 cm. A larger similar equilateral triangle is drawn using a scale factor of 4. What is the perimeter of the larger triangle in centimetres?
Answer: 72 cm
Working: The perimeter scales by the same factor as the side lengths. Perimeter = 18 × 4 = 72 cm.

Question 12: A photo measuring 8 cm by 12 cm is enlarged by a scale factor of 2.5. What are the dimensions of the enlarged photo in centimetres?
Answer: 20 cm by 30 cm
Working: Width = 8 × 2.5 = 20 cm. Length = 12 × 2.5 = 30 cm.

Question 13: Triangle X has sides measuring 6 cm, 8 cm, and 10 cm. Triangle Y is similar to Triangle X, and its longest side measures 25 cm. What is the perimeter of Triangle Y in centimetres?
Answer: 60 cm
Working: The scale factor is 25 ÷ 10 = 2.5. The perimeter of Triangle X = 6 + 8 + 10 = 24 cm. Perimeter of Triangle Y = 24 × 2.5 = 60 cm.

Question 14: Rectangle P has a perimeter of 26 cm and a width of 5 cm. Rectangle Q is similar to Rectangle P. The length of Rectangle Q is 24 cm. What is the width of Rectangle Q in centimetres?
Answer: 15 cm
Working: For Rectangle P, double the width is 10 cm, so the two lengths sum to 26 - 10 = 16 cm. Length of Rectangle P = 8 cm. Scale factor from P to Q = 24 ÷ 8 = 3. Width of Rectangle Q = 5 × 3 = 15 cm.
