=== Topic: Perimeter vs area relationships; area of triangles (A = \frac{1{2bh) and parallelograms (A = bh) ===
Domain: Measurement, Time, Area, Perimeter & Volume

--- Perimeter vs area relationships ---

Question 1: Rectangle A has a length of 6 cm and a width of 4 cm. Rectangle B has a length of 8 cm and a width of 3 cm. Calculate the difference between their perimeters.
Answer: 2 cm
Working: Perimeter of Rectangle A = 2 × (6 + 4) = 20 cm. Perimeter of Rectangle B = 2 × (8 + 3) = 22 cm. Difference = 22 - 20 = 2 cm.

Question 2: A square has a perimeter of 24 cm. A rectangle has the same perimeter of 24 cm, but has a length of 7 cm. What is the difference between the area of the square and the area of the rectangle?
Answer: 1 cm²
Working: Square side = 24 ÷ 4 = 6 cm. Square area = 6 × 6 = 36 cm². Rectangle width = (24 ÷ 2) - 7 = 5 cm. Rectangle area = 7 × 5 = 35 cm². Difference = 36 - 35 = 1 cm².

Question 3: Rectangle X and Rectangle Y both have an area of 36 cm². Rectangle X has a length of 9 cm and Rectangle Y has a length of 12 cm. What is the perimeter of Rectangle Y?
Answer: 30 cm
Working: Width of Rectangle Y = 36 ÷ 12 = 3 cm. Perimeter of Rectangle Y = 2 × (12 + 3) = 2 × 15 = 30 cm.

Question 4: A rectangle has a perimeter of 20 cm. Its length and width are both whole numbers of centimetres. What is the greatest possible area this rectangle can have?
Answer: 25 cm²
Working: Length + width = 20 ÷ 2 = 10 cm. Possible whole number pairs: 9 and 1 (area 9 cm²), 8 and 2 (area 16 cm²), 7 and 3 (area 21 cm²), 6 and 4 (area 24 cm²), 5 and 5 (area 25 cm²). The greatest area is 25 cm².

--- Area of triangles ---

Question 5: A triangle has a base of 8 cm and a perpendicular height of 5 cm. What is the area of the triangle?
Answer: 20 cm²
Working: Area of triangle = (base × height) ÷ 2 = (8 × 5) ÷ 2 = 40 ÷ 2 = 20 cm².

Question 6: A right-angled triangle has side lengths of 6 cm, 8 cm, and 10 cm. Calculate the area of the triangle.
Answer: 24 cm²
Working: The two perpendicular sides forming the right angle are 6 cm and 8 cm. Area = (6 × 8) ÷ 2 = 48 ÷ 2 = 24 cm².

Question 7: A triangle has an area of 35 cm² and a base of 10 cm. What is the perpendicular height of the triangle?
Answer: 7 cm
Working: Area = (base × height) ÷ 2, so base × height = 35 × 2 = 70. Height = 70 ÷ 10 = 7 cm.

Question 8: A triangle has an area of 48 cm² and a perpendicular height of 8 cm. What is the length of its base?
Answer: 12 cm
Working: Base × height = 48 × 2 = 96. Base = 96 ÷ 8 = 12 cm.

--- Area of parallelograms ---

Question 9: A parallelogram has a base of 9 cm and a perpendicular height of 4 cm. What is the area of the parallelogram?
Answer: 36 cm²
Working: Area of parallelogram = base × height = 9 × 4 = 36 cm².

Question 10: A parallelogram has a base of 11 cm, a slanted side of 7 cm, and a perpendicular height of 6 cm. Calculate the area of the parallelogram.
Answer: 66 cm²
Working: Area of parallelogram = base × perpendicular height = 11 × 6 = 66 cm².

Question 11: A parallelogram has an area of 84 cm² and a base of 12 cm. What is its perpendicular height?
Answer: 7 cm
Working: Perpendicular height = area ÷ base = 84 ÷ 12 = 7 cm.

Question 12: A parallelogram has a base of 16 cm and a perpendicular height of 9 cm. A square has the same area as this parallelogram. What is the perimeter of the square?
Answer: 48 cm
Working: Area of parallelogram = 16 × 9 = 144 cm². Side length of square = √144 = 12 cm (since 12 × 12 = 144). Perimeter of square = 4 × 12 = 48 cm.
