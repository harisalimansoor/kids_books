=== Topic: Translations and reflections in axes and arbitrary mirror lines (e.g. x = 14); coordinate region tests ===
Domain: Geometry: Position and Direction

--- Translations ---

Question 1: Point A has coordinates (3, -2). It is translated 4 units to the left and 5 units up. What are the coordinates of the new point?
Answer: (-1, 3)
Working: Translate x-coordinate: 3 - 4 = -1. Translate y-coordinate: -2 + 5 = 3. The new coordinates are (-1, 3).

Question 2: A rectangle has vertices at (-4, 2), (-1, 2), (-1, -3), and (-4, -3). The rectangle is translated so that the vertex at (-1, 2) moves to (5, 6). What are the new coordinates of the vertex that started at (-4, -3)?
Answer: (2, 1)
Working: The vertex moves from (-1, 2) to (5, 6). Change in x is 5 - (-1) = 6 units right. Change in y is 6 - 2 = 4 units up. Apply this translation to (-4, -3): x = -4 + 6 = 2, y = -3 + 4 = 1. The new coordinates are (2, 1).

Question 3: A triangle is translated 7 units right and 3 units down. After the translation, one of the vertices is at (2, -1). What were the starting coordinates of this vertex?
Answer: (-5, 2)
Working: Reverse the translation: 7 units left and 3 units up. x-coordinate: 2 - 7 = -5. y-coordinate: -1 + 3 = 2. The starting coordinates were (-5, 2).

Question 4: Point P is at (-6, 5) and Point Q is at (2, -1). Describe the translation from Point P to Point Q by stating the number of units moved horizontally (left or right) and vertically (up or down).
Answer: 8 units right and 6 units down
Working: Horizontal change: from -6 to 2 is 2 - (-6) = 8 units right. Vertical change: from 5 to -1 is -1 - 5 = -6, which is 6 units down.

--- Reflections in axes and arbitrary mirror lines ---

Question 5: Point B has coordinates (-5, 4). What are the coordinates of Point B after it is reflected in the x-axis?
Answer: (-5, -4)
Working: When reflecting in the x-axis, the x-coordinate stays the same and the y-coordinate changes sign. The reflected point is (-5, -4).

Question 6: Point C has coordinates (3, 7). It is reflected in the vertical mirror line x = 8. What are the coordinates of the reflected point?
Answer: (13, 7)
Working: The mirror line is x = 8. The horizontal distance from x = 3 to x = 8 is 8 - 3 = 5 units. The reflected x-coordinate is 8 + 5 = 13. The y-coordinate remains 7. The reflected point is (13, 7).

Question 7: Point D has coordinates (-2, -1). It is reflected in the horizontal mirror line y = 3. What are the coordinates of the reflected point?
Answer: (-2, 7)
Working: The mirror line is y = 3. The vertical distance from y = -1 to y = 3 is 3 - (-1) = 4 units. The reflected y-coordinate is 3 + 4 = 7. The x-coordinate remains -2. The reflected point is (-2, 7).

Question 8: A triangle has vertices at (11, 2), (15, 2), and (11, 8). The triangle is reflected in the vertical line x = 10. What are the coordinates of the reflected image of the vertex (15, 2)?
Answer: (5, 2)
Working: The vertex is at (15, 2). The mirror line is x = 10. The distance from x = 15 to x = 10 is 5 units to the left. Reflecting to the other side of x = 10 gives x = 10 - 5 = 5. The y-coordinate remains 2. The reflected vertex is (5, 2).

Question 9: Point M at (4, -3) is reflected to become point M' at (4, 9). What is the equation of the horizontal mirror line?
Answer: y = 3
Working: The x-coordinates are identical, so the reflection is across a horizontal line y = k. The line is midway between the y-coordinates: (-3 + 9) ÷ 2 = 6 ÷ 2 = 3. The mirror line is y = 3.

--- Coordinate region tests ---

Question 10: A rectangle on a coordinate grid has vertices at (-3, -1), (5, -1), (5, 4), and (-3, 4). Is the point (2, 5) inside, outside, or on the boundary of the rectangle?
Answer: outside
Working: For a point to lie inside or on the boundary of the rectangle, its x-coordinate must be between -3 and 5, and its y-coordinate must be between -1 and 4. Since the point (2, 5) has y = 5, which is greater than 4, it lies outside the rectangle.

Question 11: Four possible squares of side length 6 share the vertex at (10, 10). Their sides are all parallel to the coordinate axes. Which of the four squares contains the point (8, 13)? Give your answer by writing the four vertices of this square.
Answer: (4, 10), (10, 10), (10, 16), (4, 16)
Working: The point (8, 13) has an x-coordinate of 8 (less than 10) and a y-coordinate of 13 (greater than 10). Therefore, the square must extend left to x = 10 - 6 = 4, and up to y = 10 + 6 = 16. The vertices of this square are (4, 10), (10, 10), (10, 16), and (4, 16).

Question 12: A square has vertices at (1, 2), (7, 2), (7, 8), and (1, 8). Which of the following three points lies strictly inside the square: (4, 5), (1, 5), or (7, 9)?
Answer: (4, 5)
Working: A point is strictly inside the square if 1 < x < 7 and 2 < y < 8. The point (4, 5) satisfies both conditions (1 < 4 < 7 and 2 < 5 < 8). The point (1, 5) lies on the boundary, and (7, 9) lies outside.
