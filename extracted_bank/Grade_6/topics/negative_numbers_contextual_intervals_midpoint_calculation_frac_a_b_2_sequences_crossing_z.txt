=== Topic: Negative numbers: contextual intervals, midpoint calculation \frac{a+b{2, sequences crossing zero, endpoints given difference across zero (|A-B| = 160) ===
Domain: Number & Place Value

--- Contextual intervals ---

Question 1: The temperature at midnight in Leeds was -4 °C. By midday, the temperature had risen by 9 °C. What was the temperature at midday?
Answer: 5 °C
Working: -4 + 9 = 5 °C

Question 2: The temperature inside a freezer is -18 °C. The temperature in the kitchen is 21 °C. What is the difference between these two temperatures?
Answer: 39 °C
Working: 21 - (-18) = 21 + 18 = 39 °C

Question 3: A research submarine is at a depth of -65 metres relative to sea level. It ascends 42 metres towards the surface. What is the new depth of the submarine in metres?
Answer: -23 metres
Working: -65 + 42 = -23 metres

Question 4: A bank account has a balance of -£45. The account holder deposits £120. What is the new balance of the account in pounds?
Answer: £75
Working: -£45 + £120 = £75

Question 5: At 6:00 am, the temperature was -8 °C. By 2:00 pm, the temperature had increased by 15 °C. By 10:00 pm, the temperature had decreased by 11 °C from the 2:00 pm reading. What was the temperature at 10:00 pm?
Answer: -4 °C
Working: At 2:00 pm: -8 + 15 = 7 °C. At 10:00 pm: 7 - 11 = -4 °C.

--- Midpoint calculation ---

Question 6: What number is exactly halfway between -10 and 10 on a number line?
Answer: 0
Working: (-10 + 10) ÷ 2 = 0 ÷ 2 = 0

Question 7: Calculate the midpoint between -14 and 26.
Answer: 6
Working: (-14 + 26) ÷ 2 = 12 ÷ 2 = 6

Question 8: What number is halfway between -35 and 15 on a number line?
Answer: -10
Working: (-35 + 15) ÷ 2 = -20 ÷ 2 = -10

Question 9: Point A is at -48 and Point B is at 32 on a number line. Find the value of the midpoint between Point A and Point B.
Answer: -8
Working: (-48 + 32) ÷ 2 = -16 ÷ 2 = -8

Question 10: The midpoint between a negative number and 18 is 3. What is the negative number?
Answer: -12
Working: (a + 18) ÷ 2 = 3, so a + 18 = 6. Therefore, a = 6 - 18 = -12.

--- Sequences crossing zero ---

Question 11: A number sequence decreases by 5 each time: 12, 7, 2, ... What is the next term in the sequence?
Answer: -3
Working: 2 - 5 = -3

Question 12: A sequence decreases by the same amount each time: 26, 14, 2, ... What is the next number in the sequence?
Answer: -10
Working: Rule: subtract 12 each time. 2 - 12 = -10.

Question 13: A linear sequence starts at -15 and increases by 6 each time. What is the first positive number in this sequence?
Answer: 3
Working: -15, -9, -3, 3. The first positive number is 3.

Question 14: The 1st term of a decreasing linear sequence is 17 and the 4th term is -4. By how much does the sequence decrease each time?
Answer: 7
Working: Difference from 1st to 4th term is 17 - (-4) = 21 across 3 steps. Step size = 21 ÷ 3 = 7.

Question 15: A linear sequence begins: 21, 13, 5, -3, ... Find the 8th term of this sequence.
Answer: -35
Working: The sequence decreases by 8 each time. 1st: 21, 2nd: 13, 3rd: 5, 4th: -3, 5th: -11, 6th: -19, 7th: -27, 8th: -35.

--- Endpoints given difference across zero ---

Question 16: Two points, A and B, lie on a number line. Point A is a negative number and Point B is a positive number. The distance between Point A and Point B is 160. If Point B is 95, what is the value of Point A?
Answer: -65
Working: Point A = 95 - 160 = -65.

Question 17: The difference between two numbers is 50. One number is 18 and the other number is negative. What is the negative number?
Answer: -32
Working: 18 - 50 = -32

Question 18: Point X is at -75 on a number line. Point Y is a positive number. The interval between Point X and Point Y across zero is 140. What is the value of Point Y?
Answer: 65
Working: Point Y = -75 + 140 = 65.

Question 19: Point C is a negative number and Point D is a positive number on a number line. The difference between Point C and Point D is 120. Point D is three times as far from 0 as Point C is from 0. What is the value of Point C?
Answer: -30
Working: The total distance of 120 is split into 1 + 3 = 4 equal parts. One part = 120 ÷ 4 = 30. Since Point C is negative, Point C = -30 (and Point D = 90).
