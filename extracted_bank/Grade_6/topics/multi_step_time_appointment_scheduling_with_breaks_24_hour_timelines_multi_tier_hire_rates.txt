--- Multi-step time ---

Question 1: A film starts at 14:15. It runs for 1 hour and 40 minutes, followed immediately by 15 minutes of trailers and credits. What time does everything finish?
Answer: 16:10
Working: Total duration = 1 hour 40 minutes + 15 minutes = 1 hour 55 minutes. 14:15 + 1 hour = 15:15. 15:15 + 55 minutes = 16:10.

Question 2: Oliver travels to school in three stages. He walks for 18 minutes, rides a bus for 35 minutes, and then walks for another 12 minutes. If he arrives at school at 08:30, what time did he start his journey?
Answer: 07:25
Working: Total journey time = 18 + 35 + 12 = 65 minutes (1 hour 5 minutes). Subtract 1 hour 5 minutes from 08:30: 08:30 - 1 hour = 07:30; 07:30 - 5 minutes = 07:25.

Question 3: A runner completes a 10 km race in 52 minutes 45 seconds. Their time for the first 5 km was 25 minutes 50 seconds. How long did the runner take to complete the second 5 km? Give your answer in minutes and seconds.
Answer: 26 minutes 55 seconds
Working: Subtract 25 minutes 50 seconds from 52 minutes 45 seconds. Regroup: 52 minutes 45 seconds = 51 minutes 105 seconds. 51 min - 25 min = 26 min. 105 s - 50 s = 55 s. Result = 26 minutes 55 seconds.

Question 4: A train journey has three sections: London to Rugby takes 55 minutes, there is a 25-minute wait at Rugby, and Rugby to Birmingham takes 40 minutes. A second train leaves London 35 minutes after the first train and travels directly to Birmingham in 1 hour 25 minutes. How many minutes before or after the first train does the second train arrive in Birmingham?
Answer: Both arrive at the same time (0 minutes difference)
Working: Let first train leave at time 0. First train journey = 55 + 25 + 40 = 120 minutes (arrives at T + 120 min). Second train leaves at T + 35 min and takes 1 hour 25 minutes (85 minutes). Second train arrival = 35 + 85 = 120 minutes. Both arrive at exactly the same time (0 minutes difference).

--- Appointment scheduling with breaks ---

Question 5: A music teacher gives 4 piano lessons in an afternoon. Each lesson lasts 30 minutes, and there is a 10-minute break between consecutive lessons. If the first lesson starts at 13:30, what time does the fourth lesson end?
Answer: 16:00
Working: 4 lessons = 4 × 30 = 120 minutes. 3 breaks between lessons = 3 × 10 = 30 minutes. Total duration = 150 minutes (2 hours 30 minutes). 13:30 + 2 hours 30 minutes = 16:00.

Question 6: A dentist books appointments lasting 20 minutes each, with a 5-minute break between appointments. The dentist starts seeing patients at 09:00 and takes a 45-minute lunch break starting at 12:00. How many patient appointments can be completed before lunch begins?
Answer: 7
Working: Each appointment cycle takes 20 + 5 = 25 minutes, except the last one does not need a follow-up break if it finishes by 12:00. Time available from 09:00 to 12:00 = 3 hours = 180 minutes. 7 appointments with 6 breaks: (7 × 20) + (6 × 5) = 140 + 30 = 170 minutes (finishes at 11:50). 8 appointments with 7 breaks = (8 × 20) + (7 × 5) = 160 + 35 = 195 minutes > 180 minutes. Therefore, 7 appointments can be completed.

Question 7: A school holds parent-teacher meetings from 15:30 to 18:00. Each meeting lasts 8 minutes and there is a 2-minute changeover gap between meetings. The teacher takes one 10-minute tea break during the session. What is the maximum number of parent meetings the teacher can schedule?
Answer: 14
Working: Total time available from 15:30 to 18:00 = 2 hours 30 minutes = 150 minutes. Subtract the 10-minute tea break: 150 - 10 = 140 minutes. Each meeting slot (meeting + gap) takes 8 + 2 = 10 minutes. 140 ÷ 10 = 14 meetings. (14 meetings take 14 × 8 = 112 minutes of meetings + 13 gaps of 2 min = 26 min + 10 min break = 148 min <= 150 min). Maximum meetings = 14.

Question 8: A swimming gala has 6 races. Each race takes 4 minutes to run. Between consecutive races, there is a 3-minute gap, except after Race 3 when there is an extended 15-minute medal ceremony instead of the 3-minute gap. If the gala ends at 11:05, what time did the first race begin?
Answer: 10:14
Working: Total racing time = 6 × 4 = 24 minutes. There are 5 intervals between the 6 races: 4 short gaps of 3 minutes (4 × 3 = 12 minutes) and 1 extended ceremony of 15 minutes. Total gala duration = 24 + 12 + 15 = 51 minutes. Start time = 11:05 - 51 minutes: 11:05 - 5 minutes = 11:00; 11:00 - 46 minutes = 10:14.

--- 24-hour timelines ---

Question 9: An overnight ferry departs at 21:40 on Tuesday and arrives at its destination at 06:15 on Wednesday. How long did the ferry journey take in hours and minutes?
Answer: 8 hours 35 minutes
Working: From 21:40 to 24:00 (midnight) is 2 hours 20 minutes. From 00:00 to 06:15 is 6 hours 15 minutes. Total journey time = 2 hours 20 minutes + 6 hours 15 minutes = 8 hours 35 minutes.

Question 10: A night train leaves Edinburgh at 22:50. The journey to London King's Cross takes 7 hours and 35 minutes. At what 24-hour clock time does the train arrive in London on the following day?
Answer: 06:25
Working: 22:50 + 7 hours = 05:50 (next day). 05:50 + 35 minutes = 06:25.

Question 11: A science experiment starts recording data every 45 minutes beginning at 20:30 on Friday. What is the 24-hour clock time when the 6th recording is taken?
Answer: 00:15
Working: 1st recording is at 20:30. The 6th recording occurs after 5 intervals of 45 minutes. 5 × 45 = 225 minutes = 3 hours 45 minutes. 20:30 + 3 hours = 23:30. 23:30 + 45 minutes = 00:15 (on Saturday).

Question 12: A security guard works a night shift from 19:45 to 07:15 the next morning. During the shift, the guard takes three equal 25-minute patrol breaks and one 40-minute meal break. For how many hours and minutes during the shift is the guard actively on duty (not on a break)?
Answer: 9 hours 35 minutes
Working: Total shift length from 19:45 to 07:15: 19:45 to 24:00 is 4 hours 15 minutes; 00:00 to 07:15 is 7 hours 15 minutes. Total shift = 11 hours 30 minutes. Total break time = (3 × 25 minutes) + 40 minutes = 75 + 40 = 115 minutes = 1 hour 55 minutes. Active duty time = 11 hours 30 minutes - 1 hour 55 minutes = 9 hours 35 minutes.

--- Multi-tier hire rates ---

Question 13: A bicycle hire company charges a fixed booking fee of £4.00 plus £1.50 for every 30 minutes of hire. How much does it cost to hire a bicycle for 2 hours?
Answer: £10.00
Working: 2 hours = 4 periods of 30 minutes. Timed cost = 4 × £1.50 = £6.00. Total cost = £4.00 + £6.00 = £10.00.

Question 14: A boat hire company charges a £6.50 basic fee plus £2.95 for every 20 minutes on the water. Maya hires a boat from 13:40 to 15:00. What is the total cost of the hire?
Answer: £18.30
Working: Duration = 13:40 to 15:00 = 80 minutes. Number of 20-minute blocks = 80 ÷ 20 = 4 blocks. Cost for time = 4 × £2.95 = £11.80. Total cost = £6.50 + £11.80 = £18.30.

Question 15: A tennis club charges a non-refundable booking fee of £5.20 plus £3.40 for every 15 minutes of court time. Sam pays a total of £25.60. For how many hours and minutes was the tennis court booked?
Answer: 1 hour 30 minutes
Working: Subtract booking fee: £25.60 - £5.20 = £20.40. Number of 15-minute blocks = £20.40 ÷ £3.40 = 6 blocks. Total time = 6 × 15 minutes = 90 minutes = 1 hour 30 minutes.

Question 16: Canoe hire costs a fixed fee of £8.00 plus £2.75 for every 20 minutes (or part of 20 minutes). Paddleboard hire costs a fixed fee of £5.00 plus £3.20 for every 20 minutes (or part of 20 minutes). Which option is cheaper for a hire lasting 1 hour and 10 minutes, and by how much?
Answer: Paddleboard by £1.20
Working: 1 hour 10 minutes = 70 minutes. 70 minutes contains 3 full 20-minute blocks and 1 partial block = 4 blocks charged. Canoe cost: £8.00 + (4 × £2.75) = £8.00 + £11.00 = £19.00. Paddleboard cost: £5.00 + (4 × £3.20) = £5.00 + £12.80 = £17.80. Difference = £19.00 - £17.80 = £1.20 cheaper for Paddleboard.
