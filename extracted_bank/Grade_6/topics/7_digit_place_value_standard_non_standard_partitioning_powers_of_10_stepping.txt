=== Topic: 7-digit place value, standard/non-standard partitioning, powers of 10 stepping ===
Domain: Number & Place Value

--- 7-digit place value ---

Question 1: What is the value of the digit 7 in the number 4,729,305?
Answer: 700,000
Working: The digit 7 is in the hundred thousands place, so its value is 7 × 100,000 = 700,000.

Question 2: Write the number three million, forty thousand, five hundred and six in digits.
Answer: 3,040,506
Working: 3 millions = 3,000,000; 4 ten thousands = 40,000; 5 hundreds = 500; 6 ones = 6. Combined, the number is 3,040,506.

Question 3: In the number 8,634,291, how many times greater is the value of the digit 6 than the value of the digit 3?
Answer: 10
Working: The digit 6 has a value of 600,000. The digit 3 has a value of 30,000. 600,000 ÷ 30,000 = 60 ÷ 3 = 10.

Question 4: A 7-digit number has a 5 in the millions place, a 2 in the thousands place, and all other digits are zero. Write this number in digits.
Answer: 5,002,000
Working: Millions = 5, Hundred thousands = 0, Ten thousands = 0, Thousands = 2, Hundreds = 0, Tens = 0, Ones = 0. The number is 5,002,000.

Question 5: Write down the 7-digit number that is 200,000 more than 5,834,120.
Answer: 6,034,120
Working: 5,834,120 + 200,000: 800,000 + 200,000 = 1,000,000. 5,000,000 + 1,000,000 + 34,120 = 6,034,120.

Question 6: In a census, the population of a region is recorded as 4,382,915. Which digit is in the ten thousands place?
Answer: 8
Working: In 4,382,915, the place values from right to left are: ones (5), tens (1), hundreds (9), thousands (2), ten thousands (8), hundred thousands (3), millions (4). The digit in the ten thousands place is 8.

--- Standard and non-standard partitioning ---

Question 7: Find the missing number to complete the partition: 4,506,230 = 4,000,000 + 500,000 + [___] + 200 + 30.
Answer: 6,000
Working: 4,506,230 - 4,000,000 - 500,000 - 200 - 30 = 6,000. The missing thousands part is 6,000.

Question 8: A number is partitioned as 3,000,000 + 450,000 + 12,000 + 400 + 8. Write the total number in digits.
Answer: 3,462,408
Working: 3,000,000 + 450,000 = 3,450,000. 3,450,000 + 12,000 = 3,462,000. 3,462,000 + 400 + 8 = 3,462,408.

Question 9: Complete the non-standard partitioning: 6,250,000 = 5,000,000 + [___] thousands.
Answer: 1,250
Working: 6,250,000 - 5,000,000 = 1,250,000. 1,250,000 ÷ 1,000 = 1,250 thousands.

Question 10: The number 2,840,000 is partitioned into 2 millions, [___] ten thousands, and 40 thousands. Find the missing number of ten thousands.
Answer: 80
Working: 2 millions = 2,000,000 and 40 thousands = 40,000. 2,840,000 - (2,000,000 + 40,000) = 800,000. 800,000 ÷ 10,000 = 80 ten thousands.

Question 11: A number is partitioned into 25 hundred thousands, 14 thousands, and 7 hundreds. What is the number in digits?
Answer: 2,514,700
Working: 25 hundred thousands = 25 × 100,000 = 2,500,000. 14 thousands = 14 × 1,000 = 14,000. 7 hundreds = 7 × 100 = 700. Total = 2,500,000 + 14,000 + 700 = 2,514,700.

Question 12: Find the missing number: 7,800,450 = 7,000,000 + 700,000 + [___] + 450.
Answer: 100,000
Working: 7,000,000 + 700,000 + 450 = 7,700,450. 7,800,450 - 7,700,450 = 100,000.

--- Powers of 10 stepping ---

Question 13: What number is 10,000 less than 1,000,000?
Answer: 990,000
Working: 1,000,000 - 10,000 = 990,000.

Question 14: A sequence increases by 100,000 each time: 3,750,000, 3,850,000, 3,950,000, [___]. What is the next number in the sequence?
Answer: 4,050,000
Working: 3,950,000 + 100,000 = 4,050,000.

Question 15: Starting at 6,003,420, count back 5 steps of 10,000. What is the final number?
Answer: 5,953,420
Working: 5 steps of 10,000 is 50,000. 6,003,420 - 50,000 = 5,953,420.

Question 16: What number is 1,000,000 more than 4,289,103?
Answer: 5,289,103
Working: 4,289,103 + 1,000,000 = 5,289,103.

Question 17: A counter starts at 2,980,000 and steps forwards in ten thousands. How many steps of 10,000 are needed to reach 3,050,000?
Answer: 7
Working: 3,050,000 - 2,980,000 = 70,000. 70,000 ÷ 10,000 = 7 steps.

Question 18: Fill in the missing number in this sequence of steps of 100,000: 5,140,000, 5,040,000, [___], 4,840,000.
Answer: 4,940,000
Working: The sequence decreases by 100,000 each time. 5,040,000 - 100,000 = 4,940,000.
