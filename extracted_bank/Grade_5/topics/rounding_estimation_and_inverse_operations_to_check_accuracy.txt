=== Topic: Rounding, estimation, and inverse operations to check accuracy ===
Domain: Addition and Subtraction

--- Rounding ---

Question 1: Round 48,279 to the nearest 1,000.
Answer: 48,000
Working: The thousands digit is 8 and the hundreds digit is 2. Since 2 is less than 5, round down to 48,000.

Question 2: A runner records a total distance of 14,682 m. What is this distance rounded to the nearest 100 m?
Answer: 14,700 m
Working: The hundreds digit is 6 and the tens digit is 8. Since 8 is 5 or greater, round up from 600 to 700, giving 14,700 m.

Question 3: Round 364,520 to the nearest 10,000.
Answer: 360,000
Working: The ten-thousands digit is 6 and the thousands digit is 4. Since 4 is less than 5, round down to 360,000.

Question 4: A 5-digit whole number rounds to 40,000 when rounded to the nearest 10,000, and rounds to 38,000 when rounded to the nearest 1,000. What is the smallest possible value of this number?
Answer: 37,500
Working: To round to 38,000 to the nearest 1,000, the number must be at least 37,500. 37,500 also rounds to 40,000 to the nearest 10,000. So the smallest possible value is 37,500.

Question 5: A stadium recorded attendance of 59,614 on Saturday and 24,530 on Sunday. Round each attendance to the nearest 10,000 and add the rounded numbers.
Answer: 80,000
Working: 59,614 rounded to the nearest 10,000 is 60,000. 24,530 rounded to the nearest 10,000 is 20,000. 60,000 + 20,000 = 80,000.

--- Estimation ---

Question 6: Estimate the total of 39,215 and 41,870 by rounding each number to the nearest 10,000.
Answer: 80,000
Working: 39,215 rounds to 40,000. 41,870 rounds to 40,000. 40,000 + 40,000 = 80,000.

Question 7: Estimate the difference between 78,410 and 29,650 by rounding each number to the nearest 1,000.
Answer: 48,000
Working: 78,410 rounds to 78,000 to the nearest 1,000. 29,650 rounds to 30,000 to the nearest 1,000. 78,000 - 30,000 = 48,000.

Question 8: A museum had 58,194 visitors in May and 31,826 visitors in June. Estimate how many more visitors arrived in May than June by rounding each value to the nearest 1,000.
Answer: 26,000
Working: 58,194 rounds to 58,000. 31,826 rounds to 32,000. 58,000 - 32,000 = 26,000.

Question 9: A delivery lorry carries 12,480 kg of gravel on Monday, 8,920 kg on Tuesday, and 15,610 kg on Wednesday. Estimate the total mass carried over the three days by rounding each amount to the nearest 1,000 kg.
Answer: 37,000 kg
Working: 12,480 kg rounds to 12,000 kg. 8,920 kg rounds to 9,000 kg. 15,610 kg rounds to 16,000 kg. 12,000 + 9,000 + 16,000 = 37,000 kg.

Question 10: A school buys two sets of equipment costing £38.75 and £61.40. Estimate the total cost by rounding each amount to the nearest whole pound.
Answer: £100
Working: £38.75 rounds to £39 to the nearest whole pound. £61.40 rounds to £61. £39 + £61 = £100.

--- Inverse operations to check accuracy ---

Question 11: A pupil calculates 54,328 - 21,815 = 32,513. Write the addition calculation using these three numbers to check the subtraction.
Answer: 32,513 + 21,815 = 54,328
Working: The inverse of subtraction is addition. Adding the difference to the subtracted number gives: 32,513 + 21,815 = 54,328 (or 21,815 + 32,513 = 54,328).

Question 12: Leo works out 45,619 + 23,784 = 69,403. Write a subtraction calculation that uses 69,403 to check whether Leo's answer is correct.
Answer: 69,403 - 23,784 = 45,619
Working: To check an addition using its inverse, subtract one of the addends from the sum: 69,403 - 23,784 = 45,619 (or 69,403 - 45,619 = 23,784).

Question 13: Mia calculates 80,000 - 34,267 = 45,733. Use the inverse operation to calculate 45,733 + 34,267 to check if her answer is correct.
Answer: 80,000
Working: 45,733 + 34,267: 3 + 7 = 10, 30 + 60 + 10 = 100, 700 + 200 + 100 = 1,000, 5,000 + 4,000 + 1,000 = 10,000, 40,000 + 30,000 + 10,000 = 80,000.

Question 14: Use an inverse operation to find the missing number: missing number + 16,840 = 50,000.
Answer: 33,160
Working: The inverse of adding 16,840 is subtracting 16,840 from 50,000. 50,000 - 16,840 = 33,160.

Question 15: A student calculates 62,450 - 18,320 = 44,130. Check this calculation by adding 44,130 and 18,320. State whether the student's original answer is correct or incorrect.
Answer: Correct
Working: 44,130 + 18,320 = 62,450. Since the sum equals the original number (62,450), the calculation is correct.
