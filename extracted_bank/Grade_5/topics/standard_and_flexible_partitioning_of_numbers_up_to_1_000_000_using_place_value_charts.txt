=== Topic: Standard and flexible partitioning of numbers up to 1 000 000 using place value charts ===
Domain: Number and Place Value

--- Standard partitioning of numbers up to 1 000 000 using place value charts ---

Question 1: A place value chart shows the number 468 215. Complete the standard partition: 468 215 = 400 000 + 60 000 + [___] + 200 + 10 + 5. What is the missing number?
Answer: 8 000
Working: 468 215 has 4 hundred thousands (400 000), 6 ten thousands (60 000), 8 thousands (8 000), 2 hundreds (200), 1 ten (10), and 5 ones (5). The missing value is 8 000.

Question 2: On a place value chart, counters represent 7 hundred thousands, 0 ten thousands, 3 thousands, 9 hundreds, 0 tens, and 4 ones. What 6-digit whole number is shown?
Answer: 703 904
Working: 7 hundred thousands = 700 000, 3 thousands = 3 000, 9 hundreds = 900, and 4 ones = 4. Adding these together gives 703 904.

Question 3: What is the missing number in this place value calculation? 803 750 = 800 000 + 3 000 + [___] + 50
Answer: 700
Working: 803 750 = 800 000 + 3 000 + 700 + 50. The missing number representing 7 hundreds is 700.

Question 4: The number 950 034 is entered into a place value chart. What is the value of the digit in the ten thousands column?
Answer: 50 000
Working: In 950 034, the digit in the ten thousands column is 5. 5 ten thousands has a value of 50 000.

Question 5: Complete the expanded addition for the number shown on a place value chart: [___] + 40 000 + 6 000 + 80 = 546 080. What is the missing number?
Answer: 500 000
Working: 546 080 is partitioned into 500 000 + 40 000 + 6 000 + 80. The missing hundred thousands value is 500 000.

--- Flexible partitioning of numbers up to 1 000 000 using place value charts ---

Question 6: A place value chart contains 4 hundred thousands counters and 12 ten thousands counters. What number is represented on the chart?
Answer: 520 000
Working: 4 hundred thousands = 400 000. 12 ten thousands = 120 000. 400 000 + 120 000 = 520 000.

Question 7: Complete the flexible partition: 450 000 = 300 000 + [___]. What is the missing number?
Answer: 150 000
Working: 450 000 - 300 000 = 150 000.

Question 8: What number is missing to make the flexible partition true? 635 000 = 500 000 + 130 000 + [___]
Answer: 5 000
Working: 500 000 + 130 000 = 630 000. 635 000 - 630 000 = 5 000.

Question 9: A number is flexibly partitioned on a place value chart into 3 hundred thousands, 25 thousands, 4 hundreds, and 12 ones. What whole number does this make altogether?
Answer: 325 412
Working: 3 hundred thousands = 300 000. 25 thousands = 25 000. 4 hundreds = 400. 12 ones = 12. 300 000 + 25 000 + 400 + 12 = 325 412.

Question 10: A pupil partitions 250 000 into 24 ten thousands and some thousands. How many thousands are needed to complete the partition?
Answer: 10
Working: 24 ten thousands = 240 000. 250 000 - 240 000 = 10 000. 10 000 is equal to 10 thousands.

Question 11: What is the missing number in this flexible partition? 780 400 = 700 000 + 75 000 + [___] + 400
Answer: 5 000
Working: 700 000 + 75 000 + 400 = 775 400. 780 400 - 775 400 = 5 000.

Question 12: On a place value chart, a 6-digit number is partitioned into 8 hundred thousands, 19 thousands, 14 hundreds, and 6 ones. What is this number written in standard form?
Answer: 820 406
Working: 8 hundred thousands = 800 000. 19 thousands = 19 000. 14 hundreds = 1 400. 6 ones = 6. 800 000 + 19 000 + 1 400 + 6 = 820 406.
