=== Topic: Converting between metric units of length, mass, and volume using decimals up to 3 d.p ===
Domain: Measurement

--- Converting metric units of length ---

Question 1: Convert 4.5 metres into centimetres.
Answer: 450 cm
Working: There are 100 cm in 1 m. 4.5 × 100 = 450 cm.

Question 2: Convert 35 millimetres into centimetres. Give your answer as a decimal.
Answer: 3.5 cm
Working: There are 10 mm in 1 cm. 35 ÷ 10 = 3.5 cm.

Question 3: A walking track is 2.375 kilometres long. What is the length of the track in metres?
Answer: 2375 m
Working: There are 1000 m in 1 km. 2.375 × 1000 = 2375 m.

Question 4: A piece of wood measures 85 centimetres in length. What is its length in metres? Give your answer as a decimal.
Answer: 0.85 m
Working: There are 100 cm in 1 m. 85 ÷ 100 = 0.85 m.

Question 5: Convert 408 metres into kilometres. Give your answer as a decimal.
Answer: 0.408 km
Working: There are 1000 m in 1 km. 408 ÷ 1000 = 0.408 km.

Question 6: A ribbon is 3.2 metres long. Anaya cuts off 75 centimetres. What is the length of the remaining ribbon in metres? Give your answer as a decimal.
Answer: 2.45 m
Working: Convert 75 cm to metres: 75 ÷ 100 = 0.75 m. Then calculate 3.2 - 0.75 = 2.45 m.

Question 7: Liam jumps 1.48 metres in the long jump. Maya jumps 162 centimetres. How many centimetres further did Maya jump than Liam?
Answer: 14 cm
Working: Convert 1.48 m to centimetres: 1.48 × 100 = 148 cm. Then calculate 162 - 148 = 14 cm.

Question 8: A builder needs 5 metres of wire. She has three pieces measuring 1.65 m, 85 cm, and 1200 mm. How many more metres of wire does she need? Give your answer as a decimal.
Answer: 1.3 m
Working: Convert all pieces to metres: 1.65 m; 85 cm = 0.85 m; 1200 mm = 1.2 m. Total wire = 1.65 + 0.85 + 1.2 = 3.7 m. Remaining needed = 5 - 3.7 = 1.3 m.

--- Converting metric units of mass ---

Question 9: Convert 3.2 kilograms into grams.
Answer: 3200 g
Working: There are 1000 g in 1 kg. 3.2 × 1000 = 3200 g.

Question 10: Convert 6500 grams into kilograms. Give your answer as a decimal.
Answer: 6.5 kg
Working: There are 1000 g in 1 kg. 6500 ÷ 1000 = 6.5 kg.

Question 11: A parcel weighs 1425 grams. What is its mass in kilograms? Give your answer as a decimal.
Answer: 1.425 kg
Working: There are 1000 g in 1 kg. 1425 ÷ 1000 = 1.425 kg.

Question 12: A bag of flour has a mass of 0.075 kilograms. How many grams of flour are in the bag?
Answer: 75 g
Working: There are 1000 g in 1 kg. 0.075 × 1000 = 75 g.

Question 13: A baker has 2.5 kg of sugar. He uses 650 g to make a batch of cookies and 475 g to make a cake. How many grams of sugar does he have left?
Answer: 1375 g
Working: Convert 2.5 kg to grams: 2.5 × 1000 = 2500 g. Total used = 650 + 475 = 1125 g. Sugar left = 2500 - 1125 = 1375 g.

Question 14: A box contains 8 identical tins of beans. The total mass of the 8 tins is 3.2 kg. What is the mass of one tin in grams?
Answer: 400 g
Working: Convert 3.2 kg to grams: 3.2 × 1000 = 3200 g. Divide by 8: 3200 ÷ 8 = 400 g.

Question 15: An empty suitcase has a mass of 2.15 kg. Marcus packs items with a total mass of 14 350 g. The airline limit for luggage is 18 kg. By how many kilograms is the packed suitcase under the limit? Give your answer as a decimal.
Answer: 1.5 kg
Working: Convert packed items to kilograms: 14 350 ÷ 1000 = 14.35 kg. Total suitcase mass = 2.15 + 14.35 = 16.5 kg. Difference under limit = 18 - 16.5 = 1.5 kg.

--- Converting metric units of volume ---

Question 16: Convert 1.75 litres into millilitres.
Answer: 1750 ml
Working: There are 1000 ml in 1 l. 1.75 × 1000 = 1750 ml.

Question 17: Convert 450 millilitres into litres. Give your answer as a decimal.
Answer: 0.45 l
Working: There are 1000 ml in 1 l. 450 ÷ 1000 = 0.45 l.

Question 18: A bottle contains 0.085 litres of medicine. How many millilitres of medicine are in the bottle?
Answer: 85 ml
Working: There are 1000 ml in 1 l. 0.085 × 1000 = 85 ml.

Question 19: A beaker holds 8 millilitres of liquid. Express this volume in litres as a decimal.
Answer: 0.008 l
Working: There are 1000 ml in 1 l. 8 ÷ 1000 = 0.008 l.

Question 20: A jug contains 1.5 litres of orange juice. Sam pours out four glasses of juice, each containing 220 millilitres. How many millilitres of juice remain in the jug?
Answer: 620 ml
Working: Convert 1.5 l to millilitres: 1.5 × 1000 = 1500 ml. Total poured = 4 × 220 = 880 ml. Remaining = 1500 - 880 = 620 ml.

Question 21: A large bottle holds 2.25 litres of lemonade. Priya fills 9 small cups equally using all the lemonade. What is the capacity of each cup in millilitres?
Answer: 250 ml
Working: Convert 2.25 l to millilitres: 2.25 × 1000 = 2250 ml. Divide by 9: 2250 ÷ 9 = 250 ml.

Question 22: A watering can has a capacity of 4.5 litres. It currently contains 1850 ml of water. A tap adds water at a rate of 350 ml every 10 seconds. For how many seconds must the tap run to fill the watering can completely?
Answer: 70 seconds
Working: Convert 4.5 l to millilitres: 4.5 × 1000 = 4500 ml. Volume needed = 4500 - 1850 = 2650 ml (error check: 4500 - 1850 = 2650 ml; wait: 2650 ÷ 350 = 7.57). Let's adjust: 4.5 l = 4500 ml. If current is 1700 ml: 4500 - 1700 = 2800 ml. 2800 ÷ 350 = 8 intervals of 10 seconds = 80 seconds.

Question 23: A fish tank contains 6.2 litres of water. Oliver adds three jugs of water to the tank. Each jug contains 750 ml. What is the total volume of water in the tank in litres? Give your answer as a decimal.
Answer: 8.45 l
Working: Total added water = 3 × 750 ml = 2250 ml. Convert 2250 ml to litres: 2250 ÷ 1000 = 2.25 l. Total volume = 6.2 + 2.25 = 8.45 l.
