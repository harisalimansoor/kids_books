=== Topic: Distinguishing regular and irregular polygons based on equal sides and equal angles ===
Domain: Geometry: Properties of Shapes

--- Distinguishing regular and irregular polygons based on equal sides and equal angles ---

Question 1: A 2D shape is a regular polygon if it has two specific geometric properties. One property is that all of its sides are equal in length. What is the other property?
Answer: All of its angles are equal
Working: A regular polygon must have all sides of equal length and all interior angles of equal size.

Question 2: A triangle has side lengths of 7 cm, 7 cm, and 7 cm. Each of its three interior angles measures 60°. Is this triangle regular or irregular?
Answer: Regular
Working: All 3 sides are equal (7 cm) and all 3 angles are equal (60°), so the triangle is regular.

Question 3: A rectangle has side lengths of 8 cm, 3 cm, 8 cm, and 3 cm. All four of its interior angles are 90°. Is this rectangle regular or irregular?
Answer: Irregular
Working: Although all four angles are equal (90°), the sides are not all equal in length (8 cm and 3 cm), so the rectangle is irregular.

Question 4: A rhombus has four sides that are each 6 cm long. Two of its angles measure 70° and two measure 110°. State whether the rhombus is regular or irregular.
Answer: Irregular
Working: A regular polygon must have both equal sides and equal angles. The rhombus has equal sides, but its angles are not all equal (70° and 110°), so it is irregular.

Question 5: What is the special name for a regular quadrilateral that has four equal sides and four equal angles of 90°?
Answer: Square
Working: A regular 4-sided polygon has 4 equal sides and 4 equal right angles (90°), which is a square.

Question 6: A regular hexagon has a perimeter of 48 cm. Calculate the length of one of its sides in centimetres.
Answer: 8 cm
Working: A regular hexagon has 6 equal sides. 48 ÷ 6 = 8 cm.

Question 7: A polygon has six sides. Five of the sides measure 9 cm and one side measures 11 cm. Is this shape a regular hexagon or an irregular hexagon?
Answer: Irregular hexagon
Working: The shape has 6 sides, making it a hexagon. Because not all sides are equal in length (one is 11 cm while the others are 9 cm), it is an irregular hexagon.

Question 8: Liam says: 'If all the angles in a quadrilateral are 90°, the shape must be regular.' Name a quadrilateral that proves Liam is wrong.
Answer: Rectangle
Working: An oblong/non-square rectangle has four 90° angles, but adjacent sides have different lengths, so it is irregular.

Question 9: A regular pentagon has 5 equal sides and 5 equal angles. The sum of all its interior angles is 540°. Calculate the size of one interior angle in degrees.
Answer: 108°
Working: All 5 interior angles of a regular pentagon are equal. 540 ÷ 5 = 108°.

Question 10: A regular octagon has sides of length 7 cm. An irregular octagon has side lengths of 7 cm, 7 cm, 7 cm, 7 cm, 7 cm, 7 cm, 7 cm, and 12 cm. What is the difference between their perimeters in centimetres?
Answer: 5 cm
Working: Perimeter of regular octagon = 8 × 7 = 56 cm. Perimeter of irregular octagon = (7 × 7) + 12 = 49 + 12 = 61 cm. Difference = 61 - 56 = 5 cm.

Question 11: A 5-sided polygon has side lengths of 4 cm, 4 cm, 4 cm, 4 cm, and 4 cm. Four of its interior angles are 110° and one interior angle is 100°. State whether this polygon is regular or irregular, and give the mathematical reason why.
Answer: Irregular because its angles are not all equal
Working: Although all 5 sides are equal in length (4 cm), the interior angles are not all equal (four are 110° and one is 100°). A regular polygon must have both equal sides and equal angles.

Question 12: The interior angles of a regular hexagon add up to 720°. Work out the size of one interior angle of a regular hexagon in degrees.
Answer: 120°
Working: A regular hexagon has 6 equal angles. 720 ÷ 6 = 120°.
