=== Topic: Formal multiplication of up to 4-digit by 1-digit and 2-digit numbers ===
Domain: Multiplication and Division

--- Multiplication of up to 4-digit by 1-digit numbers ---

Question 1: Calculate 413 × 3.
Answer: 1,239
Working: Multiply each digit by 3 starting from the ones: 3 × 3 = 9, 10 × 3 = 30, 400 × 3 = 1,200. Sum: 1,200 + 30 + 9 = 1,239.

Question 2: A sports shop receives 4 boxes of tennis balls. Each box contains 235 tennis balls. How many tennis balls are there in total?
Answer: 940
Working: Multiply 235 by 4: 5 × 4 = 20 (write 0, carry 2), 3 × 4 + 2 = 14 (write 4, carry 1), 2 × 4 + 1 = 9. Total = 940.

Question 3: Calculate 3,428 × 6.
Answer: 20,568
Working: Multiply using short multiplication: 8 × 6 = 48 (write 8, carry 4), 2 × 6 + 4 = 16 (write 6, carry 1), 4 × 6 + 1 = 25 (write 5, carry 2), 3 × 6 + 2 = 20. Total = 20,568.

Question 4: A cinema sells 1,485 tickets for £7 each. How much money was taken in total?
Answer: £10,395
Working: Multiply 1,485 by 7: 5 × 7 = 35, 80 × 7 + 30 = 590, 400 × 7 + 500 = 3,300, 1,000 × 7 + 3,000 = 10,000. Total = £10,395.

Question 5: A delivery van travels 2,468 miles each month. How many miles does it travel in 7 months?
Answer: 17,276
Working: Multiply 2,468 by 7: 8 × 7 = 56 (carry 5), 6 × 7 + 5 = 47 (carry 4), 4 × 7 + 4 = 32 (carry 3), 2 × 7 + 3 = 17. Total = 17,276 miles.

Question 6: In the multiplication calculation 2,_45 × 6 = 14,070, what is the missing hundreds digit?
Answer: 3
Working: Divide 14,070 by 6 to find the multiplicand: 14,070 ÷ 6 = 2,345. The missing hundreds digit is 3.

Question 7: School A collects 2,175 plastic bottles each week for 4 weeks. School B collects 1,820 plastic bottles each week for 5 weeks. How many more bottles does School B collect than School A?
Answer: 400
Working: School A total: 2,175 × 4 = 8,700. School B total: 1,820 × 5 = 9,100. Difference: 9,100 - 8,700 = 400 bottles.

--- Long multiplication of up to 4-digit by 2-digit numbers ---

Question 8: Calculate 234 × 12.
Answer: 2,808
Working: 234 × 2 = 468; 234 × 10 = 2,340; 468 + 2,340 = 2,808.

Question 9: A farmer packs 145 eggs into each crate. How many eggs are there in 14 crates?
Answer: 2,030
Working: 145 × 4 = 580; 145 × 10 = 1,450; 580 + 1,450 = 2,030 eggs.

Question 10: Calculate 528 × 34.
Answer: 17,952
Working: 528 × 4 = 2,112; 528 × 30 = 15,840; 2,112 + 15,840 = 17,952.

Question 11: A passenger train has 18 carriages. Each carriage has 116 seats. How many seats are on the train altogether?
Answer: 2,088
Working: 116 × 8 = 928; 116 × 10 = 1,160; 928 + 1,160 = 2,088 seats.

Question 12: Calculate 2,314 × 23.
Answer: 53,222
Working: 2,314 × 3 = 6,942; 2,314 × 20 = 46,280; 6,942 + 46,280 = 53,222.

Question 13: Calculate 3,648 × 27.
Answer: 98,496
Working: 3,648 × 7 = 25,536; 3,648 × 20 = 72,960; 25,536 + 72,960 = 98,496.

Question 14: An arena sells 1,425 tickets for an event at £32 each. What is the total amount of money taken from ticket sales?
Answer: £45,600
Working: 1,425 × 2 = 2,850; 1,425 × 30 = 42,750; 2,850 + 42,750 = £45,600.

Question 15: A publisher prints 1,650 copies of a book each day for 25 days. The total order is for 45,000 copies. How many more copies do they need to print to complete the order?
Answer: 3,750
Working: Printed copies: 1,650 × 25 = 41,250 (1,650 × 5 = 8,250; 1,650 × 20 = 33,000; 8,250 + 33,000 = 41,250). Copies remaining: 45,000 - 41,250 = 3,750.

Question 16: In the multiplication calculation 1,3_4 × 26 = 35,464, what is the missing tens digit?
Answer: 6
Working: Divide 35,464 by 26: 35,464 ÷ 26 = 1,364. The missing tens digit is 6.
