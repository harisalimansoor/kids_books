=== Topic: Multiplying and dividing whole numbers and decimals by 10, 100, and 1000 ===
Domain: Multiplication and Division

--- Multiplying whole numbers by 10, 100, and 1000 ---

Question 1: Calculate 340 × 100.
Answer: 34,000
Working: Multiplying 340 by 100 shifts each digit two places to the left: 340 × 100 = 34,000.

Question 2: Work out 78 × 1000.
Answer: 78,000
Working: Multiplying 78 by 1000 shifts the digits three places to the left: 78 × 1000 = 78,000.

Question 3: A school buys 100 boxes of pencils. Each box contains 24 pencils. How many pencils does the school buy altogether?
Answer: 2400
Working: Multiply the number of boxes by pencils per box: 100 × 24 = 2400.

Question 4: Find the missing number: 65 × _____ = 65,000.
Answer: 1000
Working: 65 has moved 3 places to the left to make 65,000, so the missing multiplier is 1000.

Question 5: A warehouse stores 52 crates of bottles. Each crate holds 1000 bottles. How many bottles are stored in total?
Answer: 52,000
Working: 52 × 1000 = 52,000 bottles.

--- Dividing whole numbers by 10, 100, and 1000 ---

Question 6: Calculate 4900 ÷ 100.
Answer: 49
Working: Dividing by 100 moves all digits two places to the right: 4900 ÷ 100 = 49.

Question 7: Calculate 63 ÷ 10.
Answer: 6.3
Working: Dividing 63 by 10 moves the digits one place to the right: 63 ÷ 10 = 6.3.

Question 8: Find the result of 750 ÷ 1000 as a decimal.
Answer: 0.75
Working: Dividing 750 by 1000 shifts the digits three places to the right: 750 ÷ 1000 = 0.75.

Question 9: A factory packs 8200 screws equally into 100 bags. How many screws are in each bag?
Answer: 82
Working: 8200 ÷ 100 = 82 screws per bag.

Question 10: Work out the missing number: _____ ÷ 100 = 5.4.
Answer: 540
Working: Use the inverse operation: 5.4 × 100 = 540.
