=== Topic: Multi-step problems combining operations, simple rates, and fractional scaling ===
Domain: Multiplication and Division

--- Multi-step problems combining operations ---

Question 1: A baker packs 6 boxes of cupcakes. Each box contains 12 cupcakes. He sells 45 cupcakes in the morning. How many cupcakes does he have left?
Answer: 27
Working: Total cupcakes baked = 6 × 12 = 72. Cupcakes left = 72 - 45 = 27.

Question 2: A school buys 4 packs of whiteboards with 25 boards in each pack, and 3 packs of pens with 30 pens in each pack. How many items were bought altogether?
Answer: 190
Working: Whiteboards = 4 × 25 = 100. Pens = 3 × 30 = 90. Total items = 100 + 90 = 190.

Question 3: A cinema sells adult tickets for £8 each and child tickets for £5 each. A family buys 3 adult tickets and 4 child tickets. They pay with a £50 note. How much change do they receive?
Answer: £6
Working: Adult tickets cost 3 × £8 = £24. Child tickets cost 4 × £5 = £20. Total cost = £24 + £20 = £44. Change = £50 - £44 = £6.

Question 4: A factory produces 1 240 pencils each day. Over 5 working days, all the pencils produced are packed into boxes of 8. How many full boxes of pencils are made?
Answer: 775
Working: Total pencils in 5 days = 1 240 × 5 = 6 200. Number of full boxes = 6 200 ÷ 8 = 775.

Question 5: A charity raises money by selling 350 raffle tickets at £3 each. The total cost of the prizes is £175 and the printing costs are £25. The remaining money is shared equally between 5 local projects. How much money does each project receive?
Answer: £170
Working: Total money raised from tickets = 350 × £3 = £1 050. Total costs = £175 + £25 = £200. Remaining money = £1 050 - £200 = £850. Money per project = £850 ÷ 5 = £170.

--- Simple rates ---

Question 6: A car travels at a steady speed of 45 miles per hour. How far does the car travel in 3 hours?
Answer: 135 miles
Working: Distance = 45 × 3 = 135 miles.

Question 7: A machine prints 120 leaflets in 4 minutes at a constant rate. How many leaflets does the machine print in 1 minute?
Answer: 30
Working: Rate per minute = 120 ÷ 4 = 30 leaflets.

Question 8: Apples cost £1.80 for 1 kg. What is the total cost of 4 kg of apples?
Answer: £7.20
Working: Total cost = 4 × £1.80 = £7.20.

Question 9: A runner drinks 250 ml of water every 15 minutes during a race. How many litres of water does the runner drink in 2 hours?
Answer: 2 litres
Working: 2 hours = 120 minutes. Number of 15-minute intervals = 120 ÷ 15 = 8. Water drunk = 8 × 250 ml = 2 000 ml = 2 litres.

Question 10: A printing press produces 450 pages in 5 minutes. At this same rate, how many pages will it produce in 12 minutes?
Answer: 1 080
Working: Pages per minute = 450 ÷ 5 = 90 pages. In 12 minutes = 12 × 90 = 1 080 pages.

Question 11: Tap A fills a 60-litre water tank in 4 minutes. Tap B fills the same 60-litre tank in 6 minutes. How many litres of water will both taps together pour into the tank in 3 minutes?
Answer: 75 litres
Working: Tap A rate = 60 ÷ 4 = 15 litres per minute. Tap B rate = 60 ÷ 6 = 10 litres per minute. Combined rate = 15 + 10 = 25 litres per minute. In 3 minutes = 3 × 25 = 75 litres.

--- Fractional scaling ---

Question 12: A recipe for 4 pancakes needs 200 g of flour. How many grams of flour are needed to make 2 pancakes?
Answer: 100 g
Working: 2 pancakes is 1/2 of 4 pancakes. Flour needed = 200 ÷ 2 = 100 g.

Question 13: A piece of ribbon is 120 cm long. Maya cuts off a piece that is 3/4 the length of the ribbon. How long is the piece of ribbon Maya cuts off?
Answer: 90 cm
Working: 1/4 of 120 cm = 120 ÷ 4 = 30 cm. 3/4 of 120 cm = 30 × 3 = 90 cm.

Question 14: A large bottle holds 1 500 ml of juice. A small bottle holds 2/5 of the amount of juice in the large bottle. How much juice does the small bottle hold?
Answer: 600 ml
Working: 1/5 of 1 500 ml = 1 500 ÷ 5 = 300 ml. 2/5 of 1 500 ml = 300 × 2 = 600 ml.

Question 15: A recipe for 10 flapjacks requires 80 g of butter. How many grams of butter are required to make 15 flapjacks?
Answer: 120 g
Working: Scaling factor = 15/10 = 1 1/2. Butter for 5 flapjacks = 80 ÷ 2 = 40 g. Butter for 15 flapjacks = 80 + 40 = 120 g (or 80 × 3/2 = 120 g).

Question 16: A swimming club has 180 members. 2/3 of the members are juniors. 3/4 of the junior members are girls. How many junior members are girls?
Answer: 90
Working: Junior members = 2/3 of 180 = (180 ÷ 3) × 2 = 120. Girls = 3/4 of 120 = (120 ÷ 4) × 3 = 90.

Question 17: A standard model car is 40 cm long. A scale replica is made to 3/8 of the length of the standard model. A miniature pocket version is then made to 2/3 of the replica's length. What is the length of the miniature pocket version in centimetres?
Answer: 10 cm
Working: Length of replica = 3/8 of 40 cm = (40 ÷ 8) × 3 = 15 cm. Length of pocket version = 2/3 of 15 cm = (15 ÷ 3) × 2 = 10 cm.
