--- Columnar subtraction of numbers with more than 4 digits ---

Question 1: Calculate 68 452 − 25 181
Answer: 43 271
Working: Align the numbers into place-value columns:
  68 452
− 25 181

Ones: 2 − 1 = 1
Tens: 5 − 8 requires an exchange. Exchange 1 hundred for 10 tens: 15 − 8 = 7
Hundreds: 3 − 1 = 2
Thousands: 8 − 5 = 3
Ten thousands: 6 − 2 = 4

Result: 43 271

Question 3: A stadium has a capacity of 72 450 seats. For a football match, 58 674 tickets are sold. How many seats remain unsold?
Answer: 13 776
Working: Subtract tickets sold from total capacity: 72 450 − 58 674.

Ones: 0 − 4 (exchange 1 ten) → 10 − 4 = 6
Tens: 4 − 7 (exchange 1 hundred) → 14 − 7 = 7
Hundreds: 3 − 6 (exchange 1 thousand) → 13 − 6 = 7
Thousands: 1 − 8 (exchange 1 ten thousand) → 11 − 8 = 3
Ten thousands: 6 − 5 = 1

Total unsold seats = 13 776
