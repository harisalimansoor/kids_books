=== Topic: Roman numerals up to 1000 (M), historical years, and numeral ordering ===
Domain: Number and Place Value

--- Roman numerals up to 1000 (M) ---

Question 1: Write the number 450 as a Roman numeral.
Answer: CDL
Working: 400 is written as CD (500 - 100) and 50 is written as L. Combining these gives CDL.

Question 2: What number is represented by the Roman numeral DCCLXVIII?
Answer: 768
Working: DCC = 500 + 200 = 700. LX = 50 + 10 = 60. VIII = 5 + 3 = 8. 700 + 60 + 8 = 768.

Question 3: Write the number 944 as a Roman numeral.
Answer: CMXLIV
Working: Partition 944 into 900 + 40 + 4. 900 = CM, 40 = XL, 4 = IV. Combining them gives CMXLIV.

Question 4: What is the value of the Roman numeral CMLXXXVII?
Answer: 987
Working: CM = 900, LXXX = 80, VII = 7. 900 + 80 + 7 = 987.

Question 5: Calculate CCL + CD. Write your answer as a Roman numeral.
Answer: DCL
Working: CCL = 250 and CD = 400. 250 + 400 = 650. 650 in Roman numerals is DC (600) + L (50) = DCL.

Question 6: Evaluate DCCXX ÷ 8. Write your final answer as a Roman numeral.
Answer: XC
Working: DCCXX = 700 + 20 = 720. 720 ÷ 8 = 90. 90 written as a Roman numeral is XC.

--- Historical years ---

Question 7: A stone carving on an old gatehouse displays the year MLXVI. Write this year in Arabic numerals.
Answer: 1066
Working: M = 1000, LX = 60, VI = 6. 1000 + 60 + 6 = 1066.

Question 8: The Great Fire of London happened in the year 1666. Write 1666 in Roman numerals.
Answer: MDCLXVI
Working: 1000 = M, 600 = DC, 60 = LX, 6 = VI. Combining these gives MDCLXVI.

Question 9: A museum was founded in the year MDCCCXL. Write this year in Arabic numerals.
Answer: 1840
Working: M = 1000, DCCC = 800, XL = 40. 1000 + 800 + 40 = 1840.

Question 10: The end credits of a television programme show the year of production as MCMXCVI. Which year does this represent in Arabic numerals?
Answer: 1996
Working: M = 1000, CM = 900, XC = 90, VI = 6. 1000 + 900 + 90 + 6 = 1996.

Question 11: A stone bridge was built in the year MCXLV and widened in the year MCLXX. How many years after its construction was the bridge widened?
Answer: 25
Working: MCXLV = 1000 + 100 + 40 + 5 = 1145. MCLXX = 1000 + 100 + 70 = 1170. 1170 - 1145 = 25 years.

Question 12: A town hall was opened in the year MDCCCXXIV and celebrated a major centenary anniversary in the year MCMXXIV. How many years passed between these two dates?
Answer: 100
Working: MDCCCXXIV = 1000 + 800 + 20 + 4 = 1824. MCMXXIV = 1000 + 900 + 20 + 4 = 1924. 1924 - 1824 = 100 years.

--- Numeral ordering ---

Question 13: Write these Roman numerals in order of size, starting with the smallest: CL, XC, CC, LXX.
Answer: LXX, XC, CL, CC
Working: Convert each to Arabic numerals: CL = 150, XC = 90, CC = 200, LXX = 70. Ordered from smallest to largest: 70, 90, 150, 200, which is LXX, XC, CL, CC.

Question 14: Write the correct symbol, <, > or =, to make the statement true: CML ___ DCCCXCIX
Answer: >
Working: CML = 900 + 50 = 950. DCCCXCIX = 800 + 90 + 9 = 899. Since 950 is greater than 899, CML > DCCCXCIX.

Question 15: Write these Roman numerals in order of size, starting with the smallest: CD, CCCXC, DX, CDL.
Answer: CCCXC, CD, CDL, DX
Working: Convert to numbers: CD = 400, CCCXC = 390, DX = 510, CDL = 450. Ordering from smallest to largest gives 390, 400, 450, 510, which corresponds to CCCXC, CD, CDL, DX.

Question 16: Order these historical years from earliest to latest: MCML, MDCCC, MMV, MCMX.
Answer: MDCCC, MCMX, MCML, MMV
Working: Convert each year to Arabic numerals: MCML = 1950, MDCCC = 1800, MMV = 2005, MCMX = 1910. Ordered from earliest to latest: 1800 (MDCCC), 1910 (MCMX), 1950 (MCML), 2005 (MMV).

Question 17: Here are five Roman numerals: DCIV, DXV, DCXL, DXIX, DCXX. Which Roman numeral is the middle value when they are placed in order from smallest to largest?
Answer: DCIV
Working: Convert each to numbers: DCIV = 604, DXV = 515, DCXL = 640, DXIX = 519, DCXX = 620. In ascending order: 515 (DXV), 519 (DXIX), 604 (DCIV), 620 (DCXX), 640 (DCXL). The middle value is 604, which is DCIV.

Question 18: Which of the following Roman numerals represents the greatest value: CMLIV, CMXLIX, CMLXI, or CMLX?
Answer: CMLXI
Working: Convert each value: CMLIV = 954, CMXLIX = 949, CMLXI = 961, CMLX = 960. 961 is the greatest value, so CMLXI represents the greatest value.
