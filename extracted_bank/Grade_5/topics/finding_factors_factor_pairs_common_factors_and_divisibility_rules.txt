=== Topic: Finding factors, factor pairs, common factors, and divisibility rules ===
Domain: Multiplication and Division

--- Finding factors ---

Question 1: List all the factors of 20 in order from smallest to largest.
Answer: 1, 2, 4, 5, 10, 20
Working: Find pairs of numbers that multiply to 20: 1 × 20 = 20, 2 × 10 = 20, 4 × 5 = 20. List them in ascending order: 1, 2, 4, 5, 10, 20.

Question 2: Write down all the factors of 48 in ascending order.
Answer: 1, 2, 3, 4, 6, 8, 12, 16, 24, 48
Working: Find factor pairs of 48: 1 × 48, 2 × 24, 3 × 16, 4 × 12, 6 × 8. List in ascending order: 1, 2, 3, 4, 6, 8, 12, 16, 24, 48.

Question 3: A number is between 40 and 50. It has exactly three factors. What is the number?
Answer: 49
Working: Numbers with exactly three factors are squares of prime numbers. 7 × 7 = 49. The factors of 49 are 1, 7, and 49.

Question 4: How many factors does the number 72 have in total?
Answer: 12
Working: The factor pairs of 72 are 1 × 72, 2 × 36, 3 × 24, 4 × 18, 6 × 12, and 8 × 9. Counting all factors gives 1, 2, 3, 4, 6, 8, 9, 12, 18, 24, 36, 72, which is 12 factors.

--- Factor pairs ---

Question 5: Write down all the factor pairs of 32.
Answer: 1 and 32, 2 and 16, 4 and 8
Working: Find pairs of whole numbers that multiply to make 32: 1 × 32 = 32, 2 × 16 = 32, 4 × 8 = 32.

Question 6: A rectangle has an area of 36 cm² and whole-number side lengths in centimetres. List all the possible pairs of side lengths (length and width).
Answer: 1 cm and 36 cm, 2 cm and 18 cm, 3 cm and 12 cm, 4 cm and 9 cm, 6 cm and 6 cm
Working: Find all factor pairs of 36: 1 × 36, 2 × 18, 3 × 12, 4 × 9, 6 × 6.

Question 7: Two whole numbers multiply together to give 54. The difference between the two numbers is 3. What are the two numbers?
Answer: 6 and 9
Working: List factor pairs of 54: (1, 54), (2, 27), (3, 18), (6, 9). Find the pair with a difference of 3: 9 - 6 = 3. The numbers are 6 and 9.

Question 8: A florist has 60 flowers to place into equal bunches. Each bunch must have more than 5 flowers but fewer than 15 flowers. List all the possible numbers of flowers that could be in each bunch.
Answer: 6, 10, 12
Working: Factors of 60 are 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 10, 12, 15, 20, 30, 60. Factors strictly between 5 and 15 are 6, 10, and 12.

--- Common factors ---

Question 9: List all the common factors of 12 and 18.
Answer: 1, 2, 3, 6
Working: Factors of 12: 1, 2, 3, 4, 6, 12. Factors of 18: 1, 2, 3, 6, 9, 18. Common factors: 1, 2, 3, 6.

Question 10: Find the highest common factor (HCF) of 24 and 40.
Answer: 8
Working: Factors of 24: 1, 2, 3, 4, 6, 8, 12, 24. Factors of 40: 1, 2, 4, 5, 8, 10, 20, 40. Common factors: 1, 2, 4, 8. The highest is 8.

Question 11: Find all the common factors of 30 and 45.
Answer: 1, 3, 5, 15
Working: Factors of 30: 1, 2, 3, 5, 6, 10, 15, 30. Factors of 45: 1, 3, 5, 9, 15, 45. Common factors: 1, 3, 5, 15.

Question 12: What is the highest common factor of 36, 54, and 72?
Answer: 18
Working: Factors of 36: 1, 2, 3, 4, 6, 9, 12, 18, 36. Factors of 54: 1, 2, 3, 6, 9, 18, 27, 54. Factors of 72: 1, 2, 3, 4, 6, 8, 9, 12, 18, 24, 36, 72. The largest common factor is 18.

Question 13: A baker has 36 bread rolls and 48 cheese muffins. She packs them into identical boxes with no items left over, so each box contains the same number of rolls and the same number of muffins. What is the greatest number of identical boxes she can make?
Answer: 12
Working: Find the highest common factor of 36 and 48. Factors of 36: 1, 2, 3, 4, 6, 9, 12, 18, 36. Factors of 48: 1, 2, 3, 4, 6, 8, 12, 16, 24, 48. The greatest common factor is 12.

--- Divisibility rules ---

Question 14: Use divisibility rules to determine whether 7,524 is divisible by 9. Write Yes or No.
Answer: Yes
Working: Add the digits: 7 + 5 + 2 + 4 = 18. Since 18 is a multiple of 9, the number 7,524 is divisible by 9.

Question 15: Look at the four numbers: 340, 525, 682, 950. Which of these numbers are divisible by both 2 and 5?
Answer: 340 and 950
Working: A number divisible by both 2 and 5 must end in 0. The numbers ending in 0 are 340 and 950.

Question 16: A 4-digit number is 4,32_ with the ones digit missing. What is the smallest digit that can be put in the ones position to make the number divisible by 3?
Answer: 0
Working: Sum the given digits: 4 + 3 + 2 = 9. 9 is already a multiple of 3. The smallest digit that can be added is 0 (since 9 + 0 = 9).

Question 17: A 4-digit number is written as 6, _ 40 with the hundreds digit missing. The number is a multiple of 9. What is the missing hundreds digit?
Answer: 8
Working: For a number to be divisible by 9, the sum of its digits must be a multiple of 9. Sum of known digits: 6 + 4 + 0 = 10. The next multiple of 9 is 18. 18 - 10 = 8.

Question 18: A number is divisible by 6 if it is an even number and the sum of its digits is a multiple of 3. Is the number 8,142 divisible by 6? Write Yes or No.
Answer: Yes
Working: 8,142 ends in 2, so it is an even number. Sum of digits: 8 + 1 + 4 + 2 = 15, which is a multiple of 3. Therefore, 8,142 is divisible by 6.
