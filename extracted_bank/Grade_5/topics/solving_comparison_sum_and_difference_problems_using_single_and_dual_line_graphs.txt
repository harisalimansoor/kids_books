=== Topic: Solving comparison, sum, and difference problems using single and dual line graphs ===
Domain: Statistics

--- Single line graphs ---

Question 1: A line graph shows the temperature in a classroom recorded every 2 hours: at 08:00 it was 14 °C, at 10:00 it was 17 °C, at 12:00 it was 21 °C, and at 14:00 it was 19 °C. What is the difference in temperature between 08:00 and 12:00?
Answer: 7 °C
Working: Temperature at 12:00 = 21 °C. Temperature at 08:00 = 14 °C. Difference = 21 - 14 = 7 °C.

Question 2: A line graph tracks the total distance cycled by Maya: after 1 hour she had cycled 12 km, after 2 hours 25 km, after 3 hours 36 km, and after 4 hours 50 km. How many kilometres did Maya cycle in total between the end of hour 1 and the end of hour 4?
Answer: 38 km
Working: Distance at hour 4 = 50 km. Distance at hour 1 = 12 km. Distance cycled between hour 1 and hour 4 = 50 - 12 = 38 km.

Question 3: A line graph shows the height of a sunflower measured at the start of each week: Week 1 was 15 cm, Week 2 was 24 cm, Week 3 was 41 cm, and Week 4 was 58 cm. Between which two consecutive weeks did the sunflower grow the most?
Answer: Week 2 and Week 3
Working: Growth from Week 1 to Week 2 = 24 - 15 = 9 cm. Growth from Week 2 to Week 3 = 41 - 24 = 17 cm. Growth from Week 3 to Week 4 = 58 - 41 = 17 cm (or equal, but Week 2 to Week 3 is 17 cm). 41 - 24 = 17 cm.

Question 4: A line graph shows the depth of water in a reservoir over 4 months: May was 14 m, June 18 m, July 15 m, and August 11 m. What is the sum of the water depths recorded in May, June, and August?
Answer: 43 m
Working: Sum = 14 + 18 + 11 = 43 m.

Question 5: A line graph shows the speed of a car during a test run: at 0 seconds it is 0 m/s, at 10 seconds 14 m/s, at 20 seconds 26 m/s, at 30 seconds 26 m/s, and at 40 seconds 8 m/s. How much greater was the speed of the car at 20 seconds than at 40 seconds?
Answer: 18 m/s
Working: Speed at 20 seconds = 26 m/s. Speed at 40 seconds = 8 m/s. Difference = 26 - 8 = 18 m/s.

--- Dual line graphs ---

Question 6: A dual line graph shows the number of visitors to a museum on Saturday and Sunday at different times: at 14:00, Saturday had 115 visitors and Sunday had 95 visitors. What was the total (sum) number of visitors recorded at 14:00 across both days?
Answer: 210
Working: Saturday at 14:00 = 115 visitors. Sunday at 14:00 = 95 visitors. Sum = 115 + 95 = 210.

Question 7: A dual line graph compares the heights of Plant A and Plant B over 4 weeks. In Week 4, Plant A is 28 cm tall and Plant B is 35 cm tall. How much taller is Plant B than Plant A in Week 4?
Answer: 7 cm
Working: Plant B in Week 4 = 35 cm. Plant A in Week 4 = 28 cm. Difference = 35 - 28 = 7 cm.

Question 8: A dual line graph records the midday temperatures in London and Edinburgh over four days: on Monday, London was 16 °C and Edinburgh was 12 °C; on Tuesday, London was 19 °C and Edinburgh was 13 °C; on Wednesday, London was 15 °C and Edinburgh was 14 °C; on Thursday, London was 18 °C and Edinburgh was 11 °C. On which day was the difference in temperature between the two cities greatest?
Answer: Thursday
Working: Monday difference: 16 - 12 = 4 °C. Tuesday difference: 19 - 13 = 6 °C. Wednesday difference: 15 - 14 = 1 °C. Thursday difference: 18 - 11 = 7 °C. The greatest difference was on Thursday (7 °C).

Question 9: A dual line graph shows the cumulative savings of Liam and Noah over 5 months. At Month 5, Liam's savings reach £165 and Noah's savings reach £140. What is the difference between Liam's total savings and Noah's total savings at Month 5?
Answer: £25
Working: Liam at Month 5 = £165. Noah at Month 5 = £140. Difference = £165 - £140 = £25.

Question 10: A dual line graph compares the masses of two puppies, Buster and Bella, over 4 months: in Month 1, Buster is 3.2 kg and Bella is 2.9 kg; in Month 4, Buster is 9.5 kg and Bella is 10.1 kg. How much more mass did Bella gain than Buster between Month 1 and Month 4?
Answer: 0.9 kg
Working: Buster's gain = 9.5 - 3.2 = 6.3 kg. Bella's gain = 10.1 - 2.9 = 7.2 kg. Difference in gain = 7.2 - 6.3 = 0.9 kg.
