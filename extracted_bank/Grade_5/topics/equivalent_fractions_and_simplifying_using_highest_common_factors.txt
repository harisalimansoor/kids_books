=== Topic: Equivalent fractions and simplifying using highest common factors ===
Domain: Fractions, Decimals and Percentages

--- Equivalent fractions ---

Question 1: Complete the equivalent fraction: 3/5 = ?/20
Answer: 12
Working: Multiply the denominator by 4 to get 20 (5 × 4 = 20). Multiply the numerator by 4: 3 × 4 = 12.

Question 2: Find the missing denominator to make the fractions equivalent: 2/7 = 6/?
Answer: 21
Working: Multiply the numerator by 3 to get 6 (2 × 3 = 6). Multiply the denominator by 3: 7 × 3 = 21.

Question 3: Write an equivalent fraction for 4/6 that has a denominator of 18.
Answer: 12/18
Working: The denominator 6 is multiplied by 3 to reach 18 (6 × 3 = 18). Multiply the numerator by 3: 4 × 3 = 12. The equivalent fraction is 12/18.

Question 4: Fill in the two missing numbers to complete the chain of equivalent fractions: 3/8 = 9/? = ?/40
Answer: 24 and 15
Working: For 9/?, multiply numerator and denominator of 3/8 by 3: 8 × 3 = 24. For ?/40, multiply numerator and denominator of 3/8 by 5: 3 × 5 = 15. The missing numbers are 24 and 15.

Question 5: A baker bakes 24 loaves of bread. 18 of the loaves are wholemeal. Express the fraction 18/24 as an equivalent fraction with a denominator of 4.
Answer: 3/4
Working: Divide both the numerator and the denominator of 18/24 by 6: 18 ÷ 6 = 3 and 24 ÷ 6 = 4. The equivalent fraction is 3/4.

Question 6: Write an equivalent fraction to 7/10 that has a denominator of 100.
Answer: 70/100
Working: Multiply both the numerator and denominator by 10: 7 × 10 = 70 and 10 × 10 = 100. The equivalent fraction is 70/100.

--- Simplifying using highest common factors ---

Question 7: Find the highest common factor of 12 and 18.
Answer: 6
Working: Factors of 12: 1, 2, 3, 4, 6, 12. Factors of 18: 1, 2, 3, 6, 9, 18. The common factors are 1, 2, 3, 6. The highest common factor is 6.

Question 8: Simplify the fraction 14/21 to its simplest form by dividing by the highest common factor.
Answer: 2/3
Working: The highest common factor of 14 and 21 is 7. Divide both numerator and denominator by 7: 14 ÷ 7 = 2 and 21 ÷ 7 = 3. The simplest form is 2/3.

Question 9: Write the fraction 18/24 in its simplest form.
Answer: 3/4
Working: The highest common factor of 18 and 24 is 6. Divide both numerator and denominator by 6: 18 ÷ 6 = 3 and 24 ÷ 6 = 4. The simplest form is 3/4.

Question 10: Write the fraction 15/25 in its simplest form.
Answer: 3/5
Working: The highest common factor of 15 and 25 is 5. Divide the numerator and denominator by 5: 15 ÷ 5 = 3 and 25 ÷ 5 = 5. The simplest form is 3/5.

Question 11: In a class of 30 pupils, 12 walk to school. What fraction of the pupils walk to school? Give your answer in its simplest form.
Answer: 2/5
Working: The fraction is 12/30. The highest common factor of 12 and 30 is 6. Divide both numbers by 6: 12 ÷ 6 = 2 and 30 ÷ 6 = 5. The simplest form is 2/5.

Question 12: A piece of ribbon is 40 cm long. Maya cuts off 28 cm. What fraction of the whole ribbon did Maya cut off? Give your answer in its simplest form.
Answer: 7/10
Working: The fraction is 28/40. The highest common factor of 28 and 40 is 4. Divide both the numerator and denominator by 4: 28 ÷ 4 = 7 and 40 ÷ 4 = 10. The simplest form is 7/10.
