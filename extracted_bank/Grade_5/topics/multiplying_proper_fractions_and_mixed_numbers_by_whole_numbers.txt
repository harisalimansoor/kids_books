=== Topic: Multiplying proper fractions and mixed numbers by whole numbers ===
Domain: Fractions, Decimals and Percentages

--- Multiplying proper fractions by whole numbers ---

Question 1: Calculate 1/7 × 4. Give your answer as a proper fraction.
Answer: 4/7
Working: Multiply the numerator by the whole number: 1 × 4 = 4. The denominator stays 7. So, 1/7 × 4 = 4/7.

Question 2: Calculate 2/9 × 4. Give your answer as a proper fraction.
Answer: 8/9
Working: Multiply the numerator by the whole number: 2 × 4 = 8. The denominator stays 9. So, 2/9 × 4 = 8/9.

Question 3: Calculate 3/5 × 4. Give your answer as a mixed number.
Answer: 2 2/5
Working: Multiply the numerator by the whole number: 3 × 4 = 12, so the fraction is 12/5. Convert 12/5 to a mixed number: 12 ÷ 5 = 2 remainder 2, which gives 2 2/5.

Question 4: A bottle contains 3/8 litre of orange squash. How many litres of squash are in 5 of these bottles? Give your answer as a mixed number.
Answer: 1 7/8 litres
Working: Multiply 3/8 by 5: (3 × 5) / 8 = 15/8 litres. Convert 15/8 to a mixed number: 15 ÷ 8 = 1 with a remainder of 7, giving 1 7/8 litres.

Question 5: A baker uses 4/7 kg of flour for each loaf of bread. How many kilograms of flour does the baker need to make 6 loaves? Give your answer as a mixed number.
Answer: 3 3/7 kg
Working: Multiply 4/7 by 6: 4/7 × 6 = 24/7. Convert 24/7 to a mixed number: 24 ÷ 7 = 3 with a remainder of 3, so 3 3/7 kg.

Question 6: Eva runs 5/6 km every day for 5 days. What is the total distance Eva runs in kilometres? Give your answer as a mixed number in its simplest form.
Answer: 4 1/6 km
Working: Multiply 5/6 by 5: 5/6 × 5 = 25/6. Convert 25/6 to a mixed number: 25 ÷ 6 = 4 with a remainder of 1, so the total distance is 4 1/6 km.

--- Multiplying mixed numbers by whole numbers ---

Question 7: Calculate 2 1/5 × 3. Give your answer as a mixed number.
Answer: 6 3/5
Working: Multiply the whole number part: 2 × 3 = 6. Multiply the fraction part: 1/5 × 3 = 3/5. Combine the parts: 6 + 3/5 = 6 3/5.

Question 8: Calculate 3 2/7 × 2. Give your answer as a mixed number.
Answer: 6 4/7
Working: Multiply the whole number part: 3 × 2 = 6. Multiply the fractional part: 2/7 × 2 = 4/7. Add them together: 6 + 4/7 = 6 4/7.

Question 9: Calculate 2 3/4 × 3. Give your answer as a mixed number.
Answer: 8 1/4
Working: Multiply the whole number part: 2 × 3 = 6. Multiply the fraction part: 3/4 × 3 = 9/4. Convert 9/4 to a mixed number: 2 1/4. Add the parts together: 6 + 2 1/4 = 8 1/4.

Question 10: Each bag of soil weighs 3 2/5 kg. What is the total weight of 4 bags of soil? Give your answer as a mixed number.
Answer: 13 3/5 kg
Working: Multiply the whole number part: 3 × 4 = 12. Multiply the fraction part: 2/5 × 4 = 8/5 = 1 3/5. Add the parts: 12 + 1 3/5 = 13 3/5 kg.

Question 11: An equilateral triangle has sides of length 1 5/8 cm. Calculate the perimeter of the triangle. Give your answer as a mixed number.
Answer: 4 7/8 cm
Working: An equilateral triangle has 3 equal sides. Perimeter = 3 × 1 5/8. Multiply the whole number: 3 × 1 = 3. Multiply the fraction: 3 × 5/8 = 15/8 = 1 7/8. Add the parts: 3 + 1 7/8 = 4 7/8 cm.

Question 12: A recipe needs 2 4/9 cups of oats to make one batch of flapjacks. How many cups of oats are needed to make 4 batches? Give your answer as a mixed number.
Answer: 9 7/9
Working: Multiply 2 4/9 by 4. Whole numbers: 2 × 4 = 8. Fractions: 4/9 × 4 = 16/9 = 1 7/9. Total = 8 + 1 7/9 = 9 7/9 cups.
