=== Topic: Reflections of 2D shapes across horizontal and vertical mirror lines ===
Domain: Geometry: Position and Direction

--- Reflections of 2D shapes across horizontal mirror lines ---

Question 1: A point at (4, 6) is reflected across a horizontal mirror line at y = 4. What are the coordinates of the reflected point?
Answer: (4, 2)
Working: The point (4, 6) is 2 units above the horizontal mirror line y = 4 (6 - 4 = 2). The reflected point must be 2 units below the mirror line: 4 - 2 = 2. The x-coordinate remains unchanged at 4. The reflected point is (4, 2).

Question 2: A triangle has vertices at A(2, 1), B(8, 1), and C(5, 4). The triangle is reflected across a horizontal mirror line at y = 5. What are the coordinates of the reflected vertex C'?
Answer: (5, 6)
Working: Vertex C is at (5, 4), which is 1 unit below the horizontal mirror line y = 5 (5 - 4 = 1). The reflected vertex C' is 1 unit above the mirror line: 5 + 1 = 6. The x-coordinate remains 5. The coordinates of C' are (5, 6).

Question 3: A rectangle has vertices at (3, 2), (7, 2), (7, 5), and (3, 5). It is reflected across a horizontal mirror line at y = 6. What are the coordinates of the reflected vertex that corresponds to (7, 2)?
Answer: (7, 10)
Working: The vertex (7, 2) is 4 units below the mirror line y = 6 (6 - 2 = 4). The reflected vertex is 4 units above the mirror line: 6 + 4 = 10. The x-coordinate stays 7. The reflected vertex is (7, 10).

Question 4: A shape is reflected across a horizontal mirror line. A vertex on the original shape is at (4, 8) and its reflected vertex is at (4, 2). What is the equation of the horizontal mirror line?
Answer: y = 5
Working: The mirror line lies exactly halfway between the y-coordinates of the original and reflected points: (8 + 2) ÷ 2 = 5. Because it is a horizontal line, its equation is y = 5.

Question 5: Triangle PQR has vertices at P(1, 3), Q(5, 3), and R(3, 7). It is reflected across a horizontal mirror line at y = 8. What is the vertical distance between the highest vertex of the original triangle and the lowest vertex of the reflected triangle?
Answer: 2
Working: The highest vertex of the original triangle is R at y = 7 (1 unit below y = 8). When reflected across y = 8, R reflects to (3, 9), which is 1 unit above y = 8. The other vertices P and Q at y = 3 reflect to y = 13. So the lowest vertex of the reflected triangle is at y = 9. The vertical distance between y = 7 and y = 9 is 9 - 7 = 2 units.

Question 6: A square has vertices at (2, 1), (6, 1), (6, 5), and (2, 5). The square is reflected across a horizontal mirror line at y = 6 to form a second square. What is the total combined area of both squares in square units?
Answer: 32
Working: Side length of the original square = 6 - 2 = 4 units. Area of original square = 4 × 4 = 16 square units. Reflection preserves shape and size, so the reflected square also has an area of 16 square units. Total combined area = 16 + 16 = 32 square units.

--- Reflections of 2D shapes across vertical mirror lines ---

Question 7: A point at (3, 5) is reflected across a vertical mirror line at x = 6. What are the coordinates of the reflected point?
Answer: (9, 5)
Working: The point (3, 5) is 3 units to the left of the vertical mirror line x = 6 (6 - 3 = 3). The reflected point is 3 units to the right of the line: 6 + 3 = 9. The y-coordinate remains 5. The reflected point is (9, 5).

Question 8: A triangle has vertices at A(1, 2), B(4, 2), and C(1, 6). It is reflected across a vertical mirror line at x = 5. What are the coordinates of the reflected vertex A'?
Answer: (9, 2)
Working: Vertex A is at (1, 2), which is 4 units to the left of the vertical mirror line x = 5 (5 - 1 = 4). The reflected vertex A' is 4 units to the right of x = 5: 5 + 4 = 9. The y-coordinate remains 2. So A' is (9, 2).

Question 9: A rectangle has vertices at (2, 3), (5, 3), (5, 7), and (2, 7). It is reflected across a vertical mirror line at x = 7. What are the coordinates of the reflected vertex that corresponds to (2, 7)?
Answer: (12, 7)
Working: Vertex (2, 7) is 5 units to the left of the mirror line x = 7 (7 - 2 = 5). The reflected vertex is 5 units to the right of the mirror line: 7 + 5 = 12. The y-coordinate remains 7. The reflected vertex is (12, 7).

Question 10: A polygon is reflected across a vertical mirror line. A vertex on the original polygon is at (3, 9) and its reflection is at (11, 9). What is the equation of the vertical mirror line?
Answer: x = 7
Working: The vertical mirror line is located halfway between the x-coordinates: (3 + 11) ÷ 2 = 7. Because it is a vertical line, its equation is x = 7.

Question 11: A right-angled triangle has vertices at (4, 2), (8, 2), and (4, 5). The triangle is reflected across a vertical mirror line at x = 9. What is the horizontal distance between the vertex at (8, 2) and its reflected vertex?
Answer: 2
Working: The vertex at (8, 2) is 1 unit to the left of the mirror line x = 9 (9 - 8 = 1). Its reflected vertex is 1 unit to the right of the mirror line at (10, 2). The horizontal distance between (8, 2) and (10, 2) is 10 - 8 = 2 units.

Question 12: A quadrilateral with vertices at (1, 2), (4, 1), (5, 4), and (2, 6) has a perimeter of 16 cm. It is reflected across a vertical mirror line at x = 7. What is the perimeter of the reflected quadrilateral in centimetres?
Answer: 16
Working: Reflection is an isometry, meaning it preserves all side lengths and angles of a shape. The shape does not change size or dimensions when reflected. Therefore, the perimeter remains 16 cm.
