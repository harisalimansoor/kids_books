=== Topic: Comparing and ordering fractions whose denominators are multiples of the same number ===
Domain: Fractions, Decimals and Percentages

--- Comparing fractions whose denominators are multiples of the same number ---

Question 1: Which fraction is greater: 3/4 or 5/8?
Answer: 3/4
Working: Convert 3/4 to eighths: 3/4 = 6/8. Comparing 6/8 and 5/8, 6/8 is greater, so 3/4 is greater.

Question 2: Write <, > or = to make the statement correct: 2/5 [ ] 7/10
Answer: <
Working: Convert 2/5 to tenths: 2/5 = 4/10. Since 4/10 < 7/10, the correct symbol is <.

Question 3: Which fraction is smaller: 5/6 or 7/12?
Answer: 7/12
Working: Convert 5/6 to twelfths: 5/6 = 10/12. Comparing 10/12 and 7/12, 7/12 is smaller.

Question 4: Sam drinks 3/5 of a bottle of juice. Leo drinks 7/15 of the same sized bottle of juice. Who drinks more juice?
Answer: Sam
Working: Convert Sam's fraction to fifteenths: 3/5 = 9/15. Comparing 9/15 with Leo's 7/15, 9/15 > 7/15, so Sam drinks more juice.

Question 5: Write <, > or = to complete the statement: 11/16 [ ] 3/4
Answer: <
Working: Convert 3/4 to sixteenths: 3/4 = 12/16. Since 11/16 < 12/16, the correct symbol is <.

Question 6: Write <, > or = to complete the statement: 1 3/5 [ ] 17/10
Answer: <
Working: Convert 1 3/5 to an improper fraction: 8/5. Convert 8/5 to tenths: 8/5 = 16/10. Comparing 16/10 and 17/10, 16/10 < 17/10, so 1 3/5 < 17/10.

Question 7: Jug A contains 5/6 litre of water. Jug B contains 17/24 litre of water. Which jug contains more water?
Answer: Jug A
Working: Convert 5/6 to twenty-fourths: 5/6 = 20/24. 20/24 > 17/24, so Jug A contains more water.

Question 8: What is the smallest whole number that can replace the missing numerator to make this statement true: [ ] / 18 > 2/3 ?
Answer: 13
Working: Convert 2/3 to eighteenths: 2/3 = 12/18. For [ ] / 18 to be strictly greater than 12/18, the numerator must be greater than 12. The smallest whole number greater than 12 is 13.

Question 9: A parcel weighs 1 3/8 kg. A second parcel weighs 11/8 kg. A third parcel weighs 7/4 kg. Which parcel is the heaviest?
Answer: The third parcel
Working: Convert all masses to eighths: Parcel 1 = 1 3/8 kg = 11/8 kg. Parcel 2 = 11/8 kg. Parcel 3 = 7/4 kg = 14/8 kg. Since 14/8 is the greatest, the third parcel is the heaviest.

--- Ordering fractions whose denominators are multiples of the same number ---

Question 10: Write these fractions in order of size, starting with the smallest: 1/2, 3/8, 5/8
Answer: 3/8, 1/2, 5/8
Working: Convert 1/2 to eighths: 1/2 = 4/8. The fractions are 4/8, 3/8, 5/8. Ordered from smallest to largest: 3/8, 4/8 (1/2), 5/8.

Question 11: Order these fractions from smallest to largest: 2/3, 5/6, 7/12
Answer: 7/12, 2/3, 5/6
Working: Convert all fractions to a common denominator of 12: 2/3 = 8/12, 5/6 = 10/12, 7/12 = 7/12. Comparing numerators: 7 < 8 < 10. Ordered from smallest to largest: 7/12, 2/3, 5/6.

Question 12: Write these fractions in order, starting with the largest: 3/4, 5/8, 11/16
Answer: 3/4, 11/16, 5/8
Working: Convert all fractions to sixteenths: 3/4 = 12/16, 5/8 = 10/16, 11/16 = 11/16. In descending order: 12/16 (3/4), 11/16, 10/16 (5/8).

Question 13: Order these fractions from smallest to largest: 3/5, 7/10, 11/20, 1/2
Answer: 1/2, 11/20, 3/5, 7/10
Working: Convert each fraction to twentieths: 3/5 = 12/20, 7/10 = 14/20, 11/20 = 11/20, 1/2 = 10/20. Comparing numerators gives: 10/20, 11/20, 12/20, 14/20. Therefore, the order is 1/2, 11/20, 3/5, 7/10.

Question 14: Put these fractions in descending order (starting with the greatest): 5/6, 2/3, 7/12, 17/24
Answer: 5/6, 17/24, 2/3, 7/12
Working: Convert each fraction to twenty-fourths: 5/6 = 20/24, 2/3 = 16/24, 7/12 = 14/24, 17/24 = 17/24. In order from greatest to smallest: 20/24 (5/6), 17/24, 16/24 (2/3), 14/24 (7/12).

Question 15: Four children ran part of a 1 km race. Maya ran 3/5 km, Noah ran 13/20 km, Olivia ran 7/10 km, and Liam ran 11/20 km. Write the names of the children in order of distance run, from shortest to longest.
Answer: Liam, Maya, Noah, Olivia
Working: Convert distances to twentieths: Maya = 3/5 = 12/20 km; Noah = 13/20 km; Olivia = 7/10 = 14/20 km; Liam = 11/20 km. From shortest to longest: Liam (11/20), Maya (12/20), Noah (13/20), Olivia (14/20).

Question 16: Write these numbers in order from smallest to greatest: 1 1/4, 11/8, 1 3/16, 5/4
Answer: 1 3/16, 1 1/4, 5/4, 11/8
Working: Convert each value to sixteenths: 1 1/4 = 5/4 = 20/16; 11/8 = 22/16; 1 3/16 = 19/16; 5/4 = 20/16 (note 1 1/4 = 5/4). In ascending order: 19/16 (1 3/16), 20/16 (1 1/4 or 5/4), 20/16 (5/4 or 1 1/4), 22/16 (11/8).
