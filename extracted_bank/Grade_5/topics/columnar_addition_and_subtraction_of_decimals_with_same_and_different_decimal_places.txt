--- Columnar addition of decimals with same and different decimal places ---

Question 1: Calculate 4.36 + 2.58.
Answer: 6.94
Working: Align the decimal points in columns: 6 hundredths + 8 hundredths = 14 hundredths (write 4, carry 1 tenth). 3 tenths + 5 tenths + 1 tenth = 9 tenths. 4 ones + 2 ones = 6 ones. The total is 6.94.

Question 2: Calculate 3.245 + 1.632.
Answer: 4.877
Working: Align the digits by place value: 5 thousandths + 2 thousandths = 7 thousandths. 4 hundredths + 3 hundredths = 7 hundredths. 2 tenths + 6 tenths = 8 tenths. 3 ones + 1 one = 4 ones. The total is 4.877.

Question 5: A baker mixes 2.45 kg of flour with 0.8 kg of sugar. What is the total mass of the mixture in kilograms?

Working: Set up column addition with placeholder zeros: 2.45 + 0.80. 5 + 0 = 5 hundredths. 4 tenths + 8 tenths = 12 tenths (write 2, carry 1 one). 2 ones + 0 ones + 1 one = 3 ones. The total mass is 3.25 kg.

Question 6: Calculate 4.6 + 12.38 + 0.745.

--- Columnar subtraction of decimals with same and different decimal places ---

Question 9: Calculate 7.42 - 3.18.
Answer: 4.24
Working: Align in columns. Exchange 1 tenth for 10 hundredths: 12 hundredths - 8 hundredths = 4 hundredths. 3 tenths - 1 tenth = 2 tenths. 7 ones - 3 ones = 4 ones. The result is 4.24.

Question 10: Calculate 8.654 - 2.381.
Answer: 6.273
Working: Align digits: 4 thousandths - 1 thousandth = 3 thousandths. Exchange 1 tenth for 10 hundredths: 15 hundredths - 8 hundredths = 7 hundredths. 5 tenths - 3 tenths = 2 tenths. 8 ones - 2 ones = 6 ones. The result is 6.273.

Question 11: Calculate 9.3 - 4.76.
Answer: 4.54
Working: Write 9.3 as 9.30. Exchange 1 tenth for 10 hundredths: 10 hundredths - 6 hundredths = 4 hundredths. Exchange 1 one for 10 tenths: 12 tenths - 7 tenths = 5 tenths. 8 ones - 4 ones = 4 ones. The answer is 4.54.

Question 12: Calculate 15 - 4.68.
Answer: 10.32
Working: Write 15 as 15.00. Exchange across place values to get 10 hundredths in the hundredths column: 10 - 8 = 2 hundredths, 9 - 6 = 3 tenths, 4 - 4 = 0 ones, 1 - 0 = 1 ten. The answer is 10.32.

Question 13: A plank of wood is 4.2 m long. A carpenter cuts off a piece measuring 1.65 m. What length of wood is left in metres?
Answer: 2.55 m
Working: Calculate 4.20 - 1.65. Exchange 1 tenth for 10 hundredths: 10 - 5 = 5 hundredths. Exchange 1 one for 10 tenths: 11 - 6 = 5 tenths. 3 - 1 = 2 ones. The remaining length is 2.55 m.

Question 14: Calculate 12.1 - 4.785.
Answer: 7.315
Working: Write 12.1 as 12.100. Exchange 1 tenth to get 9 hundredths and 10 thousandths: 10 - 5 = 5 thousandths, 9 - 8 = 1 hundredth. Exchange 1 one to get 10 tenths: 10 - 7 = 3 tenths. 11 - 4 = 7 ones. The answer is 7.315.

Question 15: A jug contains 3 litres of water. Oliver pours out 1.375 litres. How many litres of water remain in the jug?
Answer: 1.625 litres
Working: Subtract 1.375 from 3.000 using column subtraction: 3.000 - 1.375. Exchange across columns: 10 - 5 = 5 thousandths, 9 - 7 = 2 hundredths, 9 - 3 = 6 tenths, 2 - 1 = 1 one. 1.625 litres remain.

Question 16: A crate filled with apples has a total mass of 18.1 kg. After some apples are sold, the total mass is 7.465 kg. What is the mass of the apples that were sold?
Answer: 10.635 kg
Working: Calculate 18.100 - 7.465 using columnar subtraction with placeholder zeros. Thousandths: 10 - 5 = 5. Hundredths: 9 - 6 = 3. Tenths: 10 - 4 = 6. Ones: 7 - 7 = 0. Tens: 1 - 0 = 1. The mass sold is 10.635 kg.
