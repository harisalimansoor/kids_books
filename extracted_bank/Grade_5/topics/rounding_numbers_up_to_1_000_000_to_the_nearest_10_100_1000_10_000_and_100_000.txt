=== Topic: Rounding numbers up to 1 000 000 to the nearest 10, 100, 1000, 10 000, and 100 000 ===
Domain: Number and Place Value

--- Rounding to the nearest 10 ---

Question 1: Round 48 376 to the nearest 10.
Answer: 48 380
Working: Look at the ones digit, which is 6. Since 6 is 5 or more, round up to 48 380.

Question 2: Round 349 298 to the nearest 10.
Answer: 349 300
Working: Look at the ones digit, which is 8. Since 8 is 5 or more, round up from 290 to 300, giving 349 300.

Question 3: The attendance at a concert was rounded to the nearest 10 as 15 480. What is the greatest possible number of people who attended?
Answer: 15 484
Working: Numbers from 15 475 to 15 484 round to 15 480 to the nearest 10. The greatest possible whole number is 15 484.

Question 4: What is the smallest whole number that rounds to 600 000 when rounded to the nearest 10?
Answer: 599 995
Working: To round up to 600 000 to the nearest 10, the ones digit must be at least 5. The smallest number is 600 000 - 5 = 599 995.

--- Rounding to the nearest 100 ---

Question 5: Round 72 649 to the nearest 100.
Answer: 72 600
Working: Look at the tens digit, which is 4. Since 4 is less than 5, round down to 72 600.

Question 6: Round 809 962 to the nearest 100.
Answer: 810 000
Working: Look at the tens digit, which is 6. Since 6 is 5 or more, round up. 809 900 rounds up to 810 000.

Question 7: A factory produced a batch of books. When rounded to the nearest 100, the number of books is 43 500. What is the smallest whole number of books the factory could have produced?
Answer: 43 450
Working: To round to 43 500 to the nearest 100, numbers must be in the range 43 450 to 43 549. The smallest whole number is 43 450.

Question 8: A 5-digit number is formed using the digits 7, 4, 9, 5, and 2 exactly once each. When rounded to the nearest 100, the number is 75 000. What is the greatest possible value of this 5-digit number?
Answer: 74 952
Working: Numbers that round to 75 000 to the nearest 100 lie between 74 950 and 75 049. Using digits {7, 4, 9, 5, 2}, numbers starting with 75 cannot have hundreds digit 0 because 0 is not available. For numbers starting with 74 9.., the remaining digits are 5 and 2. The greatest arrangement is 74 952, which rounds to 75 000.

--- Rounding to the nearest 1000 ---

Question 11: A stadium holds 68 000 spectators when rounded to the nearest 1000. What is the difference between the greatest and smallest whole number of spectators that round to 68 000?
Answer: 999
Working: The smallest whole number is 67 500 and the greatest whole number is 68 499. Difference = 68 499 - 67 500 = 999.

Question 12: A 6-digit number rounds to 240 000 when rounded to the nearest 10 000, and rounds to 236 000 when rounded to the nearest 1000. What is the smallest possible whole number it could be?
Answer: 235 500
Working: To round to 236 000 to the nearest 1000, the number must be at least 235 500. Rounding 235 500 to the nearest 10 000 gives 240 000 (since thousands digit 5 rounds up). So the smallest possible whole number is 235 500.

--- Rounding to the nearest 100 000 ---

Question 19: What is the smallest whole number that rounds to 900 000 when rounded to the nearest 100 000?

Question 20: A 6-digit whole number rounds to 700 000 to the nearest 100 000, to 650 000 to the nearest 10 000, and to 654 000 to the nearest 1000. What is the greatest possible value of this number?
Answer: 654 499
Working: To round to 654 000 to the nearest 1000, the greatest whole number is 654 499. Checking constraints: 654 499 rounded to the nearest 10 000 is 650 000 (thousands digit 4 < 5), and rounded to the nearest 100 000 is 700 000 (ten-thousands digit 5 >= 5). All constraints hold, so the greatest possible value is 654 499.
