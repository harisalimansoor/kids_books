=== Topic: Negative numbers in context, counting through zero, and finding differences across zero ===
Domain: Number and Place Value

--- Negative numbers in context ---

Question 1: The temperature in Ottawa is -6 °C. The temperature in London is 9 °C warmer than in Ottawa. What is the temperature in London?
Answer: 3 °C
Working: Start at -6 °C and add 9 °C: -6 + 9 = 3 °C.

Question 2: A department store lift starts on floor -2 in the underground car park. It travels up 4 floors. On which floor does the lift stop?
Answer: 2
Working: Start on floor -2 and move up 4 floors: -2 + 4 = 2.

Question 3: The temperature inside a commercial freezer is -21 °C. During maintenance, the temperature rises by 7 °C. What is the new temperature inside the freezer?
Answer: -14 °C
Working: Start at -21 °C and add 7 °C: -21 + 7 = -14 °C.

Question 4: At 8 pm, the temperature outside was 2 °C. Overnight, the temperature fell by 7 °C. What was the temperature by morning?
Answer: -5 °C
Working: Start at 2 °C and subtract 7 °C: 2 - 7 = -5 °C.

Question 5: The temperature at the summit of a mountain is 11 °C colder than at the base. The temperature at the base is 4 °C. What is the temperature at the summit?
Answer: -7 °C
Working: Subtract 11 °C from 4 °C: 4 - 11 = -7 °C.

Question 6: A research submarine is at a depth of -45 m relative to sea level. It descends a further 30 m, and then ascends 25 m. What is the final depth of the submarine relative to sea level?
Answer: -50 m
Working: Descend 30 m: -45 - 30 = -75 m. Ascend 25 m: -75 + 25 = -50 m.

--- Counting through zero ---

Question 7: Write the missing number to complete the linear sequence: 6, 3, 0, __, -6.
Answer: -3
Working: The sequence decreases by 3 each step: 0 - 3 = -3.

Question 8: A number sequence begins at -10 and increases by 3 each time. What is the 5th number in the sequence: -10, -7, -4, __, __?
Answer: 2
Working: 4th term: -4 + 3 = -1. 5th term: -1 + 3 = 2.

Question 9: Starting at 7, count backwards in steps of 4. What is the third number you reach?
Answer: -5
Working: 1st step: 7 - 4 = 3. 2nd step: 3 - 4 = -1. 3rd step: -1 - 4 = -5.

Question 10: What is the missing number in this linear sequence: -16, -11, -6, -1, __, 9?
Answer: 4
Working: The rule is to add 5 each time: -1 + 5 = 4. Check: 4 + 5 = 9.

Question 11: Count backwards in steps of 25: 75, 50, 25, 0, __, -50. What is the missing value?
Answer: -25
Working: The sequence counts down in steps of 25: 0 - 25 = -25.

Question 12: A number sequence begins at -24 and increases by 7 each time. What is the first positive number in this sequence?
Answer: 4
Working: -24 + 7 = -17; -17 + 7 = -10; -10 + 7 = -3; -3 + 7 = 4. 4 is the first number greater than zero.

--- Finding differences across zero ---

Question 13: At 5 am, the temperature was -4 °C. By 1 pm, the temperature had reached 8 °C. How many degrees Celsius did the temperature rise?
Answer: 12 °C
Working: From -4 °C to 0 °C is 4 °C. From 0 °C to 8 °C is 8 °C. Total rise = 4 + 8 = 12 °C.

Question 14: In Helsinki, the overnight temperature was -9 °C. In Rome, the temperature was 13 °C. What is the difference in temperature between Helsinki and Rome?
Answer: 22 °C
Working: Distance from -9 to 0 is 9. Distance from 0 to 13 is 13. Total difference = 9 + 13 = 22 °C.

Question 15: A diver is swimming at a depth of 16 m below sea level (-16 m). A helicopter flies directly overhead at an altitude of 150 m above sea level (+150 m). What is the vertical distance between the diver and the helicopter?
Answer: 166 m
Working: Distance from -16 m to 0 m is 16 m. Distance from 0 m to 150 m is 150 m. Total vertical distance = 16 + 150 = 166 m.

Question 16: A car park has spaces on basement level -3 and a restaurant is located on floor 5. How many floors apart are level -3 and floor 5?
Answer: 8
Working: From floor -3 to 0 is 3 floors. From floor 0 to floor 5 is 5 floors. Total difference = 3 + 5 = 8 floors.

Question 17: On Monday, the minimum temperature was -7 °C and the maximum was 6 °C. On Tuesday, the minimum was -3 °C and the maximum was 11 °C. Which day had the greater temperature range, and by how many degrees Celsius?
Answer: Tuesday, by 1 °C
Working: Monday range: 7 + 6 = 13 °C. Tuesday range: 3 + 11 = 14 °C. Tuesday had the greater range by 14 - 13 = 1 °C.

Question 18: A hillwalker is at an altitude of 340 m above sea level. Directly below, an explorer is inside a cave at a depth of 85 m below sea level (-85 m). What is the total vertical distance between the hillwalker and the explorer?
Answer: 425 m
Working: Distance from -85 m to sea level is 85 m. Distance from sea level to 340 m is 340 m. Total vertical distance = 85 + 340 = 425 m.
