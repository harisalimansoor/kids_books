=== Topic: Columnar addition of numbers with more than 4 digits and decimals with exchanges ===
Domain: Addition and Subtraction

--- Columnar addition of numbers with more than 4 digits ---

Question 1: Calculate 43,628 + 25,147.
Answer: 68,775
Working: Align digits in place-value columns:
  43,628
+ 25,147
8 + 7 = 15 (write 5 in ones, exchange 1 ten)
2 + 4 + 1 = 7 tens
6 + 1 = 7 hundreds
3 + 5 = 8 thousands
4 + 2 = 6 ten thousands
Total = 68,775.

Question 2: Calculate 58,746 + 26,585.
Answer: 85,331
Working: Align in columns and add from right to left:
Ones: 6 + 5 = 11 (write 1, exchange 1 ten)
Tens: 4 + 8 + 1 = 13 (write 3, exchange 1 hundred)
Hundreds: 7 + 5 + 1 = 13 (write 3, exchange 1 thousand)
Thousands: 8 + 6 + 1 = 15 (write 5, exchange 1 ten thousand)
Ten thousands: 5 + 2 + 1 = 8
Total = 85,331.

Question 3: A factory produced 124,530 bottles of juice in May and 86,785 bottles of juice in June. How many bottles of juice were produced altogether?
Answer: 211,315
Working: 124,530 + 86,785:
Ones: 0 + 5 = 5
Tens: 3 + 8 = 11 (write 1, exchange 1 hundred)
Hundreds: 5 + 7 + 1 = 13 (write 3, exchange 1 thousand)
Thousands: 4 + 6 + 1 = 11 (write 1, exchange 1 ten thousand)
Ten thousands: 2 + 8 + 1 = 11 (write 1, exchange 1 hundred thousand)
Hundred thousands: 1 + 1 = 2
Total = 211,315.

Question 4: Calculate the sum of 45,618, 38,294, and 7,543.
Answer: 91,455
Working: Align all three numbers in place-value columns:
  45,618
  38,294
+  7,543
Ones: 8 + 4 + 3 = 15 (write 5, exchange 1 ten)
Tens: 1 + 9 + 4 + 1 = 15 (write 5, exchange 1 hundred)
Hundreds: 6 + 2 + 5 + 1 = 14 (write 4, exchange 1 thousand)
Thousands: 5 + 8 + 7 + 1 = 21 (write 1, exchange 2 ten thousands)
Ten thousands: 4 + 3 + 2 = 9
Total = 91,455.

Question 5: Find the missing digit represented by the square (□) in the column addition below:

  5 4, □ 8 2
+ 2 7, 5 3 9
  ---------
  8 2, 2 2 1
Answer: 6
Working: Ones: 2 + 9 = 11 (1 written, 1 ten exchanged).
Tens: 8 + 3 + 1 = 12 (2 written, 1 hundred exchanged).
Hundreds: □ + 5 + 1 must equal 12 so that 2 is written and 1 thousand is exchanged to the thousands column (since 4 + 7 + 1 = 12 thousands).
□ + 6 = 12, so □ = 6.

Question 6: On Saturday, 48,725 people visited a museum. On Sunday, there were 6,480 more visitors than on Saturday. What was the total number of visitors across both days?
Answer: 103,930
Working: Sunday visitors: 48,725 + 6,480 = 55,205.
Total visitors: 48,725 + 55,205 = 103,930.

--- Columnar addition of decimals with exchanges ---

Question 7: Calculate 5.76 + 3.85.
Answer: 9.61
Working: Align decimal points:
  5.76
+ 3.85
Hundredths: 6 + 5 = 11 hundredths (write 1, exchange 1 tenth)
Tenths: 7 + 8 + 1 = 16 tenths (write 6, exchange 1 one)
Ones: 5 + 3 + 1 = 9 ones
Total = 9.61.

Question 8: Calculate 34.8 + 17.65.
Answer: 52.45
Working: Align decimal points using zero as a placeholder:
  34.80
+ 17.65

Tenths: 8 + 6 = 14 (write 4, exchange 1 one)
Ones: 4 + 7 + 1 = 12 (write 2, exchange 1 ten)
Tens: 3 + 1 + 1 = 5

Question 9: A runner completes three stages of a race. The first stage takes 14.85 seconds, the second stage takes 15.6 seconds, and the third stage takes 14.78 seconds. What is the total time taken in seconds?
Answer: 45.23
Working: Align with placeholders: 14.85 + 15.60 + 14.78.
Hundredths: 5 + 0 + 8 = 13 (write 3, exchange 1 tenth)
Tenths: 8 + 6 + 7 + 1 = 22 (write 2, exchange 2 ones)
Ones: 4 + 5 + 4 + 2 = 15 (write 5, exchange 1 ten)
Tens: 1 + 1 + 1 + 1 = 4
Total = 45.23.

Question 11: Find the missing digit represented by the square (□) in the column addition below:

  1 4 . □ 6
+   8 . 7 9
  ---------
  2 3 . 4 5
Answer: 6
Working: Hundredths: 6 + 9 = 15 (write 5, exchange 1 tenth).
Tenths: □ + 7 + 1 must equal 14 so that 4 is written and 1 one is exchanged (since 14 + 8 + 1 = 23 in whole numbers).
□ + 8 = 14, so □ = 6.

Question 12: A baker uses 3.75 kg of white flour, 1.875 kg of wholemeal flour, and 0.65 kg of rye flour. What is the total mass of flour used, in kilograms?
Answer: 6.275
Working: Align decimal points with placeholders:
  3.750
  1.875
+ 0.650
Thousandths: 0 + 5 + 0 = 5
Hundredths: 5 + 7 + 5 = 17 (write 7, exchange 1 tenth)
Tenths: 7 + 8 + 6 + 1 = 22 (write 2, exchange 2 ones)
Ones: 3 + 1 + 0 + 2 = 6
Total = 6.275.
