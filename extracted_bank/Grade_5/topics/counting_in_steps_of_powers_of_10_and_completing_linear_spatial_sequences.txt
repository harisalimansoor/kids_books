=== Topic: Counting in steps of powers of 10 and completing linear/spatial sequences ===
Domain: Number and Place Value

--- Counting in steps of powers of 10 ---

Question 1: What number is 1000 more than 48 352?
Answer: 49 352
Working: Add 1000 to the thousands digit: 48 352 + 1000 = 49 352.

Question 2: What number is 10 000 less than 350 400?
Answer: 340 400
Working: Subtract 10 000 from 350 400: 350 400 - 10 000 = 340 400.

Question 3: A sequence counts backwards in steps of 100 000: 840 000, 740 000, 640 000, ... What is the next number in the sequence?
Answer: 540 000
Working: Subtract 100 000 from the last term: 640 000 - 100 000 = 540 000.

Question 4: Start at 97 450. Count forwards in steps of 1000. What is the number after 3 steps?
Answer: 100 450
Working: Each step adds 1000. Three steps add 3 × 1000 = 3000. 97 450 + 3000 = 100 450.

Question 5: A stadium has 495 620 spectators. If 10 000 more spectators arrive, what is the new total number of spectators?
Answer: 505 620
Working: Add 10 000 to 495 620. 495 620 + 10 000 = 505 620.

Question 6: Starting at 1 000 000, a counter counts backwards in steps of 10 000. What number is reached after 6 steps?
Answer: 940 000
Working: 6 steps of 10 000 = 60 000. 1 000 000 - 60 000 = 940 000.

Question 7: A number has 6 digits. When 100 is added to it, the number becomes 400 050. What was the starting number?
Answer: 399 950
Working: Subtract 100 from 400 050: 400 050 - 100 = 399 950.

--- Completing linear and spatial sequences ---

Question 8: Find the missing number in this linear sequence: 12, 19, 26, ___, 40
Answer: 33
Working: The term-to-term rule is add 7 (19 - 12 = 7). 26 + 7 = 33. Check: 33 + 7 = 40.

Question 9: A linear sequence decreases by the same amount each time: 350, 315, 280, 245, ... What is the 6th term of this sequence?
Answer: 175
Working: The term-to-term rule is subtract 35 (350 - 315 = 35). 5th term = 245 - 35 = 210. 6th term = 210 - 35 = 175.

Question 10: Pattern 1 is made with 4 matchsticks to form a square. Each new pattern adds 3 more matchsticks to attach another square in a row. How many matchsticks are needed for Pattern 5?
Answer: 16
Working: Pattern 1 = 4. Pattern 2 = 7. Pattern 3 = 10. Pattern 4 = 13. Pattern 5 = 16 (or 4 + 4 × 3 = 16).

Question 11: In a linear sequence, the 1st term is 8 and the 4th term is 29. What is the 2nd term of this sequence?
Answer: 15
Working: Difference between 4th and 1st term is 29 - 8 = 21 across 3 equal steps. Step size = 21 ÷ 3 = 7. 2nd term = 8 + 7 = 15.

Question 12: Tiles are arranged in a spatial pattern: Design 1 has 5 tiles, Design 2 has 9 tiles, Design 3 has 13 tiles, and Design 4 has 17 tiles. Which design number uses exactly 41 tiles?
Answer: 10
Working: The sequence starts at 5 and adds 4 each time. Difference from Design 1 = 41 - 5 = 36. Number of steps = 36 ÷ 4 = 9. Design number = 1 + 9 = 10.

Question 13: A sequence of numbers follows a constant rule: ___, 145, ___, 205, 235. What is the 1st term of the sequence?
Answer: 115
Working: The difference between 205 and 235 is 30, so the term-to-term rule is add 30. Subtract 30 from the 2nd term (145) to find the 1st term: 145 - 30 = 115.
