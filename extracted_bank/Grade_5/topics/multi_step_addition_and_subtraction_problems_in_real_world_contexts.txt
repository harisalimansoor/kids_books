=== Topic: Multi-step addition and subtraction problems in real-world contexts ===
Domain: Addition and Subtraction

--- Multi-step addition and subtraction problems in real-world contexts ---

Question 1: On Saturday, 34 520 people attended Match A and 28 475 people attended Match B. On Sunday, 41 860 people attended Match C. How many more people attended matches on Saturday than on Sunday?
Answer: 21 135
Working: 1) Add Saturday attendances: 34 520 + 28 475 = 62 995.
2) Subtract Sunday attendance: 62 995 - 41 860 = 21 135.

Question 2: A charity sets a fundraising target of £125 000. In January, it raises £38 450. In February, it raises £46 780. How much more money does the charity need to raise to reach its target?
Answer: £39 770
Working: 1) Add the amounts raised in January and February: £38 450 + £46 780 = £85 230.
2) Subtract the total raised from the target: £125 000 - £85 230 = £39 770.

Question 3: In one month, a recycling depot collects 54 280 kg of paper and 36 945 kg of cardboard. The depot collects 120 000 kg of materials in total including plastic. How many kilograms of plastic does the depot collect?
Answer: 28 775 kg
Working: 1) Add paper and cardboard: 54 280 kg + 36 945 kg = 91 225 kg.
2) Subtract from the total collected: 120 000 kg - 91 225 kg = 28 775 kg.

Question 4: A factory has 85 400 tins of soup in stock. On Monday, it dispatches 24 650 tins. On Tuesday, it dispatches 31 875 tins. How many tins of soup remain in stock at the end of Tuesday?
Answer: 28 875
Working: 1) Add the tins dispatched: 24 650 + 31 875 = 56 525.
2) Subtract the dispatched tins from the initial stock: 85 400 - 56 525 = 28 875.

Question 5: Town A has a population of 67 430. Town B has 14 825 fewer people than Town A. Town C has 9 650 more people than Town B. What is the total population of all three towns combined?
Answer: 182 290
Working: 1) Find population of Town B: 67 430 - 14 825 = 52 605.
2) Find population of Town C: 52 605 + 9 650 = 62 255.
3) Add all three populations: 67 430 + 52 605 + 62 255 = 182 290.

Question 6: A theme park records 45 612 visitors on Friday and 68 395 visitors on Saturday. Over the three days from Friday to Sunday, there are 172 450 visitors in total. How many more visitors attended on Sunday than on Friday?
Answer: 12 831
Working: 1) Add Friday and Saturday visitors: 45 612 + 68 395 = 114 007.
2) Find Sunday visitors: 172 450 - 114 007 = 58 443.
3) Find the difference between Sunday and Friday: 58 443 - 45 612 = 12 831.

Question 7: An airline carries 248 300 passengers in July. In August, it carries 18 950 more passengers than in July. In September, it carries 32 400 fewer passengers than in August. How many passengers does the airline carry in September?
Answer: 234 850
Working: 1) Find August passengers: 248 300 + 18 950 = 267 250.
2) Find September passengers: 267 250 - 32 400 = 234 850.

Question 8: A local authority orders 150 000 books for school libraries. In the first delivery, 48 725 books arrive. In the second delivery, 53 690 books arrive. How many books are still to be delivered?
Answer: 47 585
Working: 1) Add delivered books: 48 725 + 53 690 = 102 415.
2) Subtract from the total ordered: 150 000 - 102 415 = 47 585.

Question 9: A solar farm generates 385 420 kWh of electricity in spring and 512 680 kWh in summer. In autumn, it generates 294 150 kWh. How much more electricity does the farm generate in spring and summer combined than in autumn?
Answer: 603 950 kWh
Working: 1) Add spring and summer generation: 385 420 kWh + 512 680 kWh = 898 100 kWh.
2) Subtract autumn generation: 898 100 kWh - 294 150 kWh = 603 950 kWh.

Question 10: A delivery company tracks the mileage of three vans over one year. Van A travels 42 685 miles. Van B travels 8 470 miles more than Van A. Van C travels 5 320 miles fewer than Van B. What is the total mileage travelled by all three vans combined?
Answer: 139 675 miles
Working: 1) Mileage of Van B: 42 685 + 8 470 = 51 155 miles.
2) Mileage of Van C: 51 155 - 5 320 = 45 835 miles.
3) Total mileage: 42 685 + 51 155 + 45 835 = 139 675 miles.

Question 11: A school has an annual budget of £350 000. It spends £184 750 on staff salaries, £68 420 on building maintenance, and £43 185 on learning resources. How much of the budget remains unspent?
Answer: £53 645
Working: 1) Add all spending: £184 750 + £68 420 + £43 185 = £296 355.
2) Subtract total spending from the budget: £350 000 - £296 355 = £53 645.

Question 12: A water reservoir holds 520 400 litres of water at the start of the week. Rainfall adds 145 850 litres, and 198 600 litres are pumped out to local homes. How many litres of water are in the reservoir at the end of the week?
Answer: 467 650 litres
Working: 1) Add the rainfall: 520 400 litres + 145 850 litres = 666 250 litres.
2) Subtract the water pumped out: 666 250 litres - 198 600 litres = 467 650 litres.

Question 13: A museum has 112 450 visitors in June. In July, it has 15 380 fewer visitors than in June. In August, it has 22 750 more visitors than in July. How many visitors attend the museum in August?
Answer: 119 820
Working: 1) Find July visitors: 112 450 - 15 380 = 97 070.
2) Find August visitors: 97 070 + 22 750 = 119 820.

Question 14: A company distributes 500 000 parcels across three regional hubs. Hub 1 receives 164 820 parcels and Hub 2 receives 187 450 parcels. How many more parcels does Hub 2 receive than Hub 3?
Answer: 39 720
Working: 1) Add parcels in Hub 1 and Hub 2: 164 820 + 187 450 = 352 270.
2) Find parcels in Hub 3: 500 000 - 352 270 = 147 730.
3) Find difference between Hub 2 and Hub 3: 187 450 - 147 730 = 39 720.
