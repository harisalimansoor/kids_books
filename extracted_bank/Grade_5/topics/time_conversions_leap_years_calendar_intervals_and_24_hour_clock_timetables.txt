=== Topic: Time conversions, leap years, calendar intervals, and 24-hour clock timetables ===
Domain: Measurement

--- Time conversions ---

Question 1: A runner completed a race in 3 hours and 42 minutes. How many minutes did the race take in total?
Answer: 222 minutes
Working: 1 hour = 60 minutes. 3 hours = 3 × 60 = 180 minutes. Total time = 180 + 42 = 222 minutes.

Question 2: A film lasts 138 minutes. Express this time in hours and minutes.
Answer: 2 hours and 18 minutes
Working: 138 ÷ 60 = 2 with a remainder of 18. So 138 minutes = 2 hours and 18 minutes.

Question 3: A space probe takes 3 minutes and 48 seconds to send a signal to Earth. Convert this time entirely into seconds.
Answer: 228 seconds
Working: 1 minute = 60 seconds. 3 minutes = 3 × 60 = 180 seconds. Total time = 180 + 48 = 228 seconds.

Question 4: A science experiment took 325 seconds. Write this duration in minutes and seconds.
Answer: 5 minutes and 25 seconds
Working: 325 ÷ 60 = 5 with a remainder of 25. So 325 seconds = 5 minutes and 25 seconds.

Question 5: Convert 4.75 hours into hours and minutes.
Answer: 4 hours and 45 minutes
Working: 4.75 hours = 4 whole hours + 0.75 of an hour. 0.75 × 60 minutes = 45 minutes. Total = 4 hours and 45 minutes.

--- Leap years ---

Question 6: How many days are there altogether in a leap year?
Answer: 366 days
Working: A non-leap year has 365 days. In a leap year, February has 29 days instead of 28, making 366 days in total.

Question 7: Leap years occur every 4 years. What is the next leap year after 2028?
Answer: 2032
Working: 2028 + 4 = 2032.

Question 8: How many total days are there altogether in January, February, and March during a leap year?
Answer: 91 days
Working: January has 31 days. February has 29 days in a leap year. March has 31 days. 31 + 29 + 31 = 91 days.

Question 9: A leap year has 366 days. How many full weeks and remaining days is this?
Answer: 52 weeks and 2 days
Working: 366 ÷ 7 = 52 remainder 2. So 366 days = 52 weeks and 2 days.

Question 10: A primary school opened in the leap year 2004. How many leap years occurred from 2004 to 2024 inclusive?
Answer: 6
Working: The leap years are 2004, 2008, 2012, 2016, 2020, and 2024. Counting these gives 6 leap years.

--- Calendar intervals ---

Question 11: A local leisure centre was closed for refurbishment for 5 weeks and 3 days. How many days was the leisure centre closed in total?
Answer: 38 days
Working: 1 week = 7 days. 5 weeks = 5 × 7 = 35 days. Total days = 35 + 3 = 38 days.

Question 12: A library book is borrowed on 24 April. It must be returned in exactly 21 days. On what date is the book due back? (April has 30 days.)
Answer: 15 May
Working: Days left in April = 30 - 24 = 6 days. Days remaining in May = 21 - 6 = 15. The due date is 15 May.

Question 13: A holiday activity club starts on 18 July and ends on 6 August. How many days does the club run for in total, including both the start date and the end date? (July has 31 days.)
Answer: 20 days
Working: Days in July: 31 - 18 + 1 = 14 days. Days in August: 6 days. Total days = 14 + 6 = 20 days.

Question 14: An art exhibition opens on 12 October and runs for 3 weeks and 4 days. On what date does the exhibition end? (October has 31 days.)
Answer: 6 November
Working: 3 weeks and 4 days = (3 × 7) + 4 = 25 days. From 12 October + 25 days: 12 + 25 = 37. October has 31 days, so 37 - 31 = 6 November.

--- 24-hour clock timetables ---

Question 15: A bus departs from the Town Hall at 08:45 and arrives at the Train Station at 09:32. How many minutes does the journey take?
Answer: 47 minutes
Working: From 08:45 to 09:00 is 15 minutes. From 09:00 to 09:32 is 32 minutes. 15 + 32 = 47 minutes.

Question 16: A train stops at Station B at 14:48 and reaches Station C at 15:22. How many minutes does the journey between Station B and Station C take?
Answer: 34 minutes
Working: From 14:48 to 15:00 is 12 minutes. From 15:00 to 15:22 is 22 minutes. 12 + 22 = 34 minutes.

Question 17: A coach leaves Manchester at 10:40 and arrives in London at 14:15. How long did the coach journey take in hours and minutes?
Answer: 3 hours and 35 minutes
Working: From 10:40 to 13:40 is 3 hours. From 13:40 to 14:00 is 20 minutes. From 14:00 to 14:15 is 15 minutes. Total time = 3 hours and (20 + 15) = 3 hours and 35 minutes.

Question 18: A ferry departs at 11:45. The sailing takes 1 hour and 50 minutes. At what time does the ferry arrive? Give your answer using the 24-hour clock.
Answer: 13:35
Working: 11:45 + 1 hour = 12:45. 12:45 + 15 minutes = 13:00. 13:00 + 35 minutes = 13:35.

Question 19: Three trains travel from Leeds to York:
- Train A departs at 07:15 and arrives at 08:02.
- Train B departs at 07:45 and arrives at 08:26.
- Train C departs at 08:10 and arrives at 09:01.
Which train has the shortest journey time?
Answer: Train B
Working: Train A: 07:15 to 08:02 = 47 minutes. Train B: 07:45 to 08:26 = 41 minutes. Train C: 08:10 to 09:01 = 51 minutes. Train B is the shortest at 41 minutes.
