Question 1: Use formal column subtraction to calculate 5000 - 2374.
Answer: 2626
Working: Set up in columns. Exchange 1 thousand for 10 hundreds, 1 hundred for 10 tens, and 1 ten for 10 ones. Subtract the ones: 10 - 4 = 6. Subtract the tens: 9 - 7 = 2. Subtract the hundreds: 9 - 3 = 6. Subtract the thousands: 4 - 2 = 2. The answer is 2626.

Question 2: Calculate 4002 - 1635 using formal column subtraction.
Answer: 2367
Working: Set up in columns. In the ones column, 2 is less than 5. Decompose across the zeros from the thousands: 4 thousands becomes 3 thousands, 9 hundreds, 9 tens, and 12 ones. Ones: 12 - 5 = 7. Tens: 9 - 3 = 6. Hundreds: 9 - 6 = 3. Thousands: 3 - 1 = 2. The answer is 2367.
