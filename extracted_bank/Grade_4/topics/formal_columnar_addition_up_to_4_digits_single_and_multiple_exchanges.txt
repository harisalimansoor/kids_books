=== Topic: Formal columnar addition up to 4 digits (single and multiple exchanges) ===
Domain: Addition and Subtraction

--- Single exchange ---

Question 1: Calculate 3,428 + 2,154 using column addition.
Answer: 5582
Working: Set up in columns and add from right to left:
Ones: 8 + 4 = 12 ones = 1 ten and 2 ones (write 2, exchange 1 ten)
Tens: 2 + 5 + 1 = 8 tens
Hundreds: 4 + 1 = 5 hundreds
Thousands: 3 + 2 = 5 thousands
3428 + 2154 = 5582

Question 5: Find the missing digit marked by the square [ ] in this addition calculation:
  3 [ ] 4 2
+ 2   3 8 5
-----------
  5   7 2 7
Answer: 3
Working: Add column by column:
Ones: 2 + 5 = 7
Tens: 4 + 8 = 12 (write 2, exchange 1 hundred to the hundreds column)
Hundreds: [ ] + 3 + 1 = 7, so [ ] + 4 = 7, giving [ ] = 3
Thousands: 3 + 2 = 5
The missing digit is 3.
