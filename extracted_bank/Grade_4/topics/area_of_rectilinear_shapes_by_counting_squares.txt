=== Topic: Area of rectilinear shapes by counting squares ===
Domain: Measurement, Money and Time

--- Area of rectilinear shapes by counting squares ---

Question 1: A rectilinear shape is drawn on a grid of centimetre squares. It is completely filled by 9 whole squares. What is the area of the shape?
Answer: 9 cm²
Working: Each square on the grid has an area of 1 cm². Since the shape contains 9 whole squares, the total area is 9 × 1 cm² = 9 cm².

Question 2: A rectangle is drawn on a grid of 1 cm² squares. It has 4 rows with 3 squares in each row. What is the total area of the rectangle?
Answer: 12 cm²
Working: Count the total number of centimetre squares: 4 rows of 3 squares = 4 × 3 = 12 squares. The area is 12 cm².

Question 3: An L-shaped rectilinear figure on a centimetre grid is made of a vertical column of 6 squares and a horizontal row of 2 squares attached at the bottom right. What is the area of this L-shaped figure in square centimetres?
Answer: 8 cm²
Working: Count all the squares that make up the rectilinear figure: 6 squares + 2 squares = 8 squares. Each square is 1 cm², so the total area is 8 cm².

Question 4: A T-shaped rectilinear shape is drawn on a grid where each square is 1 cm². The horizontal top bar is made of 7 squares. The vertical section underneath the middle square is made of 4 squares. What is the total area of the shape?
Answer: 11 cm²
Working: Total squares = 7 (top bar) + 4 (vertical section) = 11 squares. Since each square is 1 cm², the total area is 11 cm².

Question 5: Shape A covers 14 squares on a 1 cm² grid. Shape B covers 19 squares on the same grid. How many square centimetres larger is the area of Shape B than Shape A?
Answer: 5 cm²
Working: Area of Shape A = 14 cm². Area of Shape B = 19 cm². Difference = 19 cm² - 14 cm² = 5 cm².

Question 6: A shaded shape on a centimetre squared grid is made of 11 full squares and 4 half-squares. What is the total area of the shape in cm²?
Answer: 13 cm²
Working: 4 half-squares = 4 ÷ 2 = 2 full squares. Total full squares = 11 + 2 = 13 squares. Therefore, the area is 13 cm².

Question 7: A rectilinear shape on a grid of 1 cm² squares is made from two non-overlapping rectangles joined together. The first rectangle contains 3 rows of 5 squares. The second rectangle contains 2 rows of 4 squares. What is the total area of the rectilinear shape?
Answer: 23 cm²
Working: First rectangle = 3 × 5 = 15 squares = 15 cm². Second rectangle = 2 × 4 = 8 squares = 8 cm². Total area = 15 cm² + 8 cm² = 23 cm².

Question 8: A large square on a grid of centimetre squares measures 5 squares by 5 squares. A smaller square measuring 2 squares by 2 squares is removed from one corner. What is the area of the remaining rectilinear shape?
Answer: 21 cm²
Working: Area of large square = 5 × 5 = 25 cm². Area of removed square = 2 × 2 = 4 cm². Area of remaining shape = 25 cm² - 4 cm² = 21 cm².

Question 9: On a grid of 1 cm² squares, Shape X is a rectangle made of 4 rows of 6 squares. Shape Y is a square made of 5 rows of 5 squares. Which shape has the greater area and by how many cm²?
Answer: Shape Y by 1 cm²
Working: Area of Shape X = 4 × 6 = 24 cm². Area of Shape Y = 5 × 5 = 25 cm². Shape Y is larger by 25 - 24 = 1 cm².

Question 10: Hannah is drawing a rectilinear shape with a total area of 28 cm² on a 1 cm² grid. She has already shaded 19 squares. How many more whole squares does she need to shade to complete the shape?
Answer: 9
Working: Target area = 28 squares. Shaded so far = 19 squares. Remaining squares to shade = 28 - 19 = 9 squares.

Question 11: A cross-shaped rectilinear tile pattern on a grid of 1 m² squares consists of a central square of 4 squares (2 by 2), with an extra block of 2 squares attached to each of its 4 outer sides. What is the total area of the pattern in square metres?
Answer: 12 m²
Working: Central square = 2 × 2 = 4 squares. Four outer blocks = 4 × 2 = 8 squares. Total squares = 4 + 8 = 12 squares. Since each square is 1 m², the total area is 12 m².

Question 12: A rectilinear picture frame drawn on a centimetre grid has an outer boundary of 7 squares by 6 squares. The empty space in the middle is 4 squares by 3 squares. Counting the squares of the border, what is the area of the picture frame in cm²?
Answer: 30 cm²
Working: Total outer area = 7 × 6 = 42 squares. Inner empty area = 4 × 3 = 12 squares. Area of the frame = 42 - 12 = 30 squares = 30 cm².
