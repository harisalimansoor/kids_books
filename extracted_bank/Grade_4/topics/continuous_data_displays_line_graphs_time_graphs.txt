--- Continuous data displays: line graphs / time graphs ---

Question 1: A line graph shows the temperature in a classroom recorded every hour from 9:00 am to 3:00 pm.

At 9:00 am, the temperature was 16°C.
At 10:00 am, the temperature was 18°C.
At 11:00 am, the temperature was 20°C.
At 12:00 noon, the temperature was 21°C.
At 1:00 pm, the temperature was 23°C.
At 2:00 pm, the temperature was 22°C.
At 3:00 pm, the temperature was 19°C.

What was the temperature in the classroom at 1:00 pm?
Answer: 23°C
Working: Locate 1:00 pm on the time graph: the temperature recorded is 23°C.

Question 2: A line graph tracks the depth of water in a paddling pool as it fills over 50 minutes.

At 0 minutes, the depth was 0 cm.
At 10 minutes, the depth was 8 cm.
At 20 minutes, the depth was 16 cm.
At 30 minutes, the depth was 24 cm.
At 40 minutes, the depth was 32 cm.
At 50 minutes, the depth was 40 cm.

How many centimetres did the depth of water increase between 10 minutes and 40 minutes?
Answer: 24 cm
Working: At 40 minutes, the depth was 32 cm. At 10 minutes, the depth was 8 cm. Calculate the difference: 32 - 8 = 24 cm.

Question 3: A time graph shows the temperature inside a greenhouse during one morning:

- 8:00 am: 14°C
- 9:00 am: 17°C
- 10:00 am: 21°C
- 11:00 am: 25°C
- 12:00 noon: 28°C

Between which two consecutive hours did the temperature increase the most?
Answer: Between 9:00 am and 10:00 am and between 10:00 am and 11:00 am (4°C each)
Working: Calculate the hourly increases: 8:00 am to 9:00 am: 17 - 14 = 3°C; 9:00 am to 10:00 am: 21 - 17 = 4°C; 10:00 am to 11:00 am: 25 - 21 = 4°C; 11:00 am to 12:00 noon: 28 - 25 = 3°C. The greatest increase of 4°C occurred between 9:00 am and 10:00 am, and also between 10:00 am and 11:00 am.

Question 4: A line graph shows the distance Sam cycled over a two-hour journey.

- Start (0 minutes): 0 km
- After 30 minutes: 6 km
- After 60 minutes: 12 km
- After 90 minutes: 12 km (Sam stopped for a rest)
- After 120 minutes: 18 km

For how many minutes was Sam stopped resting during the journey?
Answer: 30 minutes
Working: The distance remained constant at 12 km from 60 minutes to 90 minutes. 90 - 60 = 30 minutes.

Question 5: A time graph shows the height of a sunflower measured every 2 days over a 10-day period:

- Day 0: 10 cm
- Day 2: 15 cm
- Day 4: 22 cm
- Day 6: 31 cm
- Day 8: 38 cm
- Day 10: 42 cm

How much taller was the sunflower on Day 8 than it was on Day 2?
Answer: 23 cm
Working: Height on Day 8 = 38 cm. Height on Day 2 = 15 cm. Difference: 38 - 15 = 23 cm.

Question 6: A continuous line graph tracks the outdoor temperature from 6:00 am to 6:00 pm.

- At 6:00 am, the temperature was 5°C.
- The temperature rose steadily to reach a maximum of 19°C at 2:00 pm.
- From 2:00 pm, the temperature fell steadily to 11°C at 6:00 pm.

What was the total difference between the maximum temperature and the minimum temperature recorded?
Answer: 14°C
Working: Maximum temperature = 19°C (at 2:00 pm). Minimum temperature = 5°C (at 6:00 am). Difference = 19 - 5 = 14°C.
