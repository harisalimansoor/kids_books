=== Topic: Fractions of quantities (unit and non-unit fractions) and inverse scaling ===
Domain: Fractions and Decimals

--- Unit fractions of quantities ---

Question 1: What is \frac{1}{6} of 54?
Answer: 9
Working: 54 ÷ 6 = 9

Question 2: A ribbon is 72 cm long. Anja cuts off \frac{1}{8} of the ribbon. How many centimetres does Anja cut off?
Answer: 9 cm
Working: 72 ÷ 8 = 9 cm

Question 3: A box contains 96 marbles. \frac{1}{4} of the marbles are blue. How many marbles are blue?
Answer: 24
Working: 96 ÷ 4 = 24

--- Non-unit fractions of quantities ---

Question 4: Calculate \frac{3}{7} of 42.
Answer: 18
Working: 42 ÷ 7 = 6, then 6 × 3 = 18

Question 5: There are 60 children in Year 4. \frac{3}{5} of them walk to school. How many children walk to school?
Answer: 36
Working: 60 ÷ 5 = 12, then 12 × 3 = 36

Question 6: A reading book has 144 pages. Liam reads \frac{5}{12} of the book. How many pages does Liam read?
Answer: 60
Working: 144 ÷ 12 = 12, then 12 × 5 = 60

Question 7: A bag contains 80 counters. \frac{3}{8} of the counters are red and the rest are yellow. How many yellow counters are in the bag?
Answer: 50
Working: 80 ÷ 8 = 10. Red counters = 3 × 10 = 30. Yellow counters = 80 - 30 = 50

--- Inverse scaling ---

Question 8: \frac{1}{5} of a number is 9. What is the number?
Answer: 45
Working: 9 × 5 = 45

Question 9: \frac{1}{3} of the length of a piece of rope is 18 cm. What is the total length of the piece of rope?
Answer: 54 cm
Working: 18 × 3 = 54 cm

Question 10: \frac{2}{3} of a number is 24. What is the number?
Answer: 36
Working: 24 ÷ 2 = 12 (which is \frac{1}{3}), then 12 × 3 = 36

Question 11: \frac{3}{4} of the pupils in a class wear jumpers. If 21 pupils wear jumpers, how many pupils are there in total in the class?
Answer: 28
Working: 21 ÷ 3 = 7, then 7 × 4 = 28

Question 12: \frac{5}{6} of a jug's full capacity is 450 ml. What is the full capacity of the jug in millilitres?
Answer: 540 ml
Working: 450 ÷ 5 = 90 ml (which is \frac{1}{6}), then 90 × 6 = 540 ml
