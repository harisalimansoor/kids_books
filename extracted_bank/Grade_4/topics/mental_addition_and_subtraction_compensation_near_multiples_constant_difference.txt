=== Topic: Mental addition and subtraction (compensation, near-multiples, constant difference) ===
Domain: Addition and Subtraction

--- Compensation ---

Question 1: Use mental compensation to calculate 456 + 199.
Answer: 655
Working: Add 200 to 456 to get 656, then subtract 1 to compensate: 656 - 1 = 655.

Question 2: Use mental compensation to calculate 734 - 298.
Answer: 436
Working: Subtract 300 from 734 to get 434, then add 2 to compensate: 434 + 2 = 436.

Question 3: A library has 2,450 books. It receives a delivery of 1,998 new books. How many books does the library have in total?
Answer: 4,448
Working: Add 2,000 to 2,450 to get 4,450, then subtract 2 to compensate: 4,450 - 2 = 4,448.

Question 4: A runner aims to complete a 5,000 m race. She has run 2,995 m so far. How many metres does she have left to run?
Answer: 2,005 m
Working: 5,000 - 3,000 = 2,000. Add 5 to compensate: 2,000 + 5 = 2,005 m.

Question 5: Calculate 38 + 497 mentally by adjusting both numbers so that 497 becomes a multiple of 100.
Answer: 535
Working: Take 3 from 38 and add it to 497: 35 + 500 = 535.

--- Near-multiples ---

Question 6: Calculate 563 + 49 mentally using near-multiples.
Answer: 612
Working: 49 is 1 less than 50. 563 + 50 = 613, then 613 - 1 = 612.

Question 7: Calculate 842 - 39 mentally using near-multiples.
Answer: 803
Working: 39 is 1 less than 40. 842 - 40 = 802, then 802 + 1 = 803.

Question 8: A shopkeeper had 1,425 bottles of juice. She sold 390 bottles over the weekend. How many bottles of juice are left?
Answer: 1,035
Working: 390 is 10 less than 400. 1,425 - 400 = 1,025, then 1,025 + 10 = 1,035.

Question 9: A school has 1,350 raffle tickets. It sells 495 tickets on Monday. How many tickets are left to sell?
Answer: 855
Working: 495 is 5 less than 500. 1,350 - 500 = 850, then 850 + 5 = 855.

--- Constant difference ---

Question 10: Use the constant difference strategy to solve 3,000 - 1,425 by subtracting 1 from each number.
Answer: 1,575
Working: Subtract 1 from each number: 2,999 - 1,424 = 1,575.

Question 11: Use the constant difference method to calculate 843 - 299 by adding 1 to both numbers.
Answer: 544
Working: Add 1 to both numbers: (843 + 1) - (299 + 1) = 844 - 300 = 544.

Question 12: A cinema has 4,000 seats. 1,567 tickets have been sold. Use the constant difference method to work out how many seats are left unsold.
Answer: 2,433
Working: Subtract 1 from both numbers to keep the difference constant: 3,999 - 1,566 = 2,433.

Question 13: Calculate 5,002 - 1,998 mentally using the constant difference method.
Answer: 3,004
Working: Add 2 to both numbers: (5,002 + 2) - (1,998 + 2) = 5,004 - 2,000 = 3,004.
