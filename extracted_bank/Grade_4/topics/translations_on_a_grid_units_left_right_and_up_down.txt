=== Topic: Translations on a grid (units left/right and up/down) ===
Domain: Geometry (Properties of Shapes, Position and Direction)

--- Translations left and right ---

Question 1: A point is plotted at (2, 5) on a coordinate grid. The point is translated 4 units right. What are the new coordinates of the point?
Answer: (6, 5)
Working: Translating 4 units right increases the x-coordinate by 4: 2 + 4 = 6. The y-coordinate stays the same at 5. The new coordinates are (6, 5).

Question 2: Point A is at (8, 3) on a coordinate grid. It is translated 5 units left to Point B. What are the coordinates of Point B?
Answer: (3, 3)
Working: Translating 5 units left decreases the x-coordinate by 5: 8 - 5 = 3. The y-coordinate does not change. Point B is at (3, 3).

Question 3: Point P is at (3, 7) and Point Q is at (9, 7). Describe the translation from Point P to Point Q.
Answer: 6 units right
Working: The y-coordinate remains 7. The x-coordinate moves from 3 to 9: 9 - 3 = 6 units in the positive horizontal direction. The translation is 6 units right.

Question 4: A counter starts at an unknown point on a grid. After moving 3 units left, the counter is at (4, 6). What were the starting coordinates of the counter?
Answer: (7, 6)
Working: To find the starting point, apply the inverse movement: 3 units right. 4 + 3 = 7. The y-coordinate is unchanged. The starting coordinates were (7, 6).

Question 5: A triangle has vertices at (1, 2), (1, 6), and (4, 2). The triangle is translated 6 units right. What are the coordinates of the translated vertex corresponding to (4, 2)?
Answer: (10, 2)
Working: Translate the vertex (4, 2) 6 units right by adding 6 to the x-coordinate: 4 + 6 = 10. The y-coordinate remains 2. The new vertex is at (10, 2).

--- Translations up and down ---

Question 6: A point is plotted at (4, 2) on a coordinate grid. The point is translated 5 units up. What are the new coordinates of the point?
Answer: (4, 7)
Working: Translating 5 units up increases the y-coordinate by 5: 2 + 5 = 7. The x-coordinate remains 4. The new coordinates are (4, 7).

Question 7: Point C is at (6, 9) on a grid. It is translated 4 units down to Point D. What are the coordinates of Point D?
Answer: (6, 5)
Working: Translating 4 units down decreases the y-coordinate by 4: 9 - 4 = 5. The x-coordinate remains 6. Point D is at (6, 5).

Question 8: Point X is at (5, 8) and Point Y is at (5, 1). Describe the translation from Point X to Point Y.
Answer: 7 units down
Working: The x-coordinate remains 5. The y-coordinate moves from 8 to 1: 8 - 1 = 7 units in the downward direction. The translation is 7 units down.

Question 9: A point is translated 6 units down and ends at coordinates (3, 2). What were the original coordinates of the point?
Answer: (3, 8)
Working: Use the inverse operation to find the original position: move 6 units up. The y-coordinate is 2 + 6 = 8. The x-coordinate stays 3. The original coordinates were (3, 8).

Question 10: A square has vertices at (2, 1), (2, 4), (5, 4), and (5, 1). The square is translated 7 units up. What are the coordinates of the highest vertex on the translated square that has an x-coordinate of 2?
Answer: (2, 11)
Working: The original vertex with x = 2 and the highest y-value is (2, 4). Translating 7 units up increases the y-coordinate by 7: 4 + 7 = 11. The new coordinates are (2, 11).
