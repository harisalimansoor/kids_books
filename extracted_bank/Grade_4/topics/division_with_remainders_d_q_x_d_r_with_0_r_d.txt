=== Topic: Division with remainders: D = q x d + r with 0 <= r < d ===
Domain: Multiplication and Division

--- Division with remainders: D = q \× d + r with 0 \≤ r < d ---

Question 1: Calculate 38 ÷ 5. Give your answer as a quotient and a remainder.
Answer: 7 r 3
Working: Find the largest multiple of 5 less than or equal to 38: 7 × 5 = 35. Subtract to find the remainder: 38 - 35 = 3. Since 3 < 5, the answer is 7 r 3.

Question 2: A baker has 29 bread rolls. He packs them into bags of 4 rolls each. How many full bags does he make, and how many rolls are left over?
Answer: 7 full bags and 1 roll left over
Working: 29 ÷ 4 = 7 with a remainder of 1 (since 7 × 4 = 28 and 28 + 1 = 29). He makes 7 full bags with 1 roll left over.

Question 3: A mystery number divided by 6 gives a quotient of 8 and a remainder of 4. What is the mystery number?
Answer: 52
Working: Use the rule Dividend = (Quotient × Divisor) + Remainder: D = (8 × 6) + 4 = 48 + 4 = 52.

Question 4: Calculate 67 ÷ 8. Write the answer in the form q r r.
Answer: 8 r 3
Working: 8 × 8 = 64. 67 - 64 = 3. Since 3 < 8, 67 ÷ 8 = 8 r 3.

Question 5: Fill in the missing remainder: 86 = (9 × 9) + ___
Answer: 5
Working: 9 × 9 = 81. 86 - 81 = 5. The missing remainder is 5.

Question 6: When dividing any whole number by 7, what is the largest possible remainder?
Answer: 6
Working: The remainder must be strictly less than the divisor (r < d). Since the divisor is 7, the possible remainders are 0, 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, or 6. The largest possible remainder is 6.

Question 7: A pupil writes that 53 ÷ 6 = 7 r 11. What is the correct quotient and remainder?
Answer: 8 r 5
Working: The remainder 11 is greater than the divisor 6, so another group of 6 can be made: 7 + 1 = 8 groups. 8 × 6 = 48, and 53 - 48 = 5. The correct answer is 8 r 5.

Question 8: 94 counters are arranged into equal rows of 8 counters. How many complete rows are made, and how many more counters are needed to complete one more row?
Answer: 11 complete rows, 2 more counters needed
Working: 94 ÷ 8 = 11 r 6 (since 11 × 8 = 88 and 94 - 88 = 6). There are 11 complete rows and 6 counters left over. To make another full row of 8 counters: 8 - 6 = 2 more counters needed.

Question 9: When a whole number is divided by 9, the quotient is 7. What are the smallest and largest possible values of this number?
Answer: Smallest: 63, Largest: 71
Working: Using D = 7 × 9 + r, the remainder r can range from 0 to 8 (0 ≤ r < 9). Smallest number: (7 × 9) + 0 = 63. Largest number: (7 × 9) + 8 = 63 + 8 = 71.

Question 10: A jar contains fewer than 50 marbles. When the marbles are shared equally into 6 pots, there are 3 marbles left over. When they are shared equally into 7 pots, there is 1 marble left over. How many marbles are in the jar?
Answer: 15
Working: Numbers less than 50 with remainder 3 when divided by 6: 9, 15, 21, 27, 33, 39, 45. Check which gives remainder 1 when divided by 7: 15 ÷ 7 = 2 r 1. So there are 15 marbles.
