=== Topic: Adding and subtracting fractions with the same denominator ===
Domain: Fractions and Decimals

--- Adding fractions with the same denominator ---

Question 1: Calculate: 2/7 + 3/7
Answer: 5/7
Working: When adding fractions with the same denominator, add the numerators and keep the denominator the same: 2 + 3 = 5, so 2/7 + 3/7 = 5/7.

Question 2: Liam walks 3/10 of a kilometre in the morning and 4/10 of a kilometre in the afternoon. What fraction of a kilometre does Liam walk altogether?
Answer: 7/10 km
Working: Add the two fractions: 3/10 + 4/10 = (3 + 4)/10 = 7/10 km.

Question 3: Calculate: 5/8 + 4/8. Give your answer as an improper fraction.
Answer: 9/8
Working: Add the numerators: 5 + 4 = 9. The denominator remains 8. So 5/8 + 4/8 = 9/8.

Question 4: Find the missing fraction in the calculation: 4/9 + ? = 8/9
Answer: 4/9
Working: Subtract 4/9 from 8/9 to find the missing fraction: 8/9 - 4/9 = 4/9.

Question 5: Three children share a large carton of juice. Maya drinks 2/12 of the carton, Ethan drinks 5/12, and Chloe drinks 3/12. What fraction of the carton do they drink in total?
Answer: 10/12
Working: Add the three fractions: 2/12 + 5/12 + 3/12 = (2 + 5 + 3)/12 = 10/12.

Question 6: A baker uses 5/6 of a kilogram of flour for white bread and 4/6 of a kilogram of flour for brown bread. Write the total amount of flour used as a mixed number.
Answer: 1 3/6 kg
Working: Add the fractions: 5/6 + 4/6 = 9/6 kg. Convert 9/6 to a mixed number: 9/6 = 1 whole and 3/6, so 1 3/6 kg (or 1 1/2 kg).

--- Subtracting fractions with the same denominator ---

Question 7: Calculate: 7/9 - 2/9
Answer: 5/9
Working: When subtracting fractions with the same denominator, subtract the numerators and keep the denominator the same: 7 - 2 = 5, so 7/9 - 2/9 = 5/9.

Question 8: A bottle contains 9/10 of a litre of water. Sophie pours out 4/10 of a litre. How much water is left in the bottle?
Answer: 5/10 l
Working: Subtract the amount poured out from the initial amount: 9/10 - 4/10 = 5/10 litres.

Question 9: Calculate: 1 - 3/8
Answer: 5/8
Working: Convert 1 whole into eighths: 1 = 8/8. Then subtract: 8/8 - 3/8 = 5/8.

Question 10: Find the missing fraction in the calculation: 11/12 - ? = 4/12
Answer: 7/12
Working: Subtract the result from the starting fraction: 11/12 - 4/12 = (11 - 4)/12 = 7/12.

Question 11: Calculate: 13/5 - 4/5. Give your answer as an improper fraction.
Answer: 9/5
Working: Subtract the numerators: 13 - 4 = 9. The denominator remains 5. So 13/5 - 4/5 = 9/5.

Question 12: A roll of ribbon is 1 whole metre long. James cuts a piece that is 2/7 of a metre long and another piece that is 3/7 of a metre long. What fraction of a metre of ribbon remains on the roll?
Answer: 2/7 m
Working: Total ribbon cut = 2/7 + 3/7 = 5/7 metre. 1 whole metre = 7/7 metre. Remaining ribbon = 7/7 - 5/7 = 2/7 metre.
