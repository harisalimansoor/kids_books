=== Topic: Multiplication and division facts up to 12 x 12 ===
Domain: Multiplication and Division

--- Multiplication facts up to 12 × 12 ---

Question 1: Calculate: $7 \times 8$
Answer: 56
Working: Recall the 7 times table or 8 times table: 7 × 8 = 56.

Question 2: Calculate: $9 \times 6$
Answer: 54
Working: Recall the 9 times table: 9 × 6 = 54.

Question 3: What is the missing number? $8 \times \Box = 72$
Answer: 9
Working: 72 ÷ 8 = 9, so 8 × 9 = 72.

Question 4: A baker packs rolls into bags of 6. How many rolls are there in 12 bags?
Answer: 72
Working: 12 × 6 = 72 rolls.

Question 5: Calculate: $12 \times 11$
Answer: 132
Working: 12 × 10 = 120, and 120 + 12 = 132.

Question 6: A school hall has 7 rows of chairs with 12 chairs in each row. How many chairs are in the hall in total?
Answer: 84
Working: 7 × 12 = 84 chairs.

Question 7: Liam says, 'If I know $6 \times 7 = 42$, I can double that to find $12 \times 7$.' What is $12 \times 7$?
Answer: 84
Working: Doubling 42 gives 42 + 42 = 84. Therefore, 12 × 7 = 84.

--- Division facts up to 12 × 12 ---

Question 8: Calculate: $56 \div 7$
Answer: 8
Working: Recall the inverse multiplication fact: 7 × 8 = 56, so 56 ÷ 7 = 8.

Question 9: Calculate: $63 \div 9$
Answer: 7
Working: 9 × 7 = 63, so 63 ÷ 9 = 7.

Question 10: What is the missing number? $96 \div \Box = 8$
Answer: 12
Working: 96 ÷ 8 = 12, so 96 ÷ 12 = 8.

Question 11: 48 pencils are shared equally among 6 pots. How many pencils are in each pot?
Answer: 8
Working: 48 ÷ 6 = 8 pencils per pot.

Question 12: Calculate: $132 \div 12$
Answer: 11
Working: 12 × 11 = 132, so 132 ÷ 12 = 11.

Question 13: What is the missing number? $\Box \div 9 = 12$
Answer: 108
Working: Using the inverse operation: 12 × 9 = 108.

Question 14: A teacher has 144 counters. She places them into equal bags of 12. How many bags does she fill?
Answer: 12
Working: 144 ÷ 12 = 12 bags.
