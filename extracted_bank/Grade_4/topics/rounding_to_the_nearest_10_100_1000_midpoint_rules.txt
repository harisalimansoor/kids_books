=== Topic: Rounding to the nearest 10, 100, 1000 (midpoint rules) ===
Domain: Number and Place Value

--- Rounding to the nearest 10 ---

Question 1: Round 74 to the nearest 10.
Answer: 70
Working: The ones digit is 4, which is less than 5, so round down to 70.

Question 2: Round 368 to the nearest 10.
Answer: 370
Working: The ones digit is 8, which is 5 or more, so round up to 370.

Question 3: Round 2,496 to the nearest 10.
Answer: 2500
Working: The ones digit is 6, which is 5 or more, so round up. Adding 1 ten to 49 tens gives 50 tens, making 2,500.

Question 4: A school library has 1,384 books. What is the number of books rounded to the nearest 10?
Answer: 1380
Working: Look at the ones digit in 1,384. It is 4, so round down to 1,380.

Question 5: What is the smallest whole number that rounds to 340 when rounded to the nearest 10?
Answer: 335
Working: Numbers from 335 to 344 round to 340. The smallest is 335.

Question 6: What is the largest whole number that rounds to 560 when rounded to the nearest 10?
Answer: 564
Working: Numbers from 555 to 564 round to 560. The largest is 564.

--- Midpoint rules ---

Question 18: What is the midpoint between 40 and 50 on a number line?
Answer: 45
Working: The number exactly halfway between 40 and 50 is 45.

Question 19: What is the midpoint between 600 and 700 on a number line?
Answer: 650
Working: The number exactly halfway between 600 and 700 is 650.

Question 20: What is the midpoint between 3,000 and 4,000 on a number line?
Answer: 3500
Working: The number exactly halfway between 3,000 and 4,000 is 3,500.

Question 21: The midpoint between 200 and 300 is 250. According to the midpoint rule, which hundred does 250 round to when rounded to the nearest 100?
Answer: 300
Working: When a number is exactly at the midpoint, the rule is to round up. Therefore, 250 rounds up to 300.

Question 22: On a number line from 8,000 to 9,000, the midpoint is 8,500. Does 8,500 round to 8,000 or 9,000 when rounded to the nearest 1000?
Answer: 9000
Working: By the midpoint rule, numbers at the midpoint round up to the next multiple of 1000. 8,500 rounds to 9,000.

Question 23: What is the smallest two-digit number that rounds up to 50 when rounded to the nearest 10 because it is at the midpoint?
Answer: 45
Working: The midpoint between 40 and 50 is 45. It is the smallest number that rounds up to 50.

Question 24: The midpoint between 4,000 and 5,000 is 4,500. Liam writes the number 4,499. Is 4,499 less than, equal to, or greater than the midpoint?
Answer: less than
Working: 4,499 is 1 less than 4,500, so it is less than the midpoint.
