=== Topic: Tenths and hundredths as fractions and decimals (0.1 = \frac{1{10, 0.01 = \frac{1{100) ===
Domain: Fractions and Decimals

--- Tenths as fractions and decimals ---

Question 1: Write $\frac{7}{10}$ as a decimal.
Answer: 0.7
Working: $\frac{7}{10}$ represents 7 tenths, which is written as $0.7$ in decimal notation.

Question 2: Write the decimal $0.4$ as a fraction with a denominator of $10$.
Answer: 4/10
Working: $0.4$ has a 4 in the tenths column, so it is equal to $\frac{4}{10}$.

Question 3: Write the mixed number $3\frac{6}{10}$ as a decimal.
Answer: 3.6
Working: 3 whole ones plus 6 tenths is equal to $3 + 0.6 = 3.6$.

Question 4: A ribbon that is $1\text{ m}$ long is cut into $10$ equal pieces. Jack uses $9$ of these pieces. What fraction and what decimal of the whole ribbon has Jack used?
Answer: 9/10 and 0.9
Working: Each piece is $\frac{1}{10}\text{ m}$. Jack uses 9 pieces, which is $\frac{9}{10}$ or $0.9$.

Question 5: Write the improper fraction $\frac{14}{10}$ as a decimal.
Answer: 1.4
Working: $\frac{14}{10} = \frac{10}{10} + \frac{4}{10} = 1\text{ one and }4\text{ tenths} = 1.4$.

Question 6: Starting at $0.7$, count on $5$ tenths. What number do you reach as a decimal?
Answer: 1.2
Working: $0.7 + 0.5 = 7\text{ tenths} + 5\text{ tenths} = 12\text{ tenths} = 1.2$.

--- Hundredths as fractions and decimals ---

Question 7: Write $\frac{37}{100}$ as a decimal.
Answer: 0.37
Working: $\frac{37}{100}$ is 37 hundredths, which is 3 tenths and 7 hundredths, written as $0.37$.

Question 8: Write $\frac{6}{100}$ as a decimal.
Answer: 0.06
Working: $\frac{6}{100}$ has 0 tenths and 6 hundredths, so a zero placeholder is needed in the tenths column: $0.06$.

Question 9: Write the decimal $0.03$ as a fraction with a denominator of $100$.
Answer: 3/100
Working: In $0.03$, the digit 3 is in the hundredths position, which equals $\frac{3}{100}$.

Question 10: Write the mixed number $4\frac{8}{100}$ as a decimal.
Answer: 4.08
Working: 4 ones plus 8 hundredths (0 tenths and 8 hundredths) is $4 + 0.08 = 4.08$.

Question 11: Complete the statement: $5\text{ tenths} + 8\text{ hundredths} = \frac{\Box}{100} = 0.58$. What number goes in the box?
Answer: 58
Working: $5\text{ tenths} = \frac{50}{100}$. $\frac{50}{100} + \frac{8}{100} = \frac{58}{100}$.

Question 12: One tenth is divided into $10$ equal parts. Write the value of one of these parts as a fraction and as a decimal.
Answer: 1/100 and 0.01
Working: Dividing one tenth ($\frac{1}{10}$) by 10 gives one hundredth, which is written as $\frac{1}{100}$ and $0.01$.

Question 13: What is the next number in this sequence of decimals: $0.97, 0.98, 0.99, \dots$?
Answer: 1.00
Working: Counting up in hundredths ($0.01$), $99\text{ hundredths} + 1\text{ hundredth} = 100\text{ hundredths} = 1\text{ whole}$ or $1.00$ ($1$).

Question 14: A number is made of $12$ tenths and $6$ hundredths. Write this number as a decimal.
Answer: 1.26
Working: $12\text{ tenths} = 1.2$ (or $1\text{ one and }2\text{ tenths}$). Adding $6\text{ hundredths}$ ($0.06$) gives $1.2 + 0.06 = 1.26$.
