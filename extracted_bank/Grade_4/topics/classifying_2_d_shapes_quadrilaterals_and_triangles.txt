=== Topic: Classifying 2-D shapes: quadrilaterals and triangles ===
Domain: Geometry (Properties of Shapes, Position and Direction)

--- Quadrilaterals ---

Question 1: What is the name of a 2-D quadrilateral that has 4 equal sides and 4 right angles?
Answer: Square
Working: A quadrilateral with 4 sides of equal length and 4 right angles is a square.

Question 2: A 4-sided polygon has exactly one pair of parallel sides. What is the name of this quadrilateral?
Answer: Trapezium
Working: A quadrilateral with exactly one pair of parallel sides is called a trapezium.

Question 3: A quadrilateral has 4 sides of equal length. Opposite sides are parallel, but it has no right angles (it has 2 acute angles and 2 obtuse angles). What is the name of this quadrilateral?
Answer: Rhombus
Working: A 4-sided shape with 4 equal sides and opposite parallel sides without right angles is a rhombus.

Question 4: A quadrilateral has two pairs of adjacent (next to each other) sides of equal length. Its opposite sides are not parallel and not equal. What is the name of this shape?
Answer: Kite
Working: A quadrilateral with two pairs of equal-length adjacent sides and no parallel sides is a kite.

Question 5: A quadrilateral has two pairs of parallel sides. Its opposite sides are equal in length (two longer sides and two shorter sides). It has 2 acute angles and 2 obtuse angles. What is the name of this quadrilateral?
Answer: Parallelogram
Working: A quadrilateral with two pairs of parallel opposite sides of different adjacent lengths and no right angles is a parallelogram.

Question 6: Both a rectangle and a parallelogram have two pairs of parallel sides and opposite sides of equal length. What property do the angles of a rectangle always have that a general parallelogram does not?
Answer: All 4 angles are right angles (90 degrees)
Working: A rectangle must have four right angles (90°), whereas a non-rectangular parallelogram has two acute and two obtuse angles.

--- Triangles ---

Question 7: A triangle has side lengths of 7 cm, 7 cm, and 7 cm. All 3 interior angles are equal. What type of triangle is this?
Answer: Equilateral triangle
Working: A triangle with all 3 sides of equal length and all 3 angles equal is an equilateral triangle.

Question 8: What is the name of a triangle in which all three sides have different lengths and all three angles are different?
Answer: Scalene triangle
Working: A triangle with three sides of different lengths is a scalene triangle.

Question 9: A triangle has side lengths of 8 cm, 8 cm, and 5 cm. It has exactly two angles of equal size. What type of triangle is this?
Answer: Isosceles triangle
Working: A triangle with exactly two sides of equal length and two equal angles is an isosceles triangle.

Question 10: A triangle has side lengths of 6 cm, 6 cm, and 9 cm. One of its angles is an obtuse angle (greater than 90°). Classify this triangle by its side lengths.
Answer: Isosceles triangle
Working: Since two sides are equal in length (6 cm and 6 cm), the triangle is classified by its sides as an isosceles triangle.

Question 11: An equilateral triangle has a perimeter of 27 cm. What is the length of each side in centimetres?
Answer: 9 cm
Working: An equilateral triangle has 3 equal sides. 27 ÷ 3 = 9 cm.

Question 12: A triangle has one right angle (90°) and two sides that are each 5 cm long. What complete classification describes this triangle based on both its angles and its side lengths?
Answer: Right-angled isosceles triangle
Working: The triangle has a right angle, making it right-angled, and two equal sides (5 cm each), making it isosceles. Together it is a right-angled isosceles triangle.
