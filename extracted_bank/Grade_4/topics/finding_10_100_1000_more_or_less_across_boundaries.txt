=== Topic: Finding 10, 100, 1000 more or less across boundaries ===
Domain: Number and Place Value

--- Finding 10 more or less ---

Question 1: What number is 10 more than 4,295?
Answer: 4,305
Working: 4,295 + 10 = 4,305 (adding 1 ten to 9 tens makes 10 tens, exchanging 10 tens for 1 hundred).

Question 2: What number is 10 less than 3,504?
Answer: 3,494
Working: 3,504 - 10 = 3,494 (exchanging 1 hundred for 10 tens: 50 tens subtract 1 ten is 49 tens).

Question 3: A runner had completed 2,998 metres. She then ran another 10 metres. How many metres has she run in total?
Answer: 3,008
Working: 2,998 + 10 = 3,008 metres (9 tens + 1 ten = 10 tens, exchanging across both the hundreds and thousands boundaries).

Question 4: A water tank contained 6,003 litres of water. 10 litres were drained out. How many litres of water are left in the tank?
Answer: 5,993
Working: 6,003 - 10 = 5,993 litres (exchanging 1 thousand to make hundreds and tens across boundaries).

--- Finding 100 more or less ---

Question 5: What number is 100 more than 1,940?
Answer: 2,040
Working: 1,940 + 100 = 2,040 (9 hundreds + 1 hundred = 10 hundreds, exchanging 10 hundreds for 1 thousand).

Question 6: What number is 100 less than 5,063?
Answer: 4,963
Working: 5,063 - 100 = 4,963 (exchanging 1 thousand for 10 hundreds: 50 hundreds subtract 1 hundred is 49 hundreds).

Question 7: A library had 3,950 books. It received a donation of 100 new books. How many books does the library have now?
Answer: 4,050
Working: 3,950 + 100 = 4,050 books (39 hundreds + 1 hundred = 40 hundreds).

Question 8: An aeroplane was flying at an altitude of 7,015 metres. It descended by 100 metres. What is its new altitude in metres?
Answer: 6,915
Working: 7,015 - 100 = 6,915 metres (70 hundreds - 1 hundred = 69 hundreds).

--- Finding 1000 more or less ---

Question 9: What number is 1000 more than 4,736?
Answer: 5,736
Working: 4,736 + 1000 = 5,736 (increasing the thousands digit by 1: 4 thousands + 1 thousand = 5 thousands).

Question 10: What number is 1000 more than 985?
Answer: 1,985
Working: 985 + 1000 = 1,985 (crossing the boundary from a 3-digit number to a 4-digit number).

Question 11: What number is 1000 less than 1,048?
Answer: 48
Working: 1,048 - 1000 = 48 (1 thousand - 1 thousand = 0 thousands, leaving tens and ones).

Question 12: A stadium had 8,024 spectators on Saturday. On Sunday, there were 1000 fewer spectators. How many spectators were there on Sunday?
Answer: 7,024
Working: 8,024 - 1000 = 7,024 spectators (8 thousands - 1 thousand = 7 thousands).
