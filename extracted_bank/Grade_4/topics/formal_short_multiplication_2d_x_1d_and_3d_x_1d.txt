=== Topic: Formal short multiplication (2D x 1D and 3D x 1D) ===
Domain: Multiplication and Division

--- Formal short multiplication (2-digit by 1-digit) ---

Question 1: Use a formal written method to calculate $43 \times 3$.
Answer: 129
Working: Multiply the ones: $3 \times 3 = 9$. Multiply the tens: $40 \times 3 = 120$. Add together: $120 + 9 = 129$.

Question 2: Use formal short multiplication to calculate $56 \times 4$.
Answer: 224
Working: Multiply the ones: $6 \times 4 = 24$, write 4 in the ones column and exchange 2 tens. Multiply the tens: $5 \times 4 = 20$ tens, plus the 2 exchanged tens gives 22 tens. Total is 224.

Question 3: A box contains 38 coloured pencils. How many pencils are there altogether in 6 of these boxes?
Answer: 228
Working: $38 \times 6$: $8 \times 6 = 48$ ones (write 8, exchange 4 tens). $3 \times 6 = 18$ tens, $18 + 4 = 22$ tens. Answer is 228 pencils.

Question 4: Calculate $87 \times 9$ using a formal column method.
Answer: 783
Working: Multiply the ones: $7 \times 9 = 63$ (write 3, exchange 6 tens). Multiply the tens: $8 \times 9 = 72$, add exchanged 6 tens: $72 + 6 = 78$ tens. Total is 783.

Question 5: A baker bakes 74 loaves of bread every day for 7 days. How many loaves of bread does the baker make in total?
Answer: 518
Working: $74 \times 7$: $4 \times 7 = 28$ (write 8, exchange 2). $7 \times 7 = 49$, $49 + 2 = 51$ tens. Total is 518 loaves.

Question 6: In the short multiplication calculation $[\;]8 \times 6 = 408$, what is the missing tens digit?
Answer: 6
Working: Multiply ones: $8 \times 6 = 48$, which gives 8 in the ones column and 4 tens exchanged. The tens calculation is $([\;] \times 6) + 4 = 40$. Therefore, $[\;] \times 6 = 36$, so the missing digit is $36 \div 6 = 6$.

--- Formal short multiplication (3-digit by 1-digit) ---

Question 7: Use formal short multiplication to calculate $231 \times 3$.
Answer: 693
Working: Multiply ones: $1 \times 3 = 3$. Multiply tens: $3 \times 3 = 9$ tens. Multiply hundreds: $2 \times 3 = 6$ hundreds. Total is 693.

Question 8: Calculate $324 \times 4$ using a formal written method.
Answer: 1296
Working: Multiply ones: $4 \times 4 = 16$ (write 6, exchange 1 ten). Multiply tens: $2 \times 4 = 8$, $8 + 1 = 9$ tens. Multiply hundreds: $3 \times 4 = 12$ hundreds. Total is 1296.

Question 9: A school buys 8 identical science kits. Each kit costs £125. What is the total cost of the science kits?
Answer: £1000
Working: $125 \times 8$: $5 \times 8 = 40$ (write 0, exchange 4). $2 \times 8 = 16$, $16 + 4 = 20$ (write 0, exchange 2). $1 \times 8 = 8$, $8 + 2 = 10$. Total is £1000.

Question 10: Use formal short multiplication to calculate $648 \times 7$.
Answer: 4536
Working: Multiply ones: $8 \times 7 = 56$ (write 6, exchange 5 tens). Multiply tens: $4 \times 7 = 28$, $28 + 5 = 33$ (write 3, exchange 3 hundreds). Multiply hundreds: $6 \times 7 = 42$, $42 + 3 = 45$ hundreds. Total is 4536.

Question 11: A factory packs 309 bottles of juice into each shipping crate. How many bottles of juice are there in 9 crates?
Answer: 2781
Working: $309 \times 9$: Multiply ones: $9 \times 9 = 81$ (write 1, exchange 8 tens). Multiply tens: $0 \times 9 = 0$, $0 + 8 = 8$ tens. Multiply hundreds: $3 \times 9 = 27$ hundreds. Total is 2781 bottles.

Question 12: A 3-digit by 1-digit multiplication has a missing tens digit: $4[\;]7 \times 5 = 2335$. What is the missing digit?
Answer: 6
Working: Multiply ones: $7 \times 5 = 35$ (write 5, exchange 3 tens). The tens calculation is $([\;] \times 5) + 3$ which ends in 3, and the hundreds gives $4 \times 5 + \text{exchanged tens} = 23$. So exchanged tens must be 3, giving $([\;] \times 5) + 3 = 33$. Thus $[\;] \times 5 = 30$, so the missing digit is 6.
