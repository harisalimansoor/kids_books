=== Topic: Equivalent fraction families and fraction simplification ===
Domain: Fractions and Decimals

--- Equivalent fraction families ---

Question 1: Write the missing numerator to complete the equivalent fraction: 1/4 = [ ]/12.
Answer: 3
Working: The denominator 4 has been multiplied by 3 to make 12 (4 x 3 = 12). Multiply the numerator by 3: 1 x 3 = 3. So, 1/4 = 3/12.

Question 2: Write the missing denominator to make the fractions equivalent: 3/5 = 18/[ ].
Answer: 30
Working: The numerator 3 is multiplied by 6 to make 18 (3 x 6 = 18). Multiply the denominator 5 by 6: 5 x 6 = 30. So, 3/5 = 18/30.

Question 3: A fraction wall shows that 2/3 is equal to 4/6, 6/9, and [ ]/12. Write the missing fraction that completes this family of equivalent fractions.
Answer: 8/12
Working: Multiply the numerator and denominator of 2/3 by 4: (2 x 4) / (3 x 4) = 8/12.

Question 4: Jack has a paper strip divided into 10 equal parts, and 6 parts are shaded. Maya has an identical strip divided into 5 equal parts. How many parts must Maya shade so her strip shows an equivalent fraction?
Answer: 3
Working: Jack's fraction is 6/10. Divide both the numerator and denominator by 2: 6/10 = (6 ÷ 2)/(10 ÷ 2) = 3/5. Maya must shade 3 parts.

Question 5: Four fractions are written below: 2/8, 3/12, 4/16, 5/25. Which one of these fractions is NOT equivalent to the others?
Answer: 5/25
Working: 2/8 = 1/4, 3/12 = 1/4, and 4/16 = 1/4. However, 5/25 = 1/5. Therefore, 5/25 is not equivalent to the others.

Question 6: Write two fractions that are equivalent to 5/6, one with a denominator of 18 and one with a denominator of 24.
Answer: 15/18 and 20/24
Working: To get a denominator of 18, multiply numerator and denominator of 5/6 by 3: (5 x 3)/(6 x 3) = 15/18. To get a denominator of 24, multiply by 4: (5 x 4)/(6 x 4) = 20/24.

Question 7: An equivalent fraction to 4/7 has a denominator that is 27 greater than its numerator. What is this fraction?
Answer: 36/63
Working: For 4/7, the difference between denominator and numerator is 7 - 4 = 3. We need a difference of 27. Scale factor = 27 ÷ 3 = 9. Multiply numerator and denominator by 9: (4 x 9)/(7 x 9) = 36/63. Check: 63 - 36 = 27.

--- Fraction simplification ---

Question 8: Simplify the fraction 4/8 to its simplest form.
Answer: 1/2
Working: Divide both numerator and denominator by their greatest common factor, 4: (4 ÷ 4) / (8 ÷ 4) = 1/2.

Question 9: Write 9/12 in its simplest form.
Answer: 3/4
Working: Divide both the numerator and denominator by 3: (9 ÷ 3) / (12 ÷ 3) = 3/4.

Question 10: Write the fraction 8/20 in its simplest form.
Answer: 2/5
Working: Divide both numerator and denominator by 4: (8 ÷ 4) / (20 ÷ 4) = 2/5.

Question 11: A baker bakes 24 biscuits. 6 of the biscuits are ginger biscuits. Write the fraction of the biscuits that are ginger in its simplest form.
Answer: 1/4
Working: The fraction is 6/24. Divide both numerator and denominator by 6: (6 ÷ 6) / (24 ÷ 6) = 1/4.

Question 12: Leo simplifies 12/18 by dividing both numbers by 2 to get 6/9. Explain what Leo must do to write the fraction in its simplest form, and state the final fraction.
Answer: Divide the numerator and denominator by 3 to get 2/3
Working: 6/9 is not in simplest form because 6 and 9 still share a common factor of 3. Dividing both by 3 gives (6 ÷ 3)/(9 ÷ 3) = 2/3.

Question 13: In a class of 30 pupils, 18 walk to school. What fraction of the class walks to school? Give your answer in its simplest form.
Answer: 3/5
Working: The fraction is 18/30. Divide numerator and denominator by their greatest common factor, 6: (18 ÷ 6) / (30 ÷ 6) = 3/5.

Question 14: A fraction simplifies to 2/3. The sum of the original fraction's numerator and denominator was 45. What was the original fraction before simplification?
Answer: 18/27
Working: In the simplified fraction 2/3, the sum of parts is 2 + 3 = 5 parts. 45 ÷ 5 = 9 per part. Numerator = 2 x 9 = 18. Denominator = 3 x 9 = 27. The original fraction was 18/27.
