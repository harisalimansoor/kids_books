=== Topic: Metric conversions (km-m, m-cm-mm, kg-g, l-ml) ===
Domain: Measurement, Money and Time

--- Converting between kilometres and metres (km and m) ---

Question 1: Convert 6 kilometres into metres.
Answer: 6000 m
Working: 1 km = 1000 m. 6 x 1000 = 6000 m.

Question 2: Convert 4500 metres into kilometres and metres.
Answer: 4 km 500 m
Working: 4000 m = 4 km, with 500 m remaining. 4500 m = 4 km 500 m.

Question 3: A runner completes a 3 km race. How many metres has she run?
Answer: 3000 m
Working: 1 km = 1000 m. 3 x 1000 = 3000 m.

Question 4: Complete the statement using <, > or =:
3200 m ___ 3 km 50 m
Answer: >
Working: 3 km 50 m = 3000 m + 50 m = 3050 m. 3200 m > 3050 m.

Question 5: A cyclist travels 2 km 350 m on Monday and 1 km 800 m on Tuesday. What is the total distance travelled in metres?
Answer: 4150 m
Working: 2 km 350 m = 2350 m. 1 km 800 m = 1800 m. 2350 m + 1800 m = 4150 m.

Question 6: A walking route is 5 km long. Tom has walked 2800 m so far. How many metres does Tom have left to walk?
Answer: 2200 m
Working: 5 km = 5000 m. 5000 m - 2800 m = 2200 m.

--- Converting between metres, centimetres and millimetres (m, cm and mm) ---

Question 7: Convert 7 metres into centimetres.
Answer: 700 cm
Working: 1 m = 100 cm. 7 x 100 = 700 cm.

Question 8: Convert 40 millimetres into centimetres.
Answer: 4 cm
Working: 10 mm = 1 cm. 40 / 10 = 4 cm.

Question 9: A piece of ribbon is 3 m 45 cm long. How long is the ribbon in centimetres?
Answer: 345 cm
Working: 3 m = 300 cm. 300 cm + 45 cm = 345 cm.

Question 10: A pencil is 12 cm 6 mm long. What is the length of the pencil in millimetres?
Answer: 126 mm
Working: 1 cm = 10 mm. 12 cm = 120 mm. 120 mm + 6 mm = 126 mm.

Question 11: A plank of wood is 2 metres long. A builder cuts off a piece measuring 65 centimetres. What is the length of the remaining wood in centimetres?
Answer: 135 cm
Working: 2 m = 200 cm. 200 cm - 65 cm = 135 cm.

Question 12: Put these lengths in order from shortest to longest:
150 mm, 1 m, 25 cm
Answer: 150 mm, 25 cm, 1 m
Working: Convert all to centimetres or millimetres: 150 mm = 15 cm; 25 cm = 25 cm; 1 m = 100 cm. Ordered: 150 mm, 25 cm, 1 m.

--- Converting between kilograms and grams (kg and g) ---

Question 13: Convert 5 kilograms into grams.
Answer: 5000 g
Working: 1 kg = 1000 g. 5 x 1000 = 5000 g.

Question 14: Convert 3200 grams into kilograms and grams.
Answer: 3 kg 200 g
Working: 3000 g = 3 kg. 3200 g = 3 kg 200 g.

Question 15: A bag of flour weighs 2 kg 400 g. What is the weight of the flour in grams?
Answer: 2400 g
Working: 2 kg = 2000 g. 2000 g + 400 g = 2400 g.

Question 16: Complete the statement using <, > or =:
4 kg 50 g ___ 4500 g
Answer: <
Working: 4 kg 50 g = 4050 g. 4050 g < 4500 g.

Question 17: A parcel weighs 1 kg 750 g. A second parcel weighs 850 g. What is the total mass of the two parcels in grams?
Answer: 2600 g
Working: 1 kg 750 g = 1750 g. 1750 g + 850 g = 2600 g.

Question 18: A baker has a 5 kg sack of sugar. She uses 1600 g for baking cakes. How many grams of sugar are left in the sack?
Answer: 3400 g
Working: 5 kg = 5000 g. 5000 g - 1600 g = 3400 g.

--- Converting between litres and millilitres (l and ml) ---

Question 19: Convert 4 litres into millilitres.
Answer: 4000 ml
Working: 1 l = 1000 ml. 4 x 1000 = 4000 ml.

Question 20: Convert 7800 millilitres into litres and millilitres.
Answer: 7 l 800 ml
Working: 7000 ml = 7 l. 7800 ml = 7 l 800 ml.

Question 21: A jug holds 1 l 650 ml of water. What is the volume of water in millilitres?
Answer: 1650 ml
Working: 1 l = 1000 ml. 1000 ml + 650 ml = 1650 ml.

Question 22: Complete the statement using <, > or =:
2 l 5 ml ___ 2050 ml
Answer: <
Working: 2 l 5 ml = 2005 ml. 2005 ml < 2050 ml.

Question 23: A bottle contains 2 litres of juice. Leo drinks 350 ml and Mia drinks 450 ml. How many millilitres of juice are left in the bottle?
Answer: 1200 ml
Working: 2 litres = 2000 ml. Total drunk = 350 ml + 450 ml = 800 ml. Remaining = 2000 ml - 800 ml = 1200 ml.

Question 24: A bucket has a capacity of 3 litres. How many 500 millilitre glasses of water are needed to fill the bucket completely?
Answer: 6
Working: 3 litres = 3000 ml. 3000 / 500 = 6 glasses.
