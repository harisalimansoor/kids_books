=== Topic: Data problem solving: comparison, sum, and difference questions ===
Domain: Statistics

--- Comparison questions ---

Question 1: A table shows the points scored by four school houses at sports day: Oak scored 425 points, Beech scored 390 points, Ash scored 460 points, and Elm scored 415 points. Write the names of the houses in order of points scored, from most to least.
Answer: Ash, Oak, Elm, Beech
Working: Compare the scores: 460 > 425 > 415 > 390. Ordering from highest to lowest gives Ash (460), Oak (425), Elm (415), Beech (390).

Question 2: A bar chart shows the mass of paper collected for recycling by three classes: Class 4A collected 24 kg, Class 4B collected 72 kg, and Class 4C collected 48 kg. How many times as much paper did Class 4B collect compared to Class 4A?
Answer: 3
Working: Class 4B collected 72 kg and Class 4A collected 24 kg. 72 divided by 24 = 3. Class 4B collected 3 times as much.

Question 3: A line graph tracks the temperature of water cooling in a science experiment over four recordings: at 0 minutes it was 80°C, at 5 minutes it was 62°C, at 10 minutes it was 48°C, and at 15 minutes it was 35°C. At which recorded time was the water temperature closest to 50°C?
Answer: 10 minutes
Working: Find the difference from 50°C for each recording: 80 - 50 = 30°C; 62 - 50 = 12°C; 50 - 48 = 2°C; 50 - 35 = 15°C. The smallest difference is 2°C at 10 minutes.

--- Sum questions ---

Question 4: A table shows the number of swimmers at a leisure centre across four sessions on Saturday: Morning session had 145 swimmers, Midday session had 218 swimmers, Afternoon session had 264 swimmers, and Evening session had 183 swimmers. What was the total number of swimmers across all four sessions?
Answer: 810
Working: 145 + 218 + 264 + 183 = 810 swimmers in total.

Question 5: A pictogram shows the number of trees planted by a conservation group over three weeks. The key shows that 1 tree symbol represents 40 trees. Week 1 has 3 symbols, Week 2 has 4 symbols, and Week 3 has 2 and a half symbols. How many trees were planted altogether over the three weeks?
Answer: 380
Working: Week 1: 3 × 40 = 120 trees. Week 2: 4 × 40 = 160 trees. Week 3: (2 × 40) + 20 = 100 trees. Total = 120 + 160 + 100 = 380 trees.

Question 6: A line graph shows the distance a cyclist travelled in each stage of a three-stage charity ride: 18 km in stage 1, 23 km in stage 2, and 19 km in stage 3. What was the total distance cycled across all three stages?
Answer: 60 km
Working: 18 + 23 + 19 = 60 km.

--- Difference questions ---

Question 7: A bar chart shows the number of books loaned by a community library: 186 books on Monday, 245 books on Tuesday, 198 books on Wednesday, and 312 books on Thursday. How many more books were loaned on Thursday than on Monday?
Answer: 126
Working: Thursday books minus Monday books: 312 - 186 = 126.

Question 8: A time graph shows the temperature inside a greenhouse from 8:00 am to 2:00 pm. At 8:00 am the temperature was 13°C, and at 2:00 pm the temperature reached 27°C. What was the difference in temperature between 8:00 am and 2:00 pm?
Answer: 14°C
Working: 27°C - 13°C = 14°C.

Question 9: A pictogram shows the number of merit stickers earned by two classes. The key states that 1 star symbol represents 8 stickers. Class 4R has 7 whole star symbols. Class 4S has 4 whole star symbols and 1 half star symbol. How many more stickers did Class 4R earn than Class 4S?
Answer: 20
Working: Class 4R: 7 × 8 = 56 stickers. Class 4S: (4 × 8) + 4 = 36 stickers. Difference: 56 - 36 = 20 stickers.
