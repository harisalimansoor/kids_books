=== Topic: Ordering and comparing numbers beyond 1000 (<, >, =) ===
Domain: Number and Place Value

--- Comparing numbers beyond 1000 (<, >, =) ---

Question 1: Write the correct symbol, <, >, or =, to make the statement true:

3,482 ___ 3,842
Answer: <
Working: Both numbers have 3 thousands. Comparing the hundreds digits, 4 hundreds is less than 8 hundreds. Therefore, 3,482 < 3,842.

Question 2: Write the correct symbol, <, >, or =, to make the statement true:

6,000 + 700 + 9 ___ 6,790
Answer: <
Working: 6,000 + 700 + 9 = 6,709. Comparing 6,709 and 6,790: both have 6 thousands and 7 hundreds, but 6,709 has 0 tens while 6,790 has 9 tens. Therefore, 6,709 < 6,790.

Question 3: A football stadium in Manchester has a capacity of 8,145 seats. A stadium in Birmingham has a capacity of 8,154 seats. Which stadium has the greater capacity?
Answer: The stadium in Birmingham
Working: Both capacities have 8 thousands and 1 hundred. Comparing the tens column, 5 tens is greater than 4 tens, so 8,154 > 8,145. Therefore, the stadium in Birmingham has the greater capacity.

Question 4: What is the smallest single digit that can be placed in the missing box to make the statement correct?

7,5_4 > 7,568
Answer: 7
Working: The thousands digits (7) and hundreds digits (5) are identical. If the tens digit is 6, 7,564 is less than 7,568. To make the number strictly greater than 7,568, the tens digit must be at least 7. The smallest digit is 7.

--- Ordering numbers beyond 1000 ---

Question 5: Write these numbers in order of size, starting with the smallest:

4,215, 4,125, 4,521, 4,251
Answer: 4,125, 4,215, 4,251, 4,521
Working: Compare thousands (all 4). Compare hundreds: 4,125 has 1 hundred (smallest). 4,215 and 4,251 have 2 hundreds (4,215 has 1 ten, 4,251 has 5 tens). 4,521 has 5 hundreds (greatest). The order from smallest to greatest is 4,125, 4,215, 4,251, 4,521.

Question 6: Write these numbers in descending order (from greatest to smallest):

9,080, 9,800, 9,008, 9,808
Answer: 9,808, 9,800, 9,080, 9,008
Working: All numbers have 9 thousands. The numbers with 8 hundreds are 9,808 and 9,800 (9,808 > 9,800). The numbers with 0 hundreds are 9,080 and 9,008 (9,080 > 9,008). Ordered from greatest to smallest: 9,808, 9,800, 9,080, 9,008.

Question 7: Four schools collected tokens for a charity appeal:
- Oak School: 5 thousands, 3 tens and 6 ones
- Pine School: 5,360
- Elm School: 5,063
- Ash School: 5,306

Order the schools from the fewest tokens collected to the most tokens collected.
Answer: Oak School, Elm School, Ash School, Pine School
Working: Convert to standard digits: Oak School = 5,036; Elm School = 5,063; Ash School = 5,306; Pine School = 5,360. Ordering the totals from least to greatest: 5,036 < 5,063 < 5,306 < 5,360. The order of schools is Oak School, Elm School, Ash School, Pine School.

Question 8: Here are four digit cards: 2, 5, 8, 3.

Use all four cards once to make:
a) The smallest possible 4-digit number.
b) The greatest possible 4-digit number.

Write both numbers.
Answer: Smallest: 2,358; Greatest: 8,532
Working: To make the smallest number, arrange digits in ascending order: 2 thousands, 3 hundreds, 5 tens, 8 ones = 2,358. To make the greatest number, arrange digits in descending order: 8 thousands, 5 hundreds, 3 tens, 2 ones = 8,532.
