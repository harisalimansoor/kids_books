=== Topic: 4-digit place value (1000s, 100s, 10s, 1s) and standard partitioning ===
Domain: Number and Place Value

--- 4-digit place value (1000s, 100s, 10s, 1s) ---

Question 1: What is the value of the digit 7 in the number 5,742?
Answer: 700
Working: In 5,742, the 5 represents 5,000, the 7 represents 700 (7 hundreds), the 4 represents 40, and the 2 represents 2. Therefore, the value of the digit 7 is 700.

Question 2: Which digit is in the tens column in the number 8,319?
Answer: 1
Working: In the number 8,319, 8 is in the thousands column, 3 is in the hundreds column, 1 is in the tens column, and 9 is in the ones column.

Question 3: A number has 6 thousands, 0 hundreds, 4 tens, and 9 ones. What is the number in digits?
Answer: 6,049
Working: 6 thousands = 6,000, 0 hundreds = 0, 4 tens = 40, and 9 ones = 9. Combining these gives 6,049.

Question 4: Jack makes a 4-digit number using place value counters. He uses four 1,000 counters, three 100 counters, zero 10 counters, and eight 1 counters. What number has Jack made?
Answer: 4,308
Working: 4 × 1,000 = 4,000; 3 × 100 = 300; 0 × 10 = 0; 8 × 1 = 8. 4,000 + 300 + 0 + 8 = 4,308.

Question 5: What number is formed if the tens digit of 7,452 is increased by 3 and all other digits stay the same?
Answer: 7,482
Working: The tens digit in 7,452 is 5. Increasing the tens digit by 3 gives 5 + 3 = 8 tens. Keeping the other digits the same gives 7,482.

Question 6: Using the digit cards 4, 7, 2, and 9 exactly once each, write the largest possible 4-digit number that has an even number in the ones column.
Answer: 9,742
Working: To make the number even, the ones digit must be 2 or 4. To make the largest number overall, place the largest available digits in the highest place value columns: 9 in the thousands, 7 in the hundreds, 4 in the tens, leaving 2 in the ones column. This gives 9,742.

--- Standard partitioning ---

Question 7: Complete the calculation to show the standard partitioning of 3,845:
3,845 = 3,000 + ___ + 40 + 5
Answer: 800
Working: 3,845 partitioned into thousands, hundreds, tens, and ones is 3,000 + 800 + 40 + 5. The missing number is 800.

Question 8: What 4-digit number is represented by 5,000 + 700 + 9?
Answer: 5,709
Working: 5,000 + 700 + 0 tens + 9 ones = 5,709.

Question 9: In a part-whole model, the whole is 6,427. Three of the parts are 6,000, 20, and 7. What is the missing fourth part?
Answer: 400
Working: Standard partitioning of 6,427 gives 6,000 + 400 + 20 + 7. The parts given are 6,000, 20, and 7, so the missing part representing the hundreds is 400.

Question 10: Fill in the missing number to complete the equation:
9,034 = 9,000 + ___ + 4
Answer: 30
Working: 9,034 has 9 thousands (9,000), 0 hundreds (0), 3 tens (30), and 4 ones (4). Therefore, 9,034 = 9,000 + 30 + 4. The missing number is 30.

Question 11: A stadium sells 8,605 tickets for a football match. Write this total partitioned into its standard place value parts using an addition sentence.
Answer: 8,000 + 600 + 5
Working: 8,605 has 8 thousands, 6 hundreds, 0 tens, and 5 ones. Partitioned in standard form, it is written as 8,000 + 600 + 5.
