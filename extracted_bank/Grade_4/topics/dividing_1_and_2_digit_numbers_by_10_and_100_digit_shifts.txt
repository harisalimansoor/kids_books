=== Topic: Dividing 1- and 2-digit numbers by 10 and 100 (digit shifts) ===
Domain: Fractions and Decimals

--- Dividing 1- and 2-digit numbers by 10 ---

Question 1: Calculate $6 ÷ 10$.
Answer: 0.6
Working: Dividing 6 by 10 shifts the digit 1 place to the right, from the ones column into the tenths column, giving 0.6.

Question 2: Calculate $53 ÷ 10$.
Answer: 5.3
Working: Dividing 53 by 10 shifts each digit 1 place to the right: 5 tens become 5 ones and 3 ones become 3 tenths, giving 5.3.

Question 3: A piece of wood is $85\text{ cm}$ long. It is cut into 10 equal pieces. How long is each piece in centimetres?
Answer: 8.5 cm
Working: 85 divided by 10 shifts the digits 1 place to the right: $85 \÷ 10 = 8.5\text{ cm}$.

Question 4: When 74 is divided by 10, what is the value of the digit 4 in the answer?
Answer: 4 tenths
Working: $74 \÷ 10 = 7.4$. In 7.4, the digit 7 is 7 ones and the digit 4 is 4 tenths (or 0.4).

Question 5: Fill in the missing number: $\square \÷ 10 = 3.9$
Answer: 39
Working: The inverse of dividing by 10 is multiplying by 10. $3.9 \× 10 = 39$.

--- Dividing 1- and 2-digit numbers by 100 ---

Question 6: Calculate $8 \÷ 100$.
Answer: 0.08
Working: Dividing 8 by 100 shifts the digit 8 two places to the right, from the ones column into the hundredths column, giving 0.08.

Question 7: Calculate $47 \÷ 100$.
Answer: 0.47
Working: Dividing 47 by 100 shifts each digit 2 places to the right: 4 tens become 4 tenths and 7 ones become 7 hundredths, giving 0.47.

Question 8: Calculate $60 \÷ 100$. Give your answer as a decimal.
Answer: 0.6
Working: Dividing 60 by 100 shifts the digits 2 places to the right: 6 tens become 6 tenths, giving 0.6 (or 0.60).

Question 9: When the 2-digit number 91 is divided by 100, what is the value of the digit 1 in the answer?
Answer: 1 hundredth
Working: $91 \÷ 100 = 0.91$. The digit 9 has a value of 9 tenths and the digit 1 has a value of 1 hundredth (or 0.01).

Question 10: A ball of string of length $92\text{ m}$ is shared equally into 100 small pieces. What is the length of each piece in metres?
Answer: 0.92 m
Working: $92 \÷ 100 = 0.92$. Each piece is $0.92\text{ m}$ long.

Question 11: Fill in the missing number: $\square \÷ 100 = 0.25$
Answer: 25
Working: The inverse of dividing by 100 is multiplying by 100. $0.25 \× 100 = 25$.
