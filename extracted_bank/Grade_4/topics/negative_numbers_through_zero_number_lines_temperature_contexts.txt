=== Topic: Negative numbers through zero (number lines, temperature contexts) ===
Domain: Number and Place Value

--- Number lines ---

Question 1: A number line has markings going up in ones. Start at 4 and count back 7. Which number do you land on?
Answer: -3
Working: Counting back 4 from 4 reaches 0. Counting back another 3 reaches -3 (4 - 7 = -3).

Question 2: A number line sequence counts backwards in equal steps of 2: 6, 4, 2, 0, __, -4, -6. What is the missing number?
Answer: -2
Working: The rule is subtracting 2 each time. 0 - 2 = -2.

Question 3: On a horizontal number line with markings from -6 to 6, what number lies exactly halfway between -6 and 6?
Answer: 0
Working: -6 and 6 are equal distances from zero on opposite sides of the number line, so the midpoint is 0.

Question 4: On a number line, point A is marked at -8 and point B is marked at 5. What is the total distance in units between point A and point B?
Answer: 13
Working: The distance from -8 to 0 is 8 units. The distance from 0 to 5 is 5 units. Total distance = 8 + 5 = 13 units.

Question 5: A counter starts at 6 on a number line and moves backwards in 4 equal jumps of 3. What number does the counter land on?
Answer: -6
Working: 4 jumps of 3 = 4 × 3 = 12 units backwards. 6 - 12 = -6.

--- Temperature contexts ---

Question 6: At 8:00 pm the temperature was 3 °C. By midnight, the temperature had fallen by 5 °C. What was the temperature at midnight?
Answer: -2 °C
Working: 3 - 5 = -2 °C.

Question 7: At 6:00 am the temperature outside was -4 °C. By midday, it had risen by 9 °C. What was the temperature at midday?
Answer: 5 °C
Working: -4 + 9 = 5 °C.

Question 8: In London the temperature is 4 °C. In Oslo the temperature is -6 °C. How many degrees colder is Oslo than London?
Answer: 10 °C
Working: From 4 °C down to 0 °C is 4 °C. From 0 °C down to -6 °C is 6 °C. Difference = 4 + 6 = 10 °C.

Question 9: Inside a freezer the temperature is -18 °C. The room temperature in the kitchen is 21 °C. How many degrees warmer is the kitchen than the freezer?
Answer: 39 °C
Working: Distance from -18 °C to 0 °C is 18 °C. Distance from 0 °C to 21 °C is 21 °C. Total difference = 18 + 21 = 39 °C.

Question 10: On Monday night the temperature was -3 °C. On Tuesday night it was 5 °C colder than Monday night. On Wednesday night it was 4 °C warmer than Tuesday night. What was the temperature on Wednesday night?
Answer: -4 °C
Working: Tuesday night: -3 - 5 = -8 °C. Wednesday night: -8 + 4 = -4 °C.
