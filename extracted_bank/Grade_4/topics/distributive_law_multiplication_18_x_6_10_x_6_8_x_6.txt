Domain: Multiplication and Division

--- Distributive law multiplication (18 x 6 = 10 x 6 + 8 x 6) ---

Question 1: Fill in the missing number to complete the statement:
$17 \times 5 = (10 \times 5) + (\square \times 5)$
Answer: 7
Working: Partition 17 into tens and ones: $17 = 10 + 7$.
Using the distributive law: $17 \times 5 = (10 \times 5) + (7 \times 5)$.
The missing number is 7.

Question 2: Fill in the missing number to complete the calculation:
$14 \times 6 = (10 \times 6) + (4 \times 6) = 60 + \square$
Answer: 24
Working: $10 \times 6 = 60$
$4 \times 6 = 24$
$60 + 24 = 84$
The missing number is 24.

Question 3: Use the distributive law to calculate $19 \times 4$ by working out $(10 \times 4) + (9 \times 4)$.
Answer: 76
Working: $10 \times 4 = 40$
$9 \times 4 = 36$
$40 + 36 = 76$

Question 4: Fill in the missing number to make the number sentence correct:
$(10 \times 7) + (6 \times 7) = \square \times 7$
Answer: 16
Working: Combine the tens and ones: $10 + 6 = 16$.
So $(10 \times 7) + (6 \times 7) = 16 \times 7$.
The missing number is 16.

Question 5: Fill in the missing number:
$23 \times 4 = (20 \times 4) + (\square \times 4)$
Answer: 3
Working: Partition 23 into 20 and 3.
$23 \times 4 = (20 \times 4) + (3 \times 4)$.
The missing number is 3.

Question 6: A box contains 16 packs of stickers. There are 8 stickers in each pack.
Work out the total number of stickers by calculating $(10 \times 8) + (6 \times 8)$.
Answer: 128
Working: $10 \times 8 = 80$
$6 \times 8 = 48$
$80 + 48 = 128$

Question 7: Fill in the missing number:
$18 \times 9 = (10 \times 9) + (8 \times \square)$
Answer: 9
Working: By the distributive law, multiplying 18 by 9 means multiplying both 10 and 8 by 9.
$18 \times 9 = (10 \times 9) + (8 \times 9)$.
The missing number is 9.

Question 8: A classroom has 6 rows of desks. There are 15 desks in each row.
Use partitioning into tens and ones to write and solve the calculation for the total number of desks.
Answer: 90
Working: Partition 15 into $10 + 5$.
$15 \times 6 = (10 \times 6) + (5 \times 6)$
$10 \times 6 = 60$
$5 \times 6 = 30$
$60 + 30 = 90$

Question 9: Work out the value of $(20 \times 7) + (5 \times 7)$ to find the product of $25 \times 7$.
Answer: 175
Working: $20 \times 7 = 140$
$5 \times 7 = 35$
$140 + 35 = 175$

Question 10: Fill in the missing number to make the statement true:
$(\square \times 6) + (7 \times 6) = 17 \times 6$
Answer: 10
Working: Partitioning 17 gives $10 + 7$.
$17 \times 6 = (10 \times 6) + (7 \times 6)$.
The missing number is 10.
