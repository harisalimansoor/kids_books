=== Topic: Perimeter of rectilinear shapes in cm and m ===
Domain: Measurement, Money and Time

--- Perimeter of rectilinear shapes in cm and m ---

Question 1: A rectangle has a length of $14\text{ cm}$ and a width of $6\text{ cm}$. What is the perimeter of the rectangle?
Answer: 40 cm
Working: Perimeter = (14 + 6) × 2 = 20 × 2 = 40 cm (or 14 + 6 + 14 + 6 = 40 cm).

Question 2: A square playground has sides of length $18\text{ m}$. Calculate the perimeter of the playground.
Answer: 72 m
Working: Perimeter of a square = 4 × side length = 4 × 18 = 72 m.

Question 3: An L-shaped rectilinear room has six sides. Starting from the top horizontal edge and going clockwise, the side lengths are $9\text{ m}$, $4\text{ m}$, $3\text{ m}$, $5\text{ m}$, $6\text{ m}$, and $9\text{ m}$. What is the perimeter of the room?
Answer: 36 m
Working: Perimeter = sum of all outer sides = 9 + 4 + 3 + 5 + 6 + 9 = 36 m.

Question 4: The perimeter of a square tile is $36\text{ cm}$. What is the length of one side of the tile?
Answer: 9 cm
Working: Side length = Perimeter ÷ 4 = 36 ÷ 4 = 9 cm.

Question 5: An L-shaped rectilinear shape has a total horizontal width of $12\text{ cm}$ along the bottom and a total vertical height of $10\text{ cm}$ on the left. The top horizontal edge is $7\text{ cm}$ and the far right vertical edge is $4\text{ cm}$. Calculate the perimeter of the shape.
Answer: 44 cm
Working: Missing inner horizontal edge = 12 - 7 = 5 cm. Missing inner vertical edge = 10 - 4 = 6 cm. Perimeter = 12 + 10 + 7 + 4 + 5 + 6 = 44 cm.

Question 6: A rectangle has a perimeter of $48\text{ cm}$. Its length is $16\text{ cm}$. What is the width of the rectangle?
Answer: 8 cm
Working: Sum of two lengths = 16 × 2 = 32 cm. Sum of two widths = 48 - 32 = 16 cm. Width = 16 ÷ 2 = 8 cm.

Question 7: A rectilinear garden has an overall length of $15\text{ m}$ along the bottom and an overall height of $11\text{ m}$ along the left side. The top edge is $8\text{ m}$ and the right-hand edge is $6\text{ m}$. A corner is indented with two inner perpendicular edges. What is the total perimeter of the garden?
Answer: 52 m
Working: The missing horizontal edge = 15 - 8 = 7 m. The missing vertical edge = 11 - 6 = 5 m. Perimeter = 15 + 11 + 8 + 5 + 7 + 6 = 52 m.

Question 8: Shape A is a rectangle measuring $9\text{ m}$ by $5\text{ m}$. Shape B is a square with the exact same perimeter as Shape A. What is the length of one side of Shape B?
Answer: 7 m
Working: Perimeter of Shape A = (9 + 5) × 2 = 28 m. Side length of Shape B = 28 ÷ 4 = 7 m.

Question 9: Two identical rectangles, each with a length of $12\text{ cm}$ and a width of $5\text{ cm}$, are joined end-to-end along their shorter sides to make one longer rectangle. What is the perimeter of the new shape?
Answer: 58 cm
Working: New length = 12 + 12 = 24 cm. New width = 5 cm. Perimeter = (24 + 5) × 2 = 29 × 2 = 58 cm.

Question 10: A rectilinear shape is made by placing a small square with sides of $4\text{ cm}$ on top of a rectangle measuring $10\text{ cm}$ by $6\text{ cm}$, so that one side of the square rests along the middle of the $10\text{ cm}$ top side. What is the perimeter of the combined rectilinear shape?
Answer: 40 cm
Working: Perimeter of rectangle = (10 + 6) × 2 = 32 cm. Adding the square adds 3 of its sides (3 × 4 = 12 cm) but covers 1 side (4 cm) on the rectangle: 32 + 12 - 4 = 40 cm.
