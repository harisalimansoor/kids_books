=== Topic: Counting in multiples of 6, 7, 9, 25, 1000 ===
Domain: Number and Place Value

--- Counting in multiples of 6 ---

Question 1: What is the next number in this sequence?

18, 24, 30, 36, ___
Answer: 42
Working: The sequence increases by 6 each time. 36 + 6 = 42.

Question 2: Count backwards in multiples of 6 to find the missing number:

66, 60, 54, ___, 42
Answer: 48
Working: The sequence decreases by 6 each time. 54 - 6 = 48.

Question 3: A baker packs bread rolls into bags of 6. She has already packed 48 rolls. If she packs 4 more full bags, how many rolls will she have packed in total?
Answer: 72
Working: Counting on 4 multiples of 6 from 48: 54, 60, 66, 72. (4 x 6 = 24; 48 + 24 = 72 rolls).

--- Counting in multiples of 7 ---

Question 4: Complete the number sequence by filling in the missing number:

14, 21, 28, ___, 42
Answer: 35
Working: The sequence counts up in multiples of 7. 28 + 7 = 35.

Question 5: Starting at 84, count backwards in multiples of 7. What is the third number you say after 84?
Answer: 63
Working: Counting backwards in 7s: 1st number is 77, 2nd number is 70, 3rd number is 63.

Question 6: A netball tournament lasts 7 weeks. Each week, a team plays 7 matches. By the end of week 5, how many matches has the team played in total?
Answer: 35
Working: Count in multiples of 7 five times: 7, 14, 21, 28, 35 matches.

--- Counting in multiples of 9 ---

Question 7: Write the next number in this sequence:

27, 36, 45, 54, ___
Answer: 63
Working: The sequence increases by 9 each time. 54 + 9 = 63.

Question 8: Find the missing number in this sequence:

108, 99, 90, ___, 72
Answer: 81
Working: The sequence counts down in 9s. 90 - 9 = 81.

Question 9: Liam starts at 0 and counts up in multiples of 9. Is 74 in Liam's sequence? Write Yes or No.
Answer: No
Working: Multiples of 9 around 74 are 9 x 8 = 72 and 9 x 9 = 81. 74 is not a multiple of 9, so the answer is No.

--- Counting in multiples of 25 ---

Question 10: What is the missing number in this sequence?

25, 50, 75, ___, 125
Answer: 100
Working: The sequence increases by 25 each time. 75 + 25 = 100.

Question 11: Count backwards in multiples of 25 from 350:

350, 325, 300, 275, ___
Answer: 250
Working: The sequence decreases by 25 each time. 275 - 25 = 250.

Question 12: A sports shop sells shuttlecocks in tubes of 25. The coach buys 9 full tubes. How many shuttlecocks does the coach buy in total?
Answer: 225
Working: Counting in 25s nine times: 25, 50, 75, 100, 125, 150, 175, 200, 225 shuttlecocks.

--- Counting in multiples of 1000 ---

Question 13: What is the next number in this sequence?

3000, 4000, 5000, 6000, ___
Answer: 7000
Working: The sequence increases by 1000 each time. 6000 + 1000 = 7000.

Question 14: Count backwards in thousands to fill in the missing number:

9000, 8000, ___, 6000, 5000
Answer: 7000
Working: The sequence decreases by 1000 each time. 8000 - 1000 = 7000.

Question 15: A runner tracks their distance in metres. They run 1000 m every day for a week (7 days). How many metres have they run altogether by the end of the week?
Answer: 7000
Working: Counting in 1000s for 7 days gives 1000, 2000, 3000, 4000, 5000, 6000, 7000 m.
