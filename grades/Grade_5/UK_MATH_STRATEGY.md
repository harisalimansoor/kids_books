# UK Mathematics Question Generation Strategy: Grade 5 (Year 5)

## 1. Scope & Source Provenance
- **Stage:** Key Stage 2 Year 5 (Ages 9–10).
- **Authoritative Baseline:** England Mathematics Programme of Study (statutory National Curriculum Year 5).
- **Pedagogical Sources:** Oak National Academy Year 5 Maths, Cambridge Primary Mathematics Stage 5.
- **Strict Scope Boundary:** Numbers to 1,000,000; prime, composite, square, and cube numbers; long multiplication (up to 4-digit by 2-digit); thousandths decimals; percentages (%); area of rectangles (A = l x w); volume; timetables.

## 2. Topic Taxonomy & Objectives
- **number_and_place_value:** Read, write, order, and compare numbers to at least 1,000,000 and determine value of each digit; count forwards or backwards in steps of powers of 10; interpret negative numbers in context; round any number up to 1,000,000 to nearest 10, 100, 1000, 10,000, 100,000; Roman numerals to 1000 (M).
- **addition_and_subtraction:** Add and subtract whole numbers with more than 4 digits using formal written methods (columnar); add and subtract numbers mentally with increasingly large numbers.
- **multiplication_and_division:** Identify multiples and factors; prime numbers up to 100 and prime factors; multiply numbers up to 4 digits by a 1- or 2-digit number using a formal written method; divide numbers up to 4 digits by a 1-digit number; recognize and use square numbers and cube numbers.
- **fractions_decimals_percentages:** Compare and order fractions whose denominators are all multiples of the same number; identify, name, and write equivalent fractions; recognise mixed numbers and improper fractions and convert between them; add and subtract fractions with denominators that are multiples; multiply proper fractions and mixed numbers by whole numbers; read and write decimal numbers as fractions; decimals to three decimal places; recognise % and solve simple percentage problems (e.g. 50%, 25%, 10%).
- **measurement:** Convert between different units of metric measure (km/m, cm/m, cm/mm, kg/g, l/ml); calculate perimeter of composite rectilinear shapes; calculate and compare area of rectangles (including squares) using standard units (cm², m²); estimate volume and capacity.
- **time:** Solve problems involving converting between units of time; read and interpret timetables (bus, coach, train schedules).
- **geometry:** Identify 3D shapes from 2D representations; estimate and compare acute, obtuse, and reflex angles; angles at a point (360°), angles on a straight line (180°); coordinates in all four quadrants; reflection and translation.
- **statistics:** Solve comparison, sum, and difference problems using information presented in a line graph; complete, read, and interpret information in tables, including timetables.
