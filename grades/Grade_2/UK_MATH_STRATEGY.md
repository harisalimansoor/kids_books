# UK Mathematics Question Generation Strategy: Grade 2 (Year 2)

## 1. Scope & Source Provenance
- **Stage:** Key Stage 1 Year 2 (Ages 6–7).
- **Authoritative Baseline:** England Mathematics Programme of Study (statutory National Curriculum Year 2).
- **Pedagogical Sources:** Oak National Academy Year 2 Maths, Cambridge Primary Mathematics Stage 2.
- **Strict Scope Boundary:** Numbers to 100; 2-digit addition and subtraction with and without regrouping; times tables 2, 5, 10 (and introducing 3); telling time to 5 minutes (quarter past, quarter to); fractions 1/3, 1/4, 2/4, 1/2, 3/4. No 3-digit operations or column long multiplication.

## 2. Topic Taxonomy & Objectives
- **number_and_place_value:** Read and write numbers to 100 in numerals and words; partition 2-digit numbers into tens and ones; compare and order numbers using <, >, =; count in 2s, 3s, 5s, 10s from any number.
- **addition_and_subtraction:** Recall addition and subtraction facts to 20 fluently, and derive related facts up to 100 (e.g. 30 + 70 = 100); add and subtract 2-digit numbers; show that addition is commutative and subtraction is not.
- **multiplication_and_division:** Recall multiplication and division facts for 2, 5, and 10 times tables; recognise odd and even numbers; write statements using x, ÷, and =.
- **fractions:** Recognise, find, name and write fractions 1/3, 1/4, 2/4, 1/2, 3/4 of shapes, lengths, or quantities; write simple equivalent fractions (2/4 = 1/2).
- **measurement:** Choose and use appropriate standard units to estimate and measure length/height (m, cm), mass (kg, g), temperature (°C), capacity (l, ml).
- **money:** Recognise symbols for pounds (£) and pence (p); combine coins and notes to make amounts; solve practical problems giving change.
- **time:** Tell and write the time to 5 minutes, including quarter past/to the hour; draw hands on clock faces; compare and sequence intervals of time; minutes in an hour, hours in a day.
- **geometry:** Identify and describe properties of 2D shapes (number of sides and vertical line of symmetry) and 3D shapes (edges, vertices, faces); order and arrange combinations of mathematical objects in patterns and sequences.
- **statistics:** Interpret and construct simple pictograms (keys of 1, 2, 5, 10), tally charts, block diagrams, and simple tables; answer 'how many more' and 'how many fewer'.
