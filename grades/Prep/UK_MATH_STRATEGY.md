# UK Mathematics Question Generation Strategy: Prep / Pre-Reception (Ages 3.5–4.5)

## 1. Scope & Source Provenance
- **Stage:** EYFS Pre-Reception / Cambridge Early Years Step 2 / Oak Reception Prep.
- **Pedagogical Target:** Ages 3.5–4.5 bridging into formal reception mathematics.
- **Strict Scope Boundary:** Numbers 1–10; perceptual subitising up to 3 objects; no formal arithmetic symbols; emphasis on cardinality and number conservation.

## 2. Topic Taxonomy & Objectives
- **counting_and_cardinality:** Counting reliably up to 10 objects; recognising that the final number count is the total amount (cardinality); subitising 1 to 3 items without counting.
- **sorting_and_classifying:** Sorting everyday items by 1 clear attribute (colour, shape, size); identifying the odd one out in a group.
- **number_composition:** Understanding that numbers are made of smaller parts within 5 (e.g. 4 is 2 and 2, or 3 and 1).
- **measurement_and_comparison:** Comparing lengths and heights directly (taller, shorter, longer); capacity (full, empty, half full); mass (heavier, lighter).
- **patterns_and_geometry:** Recognising circles, squares, triangles, rectangles; completing AB and ABB repeating patterns.
- **daily_routines_and_time:** Sequencing events in the child's daily routine: morning, afternoon, bedtime; understanding before and after.

## 3. Two-Character Story Guidelines
- Features {user_name} and companion actively cooperating on activities: packing picnic baskets, tidying toy shelves, planting flower seeds, sorting socks.
