# UK Mathematics Question Generation Strategy: Nursery (Ages 3–4)

## 1. Scope & Source Provenance
- **Stage:** Early Years Foundation Stage (EYFS) - Nursery / Cambridge Early Years Step 1 / Oak Early Years.
- **Pedagogical Target:** Ages 3–4 foundational mathematical experiences.
- **Strict Scope Boundary:** Numbers strictly 1–5; direct sensory comparisons; no formal symbols (+, -, =) or written equations; highly contextual, playful storytelling.

## 2. Topic Taxonomy & Objectives
- **counting:** Count verbally and point 1-to-1 to up to 5 physical items (apples, blocks, teddy bears, ducklings).
- **comparison:** Direct visual comparison of two objects: bigger / smaller, taller / shorter, longer / shorter, more / fewer.
- **shapes:** Explore and name primary 2D shapes in the everyday environment: circle (round like a plate/coin), square (4 straight sides like a window/biscuit), triangle (3 sides like a slice of watermelon).
- **spatial_and_position:** Understand real-world positional concepts: in, on, under, inside, outside, top, bottom, up, down.
- **patterns:** Notice, copy, and continue simple AB repeating patterns (e.g. red block, blue block, red block...).
- **measurement:** Direct physical sensory language: heavy / light, full / empty, warm / cold.

## 3. Two-Character Story Guidelines
- Involves {user_name} and a loving companion ({mother_name}, {father_name}, {sibling_name}, {grandmother_name}, {grandfather_name}).
- Relatable early childhood settings: nursery rug, garden, kitchen snack time, park sandpit, bathtime toys.
