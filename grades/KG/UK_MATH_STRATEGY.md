# UK Mathematics Question Generation Strategy: Kindergarten / Reception (Ages 4–5)

## 1. Scope & Source Provenance
- **Stage:** Early Years Foundation Stage (EYFS) Reception / Early Learning Goals (ELGs) / Cambridge Primary Stage 1 Prep / Oak Reception Maths.
- **Pedagogical Target:** Ages 4–5 preparation for Year 1.
- **Strict Scope Boundary:** Numbers within 20 (deep fluency within 10); number bonds to 5 and 10; subitising to 5; doubles to 10 (double 1 to 5); sharing between 2 characters.

## 2. Topic Taxonomy & Objectives
- **number_and_place_value:** Count to 20 forwards and backwards; subitise up to 5 items on a 5-frame or die pattern; 1 more and 1 less than any number up to 10; compare groups using 'more than', 'fewer than', 'same'.
- **addition_and_subtraction:** Combine two groups of objects to find a total within 10; take away objects and count how many remain; use part-part-whole language and ten-frames; number bonds to 5 and 10.
- **doubles_and_sharing:** Explore even sharing of small quantities between {user_name} and companion; discover doubles up to double 5 (2, 4, 6, 8, 10).
- **measurement:** Compare lengths, heights, and weights using non-standard units (cubes, paperclips); capacity (full, empty, nearly full, half full).
- **time_and_routines:** Chronological language: first, next, then, finally; days of the week; daytime vs nighttime; estimating short durations.
- **geometry_and_spatial_reasoning:** Identify 2D shapes (circle, square, triangle, rectangle) and simple 3D shapes (cube, sphere, cylinder); positional terms (behind, in front of, next to, between).
- **patterns:** Construct and continue repeating patterns (ABB, AAB, ABC).

## 3. Two-Character Story Guidelines
- Two characters ({user_name} and {mother_name}, {father_name}, etc.) exploring math through imaginative play: building block towers, sharing snack biscuits, baking cupcakes, playing shop.
