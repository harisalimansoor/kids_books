# UK Mathematics Question Generation Strategy: Grade 1 (Year 1)

## 1. Scope and Source Provenance
- **Target Year / Stage:** Year 1 (Ages 5–6), Key Stage 1.
- **Authoritative Baseline:** England Mathematics Programme of Study (statutory National Curriculum).
- **Pedagogical Sources:**
  - Oak National Academy / Mathematics Education Innovation (MEI) Units 3–18.
  - Cambridge Primary Mathematics Stage 1.
- **Strict Scope Boundary:** Year 1 content only. Grade 2+ concepts (formal column addition/subtraction with regrouping, times tables recall beyond unitising/grouping in 2s, 5s, 10s, telling time to 5 minutes, 3-digit numbers, formal area in cm², decimals) are strictly excluded.

## 2. Curriculum Topics & Progression
- **Number and Place Value (NPV):** Count to 100 forwards/backwards from any number; count in 2s, 5s, 10s; 1 more / 1 less within 100; teen numbers as '10 and a bit'; odd/even pairing; ordinals 1st to 10th.
- **Addition and Subtraction (AS):** Read/write equations with +, -, =; number bonds to 10 and 20; part-part-whole and 'First, Then, Now' stories; mental operations within 100 without regrouping.
- **Multiplication and Division (MD):** Equal groups of 2, 5, 10; repeated addition; sharing equally; doubling numbers within 10; halving even numbers within 20.
- **Fractions (FR):** Recognise, find, and name a half (1/2) and a quarter (1/4) of an object, shape, or quantity; 2/4 = 1/2.
- **Measurement (MEA):** Compare and measure lengths (cm, m), mass (g, kg), capacity (ml, l) using standard and non-standard units.
- **Money (MON):** Recognise UK coins (1p, 2p, 5p, 10p, 20p, 50p, £1, £2) and notes (£5, £10, £20); simple totals and change.
- **Time (TIM):** Chronological sequencing; days of the week, months of the year; analogue clocks showing o'clock and half past ONLY.
- **Geometry (GEO):** 2D shapes (rectangles, squares, circles, triangles, pentagons, hexagons); 3D shapes (cubes, cuboids, pyramids, spheres, cylinders, cones); turns (whole, half, quarter).
- **Statistics (STA):** Simple tally charts, pictograms (1:1), Carroll and Venn diagrams.
