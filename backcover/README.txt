Backcover Image Folder
======================
Place your backcover image here (e.g. backcover.jpg, backcover.png, etc.).

• Whenever a workbook is generated, this backcover image will automatically be appended as the final page of every PDF.
• To change the backcover, simply replace this image with your new image.
• Supported formats: .jpg, .jpeg, .png, .webp, .bmp
