=== Topic: Working with Numbers to 10 ===

Count out loud to 10.
How many? Write the number in the box.
Draw the domino pattern for 6.
Is 6 an odd or even number?
Draw 1 more. Count how many objects there are now. Complete the number sentence.
Use counters and a ten frame to help you add 1 more.
Write a number sentence for the story: There are 3 children playing football. 2 more children join them.
Complete the number sentence for this story.
Tell your partner what is happening in this story and write the number sentence.
How many counters are there? Combine the parts to find the whole.
Use the part-whole diagram to combine the parts and find the total.
Estimate the total number of spots on each domino. Write a number sentence for each.
Complete each part-whole diagram and write a number sentence for each.
If 10 is the whole, what could the two parts be? Use counters to help you find all the ways to make 10.
Cross out 1 bead and 1 counter. Count how many there are now.
Write the number sentence for this subtraction story: There were 5 ducks in the pond. 2 ducks left the pond.
Write a subtraction sentence for this story: 5 take away 1 equals 4.
Find out how many are left over after taking a part away from the whole.
Complete each part-whole diagram and write a subtraction sentence for each.
Complete each subtraction wall and find the missing numbers.
