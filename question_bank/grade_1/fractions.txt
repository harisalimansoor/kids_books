=== Topic: Fractions ===

This cookie is a whole. How many parts is the cookie cut into? Are they the same as each other?
This bar of chocolate is a whole. How many parts is the chocolate cut into? Are they the same as each other?
Find half of a square.
Is this shape cut in half?
Draw a line on each shape to show two halves.
Draw a line on each shape to show two pieces that are not halves.
Draw a ring around the shapes that have been folded in half.
Draw the other half to make a whole house.
Draw half of each of these shapes.
Fold one square in half corner to corner. Cut along the fold line. How many triangles do you have?
Fold one of the triangles in half. Cut along the fold. How many small triangles do you have now?
Fold the other square side to side. Cut along the fold. How many halves do you have?
Fold them in half again and cut along the fold. How many small squares do you have now?
Make a pattern using all your shapes so that the edges fit together.
Compare your pattern with your partner’s. Is it the same or different?
Draw a ring around the shapes that show a half.
Colour one half of each shape.
Put a ✓ in the box if half of the shape is coloured. Put a ✗ in the box if half is not coloured.
Colour the shapes that have equal parts.
Cross out the shapes that do not have equal parts.
