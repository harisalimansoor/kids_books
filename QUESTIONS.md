# Example Questions by Category

Sample questions with placeholders — static scenes, counts 1–10, no containers, no sequential actions.

## ADDITION
1. Look at the picture! {grandmother_name} is holding 2 stickers, {mother_name} has 5 stickers in front of her, and {user_name} are showing 2 stickers. How many stickers can {user_name} count altogether?
2. Look at the picture! {grandfather_name} has 6 marbles in front of him and {father_name} has 3 marbles in front of him. How many marbles are there altogether?
3. Look at the picture! {mother_name} has 4 books in front of her, {sibling_name} has 0 books in front of him, and {user_name} have 5 books in front of {user_name}. How many books can {user_name} count altogether?
4. Look at the picture! {user_name} have 3 apples in front of {user_name} and {grandmother_name} has 7 apples in front of her. How many apples are there altogether?

## SUBTRACTION
1. Look at the picture! {grandfather_name} has 9 mangoes in front of him and 4 of them are crossed out. How many mangoes are not crossed out?
2. Look at the picture! {grandmother_name} has 7 apples in front of her, and 3 of them have a red X on them. How many apples are left?
3. Look at the picture! {mother_name} has 10 pencils in front of her, and 2 pencils are crossed out. How many pencils are still there?
4. Look at the picture! There are 8 balls on the floor in front of {user_name}, and 5 of them are crossed out. How many balls are left?

## MULTIPLICATION & DIVISION
1. Look at the picture! {grandmother_name} has 2 marbles in front of her, and there are 3 groups of 2 marbles next to them. How many marbles are there altogether?
2. Look at the picture! There are 8 blocks arranged in rows in front of {user_name}. Each row has 2 blocks. How many rows of 2 blocks are there?
3. Look at the picture! There are 9 blocks arranged in a square in front of you. How many blocks are in each row?
4. Look at the picture! There are 10 blocks in a row in front of {user_name}, grouped into sets of 2. How many sets of 2 blocks are there?

## FRACTIONS
1. Look at the picture! {grandmother_name} has 4 books in front of her. Half of them are in a coloured circle. How many books are inside the circle?
2. Look at the picture! There are 8 cookies on the table in front of {user_name}, divided into 4 equal groups. One group is circled. How many cookies are in the circled group?
3. Look at the picture! There are 6 oranges in front of {user_name}. Each orange is cut into 2 halves. How many orange halves are there altogether?
4. Look at the picture! There are 10 stickers on the floor in front of {user_name}, divided into 5 equal groups. One group is circled. How many stickers are in the circled group?

## MONEY
1. Look at the picture! {grandmother_name} has 4p in coins in front of her and 5p in coins next to them. How much money is in front of {grandmother_name} altogether?
2. Look at the picture! There are 6 pencils on the table in front of {user_name}. Each pencil has a price tag showing 2p. How much does it cost to buy all 6 pencils?
3. Look at the picture! There are three 10p coins on the table in front of {user_name}. How much money is there?
4. Look at the picture! {father_name} has 8p in coins in front of him and 3p in coins next to them. How much money does {father_name} have altogether?

## TIME
1. Look at the picture! There is a calendar for October on the wall. How many days are there in October?
2. Look at the picture! {mother_name} is pointing to a clock showing 1 hour. There are 60 small marks around the clock face. How many minutes are in 1 hour?
3. Look at the picture! There is a calendar for June on the wall. {sibling_name} is pointing to it. How many days are there in June?
4. Look at the picture! There is a clock in front of {user_name}. The clock shows 30 minutes. How many minutes are shown on the clock face?

## MEASUREMENT
1. Look at the picture! There is a ruler next to a piece of squared paper on the table. {mother_name} is showing it. How long is the piece of squared paper?
2. Look at the picture! {grandmother_name} is holding a measuring jug with water in it, showing 1 litre. How many millilitres are in the jug?
3. Look at the picture! There is a 1 litre container in front of {user_name}. Next to it is a measuring cup. {father_name} is pointing to the numbers. How many millilitres does the 1 litre container hold?
4. Look at the picture! There is a block on the table with a label showing 1 gram. {grandfather_name} is standing next to it. What can {user_name} use to measure the weight of this block?

## GEOMETRY
1. Look at the picture! {mother_name} is holding a piece of paper in front of her. It has 4 equal sides and 4 corners. What shape is it?
2. Look at the picture! {grandmother_name} is holding a piece of paper in front of her. It is round like a plate. What shape is it?
3. Look at the picture! There are different shapes drawn on the squared paper in front of {user_name}. {sibling_name} is pointing to them. How many of each shape are there?
4. Look at the picture! There are 3 boxes in front of {user_name}. One box has cubes, one has spheres, and one has pyramids. {grandfather_name} is pointing to one shape that is in the wrong box. Which shape should not be there?

## NUMBER & PLACE VALUE
1. Look at the picture! There is a number line in front of {user_name} with numbers on stones. {mother_name} is pointing to the stone with 8. What number is on the stone just before 8?
2. Look at the picture! There is a number line on the floor in front of {user_name}. You can see 45 and 46, and an arrow pointing to the next number. {grandfather_name} is standing next to it. What number comes after 46?
3. Look at the picture! There are 7 number cards on the table: 10, 15, 20, 25, 30, 35, 40. {sibling_name} is pointing to the gap. One number between 20 and 25 is missing. What number is missing?
4. Look at the picture! There are blocks with numbers in a row: 11, 12, ?, 14. {grandmother_name} is pointing to the blank block. What number is on the missing block?

## GENERAL MATH
1. Look at the picture! There are 9 balls on the floor in front of {user_name}. 3 of them have a red X and 2 more have a blue X. How many balls have no X marks?
2. Look at the picture! There are 10 children standing in a line. 6 of them are wearing blue shirts and the rest are wearing pink shirts. {mother_name} is pointing to them. How many children are wearing pink shirts?
3. Look at the picture! There are 8 birds on a tree branch and 4 birds in the sky. 5 of the birds have a red X on them. {grandfather_name} is pointing to the birds. How many birds have no X mark?
4. Look at the picture! There are 6 toy cars in front of {sibling_name} and 4 toy cars in front of {user_name}. How many toy cars are there altogether?
